## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** LOs and mortgage shops know referrals aren't enough, but paid acquisition feels expensive, opaque, and risky — especially when prior agencies delivered volume without quality.

**Why alternatives fall short:**

- Generic marketing agencies don't understand mortgage products, compliance, or ICP nuance (DSCR vs RM vs purchase)
- DIY Meta ads burn budget without creative iteration discipline or funnel-stage messaging
- Lead vendors sell shared leads with no control over qualification or nurture
- In-house hires are slow to hire, expensive, and rarely span creative + media + ops

**What it costs them:** Wasted ad spend, LO time on bad appointments, slow pipeline growth, compliance anxiety, and opportunity cost vs competitors who already run paid systems

**Emotional tension:** Skepticism from past agency burns; fear of looking scammy in ads; pressure to grow production without hiring a full marketing department

## Competitive Landscape

**Direct:** Other mortgage-focused marketing agencies and Meta ads shops for LOs — often weak on creative iteration, compliance guardrails, or end-to-end funnel + setter integration

**Secondary:** Lead vendors, Zillow/referral platforms, in-house media buyers — solve "leads" without owning creative quality, qualification, or nurture

**Indirect:** Referral-only growth, conferences, realtor partnerships — zero ad spend but capped and unpredictable

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators:**

- Product-specific DNA pods (DSCR, reverse mortgage) with compliance guardrails and ICP intelligence baked in
- Creative studio with format modules (UGC, testimonial, educational) and ideation matrices — not one-size ad templates
- Full stack: ads → landing/VSL → nurture → setter scripts in one operating system
- Operating docs and AI-ready knowledge base for repeatable fulfillment quality
- Pain-point creative targeting — messaging IS the targeting; no demographic filters needed; ad creative self-selects the right audience by speaking to specific life experiences (widow, healthcare costs, inflation, mortgage payment elimination), not product labels; the algorithm finds the right people when the message is precise
- Outcome-first messaging doctrine — all creative, funnel copy, and call scripts lead with the borrower's desired outcome, never the product name; the product is prescribed after the pain is diagnosed (doctor analogy); avoids triggering product stigma before trust is established
- Angle-matched funnel architecture — each ad angle has a dedicated landing page and messaging track; the message stays congruent from ad impression to booked call; no native autofill forms; custom thank-you pages pre-warm leads before any contact is made
- Live human call reps trained on mortgage products and objections — real person setters (not AI) call within 5 minutes of form submission, 12 hours/day, 7 days/week; represent as the LO's assistant; qualify on the call, then book calendar appointments or live-transfer; LO only handles calls that are already pre-qualified
- Pixel conditioning system — proposal-made and funded-loan events fire back to Meta as downstream conversion signals; algorithm progressively learns the highest-value audience profile across improvement tiers; quality compounds over time without increasing spend

**How we do it differently:** Build per-product marketing doctrine, angle libraries, and funnel-stage messaging before scaling spend; iterate creative in batches with KPI scorecards. Most competitors market to the 2–10% who already know about a product (e.g. searching "reverse mortgage calculator"). Waiz targets the 80–90% who don't know or hold misconceptions — a larger, cheaper, less competitive pool that converts better when reached with outcome-first messaging.

**Why that's better:** Faster learning loops, safer claims, higher relevance to the borrower/investor ICP, less LO time wasted on unqualified calls

**Why customers choose us:** They want an operator who already solved mortgage-specific acquisition — not a generalist agency learning on their dime

## Pricing Model

**Business model:** Retainer + ad spend performance kicker. Team tiers add a per-loan performance fee that activates above a volume threshold. A rev-share upgrade is available for proven accounts (6+ months, clean attribution).

**Tier summary:**

| Tier | LO Count | Min Ad Spend | Setup | Monthly | Ad Spend Kicker | Performance Kicker |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Solo | 1 LO | $2,000/month | $2,500 | $1,689 | 10% over $4k | None |
| Team Starter | 2–4 LOs | $4,000/month | $3,500 | $3,200 | 8% over $8k combined | $500/funded loan after 4+ loans/month |
| Team Growth | 5–10 LOs | $10,000/month | $5,000 | $5,500 | 7% over $15k combined | $600/funded loan after 8+ loans/month |
| Branch | 10–20+ LOs | $20,000/month | $7,500 | $8,000–12,000 (custom) | 5–6% over $25k (negotiated) | Custom |

**Performance rev-share option (proven accounts):** Reduces base retainer to $1,500–2,500/month in exchange for a $1,200–1,500/funded loan fee with no threshold. Requires 6+ months on standard retainer, clean attribution, and a signed addendum. Gabe must approve. Full details in [pricing-architecture.md](../docs/acquisition/offer/pricing-architecture.md).

**Performance kicker framing:** The kicker is positioned as a risk-removal close — the client only pays more when the team closes loans above the threshold. It aligns incentives and makes the retainer feel like a floor, not a ceiling.

**Pricing authority:** Gabe owns all pricing decisions. Setters never quote or discuss pricing.
