## Product Overview

**One-liner:** Waiz Media builds and runs paid acquisition systems for mortgage and real-estate professionals who need qualified leads, not vanity metrics.

**What it does:** Waiz Media is a performance marketing agency specializing in Meta ads, funnel architecture, creative production, and fulfillment operations for loan officers and mortgage-adjacent businesses. The team designs campaigns, produces ad creative (UGC, testimonial, educational formats), manages media buying, and supports lead nurture through booking and setter workflows.

**Product category:** Performance marketing agency / lead generation partner for mortgage professionals

**Product type:** B2B service (done-for-you marketing + fulfillment)

**Business model:** Client retainer / fulfillment engagement for ad management, creative, and lead systems. Pricing per approved pricing sheet — do not quote unapproved numbers in content.

## Target Audience

**Target companies:** Independent loan officers, mortgage brokers, and small-to-mid mortgage shops; real-estate investor lending lines (DSCR refinance); reverse mortgage specialists

**Decision-makers:** LO / broker owner, marketing lead, operations lead, sometimes branch manager

**Primary use case:** Generate consistent, qualified inbound leads (calls, form fills, booked appointments) from paid social — without the LO building an internal marketing team

**Jobs to be done:**
- Get more qualified conversations with borrowers/investors who match their lending products
- Replace inconsistent referral-only growth with a predictable lead pipeline
- Offload creative, media buying, and funnel execution to operators who understand mortgage compliance

**Use cases:**
- DSCR refinance campaigns for real-estate investors
- Reverse mortgage lead acquisition for retiree homeowners
- Pre-call nurture and setter workflows for high-ticket LO sales
- LinkedIn outbound for loan officer prospecting (Waiz acquisition lane)

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** LOs and mortgage shops know referrals aren't enough, but paid acquisition feels expensive, opaque, and risky — especially when prior agencies delivered volume without quality.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Generic marketing agencies don't understand mortgage products, compliance, or ICP nuance (DSCR vs RM vs purchase)
- DIY Meta ads burn budget without creative iteration discipline or funnel-stage messaging
- Lead vendors sell shared leads with no control over qualification or nurture
- In-house hires are slow to hire, expensive, and rarely span creative + media + ops

**What it costs them:** Wasted ad spend, LO time on bad appointments, slow pipeline growth, compliance anxiety, and opportunity cost vs competitors who already run paid systems

**Emotional tension:** Skepticism from past agency burns; fear of looking scammy in ads; pressure to grow production without hiring a full marketing department

## Competitive Landscape

**Direct:** Other mortgage-focused marketing agencies and Meta ads shops for LOs — often weak on creative iteration, compliance guardrails, or end-to-end funnel + setter integration

**Secondary:** Lead vendors, Zillow/referral platforms, in-house media buyers — solve "leads" without owning creative quality, qualification, or nurture

**Indirect:** Referral-only growth, conferences, realtor partnerships — zero ad spend but capped and unpredictable

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators:**
- Product-specific DNA pods (DSCR, reverse mortgage) with compliance guardrails and ICP intelligence baked in
- Creative studio with format modules (UGC, testimonial, educational) and ideation matrices — not one-size ad templates
- Full stack: ads → landing/VSL → nurture → setter scripts in one operating system
- Operating docs and AI-ready knowledge base for repeatable fulfillment quality

**How we do it differently:** Build per-product marketing doctrine, angle libraries, and funnel-stage messaging before scaling spend; iterate creative in batches with KPI scorecards

**Why that's better:** Faster learning loops, safer claims, higher relevance to the borrower/investor ICP, less LO time wasted on unqualified calls

**Why customers choose us:** They want an operator who already solved mortgage-specific acquisition — not a generalist agency learning on their dime

## Objections

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "I've been burned by agencies before" | Anchor on qualification + creative iteration + transparent KPIs; show product-specific playbooks and compliance standards |
| "I don't know if paid leads will be quality" | Separate TOF learning from BOF conversion; pre-call nurture + setter qualification; scorecard-driven testing |
| "Ads feel scammy / compliance scares me" | Guardrailed copy standards, disclosure patterns, dramatization rules for testimonials; compliance review gates |
| "I can do this myself cheaper" | True cost = LO time + learning curve + wasted spend; we compress time-to-validated creative and funnel |

**Anti-persona:** LOs who want guaranteed approvals/rates in ads, refuse compliance guardrails, need purchase financing when product is refinance-only, or want cheapest lead volume over qualification
