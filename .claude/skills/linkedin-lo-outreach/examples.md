# LinkedIn LO Outreach — Examples

## Good setter opener (Angle 2, Professional)

> Hey Tom, your post on closing four HECMs in March stood out — rare consistency in this rate stretch. How’s April tracking against that?

**Why:** Specific post detail, one question, no pitch.

---

## Bad setter opener

> Hi Tom! I came across your profile and was curious about your business. We help loan officers get more leads. Would love to connect!

**Fails:** `curious`, generic compliment, pitch in message 1, no specific signal.

---

## Good Gabriel permission pivot

> That referral dip is what I’m hearing from almost every shop in reverse right now. When I hit the same wall, the lever wasn’t rates — it was conversation volume at the top of the funnel. Mind if I bounce a couple thoughts off you?

**Why:** I-moment, climate alignment, permission before advice.

---

## Bad Gabriel reply

> Great to hear! We’re a full-service agency for reverse mortgage LOs with AI follow-up and Meta ads. Here’s my Calendly: [link]

**Fails:** Pitch before discovery, Calendly too early, no permission, paragraph dump.

---

## Good value bump

> Hey Lisa — you mentioned outsourcing content wasn’t bringing real conversations, only vanity metrics. One thing I’ve seen fix that in reverse is tightening the message to homeowner problems instead of LO jargon. If you want, I can send the short checklist we use before we touch ads.

**Why:** Tied to her exact words; low pressure; optional next step.

---

## Bad value bump

> Just circling back — any thoughts on my last message?

**Fails:** Banned phrase, no new value, no tie to stated pain.

---

## Good book ask

> Glad that’s useful. I can walk through how the acquisition system works for reverse-only shops in 25 minutes. I have Tuesday 10am or 2pm ET, or Wednesday 11am — which works? Send your best email and I’ll shoot the invite.

**Why:** Specific times, email for invite, short.

---

## Good connect micro-note

> Hey Tom — saw your comment on Dan's post about referral volume. Would be good to connect.

**Why:** Under 200 chars, one signal, zero pitch.

---

## Good setter bump (48–72h)

> Hey Tom — bumping this once. You’d mentioned referrals feeling thin — is that still the main headache or did Q2 pick up?

---

## Good ghost touch 3 (opt-out)

> Tom — happy to leave you alone if timing’s wrong, just say the word. If volume’s still the issue, I’m here.
