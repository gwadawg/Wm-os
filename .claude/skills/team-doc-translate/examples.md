## After (team — design-oriented)

```markdown
SETTER DAILY OPERATIONS
=====================

AT A GLANCE
• Who: Setter
• When: Every working day before outreach and calls
• Outcome: Priority list worked, outbound done, EOD checklist complete
• Questions: Escalate to Gabriel

What this is for
----------------
This is your daily rhythm for hunting new appointments and servicing booked calls.
Two modes matter: Hunt days (no closings) and Normal days (calls + hunting).

Before you start
----------------
• CRM and priority list open
• Yesterday's notes reviewed
• Training block protected at 9:30 AM

How to do it
------------
Phase 1 — Morning prep (6:00–9:00)
1. Review prep and new notes.
2. Work the priority list in order.

Phase 2 — Hunt or split day
• Hunt day: priority list + outbound blocks all day.
• Normal day: take scheduled close calls, then return to priority list.

Done right looks like
---------------------
• Every priority-list block used or consciously deferred
• Outbound block completed on hunt days
• EOD checklist signed off

When to get help
----------------
Pricing, deal structure, or unclear disqualification → Gabriel.

Related procedures
------------------
• Intro Call Qualification
• No Shows And Show Rate
```
