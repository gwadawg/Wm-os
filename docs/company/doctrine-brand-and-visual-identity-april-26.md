# Waiz Media Brand And Visual Identity

## Purpose

This doctrine defines the visual direction for Waiz Media assets so internal teams and AI agents can produce consistent, brand-aligned designs.

## Scope

Use this document for website sections, landing pages, sales materials, internal design prompts, client-facing documents, UI mockups, presentation assets, and any AI-generated visual direction.

This document covers visual style, color palette, typography direction, design rules, and overall brand feel. It does not define offer architecture, sales copy, or company positioning in detail.

## Owner

Operations owns this document until a dedicated brand owner is assigned.

## When To Use

Use this document when:

- Creating design prompts for AI tools.
- Designing landing pages or funnels.
- Creating client-facing documents or presentations.
- Reviewing whether a visual asset fits Waiz Media.
- Updating website or SaaS-style interface components.

## Brand Identity

Waiz Media should look like a modern marketing agency with a professional fintech aesthetic and clean SaaS-style interface.

The visual identity should signal:

- Premium.
- Data-driven.
- Growth-focused.
- Modern.
- Professional.
- Technology-enabled.

## Color Palette

| Color | Hex Code | Primary Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Primary Navy Blue | `#061A4A` | Headers, hero sections, strong emphasis. |
| Secondary Royal Blue | `#0E2F73` | Gradients and supporting elements. |
| Accent Blue | `#4FA3FF` | Icons, links, highlights, and small emphasis states. |
| Growth Accent Green | `#7CFF7A` | Growth indicators, arrows, performance moments, and positive change. |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Primary background. |
| Light Background | `#F5F7FB` | Secondary background and soft section contrast. |
| Dark Text | `#0B1220` | Body text and readable foreground content. |

## Design Rules

- Use generous white space.
- Make white the primary background.
- Use navy blue for headers, hero sections, and strong emphasis.
- Use royal blue for gradients and supporting elements.
- Use accent blue for icons, links, highlights, and subtle UI emphasis.
- Use green sparingly for growth, arrows, performance, or positive change.
- Keep layouts minimal and easy to scan.
- Prioritize contrast and readability.

## Visual Style

Waiz Media design should use:

- Clean modern SaaS design.
- High-contrast typography.
- Minimal layout.
- Professional marketing agency aesthetics.
- Technology and growth-oriented visual cues.

Avoid:

- Cluttered sections.
- Overly playful visual systems.
- Generic stock-agency visuals.
- Low-contrast text.
- Heavy use of green outside performance or growth moments.

## Typography Direction

Use typography that feels modern, confident, and readable.

Headline direction:

- Bold modern sans-serif.
- Large and confident.
- Clear hierarchy.

Body direction:

- Clean sans-serif.
- Easy to read.
- Strong contrast against the background.

## Overall Feeling

Every Waiz Media visual asset should feel like:

- A premium marketing agency.
- A data-driven growth partner.
- A modern fintech interface.
- A company that builds serious acquisition infrastructure.

## AI Design Prompt Baseline

Use this baseline when prompting AI design tools:

> Design using the Waiz Media brand style: modern marketing agency, professional fintech aesthetic, clean SaaS-style UI, lots of white space, navy headers, royal blue supporting gradients, accent blue highlights, sparse growth green for performance moments, high-contrast modern sans-serif typography, minimal layout, and a premium data-driven growth feel.

## Quality Bar

A Waiz Media design is on-brand when it:

- Looks premium and credible to reverse mortgage loan officers.
- Feels modern without becoming trendy or gimmicky.
- Uses white space and contrast to create clarity.
- Uses the approved color palette intentionally.
- Feels like business infrastructure, not a generic marketing template.
- Supports trust, growth, and operational competence.
