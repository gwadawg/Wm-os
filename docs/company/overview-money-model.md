# Waiz Media Money Model

## Purpose

This document is how Waiz Media gets paid. It names the product lanes,
the two offers sold to new clients, and the pricing shape of each
offer.

## Scope

Use this when a proposal, script, prompt, or sales conversation needs
the commercial model.

Dollar amounts are not in this document. Startup fees, retainers,
per-conversation rates, and ad-spend percentages live on the founder
pricing sheet. Do not invent them.

What each offer includes is in
[Client Offers](../acquisition/offer/client-offers.md). This page
keeps the pricing shape and the rules. It does not repeat the
deliverable list.

Existing clients stay on the structure in their current agreement.
This document does not reprice them.

## Owner

Operations owns the structure. Gabe owns pricing, deal structure, and
which offer a prospect is shown.

## When To Use

- Writing or revising a proposal, quote, or sales script.
- Deciding which pricing shape a new client is on.
- Checking that an AI output did not invent a third way to get paid.
- Training someone on what Waiz sells now.

## The Model

Waiz Media sells acquisition to loan officers on two product lanes.
Each new client is on one of two offers. Both offers use the same
three-part price. The third part is what changes.

| | Core offer | Leads-only offer |
|---|---|---|
| Product lane | DSCR or reverse mortgage | DSCR or reverse mortgage |
| What they buy | A system that ends in a real conversation | A system that ends in a lead |
| Startup | Yes | Yes |
| Retainer | Yes | Yes |
| Third leg | Pay per legitimate conversation | Percent of that month's ad spend |

Amounts inside each leg vary by who the offer is made to. The shape
does not vary by lane. DSCR and reverse mortgage use the same shape.

The leads-only offer does not have an official name yet. "Leads-only"
is the working name.

## Products

| Lane | Who the client serves |
|---|---|
| DSCR | Loan officers originating debt-service-coverage loans, typically for real-estate investors. |
| Reverse mortgage | Loan officers originating reverse mortgages, typically for homeowners 62 and older. |

Forward mortgage, generalist loan officer marketing, and other niches
are out of scope unless Gabe approves a separate offer.

## Pricing Rule

Pricing is founder-only. Quote startup, retainer, per-conversation,
or ad-spend percent only from the current founder pricing sheet.

Setters do not quote, negotiate, or discuss pricing or deal structure.
Pricing questions go to Gabe.

## Offer Routing Logic

New clients are offered the core offer or the leads-only offer.

| If the client wants | Offer |
|---|---|
| Waiz to prequalify leads and deliver a live transfer or a booking on their calendar | Core offer |
| The marketing system and the leads, and they will work the leads themselves | Leads-only offer |

There is no default downsell. Do not add a third offer, a Boot Camp
path, a revenue share, or a pay-later deal unless Gabe approves it
for that prospect.

The qualification gates that decide who is shown which offer are still
open. Until those gates exist, Gabe chooses the offer.

## Operating Rules

| Rule | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Two offers | New clients are quoted on the core offer or the leads-only offer. |
| Same shape on both lanes | DSCR and reverse mortgage share the pricing shape. Amounts can differ by buyer. |
| Startup and retainer always | Both offers start with a startup fee and a retainer. The third leg is additional. |
| Pay per legitimate conversation | On the core offer, the extra fee applies only to a real introduction or conversation. A conversation that is not legitimate is not billed. |
| Percent of ad spend | On the leads-only offer, the extra fee is a percentage of that month's ad spend. |
| Exclusive leads | Leads produced for a client belong to that client. There is no shared lead pool. |
| Existing clients stay put | Signed clients keep their current agreement until Gabe changes it. |
| Gabe owns the exception | Any structure outside these two offers needs Gabe's approval before it is offered. |

## What This Model Is Not

- Not a percentage of commission or a funded-loan split.
- Not work-now-pay-later.
- Not performance-only. The startup fee and the retainer are due even when the third leg is small.
- Not a shared lead list. The leads-only offer is a marketing and funnel system with lead delivery.
- Not a reverse-mortgage-only model. DSCR is a current product lane.

## Quality Bar

Anything that describes how Waiz gets paid should:

- Name the lane: DSCR or reverse mortgage.
- Name the offer: core or leads-only.
- State the price as startup, retainer, and the third leg for that offer.
- Pull dollar amounts only from the founder pricing sheet.
- Leave existing clients on their signed structure.

## Metrics

- Share of new clients on the core offer versus the leads-only offer.
- Startup fees collected.
- Retainer retention.
- Legitimate conversations delivered, and conversation fees billed, on the core offer.
- Share of core-offer conversations rejected as not legitimate.
- Ad spend and the ad-spend fee billed on the leads-only offer.
