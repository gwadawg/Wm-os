# Personal Brand DNA

*Founder-confirmed 2026-06-17 via grilling session. knowledge-capture may propose
updates — confirm before changing positioning.*

## 1. Brand Overview

**Public name / handle:** Gabe Goertzen / @gabeegoertzen

**One-liner:** The guy who takes massive risk and does extraordinary things — in
business, physical activity, sports, and personal development — with extreme
beliefs and controversial, straight-shooting takes on what it actually takes to
win.

**What you talk about (2–3 sentences):** Building Waiz Media from nothing, the
ambitious young-entrepreneur lifestyle, travel, money concepts, tactical AI and
operations, and harsh personal-development truths. Mix of journey content,
hot takes, and high-production style edits — not always talking-head.

**Content category:** Operator-in-public / creator-educator (lifestyle-adjacent)

**Platforms (primary):** Instagram Reels, Meta Ads (repurpose), style-edit /
concept tests

## 2. Target Audience

**Who watches your content:** Upcoming entrepreneurs, aspiring entrepreneurs,
loan officers, and people attracted to ambitious young-entrepreneur lifestyle,
travel, money, and operator content.

**What they want from you:** Permission to take risk, tactical how-to's (AI,
ops), inspiration from the journey, identity ("this is the life I'm building"),
and unfiltered opinions that cut through soft motivational content.

**Primary use case:** Follow for the full picture — journey, beliefs, lifestyle,
and operator lessons. Waiz Media account stays focused on LO lead generation;
personal page is the umbrella brand.

**Jobs to be done:**
- See what's possible when you bet on yourself and go all-in
- Learn tactical AI, ops, and business lessons from someone building in real time
- Feel part of an ambitious, travel-capable, money-aware entrepreneur identity

## 4. Problems & Pain Points (audience)

**Core problem:** They want the ambitious entrepreneur life but are stuck in
comfort, soft advice, or niche B2B content that doesn't speak to who they're
becoming.

**Why alternatives fall short:** Most creator content is either too compassionate
/ motivational without accountability, too generic lifestyle, or too narrow
(product-only B2B). Few combine risk, ops tactically, and unfiltered truth.

**Emotional tension:** Fear of going all-in; guilt about wanting money and
status; skepticism that "sell everything and build" is real; FOMO on personal
brand being "too late."

## 6. Differentiation

**What makes your perspective unique:** Lived the burn-the-boats arc (sold
everything → zero → solo scale → banks/lenders/high-value partners). Not
theory. Goofy + aggressive + elaborative. Will say the uncomfortable thing.
Also tests non-talking-head edits and concept formats.

**Proof you can speak on this:** Built Waiz Media with no partners from nothing;
partnerships with banks, lenders, and high-value partners; operator running AI
and ops systems daily.

## 7. Objections & Anti-Audience

| Objection / pushback | Response |
|---------------------|----------|
| "Too harsh / not compassionate enough" | Intentional — comfort doesn't change behavior; accountability does |
| "Just another agency guy flexing" | Journey + tactics + beliefs, not just client wins; personal ≠ Waiz sales |
| "All talk, no substance" | Tactical AI/ops how-to's and documented journey, not just hot takes |

**Anti-audience:** People who want gentle validation only, hate risk talk, or
want guaranteed outcomes without sacrifice. Not for those offended by strong
language or controversial frames.

## 8. Switching Dynamics (why they follow / stay)

**Push:** Soft motivational content that doesn't move the needle; niche B2B
pages that ignore lifestyle; fear of starting personal brand too late.

**Pull:** Permission to go extreme; proof from someone who did it; mix of
entertainment (edits, concepts) and operator substance.

**Habit:** Consuming generic entrepreneur Instagram — low stakes, no
accountability.

**Anxiety:** Looking stupid on camera; saying the wrong controversial thing;
investing time in brand vs. business (Gabe's North Star is still the business).

## 10. Brand Voice

**Tone:** Goofy, somewhat aggressive on concepts, elaborative, well-spoken,
straightforward, occasionally harsh (Hormozi-adjacent accountability)

**Style:** Story-first AND hot-take-first — test both. Talking head plus
high-production style edits (cool sounds, concepts not always featuring Gabe).

**Personality (3–5 adjectives):** Goofy, aggressive (conceptually), elaborative,
well-spoken, risk-forward, controversial

**On camera:** High energy acceptable; swearing fine; vary format — don't lock
into one mode. Test concept edits and audio-driven pieces alongside direct
camera.
