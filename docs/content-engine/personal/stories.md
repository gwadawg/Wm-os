## Stories

### S1 — One-way ticket to Rio (burn the boats)

**Setup:** Age 22–23. Six-figure LO job for his age. Deep dream to move abroad
and build something he owned — not just sell someone else's product. Sold
everything: quit job, gave up car, sold furniture, sold apartment. Bought a
one-way ticket to Rio de Janeiro with no clear business plan. Worked on the
floor of his apartment. Solo — no partners.

**Tension:** Months of cold calls and sales calls — hundreds of leads, door shut
in face repeatedly. Didn't know how he'd make payroll. Hell period but woke up
with a smile and showed up anyway. Built Waiz from that floor.

**Turn:** Kept showing up → scaled Waiz solo into banks, lenders, and
high-value partners.

**Lesson / belief link:** B1, B3, B7 (show up when it's hell), B8 (individual
risk from sports)

**Best formats:** reel (multi-part series), carousel (timeline), trial-concept

**Sensitive / skip:** Specific payroll numbers, client/partner names unless
approved. Rio location is on-brand — confirm if ever want to anonymize.

**Series potential:** Part 1 = the quit | Part 2 = the cold call desert | Part
3 = payroll hell | Part 4 = what broke through

### S2 — Bottom of the LO team → Presidents Club

**Setup:** Age 18–19. Got into mortgage, went all-in, studied hard for licensing,
became a loan officer. Passionate about sales and mortgages.

**Tension:** Was not a good LO at first — bottom of the team for months.

**Turn:** Turning point: went all-in again, put heart into it, worked up to
Presidents Club and top producer on the team.

**Lesson / belief link:** B7 (show up after failure), B2 (soft motivation doesn't
build top producers)

**Best formats:** reel, carousel (before/after mindset shift)

**Sensitive / skip:** Team/company name unless approved.

**Hook seed:** "I was the worst LO on my team for months — here's what changed"

### S5 — Magic, skate, surf → entrepreneur mindset

**Setup:** Grew up doing magic (magician), skateboarding, surfing — individual
sports and performance, not team-dependent.

**Tension:** Each sport forced a different mental reprogram:
- **Magic:** sell, speak to crowds, guide attention, read communication differently
- **Skate:** massive risk, fall, get back up — repeat
- **Surf:** face fear, enter unknown water, extreme patience — days without catching a wave

**Turn:** Aggregate of these passions built the character and risk tolerance
that made Rio and solo scaling possible.

**Lesson / belief link:** B8 (individual sports → individual risk), B9 (magic →
sales/communication)

**Best formats:** reel (metaphor series — one sport per video), carousel (3 sports
→ 3 business lessons)

**Sensitive / skip:** None flagged.

**Series potential:**
- "Skateboarding taught me more about business than any course"
- "Surfing is the best metaphor for entrepreneurship nobody uses"
- "Being a magician is why I'm good at sales"

### S6 — ADHD, hyperfocus, and building systems

**Setup:** ADHD — distracted, hyper-focused on things he likes. Constant internal
battle between scatter and deep focus.

**Tension:** Willpower alone doesn't work; disorder makes unstructured life harder
at scale.

**Turn:** Learned to structure, systemize, and operationalize life and business so
ADHD becomes manageable — directly feeds AI/ops content and how Waiz runs.

**Lesson / belief link:** B5 (leverage/systems), B10 (ADHD managed through ops,
not motivation)

**Best formats:** reel, carousel (systems for ADHD operators), searchable how-to

**Sensitive / skip:** Medical framing — keep to lived experience + systems, not
clinical advice.
