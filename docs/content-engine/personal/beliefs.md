# Beliefs Library

Core beliefs, philosophies, and reframes you repeat on camera. Each entry =
content fuel for reels and carousels.

## How to use

- Tag ideas in `angle-library.md` with belief ID (B1, B2, …)
- One belief can spawn many angles (story, contrarian, proof, myth-bust)

## Beliefs

### B1 — Extraordinary outcomes require extraordinary risk

**Belief:** If you're not willing to take massive risk, you don't get
extraordinary results — in business, sport, or life.

**Why you hold it:** Six-figure LO job at 22–23 → sold everything → one-way
ticket to Rio with no business plan → scaled solo to banks and high-value
partners.

**Content angles:**
- "Safe plans produce safe lives"
- Six-figure job vs. one-way ticket — the actual tradeoff
- What "massive risk" looked like (car, apartment, furniture, job — all gone)

**Counter-argument you address:** "Not everyone can take that kind of risk" —
acknowledge context but don't soften the frame.

---

### B2 — Soft motivation is a trap

**Belief:** Over-compassionate personal development keeps people comfortable;
accountability and harsh truth change behavior (Hormozi-adjacent).

**Why you hold it:** Was bottom of LO team for months — didn't need comfort,
needed to go harder. Audience drowning in gentle content.

**Content angles:**
- "Nobody needs another 'you're doing great' post"
- Bottom of the team → Presidents Club without hand-holding
- Comfort vs. growth

**Counter-argument you address:** "That's toxic" — harsh truth ≠ cruelty; you're
pro-results.

---

### B3 — Burn the boats or stay on the shore

**Belief:** Half-commitment is the real killer — selling everything / going
all-in creates the pressure that forces creative solutions.

**Why you hold it:** Quit six-figure job, sold car/furniture/apartment, one-way
ticket to Rio, no Plan B. Worked on apartment floor with no clear offer.

**Content angles:**
- Plan B is why Plan A fails
- Itemized burn-the-boats list (job, car, apartment, furniture, one-way ticket)
- Solo founder — no partners to hide behind

**Counter-argument you address:** "Reckless" — had sales skills, work ethic, and
years of grind before the jump; not blind gambling.

### B7 — Wake up smiling and show up anyway

**Belief:** The grind period isn't glamorous — cold calls, payroll fear, doors
slammed — but the win is never quitting the day after getting beat.

**Why you hold it:** Months of cold calling in Rio; didn't know how payroll would
hit; still showed up with a smile.

**Content angles:**
- "Everyone wants the highlight reel — nobody wants month 4 of cold calls"
- Payroll hell story (without specific numbers if preferred)
- Door shut in face → same time tomorrow

**Counter-argument you address:** "Toxic positivity" — not pretending it's fine;

### B8 — Individual sports build individual risk tolerance

**Belief:** Skateboarding, surfing, and solo performance sports reprogram you to
take individual risk, fall, and return — without a team to blame.

**Why you hold it:** Skate = fall and get back up. Surf = fear, patience, days
with no wave. Magic = solo performance under pressure.

**Content angles:**
- Skateboarding metaphor reels (fall → get up → business)
- Surfing = patience in business (wait for the wave)
- Why team sports don't teach what solo risk teaches

**Counter-argument you address:** "Stretch metaphor" — tie to specific Rio/LO
moments, not abstract only.

### B10 — ADHD is managed with systems, not motivation

**Belief:** Hyperfocus and distraction aren't fixed by trying harder — you
