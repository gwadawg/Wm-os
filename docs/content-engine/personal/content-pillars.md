# Personal Content Pillars

Five pillars confirmed 2026-06-17. Tag every idea with one primary pillar.

## Pillar criteria (from content-strategy)

Good pillars should:
- Align with what you can authentically speak about
- Match what your audience cares about
- Have social interest (shareable) and/or search interest (searchable)
- Be broad enough for many subtopics

## Pillars

### Pillar 1: Burn-the-boats / building in public

**Rationale:** Core identity — massive risk, solo scale, journey from zero to
banks and high-value partners. Differentiates from generic agency content.

**Subtopic clusters:**
- Selling everything and starting from nothing
- Scaling Waiz solo (no partners)
- Wins, failures, and lessons in real time
- Working with banks, lenders, and high-value partners
- What "extraordinary" actually looks like day-to-day

**Searchable vs shareable:** shareable (story-driven)

---

### Pillar 2: Extreme beliefs / harsh truths (personal development)

**Rationale:** Hormozi-adjacent accountability content — controversial, direct,
may make people uncomfortable. High share potential.

**Subtopic clusters:**
- Risk as a requirement, not a bonus
- Comfort vs. growth — calling out soft motivation
- Controversial takes on discipline, sacrifice, identity
- "Doing what it takes" frames (physical, mental, business)
- Myth-busts on compassionate-only personal development

**Searchable vs shareable:** shareable (contrarian / hot take)

---

### Pillar 3: Ambitious lifestyle / travel / identity

**Rationale:** Attracts lifestyle-curious audience; complements operator content
with aspirational identity.

**Subtopic clusters:**
- Travel and location freedom as byproduct of building
- Young entrepreneur lifestyle (money + ambition + experience)
- Aesthetic / concept edits that sell the identity without flexing
- Sports, physical activity, and mental edge
- Social currency — why personal brand matters even when business is North Star

**Searchable vs shareable:** both (lifestyle discovery + shareable identity)

---

### Pillar 4: Tactical AI + operations

**Rationale:** Substance layer — how-to's that prove operator credibility beyond
motivation.

**Subtopic clusters:**
- AI workflows in agency / business ops
- Systems, automation, and leverage lessons learned
- Tool stacks and what actually moved the needle
- Operator mistakes with AI and ops
- Building fulfillment OS thinking in public

**Searchable vs shareable:** searchable (how-to) + shareable (contrarian tool
takes)

---

### Pillar 5: Format / concept lab (edits + experiments)

**Rationale:** Not all content is talking-head Gabe — test cool edits, sounds,
and concepts to grow craft and reach.

**Subtopic clusters:**
- High-production style edits (audio-driven, visual concepts)
- Hook and format experiments
- Swipe-adapted structures from @marcel.stxm / peers
- Trial concepts before full story pieces
- Trending audio / visual formats with brand-adjacent messaging

**Searchable vs shareable:** shareable (format-forward)

## Pillar → format fit

| Pillar | Best formats | Notes |
|--------|--------------|-------|
| Burn-the-boats | reel, trial-concept | Story arcs, milestone updates |
| Extreme beliefs | reel, carousel | Hot takes, myth-bust carousels |
| Lifestyle / travel | reel, concept edit | Aesthetic + identity; not always on camera |
| AI + ops | carousel, reel | How-to carousels; screen + talk |
| Format lab | trial-concept, reel | Low narrative — test hook/format first |
