# 01 — Anchor: Ferrari Value Frame

## Hook (on-screen + open line)

**Text overlay:** `They can't afford $200? That's not a lead problem.`

**Reframed open (caption or VO intro if needed):**
> When a borrower says they can't pay for counseling or an appraisal — most LOs blame the lead. Wrong frame.

## Thesis

One clip, one tactic: **"can't afford upfront costs" is almost always a value
conveyance problem**, not affordability. Use the Ferrari frame + cash-out math
to reframe without attacking the borrower — and coach your team to see it the
same way.
