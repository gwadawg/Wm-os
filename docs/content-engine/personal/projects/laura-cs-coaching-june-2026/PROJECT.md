## Content thesis

Gabe coaches Laura on **client success as sales**: reframe retention calls when LOs
say leads are bad, budget is tight, or they want to quit — without attacking,
without fake authority, and with word tracks that land.

**Reframe for audience:** Speak to camera as "if you're in CS, sales, or running
an agency with clients who want to churn" — not "Laura, on our call with Tom…"

## Production notes (all clips)

- **Source footage:** Fathom — talking head; occasional screen share (skip share segments)
- **Production format:** `call-clip-edit` — trim dead air, cut Laura interjections where needed, hook text overlay on open

- **Skip:** Client names (Tom, Tony, Jesse), specific pricing ($550 call center, $3,500 spend), Slack/scheduling chatter
- **Sensitive:** Laura's Brazil/age/gender insecurity — keep in clip 09 only if publishing; genericize for public
