## Content thesis

Gabe coaches Pedro on **salvaging dead or ghosted leads** by calling back as a
**second or third voice** — posing as customer success / file closure, not sales.
Lower the guard → replay their words → pull the real objection → re-book.

**Reframe for audience:** Speak to camera as "if you're a setter, LO, or agency
owner with leads that went cold" — not "Pedro, on our call…"
