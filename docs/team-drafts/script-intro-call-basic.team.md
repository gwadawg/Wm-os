# Full Process

Word-for-word guide for setter intro calls: low-pressure discovery, operational qualification, demo booking, and pre-call nurture — without pitching programs or pricing on this call.

- Who: Setter
- When: Every live intro: booked slot, early confirmation call, dialer connect, or intro no-show recovery.
**Before You Start**

Every live intro: booked slot, early confirmation call, dialer connect, or intro no-show recovery. Pick the opening in Stage 1 — Introduction, then run Stages 2–7 (shared).

Stage 1 intro calls (all entry types). Qualification rules (FUN, Boot Camp, financial hard-qualify) live in Intro Call Qualification Framework. Do not use Hell/Bridges/Heaven/Fuel on this call — that depth is for demo/closer discovery.

# How To Do It

## Owner

## North Star

**What this call is:**

A short discovery conversation — not a sales call, not a demo. You gather information about their situation, see if they’re a fit, and if they qualify, you book them with the right person to walk through how everything works in detail.

**What this call is not:**

Closing, full objection handling, or explaining programs, packages, and investment. Save that for the demo.

**Your job:**

Make it feel like a natural conversation. They should feel like we’re deciding if they qualify for the next step — not that they’re being pitched. That frame increases show rates and how much they value the demo.

**Handoff target:**

After a qualified intro, the closer gets FUN confirmation, motivator notes, a booked demo, pre-call video commitment, and verified contact info.

## Call Checklist

Run the call in this order. Don’t hang up until all five are checked. Word-for-word script for each step is below.

- [ ] 1. Frame — Stage 2: not a sales call; your job is fit + no wasted time; quick prep for strategy call; can send resources
- [ ] 2. Qualifying questions — Stage 3: discovery + states, role, forward/reverse, lead gen, production, timeline. FUN pass or route out (framework) — don’t book if not qualified
- [ ] 3. Sold demo — Stage 4: ICP track picked, label back, track-specific pitch, they agreed
- [ ] 4. Tie down appointment + confirm info — Stage 5 book in GHL on the call + Stage 7 date/time locked, phone/email correct, they get our messages, calendar accepted
- [ ] 5. Tie down — commit to watching material — Stage 6: pre-call video sent, they said they’ll watch before the strategy call
📋 After hang-up: post-call notes for [CLOSER NAME].

## Setter Boundaries

Prospects get frustrated when the setter can’t answer programs and pricing — because this call isn’t built for that. Use these lines; do not improvise packages or numbers.

**Frame rule:**

Never pitch on command. Full methodology: Flip the Frame — company description.

**If they ask “What do you do?” / want a rundown**

Full framework: Flip the Frame — company description. Do not pitch on command.

**Default — Framework 1 (reclaim the frame)**

**✉️ COPY & PASTE**

```

But before I do, because what we do looks really different depending on where someone’s at in their business… do you mind if I ask you a couple quick questions first? ```

📋 → Discovery transition. Do not feature-dump.

**If they need a description first — Framework 2 (pick ICP track)**

📋 Pick track from qualifying — use that track’s “Who we are” script (not the generic version).

📋 If yes / “that’s familiar” → Discovery transition.

**✉️ COPY & PASTE**

**If they push price / “what does it cost?”**

**First try**

**✉️ COPY & PASTE**

```

```

**If yes — ballpark range (wide, custom, then fit check)**

Use the current approved intro range from the live pricing sheet (founder-approved). Do not invent numbers.

**✉️ COPY & PASTE**

```

```

**✉️ COPY & PASTE**

```
The strategy call is where we map that to your situation and give you an actual recommendation — not a generic quote.
```

**✉️ COPY & PASTE**

```
With that wide range in mind — does something in that ballpark realistically work for you if the fit and ROI make sense?
```

**📋 Rules for the range**

- [LOW] / [HIGH] = approved sheet only (update when pricing changes).
- Keep the range wide — purpose is ballpark fit, not a quote.
- Anchor customization (speed + size of build) — not “cheap option vs expensive option.”
- One pass, then move on — no ROI math, no line-item breakdown, no negotiating.
- If they fixate on [LOW] only → “Most LOs at your production level aren’t at the bottom of that range — [CLOSER NAME] will show you what actually fits.”

→ tag Gabriel per Watchshift SOP; note in CRM.

**If ballpark is clearly out of reach**

**Stage 3 — Qualifying questions**

Dig when answers are vague — don’t accept surface-level. Listen more than you talk after Framework 1 or 2; let them say out loud why they need help.

#### Discovery transition (then full qualifiers)

Use after Framework 1 or 2, or after Stage 2 permission if they didn’t ask for a rundown yet.

**✉️ COPY & PASTE**

```
Awesome. So before I go any deeper — I want to make sure I actually understand your situation, because the way we set things up is pretty tailored. Mind if I ask you a few things?
```

**Discovery quartet**

— ask naturally; capture verbatim:

1. > How are you generating leads right now?
1. > How many conversations are you having with homeowners per week, on average?
1. > What does production look like right now — and where do you want it to be?
1. > What’s been the biggest thing holding that back?
📋 When they answer #4, you often have the demo closer’s best material. Don’t interrupt — note exact words.

#### After qualifying — routing

**FUN gate**

— Before Stage 4, confirm per Intro Call Qualification Framework:

- F — Can they invest? (soft cues first; hard qualify only if needed)
- U — Moving in the next few weeks?
- N — Problem we solve (pipeline, lead quality, consistency — not “hire more closers”)
| Outcome | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| FUN pass | Continue to Stage 4 |
| Not urgent / clear blockers | Do not force demo. Send pre-call nurture; book 15-min check-in closer to their decision window (framework — Urgency) |
| FUN fail / not DFY | Disqualifying and Financial Qualification — Boot Camp or polite disqualify |

**“What do you do?”** — Framework 1: ask permission before explaining. Framework 2: ICP “who we are” from ICP tab.

**Programs / how it works?** — “[CLOSER NAME] walks that on the strategy call using what we learned today.”

# ICP Nuanced Responses

Pick **one track** before Stage 4 demo pitch. If two fit, lead with primary lead source today.

| Track | Pick when… |
| --- | --- |
| **1 — Referral LO** | Mostly referrals/sphere — production swings when referrals slow |
| **2 — Marketing / system LO** | Already ran ads, agency, bought leads — inconsistent or burned |
| **3 — Forward → reverse** | Forward-focused or new to reverse — exploring reverse growth |

## Track 1 — Referral LO

**Who we are (Framework 2):**

**✉️ COPY & PASTE**

```
Yeah, so — you know how there's a lot of reverse LOs out there who are really good at what they do... but every month it's kind of a guessing game? Some months are great, then it dries up — not because they're bad at sales, just not enough conversations with the right people.

What we do is take over that entire front end — lead gen, prospecting, pre-qualification, appointment setting — so the only thing you're doing is showing up to calls with people who actually want to talk about a reverse.

Does that sound like where you're at?
```

**Demo pitch:** Strategy call with [CLOSER NAME] — predictability on top of referrals; map appointments + [THEIR GOAL]; programs on strategy call.

## Track 2 — Marketing / System LO

**Who we are (Framework 2):**

**✉️ COPY & PASTE**

```
Yeah, so — a lot of reverse LOs have tried marketing already — Facebook, an agency, bought leads — but it's junk, shared, or people don't remember opting in. You're busy but the front end still isn't predictable.

What we do is run the full front end only for reverse — ads, landing page, pre-qual, booking — customized per LO. We're fixing the system, not selling more volume that doesn't fund.

Does that sound relevant?
```

**Demo pitch:** [CLOSER NAME] reviews what you run now — what to keep, change, or replace. Often send [burnt-by-agency video here](docs/acquisition/marketing/pre-call-objection-videos.md).

## Track 3 — Forward → Reverse

**Who we are (Framework 2):**

**✉️ COPY & PASTE**

```
A lot of LOs we talk to are strong on forward but reverse is a different acquisition game. If the front end isn't built right, you're learning two hard things at once.

What we do is only reverse — we run the whole front end so you're showing up to calls with people who already want to talk reverse, while you focus on product and closing.

Does that sound like what you're trying to do?
```

**Demo pitch:** Clear path into reverse without DIY-ing the whole machine. Must capture “why reverse vs forward?” for closer.
