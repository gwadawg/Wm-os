**WAIZ MEDIA**

**Call Center — Appointment-Setting Call Script**

How a call-center agent opens, frames, qualifies, and hands off a reverse-mortgage borrower — without burning the LO's time.

*Call Center & Client Success  |  Internal Use Only  |  May 2026*

# 1. Overview

> **📌 NORTH STAR**
>
> You are the **assistant** — not the loan officer. Set that frame in the first 10 seconds, gather enough notes to brief the LO, then transfer or book.

- **Who uses this:** Call-center agents (Bernardo, Luka, future hires) calling reverse-mortgage borrowers on behalf of client LOs (Jesse Beard, Community First, Ali, etc.)
- **When to use:** Fresh outbound dials, re-dials on unreached leads, callback after "not a good time," morning-of confirmation on AI-booked appointments
- **Pair with:** Live Transfer Warm Hand-off Script (next published doc), Call Center Script Boundary Rules

**CONTEXT**

The borrower talked to a form or an ad — not to the LO. If you let them dump the whole story on you, they have to repeat it on the transfer, and the LO ends up paying us to waste their time. The whole job of this call is to **set the frame, gather brief notes, and hand off clean**.

# 2. The One Job

Get the borrower **live-transferred to the LO** or **booked on the LO's calendar**. Live transfer is always the first move. Booking is the fallback.

# 3. Core Frames (read before every shift)

| # | Frame | What it means in practice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Assistant Frame** | "I'm the assistant for [LO]" in the first 10 seconds. Borrower stops over-explaining. |
| 2 | **Be Human First** | Break the robot opening. Smile. Joke. Pivot with "anyways…" |
| 3 | **Match Energy** | Soft 78-year-old gets soft. Loud, busy guy gets quick. |
| 4 | **Never End on "Okay"** | Every objection gets one more thread — right person's number, callback time, etc. |
| 5 | **Transfer First** | Always try the live transfer before booking. Booking is plan B. |

> **🚨 CRITICAL MISTAKE TO AVOID**
>
> Do not skip the Assistant Frame. If the borrower thinks you are the loan officer, they will tell you their whole story and resent repeating it. The LO will feel the call center wasted their time. This is the single most common reason transfers go badly.

# 4. Stage 1 — Connection (first 15 seconds)

**Greet by name, ID where you are calling from, ask how they are.**

**✉️ COPY & PASTE — OPENING**

```
Hey, [BORROWER NAME]?
Hey [NAME], this is [AGENT NAME] calling from [LO NAME]'s office — how are you doing?
```

> **📌 RULE**
>
> Say "from **[LO NAME]'s office**" — not "from Community First National Bank" or any institution name as the headline. You are calling **on behalf of** the LO, not as the bank.

## Break the robot response

Pick one. Rotate them so they stay natural.

**✉️ COPY & PASTE — RESPONSES**

```
Doing great — better than I deserve, I'll tell you that. Thanks for asking. But anyways…
```

```
Not bad — it's [DAY], how can I complain? But anyways…
```

```
Doing really good — beautiful day outside, nothing to complain about. Anyways…
```

```
Doing pretty good — one day closer to the weekend, right? Anyways…
```

> **💡 PRO TIP**
>
> Always end on "**anyways…**" That is the pivot back to business. Without it the small talk drags and you sound like a friend who showed up uninvited.

## Bonus relate-lever (use when it fits)

Whatever the borrower says — mirror it.

| They say | You say |
| --- | --- |
| "My kid spends all my money" | "Ha, my brother is the same way with my dad. Anyways…" |
| "I'm in Michigan" | "Oh I've heard great things about Michigan. Anyways…" |
| "Long day" | "Long day already, huh? Anyways…" |

> **💡 PRO TIP**
>
> Smile while dialing. They hear it in your voice. If you stutter — laugh about it. "Sorry, don't know why I said that." Sound human.

> **🚨 WATCH FOR THIS**
>
> Do not reference weather you cannot verify ("snowing in Kansas") or a city you do not actually live in. Stay generic — "beautiful day outside," "getting a little chilly," "one day closer to the weekend." Never get trapped into geography questions.

# 5. Stage 2 — The Assistant Frame (non-negotiable)

This is the section the coaching call was built around. Do not skip it. This is what stops the borrower from dumping their story on you.

**✉️ COPY & PASTE — ASSISTANT FRAME**

```
So [NAME] — just so you know up front, I'm not the loan officer. My name is [AGENT NAME], I'm the assistant for [LO NAME] over at [COMPANY]. They saw your inquiry come in for tapping into your home equity, and they asked me to give you a quick call.

The reason I'm calling is just to get a little clarity on your situation, so I can pass clean notes over and introduce you to [LO NAME] directly. Is this a good time for you to chat for a couple minutes?
```

**Why this works**

| Effect | What changes for the borrower |
| --- | --- |
| Tells them you are not the decision-maker | They keep it short |
| Previews the transfer | They know what's coming next |
| Asks permission | They feel respected, not pitched |

## If they ask "Who is this with?"

**✉️ COPY & PASTE**

```
So I'm calling on behalf of [LO NAME] — they're a reverse mortgage specialist at [COMPANY]. They got your information when you inquired about tapping into your home equity. I'm part of their intake team.
```

# 6. Stage 3 — Quick Qualification (notes for the LO)

You are gathering enough to **brief the LO** — not enough to underwrite the loan. Keep it tight. 3–5 questions max.

**✉️ COPY & PASTE — OPENER**

```
Beautiful. So [LO NAME] asked me to grab a few quick things before I introduce you — what are you looking to accomplish here? How much were you hoping to access?
```

## Follow-up questions (ask only what's needed)

| Ask | Why |
| --- | --- |
| "What's that going to be for — anything specific?" | Use of funds — biggest LO context item |
| "About how much is the home worth, ballpark?" | Eligibility signal for stricter programs |
| "Do you still have a balance on the mortgage, or is it paid off?" | LO opens the call with insight |
| "Are you the only one on the title, or is there a spouse?" | Title / co-borrower flag |

> **📌 RULE**
>
> Listen more than you talk. Capture in **their words**. Do not interrupt to "fix" the way they explain it.

## Hard disqualifiers (when to stop and escalate)

For strict programs (e.g., Community First), stop the qualification and escalate to your manager before transferring or booking.

| Condition | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Manufactured home on leased land | Polite close. Send to manager. |
| Home value clearly below program minimum | Don't transfer. Note + escalate. |
| Borrower not on title / not the homeowner | Get the right person's contact. Escalate. |
| Borrower already has an active LO closing the loan | Polite close. Note in CRM. |

> **🚨 CRITICAL MISTAKE TO AVOID**
>
> Do **not** silently disqualify a lead in the dialer. Every borderline lead goes to the manager so reporting stays clean and we know what to fix upstream.

# 7. Stage 4 — Transfer or Book

## Soft pre-frame before the transfer attempt

**✉️ COPY & PASTE**

```
Awesome — that's actually exactly the kind of situation [LO NAME] specializes in. What I'd love to do, if you've got a minute, is just hop you on a quick intro with them right now so you don't have to repeat all this to me later. Let me see if I can grab them.
```

> **📌 RULE**
>
> Always try the live transfer first. Booking is plan B. The exact hand-off language lives in the **Live Transfer Warm Hand-off Script**.

## If transfer is not possible — book

**✉️ COPY & PASTE**

```
No worries — let me get you on [LO NAME]'s calendar. What works best for you — sometime later today, or first thing tomorrow?
```

## Always pull the appointment closer

Try the closest slot first. Every day further out drops the show rate.

| Try first | Then | Then | Last resort |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Later today | Tomorrow morning | Tomorrow afternoon | 2+ days out |

**✉️ COPY & PASTE — PULL-FORWARD**

```
Are you sure there's nothing earlier? They've got a window at [EARLIER TIME] — would save you the wait.
```

```
They're a little packed that day — let me see if I can fit you in. Is there any reason [DATE/TIME] wouldn't work for you?
```

## Book the calendar invite NOW

**✉️ COPY & PASTE — CONFIRMATION**

```
Cool — putting you in for [DATE/TIME]. You'll get a text and an email with the calendar invite in just a second. Can you do me a quick favor and confirm it on your end so nobody gets double-booked?

And if you don't recognize the number that calls you, that'll be [LO NAME]. Save this number so you don't miss them.
```

> **⭐ STANDARD**
>
> Book through GoHighLevel (or the LO's CRM) **while the borrower is on the line**. Confirm they got the text and email before you hang up.

# 8. Stage 5 — Wrap-Up

**✉️ COPY & PASTE**

```
Perfect — you're all set for [DATE/TIME]. I'll pass [LO NAME] the notes from our conversation so they're ready when you guys connect. If anything changes on your end, just shoot us a text at this number. Have a great rest of your day, [NAME].
```

# 9. Call Variant — AI-Booked Appointment Confirmation

Use the morning of an AI-booked appointment to confirm + warm the borrower up.

**✉️ COPY & PASTE — OPENING**

```
Hey [NAME], this is [AGENT NAME] from [LO NAME]'s office — how are you doing? [break the robot]

So I saw you've got an appointment scheduled with [LO NAME] for [TIME] today — just wanted to give you a quick call to confirm you're still good for that, and grab a few notes so [LO NAME] can hit the ground running when you guys talk.
```

Then run a **light** version of Stage 3 (quick qualification — 2–3 questions max), then offer a pull-forward.

**✉️ COPY & PASTE — PULL-FORWARD ON AI-BOOKED**

```
Real quick — any chance you've got time before [SCHEDULED TIME]? [LO NAME] actually has a window open at [EARLIER TIME] — would save you the wait.
```

> **💡 PRO TIP**
>
> If you successfully **pull an AI-booked appointment forward** by phone, that still counts as a credited booking. Worth doing.

# 10. Call Variant — Callback After "Not a Good Time"

**✉️ COPY & PASTE**

```
Hey [NAME], this is [AGENT NAME] calling back from [LO NAME]'s office — last time we connected you mentioned now wasn't great and to try [TIME]. How are you doing? [break the robot]

Good news — [LO NAME] is actually around right now and has a quick window. Got a couple minutes for me to introduce you?
```

# 11. Objection Quick Reference

You are the front desk. Deeper objection-handling on the loan itself is the LO's job. Your job: keep the conversation alive long enough to hand off.

| They say | You say |
| --- | --- |
| "My wife / kid / friend handles this" | "Beautiful — what's the best way to reach them? I'd love to get them on a quick call with [LO NAME]." |
| "I'm not really interested right now" | "Totally fair — can I ask what changed? Last time we heard from you, you were looking into [USE OF FUNDS]." |
| "I already have a loan officer working on this" | "Got it — are you actively closing with them, or is it still early conversation? Would it hurt anything for [LO NAME] to give you a quick second opinion?" |
| "Where did you get my number?" | "You came in through one of our inquiry forms. I've got your info as [NAME] looking at [USE OF FUNDS] — does that ring a bell?" |
| "Is this a scam?" | "Totally hear you — I'm with [LO NAME]'s office at [COMPANY]. I can text you their direct line and website so you can call them back if it makes you more comfortable." |
| "Just send me information by email" | "Of course — I'll have [LO NAME] send you something directly. Best email is [EMAIL]?" — then pivot back to "And just so they can send the right info, what was the use of funds you were looking at?" |
| Sounds like a kid / prank | "All right, no worries — sorry to bother you. Have a great day." (Hang up politely. Mark CRM.) |

> **📌 RULE**
>
> Never end an objection with **"okay"** and a hang-up. Always pull one more thread — right person's number, callback time, or one clarifying question.

# 12. Done Right Looks Like

- ⭐ Assistant Frame set within the first 10 seconds
- ⭐ Borrower never thinks you are the LO
- ⭐ Maximum 3–5 qualification questions before transfer attempt
- ⭐ Live transfer attempted first on every qualified lead
- ⭐ Booking confirmed via text and email before hang-up
- ⭐ Every borderline lead is escalated to the manager — not silently dispositioned
- ⭐ Notes captured in CRM within 60 seconds of hang-up: name, use of funds, balance, home value, vibe

# 13. When To Get Help

- Borderline qualification (low value, leased land, title questions) → **manager**
- Borrower asks for pricing or loan-specific advice → **transfer to LO, do not answer**
- Repeat call from an upset borrower → **manager**
- Unsure if a frame, line, or rule applies → **manager**
