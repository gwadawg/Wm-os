How to attract homeowners with real equity and stable finances — better ads, better scripts, better qualification.

# 1. Overview

> **📌 NORTH STAR**
>
> We do not optimize for the cheapest lead. We optimize for **qualified conversations** — homeowners 62+ with equity who understand what they clicked.

- **Who uses this:** Media buyers, creative (Manus/Gabriela), Client Success when diagnosing qual rate
- **When:** Lead-to-qual below ~45%, too many "no equity" disquals, LO wants closable leads not volume
- **Pair with:** RM Ad Playbook (TOF/MOF/BOF), Constraint Troubleshooting on Drive

**CONTEXT**

"Good credit" on HECM usually means **pays property taxes and insurance** — not a 740 FICO. "Quality" means **equity + intent + they finished qual questions**.

# 2. What to do first (before new hooks)

| Priority | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Pull **top 3 disqual reasons** from GHL this week |
| 2 | Shift **30–40%** of new ads to **Strategic Retiree** + **Legacy Planner** angles |
| 3 | Add **home value band** + **mortgage balance** on the landing page |
| 4 | Check **message match** — ad promise = LP headline |
| 5 | Track **lead-to-qual %** and **CQPCONV** — not CPL alone |

> **🚨 GOLDEN RULE**
>
> If **cost per qualified conversation (CQPCONV)** is healthy (under ~$149), **do not** chase lower CPL by going broader. You may be buying worse leads.

# 3. Archetypes — who to target for quality

| Archetype | Use for quality? | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Strategic Retiree** | ✅ Primary | Planner mindset, often strong equity, highest close rate when engaged |
| **Legacy Planner** | ✅ Primary | Family/legacy frame, often paid-off or high equity |
| **Financially Squeezed** | ⚠️ Selective | Only with equity signals (paid-off, value bands on LP) |
| **Security-Seeker** | ❌ Deprioritize | Volume play — thinner equity, more charge-risk |

## WHAT THEY SAY vs WHAT'S ACTUALLY TRUE

| WHAT THEY SAY | WHAT'S ACTUALLY TRUE |
| --- | --- |
| "We need better credit scores on leads" | HECM has **no FICO floor** — we need people who **keep taxes/insurance current** |
| "Lower CPL = better campaign" | Lower CPL often = **curiosity clicks** — watch qual % and CQPCONV |
| "More urgency in the ad" | Urgency **hurts** in 62+ financial ads — confirms "sales pitch" fear |
| "Target interests: personal finance" | Andromeda wins on **creative diversity**, not narrow targeting |

# 4. Angle cheat sheet (Meta creative)

## Strategic Retiree — optimization (best for equity + stability)

**Lead with:** portfolio preservation, standby line of credit, sequence-of-returns, "equity you already built"

**Never lead with:** help, assistance, desperation, "act now," reverse mortgage in line 1

**✉️ STATIC — PRIMARY TEXT**

```
If you're pulling from retirement accounts when the market's down, you're not alone — many homeowners 62+ are looking at a different tool: accessing equity they've already built in their home, without a required monthly mortgage payment. Federally-insured. Educational call only — see if this may fit your plan.
```

```
A Strategic Buffer — Not a Last Resort
```

# 5. Video scripts (45–60 seconds)

**Rules for all scripts:** Calm, educational, LO on camera preferred. **Do not** open with "reverse mortgage." **Do not** guarantee amounts. CTA = free guide / educational call.

---

## Script A — Strategic Retiree (401k vs equity)

**✉️ COPY & PASTE — VIDEO**

```
HOOK (0–3s): "If you're retired and still watching your investment accounts when the market dips — this is for you."

BODY: "I'm [Name], and I work with homeowners 62 and older. A lot of smart retirees don't want to sell investments at the wrong time. There's a federally-insured home equity program that may let you access equity you've already built — with no required monthly mortgage payment. It's not for everyone. But for the right homeowner, it can be a strategic buffer — like a standby line of credit that can grow over time on the unused portion."

TRUST: "You keep the title. You stay in the home. Your heirs are protected by federal non-recourse rules."

CTA: "Tap below for a free educational guide — see if you may qualify. No pressure."

ON-SCREEN TEXT: May qualify · Educational only · Not tax or legal advice
```

# 6. Landing page — qual questions to add

> **💡 FRICTION IS A FEATURE**
>
> More questions = fewer leads — but **higher intent**. Watch lead-to-qual %, not form count.

| Question | Options (example) |
| --- | --- |
| Do you own your home? | Yes / No (disqual No) |
| Are you 62 or older? | Yes / No |
| Approximate home value | Under $300k · $300–500k · $500–750k · $750k+ |
| Mortgage balance | Paid off · Low · Moderate · High |
| Primary residence? | Yes / No |
| Main goal | End payment · LOC buffer · Legacy/family · Home repairs |

**LP headline must match the ad** — e.g. ad says "eliminate payment" → LP says the same, not "reverse mortgage application."

# 7. Weekly disqual → fix loop

| If disqual reason is… | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Under 62 | Tighten age qual; check ad copy isn't attracting adult children |
| Renter | Add homeowner FAQ; exclude renter language |
| No equity / low value | Shift to Strategic/Legacy; add value + mortgage questions |
| Already has RM | Exclude in CRM; BOF only for nurture |
| "Didn't understand ad" | Message mismatch — rewrite LP headline |

# 9. Compliance reminders

| Never | Always |
| --- | --- |
| "You qualify" / guaranteed $ | "May qualify" / "could access" |
| "Reverse mortgage" in cold hook | Outcome: payment, equity, security |
| Urgency / act now | Calm, educational tone |
| Tax or legal advice | Direct to their advisors |
