# Ops VA — PJ Comp Design

## Purpose

Payment ladder for the Ops contractor (Brazil PJ). Locked through **L5**.
Nothing above L5 is priced or defined here.

## Status

**LOCKED through L5** on 2026-10-26. Hire-facing wording lives in the
[payment agreement](ops-va-pj-payment-agreement.md),
[statement of work](ops-va-pj-sow.md), and
[level checklist](ops-va-pj-level-checklist.md).

Fill the hire’s name, PJ id, start date, and core hours. Accountant or
lawyer reviews the PJ wrapper before signature. The ladder text does not
wait on that review.

## Scope

| In | Out |
|---|---|
| Training, Holding, L2, L3, L4, L5 | L5B, L6, and any pay above R$10,000 |
| Skills demonstrated on real work | Tenure raises |
| Base only | A commission formula for ops |

Levels above L5 get their own written addendum before they can be offered.
Until that addendum is signed, the top of this agreement is **L5**.

## How pay moves

1. She is paid for the seat she can hold today.
2. From that day she is already doing the next seat’s work, with the founder guiding, until that work meets the next standard without the founder.
3. The current seat still has to hold while she practices the next one. If it slips, the next raise waits.
4. A level signs on **3 to 5** real cases. Three is the floor. Five is the most she needs to show. The founder signs inside that range when he would hand her the next case without a briefing. Reaching five does not sign the level if he would still have to brief it.
5. The new base starts the **next full pay month** after both parties initial the checklist. No retroactive base unless the founder writes an exception.
6. If the work of a signed level sits back with the founder for a full calendar month, the level steps down the following month. The month already underway stays at the signed level.

## Monthly base

| Level | Base (BRL) | Seat |
|---|---|---|
| Training | R$4,000 | Month 1 only |
| Holding | R$5,000 | Working, founder still carrying it |
| **L2** | **R$6,000** | VA. Fundamentals, guided |
| **L3** | **R$7,000** | VA seat without the founder. Learning automations with help |
| **L4** | **R$8,000** | Snapshot bugs, workflow improvements, internal builds |
| **L5** | **R$10,000** | Ships systems on one light review |

Payday is the **9th**, paying the prior month, same as the other PJ agreement.

## Operating consequences

- At **L3** she is the named VA on ClickUp **A2P** and **Tech Buildout**. Tech subtasks stay with Christian until that level is signed.
- An edit on one client’s subaccount is hers from **L4**. A change to the master GHL snapshot, the template every client is built from, is shown to the founder once before it publishes.
- Media buying strategy, budgets, client KPI calls, Funnel, and Media Buying stay with the media buyer at every level.
- This pack is owner-only. It does not go to the team Drive.
