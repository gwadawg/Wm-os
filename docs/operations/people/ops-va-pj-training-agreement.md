# Ops VA — Training Month Agreement

This covers **one training month only**. It is a contractor agreement. It is not a CLT employment contract, and it is not the long-term ladder. The ladder draft is a separate conversation. Signing this does not accept those levels, and it does not promise them.

Your accountant or lawyer should review the PJ wording before signature. Give her this draft with time to read it. Do not ask her to sign it on a call.

## 1. What this month is

You are learning Waiz Media’s tools and flow, on real work, with the founder guiding you.

By the end of the month you should be able to:

- Find a client in ClickUp
- Follow a tech-setup checklist (A2P and Tech Buildout) with the founder reviewing before anything goes out
- Write a ticket note on GHL, Hot Prospector, or Closebot that says what broke, what you changed, and where

You do not change a live client system without asking first. You do not publish anything to the master GHL snapshot.

## 2. Pay

- **R$4.000** flat for the training month, invoiced in BRL.
- You handle your own taxes and PJ compliance.
- Payday is the **9th** of the following month, after your invoice, unless the Company pays sooner in writing.
- If the month ends early, pay is pro-rated: R$4.000 ÷ the number of days in the training period × days worked through the end date you were told.
- There is no charge back for training if you leave. There is nothing to repay.

## 3. Your time

Core hours above are when you are available and responsive. Outside those hours, your time is yours. This agreement does not require you to drop other work. If either of us wants exclusivity later, that is a separate written deal, with pay that matches it.

## 4. What we owe each other

You:

- Show up for the core hours, or say so before you cannot.
- Ask when the flow is unclear. During this month, those questions are the work.
- Flag a blocker the same day. Flag a deadline before it slips.
- Keep client and company information inside company tools. Do not copy it into a personal account.

The Company:

- Gives you access, a person to ask, and real work to learn on. Not only videos.
- Tells you before the month ends whether we want to continue, and on what written terms.
- Pays the training month as written here, including a pro-rated amount if it ends early.

## 5. What this is not

- Not CLT employment.
- Not a promise of a seat after the end date.
- Not acceptance of the level ladder (R$5.000 through R$10.000). That draft stays a draft until we both sign something else.
- Not a non-compete. You may keep other clients and other work.
- Work you create for Waiz Media in this month belongs to the Company. Skill and knowledge you already had stay yours.

## 6. Ending

Either of us may end the month early, for any reason, by a written message. That is a normal outcome of a trial month, not a penalty.

On the end date, tool access closes unless we agree otherwise in writing. You invoice for the days worked. The Company pays on the normal payday, or sooner if it chooses.

## 7. After the end date

Continuing means a new written agreement. Silence does not extend this one, and it does not start the ladder.
