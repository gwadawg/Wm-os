# Lead Media Buyer — Soft Trial Agreement (2 months)

**This is a soft trial agreement** — clear on pay and expectations for the first
two months. It is **not** a full employment contract and not deep legal counsel.
Brazil PJ tax/legal details stay with the Company’s accountant if needed.

## Parties

| Field | Value |
|-------|--------|
| Company | Waiz Media (“Company”) |
| Contractor | ________________________________ (“you”), Brazil PJ |
| PJ / tax ID | ________________________________ |
| Trial start | ________________________________ |

## 1. What this is

- A **2-month testing phase** so both sides can see if the seat is a fit.
- During these **first 2 months**, **either party may end the engagement** for any
  reason with **written notice** (email or message is enough).
- After the trial, if both want to continue, we align on ongoing pay, levels, and
  commissions (see the fuller payment pack if used). Nothing in this soft agreement
  locks long-term salary, levels, or commissions beyond the trial terms below.

## 2. Pay during the trial (agreed)

| Month | Pay | Notes |
|-------|-----|--------|
| **Month 1** | **R$4,000** | Flat. No commissions. |
| **Month 2** | **R$4,000** | Flat. No commissions during trial. |

- Currency: **BRL**. You invoice as PJ.
- **Payday: 9th of each month** for the **prior** month’s work (e.g. Month 1 work →
  paid on the 9th of the following month), after a simple invoice.
- If the trial ends mid-month, Company pays a **pro-rata** share of that month’s
  R$4,000 for days worked.

## 3. What we expect from you (trial)

Plain expectations — not a full job description:

1. Show up consistently and communicate clearly.
2. Learn Waiz systems / OS and follow the buying process we teach.
3. Work assigned accounts carefully; ask before big changes you are unsure about.
4. Help with creative/ad concepts as asked (learning mode in trial — not expected to
   own the full creative system yet).
5. Hit toward the **KPI standards we set for your accounts** (see §4).
6. Be coachable; flag problems early.

## 4. KPIs (subject to change)

- During the trial, Company will share the **KPI targets** for accounts you touch
  (e.g. CPQL / related buying standards).
- Those KPI standards are **subject to change** as we learn the accounts, geos, and
  what “good” looks like for this seat. Changes will be communicated in writing.
- Missing a KPI in month 1–2 is a **coaching signal**, not an automatic firing clause —
  but repeated disregard for process or communication *is* a reason either side may
  end the trial under §1.

## 5. What this trial is not

- Not CLT employment.
- Not a guarantee of hire after 2 months.
- Not a lock on R$5k / R$6k / R$7k levels or commissions — those are discussed if we
  continue after the trial.
- Not deep legal terms (IP, non-compete, liability caps, etc.). If we continue long
  term, we can add a fuller agreement.

## 6. Ending the trial

- **Either party** may end during the 2-month testing phase with written notice.
- Final pay = days worked (pro-rata) for the current month + any unpaid prior month,
  on the normal payday cycle (or sooner if Company chooses).
- Access to ad accounts and tools ends on the last working day unless otherwise agreed.

## 7. Signatures

By signing, we agree this soft trial covers **initial pay**, **mutual exit during
months 1–2**, and **expectations** as written above.
