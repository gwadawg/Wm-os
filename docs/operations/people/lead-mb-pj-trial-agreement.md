# Lead Media Buyer — Soft Trial Agreement

**This is a soft agreement** — clear on pay phases, training exit, and mutual trial
freedom. It is **not** a CLT contract and not deep legal counsel.

## 1. How the phases work

```text
START (may be mid-month)
    │
    ▼
TRAINING  ── hourly R$25/hr ── until you pass the competency test
    │         (length depends on how fast you learn)
    ▼
SALARY    ── only after pass + written FT/PT seat confirmation
    │         Full-time = R$4,000/month (still in trial window)
    ▼
COMMISSIONS eligible after one full month of work on salary
```

**Trial window (mutual freedom):** From start through the end of your **first full
month on salary**, **either party may end** the engagement for any reason with
**written notice** (email or message is enough). That is the “trial” — freedom for
both sides, not a punishment clause.

## 2. Training period (hourly)

- Pay: **R$25 per hour**, tracked and invoiced in **BRL** as PJ.
- You start mid-month or whenever agreed; training pay is **hourly**, not a fraction
  of R$4,000.
- Training **ends as soon as you fully pass** the Company competency check on:
  1. Understanding mortgages (product basics needed for the seat)
  2. Software / tools we use
  3. Ad account setup
  4. Client onboarding
  5. Managing an account
- How long training lasts depends on **how long you take to learn**. There is no
  fixed calendar end date for training.
- Company confirms pass **in writing** (email/message). That note is the switch from
  hourly → salary.

## 3. After training — salary (still trial)

- Salary starts only after you **pass** the competency check **and** Company confirms
  in writing whether the seat is **full-time** or **part-time** (and the matching pay).
- **Full-time:** **R$4,000 per month** (no longer hourly), starting the day after pass
  *and* that written seat-load confirmation (whichever is later).
- **Part-time:** Company states hours and pay in that same written note (e.g. reduced
  monthly salary or continued hourly). Do **not** assume full R$4,000 for a part-time load.
- If work is still light after pass, Company may keep you on **hourly R$25** until it
  confirms a salary seat in writing.
- First salary month may be **pro-rated by calendar days** if it does not start on
  the 1st (daily rate = monthly salary ÷ days in that calendar month × days on salary).
- You remain in the **trial window** until the end of your first full month on salary
  (§1). If you stay hourly longer, the trial window still runs through the end of your
  first full month once salary begins.

## 4. Commissions

- **No commissions** during training (hourly phase).
- After you are on salary, you become eligible for commissions only after completing
  **one full month of work on salary**.
- Commission rules (CPQL hits, etc.) are set separately and may follow the Company’s
  media-buyer commission appendix when activated. KPI targets used for commissions
  are **subject to change** with written notice.

## 5. Payday

- **Payday: 9th of each month** for the **prior** month’s approved hours and/or salary
  (and commissions only when eligible), after a simple PJ invoice.
- Mid-month start: Month 1 invoice = **hours × R$25** for training days (plus any
  salary days if you already passed in that same calendar month).

## 6. What we expect

1. Show up, communicate clearly, be coachable.
2. Learn systems and the process we teach.
3. Ask before big account changes you are unsure about.
4. Help with creative/ad concepts as asked while learning.
5. Work toward the KPI standards we set for your accounts — those standards are
   **subject to change** with written notice as we learn the book.
6. Flag problems early.

Missing a KPI early is a **coaching signal**. Ignoring process or going dark is a
reason either side may end under the trial window.

## 7. What this is not

- Not CLT employment.
- Not a guarantee you continue after the trial window.
- Not a lock on higher levels (R$5k / R$6k / R$7k) — those are only if we continue
  and you unlock them later.
- Not deep legal terms. If we continue long term, we can add a fuller agreement.

## 8. Ending during the trial window

- Either party may end with written notice during training or during the first full
  salary month (§1).
- Final pay = approved hours at R$25 and/or pro-rated salary for days worked, plus
  any unpaid prior period, on the normal payday cycle (or sooner if Company chooses).
- Access to ad accounts and tools ends on the last working day unless otherwise agreed.
