# Lead Media Buyer — PJ Statement of Work

## Purpose

Define what the Lead Media Buyer contractor owes at each level: senior Meta buying,
client clarity, and progressive creative ownership.

## Mission

Own assigned client ad accounts to hit written **CPQL** targets, keep buying hygiene
tight, communicate clearly with clients and Client Success, and grow into owning
creative packages and (at L4) production/editing — inside Waiz Media OS systems.

## Engagement

- Brazil **PJ** · reports to founder · paid per [Payment agreement](lead-mb-pj-payment-agreement.md)
- Seat is **senior to a standard media-buyer buying seat** (scope + ladder), not a
  peer-only replica of existing buyer plans

## Core accountabilities (all levels)

1. **Buying:** structure, budgets, testing cadence, daily/weekly account hygiene
2. **Targets:** pursue written per-account KPIs; document tests and learnings
3. **Fulfillment flags:** notify Client Success early when booking/flow metrics slip
4. **Client clarity:** updates, wins, changes; KPI reviews required from **L2+**
5. **OS compliance:** work inside Wm-os media-buying SOPs and the Media Buyer Daily OS
6. **Scorecard:** graded monthly on the media-buyer scorecard (feedback every month;
   commission gate from L2+)

## Creative progression (matches pay ladder)

| Level | Creative / scope expectation |
|-------|------------------------------|
| **L1** | Learn the creative system; contribute angles and notes; does **not** own production-ready packages |
| **L2** | Full buying seat basics: manage accounts, onboard a client, basic funnel, headlines, KPI client reviews |
| **L3** | Own creative process: bring founder **production-ready packages** — clear analysis + scripts/concepts fully written for ads to be produced |
| **L4** | Produce and edit ads end-to-end |

## Month 1 ramp

- Systems access, OS walkthrough, shadow live accounts
- Supervised reps: onboard, basic funnel, headlines
- End of Month 1 founder check-in — stay L1 or begin L2 checklist (no auto-raise)

## Explicitly out of level gates

- Mentoring other media buyers (including Christian) is **not** required to unlock levels
