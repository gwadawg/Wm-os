# Lead Media Buyer — PJ Payment & Expectations Agreement

**Parties**

- **Company:** Waiz Media (founder as authorized signatory)
- **Contractor:** {{HIRE_NAME}} (“Contractor”), Brazil PJ
- **PJ / tax ID:** {{PJ_TAX_ID}}
- **Start date:** {{START_DATE}} (defines Month 1 calendar)

This is a **payment and expectations agreement** for independent contractor services.
It is **not** a CLT employment contract. Brazilian PJ legal/tax wording should be
finalized by the Company’s accountant or lawyer before signature.

## 1. Engagement

Contractor provides Lead Media Buyer services as described in the
[Statement of Work](lead-mb-pj-sow.md), invoiced monthly in **Brazilian reais (BRL)**.

Contractor is responsible for their own taxes, contributions, and PJ compliance.

## 2. Month 1 — Ramp

| Component | Amount |
|-----------|--------|
| Base | **R$4,000** (flat) |
| Commissions | **R$0** |

Month 1 focuses on systems access, OS walkthrough, shadowing live accounts, and
supervised onboard / funnel / headline reps. End of Month 1: founder check-in.
No automatic raise.

## 3. Month 2+ — Base by level

| Level | Base (BRL/month) | Commissions |
|-------|------------------|-------------|
| **L1** | **R$4,000** | None |
| **L2** | **R$5,000** | Per [Commission appendix](lead-mb-pj-commission-appendix.md) |
| **L3** | **R$6,000** | Same as L2 |
| **L4** | **R$7,000** | Same as L2 |

Contractor starts Month 2 at **L1** unless founder has already signed an unlock on the
[Level checklist](lead-mb-pj-level-checklist.md).

## 4. Level raises

Levels unlock by **skills demonstration only** (founder sign-off on the checklist).
New base starts the **next full pay month** after sign-off (no retroactive base unless
founder writes an exception).

| Level | Unlock when Contractor can… |
|-------|------------------------------|
| L2 | Manage all accounts · onboard a client · create a basic funnel · understand/write headlines · run KPI client reviews |
| L3 | Own creative process: clear analysis + scripts/concepts fully written for production |
| L4 | Produce and edit ads end-to-end |

Mentoring other team members is **not** a level gate.

## 5. Commissions (L2+ only)

- Available only after **L2** (or higher) unlock.
- **R$100** per eligible account at **CPQL** KPI that month.
- Monthly media-buyer scorecard must be **≥ B**, or commissions = **R$0** (base still pays).
- Full rules: [Commission appendix](lead-mb-pj-commission-appendix.md).
- Cash commissions do **not** pay until: L2+ unlock, written CPQL targets on scored
  accounts, and one **shadow EOM** month approved by founder.

## 6. Payday

- **Pay day: 9th of every month** (BRL).
- Pays the **prior calendar month** base (level then in force) + approved commissions.
- Example: September work → approve early October → paid **9 October**.
- If the 9th is not a banking day, pay the next banking day (per accountant).
- Contractor prepares the EOM sheet; founder approves; Contractor invoices; Company pays.

## 7. Changes

Company may adjust rates, floors, grace, or level criteria with **30 days’ written
notice**, typically at quarterly review. Structure changes should not conflict with
work already completed in a closed pay month without written agreement.
