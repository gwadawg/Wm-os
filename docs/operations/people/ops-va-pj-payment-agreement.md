# Ops VA — PJ Payment & Expectations Agreement

**Parties**

- **Company:** Waiz Media (founder as authorized signatory)
- **Contractor:** {{HIRE_NAME}} (“Contractor”), Brazil PJ
- **PJ / tax ID:** {{PJ_TAX_ID}}
- **Start date:** {{START_DATE}} (defines Month 1)
- **Core hours:** {{CORE_HOURS}} ({{TIMEZONE}})

This is a **payment and expectations agreement** for independent contractor
services. It is **not** a CLT employment contract. Brazilian PJ legal and tax
wording should be finalized by the Company’s accountant or lawyer before
signature.

The ladder through **L5** is founder-locked. See
[Ops VA — PJ Comp Design](ops-va-pj-comp-design.md).

## 1. Engagement

Contractor provides Ops services as described in the
[Statement of Work](ops-va-pj-sow.md), invoiced monthly in **Brazilian reais (BRL)**.

Contractor is responsible for their own taxes, contributions, and PJ compliance.

Through **L2** the seat is the **Ops VA** tier. From **L3** it is the same
engagement, held without the founder in the daily work.

## 2. Month 1 — Training

| Component | Amount |
|---|---|
| Base | **R$4,000** (flat) |

Month 1 is access, shadowing, and supervised reps on real tickets and a live
Tech Buildout. End of Month 1 is a check-in. There is no automatic raise.

She leaves the month able to find a client in ClickUp, follow the Tech
Buildout checklist, and write a ticket note the founder can read.

## 3. Holding

| Component | Amount |
|---|---|
| Base | **R$5,000** |

Holding is the seat that was budgeted. It runs after Month 1 until **L2** is
signed. She is working, and the founder is still diagnosing, rewriting, or
reassigning the work.

## 4. Base by level

| Level | Base (BRL/month) | Seat |
|---|---|---|
| **L2** | **R$6,000** | VA. Fundamentals, with the founder guiding |
| **L3** | **R$7,000** | Same VA seat, without the founder |
| **L4** | **R$8,000** | Snapshot bugs, workflow improvements, internal builds |
| **L5** | **R$10,000** | Ships systems on one light review |

No level above L5 exists in this agreement. A higher level requires a written
addendum signed by both parties before it can be paid.

## 5. How a level is signed

She is paid for the seat she can hold. From that day she is already doing the
next seat’s work, with the founder guiding, until that work meets the next
standard without the founder.

The current seat still has to hold while she practices the next one. If it
slips, the next raise waits.

A level signs on **3 to 5** real cases, except where a section below states a
time stretch instead of a case count. Three is the floor. Five is the most she
needs to show. The founder signs inside that range when he would hand her the
next case without a briefing. Reaching five does not sign the level if he
would still have to brief it.

The new base starts the **next full pay month** after both parties initial the
[level checklist](ops-va-pj-level-checklist.md). No retroactive base unless the
founder writes an exception.

She may ask for a review any week she believes the evidence exists. The
founder also reviews at day 30, then monthly.

## 6. L2 — R$6,000 — Ops VA

She owns the fundamentals and is still learning how the company flows. The
founder teaches, and reviews before work goes out.

- She closes tickets in GHL, Hot Prospector, and Closebot. The note says what broke, what she changed, and where. The founder still spot-checks.
- She runs **A2P** and **Tech Buildout** on a client. The founder reviews before **Tech QA**. She does not hand the founder the client to finish.
- She takes team requests that are those systems: a form, a calendar, routing, a pixel, a booking path, a phone, a Closebot flow. Fires and a live onboarding come first.
- She is already practicing the L3 door with the founder: a full onboarding, Closebot and the AI agent, and writing an SOP.

**L2 is signed when** she is in that guided seat: she has driven A2P and Tech
Buildout with the founder reviewing before Tech QA, and her ticket notes are
usable. Independence on that seat is L3, not L2.

## 7. L3 — R$7,000

**To enter, all of this is already true without the founder:**

- **3 to 5** clients have been taken through the tech lane from payment to Account Live. A2P and Tech Buildout reached **Tech QA = Complete** with her as owner. She knows the stages. The CSM moves the stage. The media buyer keeps Funnel, Media Buying, strategy, budgets, and the client KPI call.
- She runs Closebot bugs and fixes the AI agent. She diagnoses, fixes what is in her reach, and when something has to go higher she sends it up with the cause, what she already tried, and a recommendation. The founder is not the first stop.
- She has written SOPs from work she has already done, and she has updated a broken process so the next run follows the new version.

At L3 she is the named VA on ClickUp **A2P** and **Tech Buildout**.

**While she is at L3** she helps on automations and on other advanced tasks,
with the founder still available. The aim is clarity: how the automations
actually fire, and the related skills. She does not have to be strong at
building automations, or at systems, to leave L3.

**She leaves for L4 when all three are true:**

- On **3 to 5** live clients she can show the snapshot that subaccount came from, the values and fields a workflow reads, and which of those came from the client.
- She can walk the company, systems-wise, across onboarding, client success, media buying, and the call center, and where those meet in Mr. Waiz, ClickUp, Make, GHL, Closebot, and Hot Prospector.
- For **3 to 5** straight weeks the founder did not have to fix a broken system. If the founder has to step in and fix one, the stretch starts over.

## 8. L4 — R$8,000

Everything required to enter L3, and the L4 entry standard above, stays hers.
Onboardings, Closebot, the AI agent, SOPs, and broken systems do not come back
to the founder.

She goes deep on client bugs inside the snapshots. **3 to 5** on real clients.
She finds the workflow, the value, or the field that is wrong, fixes it, and
it stays fixed.

She improves workflows that already exist, including when nothing is broken.
**3 to 5** of those. A manual step removed, a repeat failure stopped, or a
workflow changed so it matches how that client’s information arrives. The
founder did not design the fix.

An edit on that client’s subaccount, she ships. An edit to the **master GHL
snapshot**, the template every client is built from, she shows the founder
once before it publishes. She does the work. The founder sees it because it
lands on every account.

She also takes larger development tasks in the internal software, and bigger
projects, while that back end keeps running. Shipping a system on one light
review is L5.

## 9. L5 — R$10,000

**3 to 5** systems she specced, built, and shipped. Each one got a single
review from the founder, and the notes were minor: a label, a field, an edge
case. Then it went live. A review that changes the design does not count. A
single automation does not count. A system has more than one step and a
defined done state.

The L4 back end stays hers while she builds.

If the founder approves another VA seat and the pay, she runs that person
from day one: assignments, review, and quality. She may propose the hire. The
founder still approves the seat and the pay. She does not wait on a hire to
reach L5.

## 10. What stays with the Company

At every level the founder keeps:

- The offer, pricing, and anything that changes a client promise
- Spend, and any new headcount
- A permission that moves money, production access, or compliance
- Publishing a change to the master GHL snapshot

The media buyer keeps account strategy, budgets, client KPI calls, Funnel,
and Media Buying.

## 11. When the week is full

1. Something that stops leads or appointments
2. A live **A2P** or **Tech Buildout**
3. System tasks already assigned
4. Team requests on those systems
5. New builds

## 12. Conduct at every level

- A blocker is written within 30 minutes.
- A deadline slip is flagged before it slips, with a new time.
- A problem arrives as one problem, three options, and one recommendation.
- The daily EOD is sent.
- She follows the written process, or proposes a written change.

A miss pauses the next unlock. The current base holds through a 30-day
correction. Questions at L2 are about how the flow works. From L3, questions
to the founder are permissions, money, and client promises.

## 13. Payday

- **Pay day: the 9th** of every month (BRL).
- Pays the **prior calendar month** at the level then in force.
- If the 9th is not a banking day, pay the next banking day.
- Contractor invoices. Founder approves. Company pays.

## 14. Step-down

If the work of a signed level sits back with the founder for a full calendar
month, the level steps down the following month. The month already underway
is paid at the signed level.

## 15. Changes

The Company may adjust this ladder with **30 days’ written notice**. A closed
pay month is not reopened. No pay above L5 is created by this section. A
level above L5 requires its own signed addendum.
