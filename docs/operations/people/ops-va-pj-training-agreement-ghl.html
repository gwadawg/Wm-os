The Parties agree to this Service Agreement on the terms below. This is a contract for services. It is not a CLT employment contract. Nothing above the training month is priced here. A later level of pay requires a written amendment signed by both Parties.

1. Object

1.1. The Contractor will provide operations training services to the Client, consisting of:

1.1.1. Learning and practicing the Client’s tools on real work, with the Client’s founder guiding: ClickUp, GoHighLevel, Hot Prospector, and Closebot.

1.1.2. Shadowing and supervised work on client tech setup (A2P and Tech Buildout). The Contractor does not submit Tech QA, and does not change a live client system, without the founder’s prior review.

1.1.3. Writing ticket notes that state what broke, what was changed, and where.

1.1.4. Asking when the flow is unclear, and flagging a blocker the same day and a deadline before it slips.

1.2. The Contractor will use the operational care expected of someone learning this seat.

1.3. Each Party states that it has experience in the activities this Agreement assigns to it, and that it knows Article 157 of the Brazilian Civil Code. Neither Party is aware of any fact that would make this engagement an unconscionable bargain (lesão).

1.4. The obligations are proportionate to the fees in Annex I at the time of signing.

1.5. Work that is not in clause 1.1, including any level after this training month, is performed only after both Parties approve it in a written amendment. A conversation about future pay is not that amendment.

1.6. If a task is outside clause 1.1, or if there is doubt, the Contractor waits for the Client’s written go-ahead.

2. Term

2.1. The first full training month is a calendar month, the 1st through the last day of ______________ (month and year). Start date: ______________. If the start date falls before that 1st, the days from the start date through the last day of the prior month are a stub. The stub is paid by the hour. The full month is the flat fee. This Agreement does not renew itself. It continues only if both Parties sign a new agreement or a written amendment. Silence is not a renewal.

3. Price and payment

3.1. Fees and payment terms are in Annex I, which is part of this Agreement and is signed with it.

4. Contractor’s obligations

4.1. The Contractor performs the services. There is no employment relationship with the Client, as clause 7 states.

4.2. The Client does not grant the Contractor exclusivity. The Contractor may serve other clients, and will keep the confidentiality, non-solicitation, and data duties in this Agreement.

4.3. The Contractor will also:

a) Meet the deadlines in this Agreement and in any schedule the Parties set in writing.

b) Give the Client the information the Client reasonably needs about the work.

c) Follow the security practices of the tools and accounts the Client opens for the work.

d) On request, provide clarifications about the work and proof that the Contractor is current on the taxes due on the invoices it issues.

e) Indemnify the Client, or a third party, for loss caused in performing this Agreement by negligence, lack of skill, intent, or a breach of confidentiality, whether by the Contractor or by anyone the Contractor uses.

f) Be responsible for any obligation the Contractor takes on with a third party in order to perform this Agreement.

g) Tell the Client about any change that would affect this Agreement.

h) Keep confidential the Client’s information, its clients’ information, and its methods. The Contractor will not use the Client’s access, files, or materials for any other client or any other work. A breach of this item is subject to the fine in clause 8.5.

i) Comply with Brazil’s General Data Protection Law (Law 13,709/2018).

j) Correct, at no extra charge, irregularities in the Contractor’s own work that the Client points out during the term.

k) Stay current with the Contractor’s own taxes, suppliers, and any people the Contractor pays. Those people have no claim on the Client.

4.4. If a lawsuit or administrative claim reaches the Client because of the Contractor’s fault, the Contractor bears that outcome and reimburses what the Client spends, including attorney fees of 20% (twenty percent) and court costs, plus interest of 1% (one percent) a month and monetary correction by the IGPM/FGV index from the date the Client spent the money.

4.5. The Contractor is liable for damage to the Client or to third parties caused in performing this Agreement, including by omission, lack of skill, or negligence.

4.6. If the Client is sued on the facts in clause 4.5, the Client may hold back from later payments an amount equal to the claim, plus costs incurred.

4.7. The Contractor is jointly liable for acts of its legal representatives, agents, employees, and subcontractors it supervises, when those acts harm the Client or a third party.

4.7.1. That liability does not extend to people the Contractor did not hire and does not supervise, unless the Contractor’s own fault is shown.

4.8. The Contractor may not bind the Client, give guarantees in the Client’s name, or represent the Client, including toward the Client’s customers, except with prior written authority and inside that authority.

4.9. The Contractor states that it is free to sign this Agreement and to perform it, and that no law or other contract blocks that performance.

5. Client’s obligations

5.1. The Client will:

a) Pay the fees on the schedule in Annex I.

b) Give the Contractor the access, the context, and a person to ask, so the training is on real work.

c) Reimburse extra expenses only as Annex I allows.

6. Termination

6.1. Either Party may end this Agreement at any time, without cause and without owing the other an indemnity for the ending itself.

6.2. Either Party may end this Agreement for cause, without prior notice, if:

a) The other Party breaches this Agreement.

b) The other Party has bankruptcy decreed, or files for judicial or extrajudicial reorganization.

c) The other Party assigns this Agreement without the prior written consent required below.

d) Both Parties sign a termination instrument.

e) The services are performed with technical failure, omission, negligence, lack of skill, or recklessness, or in an unsatisfactory way.

f) A Party acts in a way that harms the Client’s commercial reputation with third parties.

g) A force majeure event makes performance impossible, under clause 13.1.

6.3. Tax duties survive for 10 (ten) years after the end. Labor and social-security duties, if any ever arise despite clause 7, survive until paid.

6.4. A Party may set off amounts the other owes it, including losses from a breach, without waiving full compensation for loss.

6.5. On the end date the Contractor returns every material, client list, contact, key, login, and password of the Client, keeps no copy, and stops using every access the Client granted. The fine for each breach of this clause is R$ 10.000,00 (ten thousand reais).

6.6. Ending this Agreement does not erase rights and duties the Parties already have toward each other or toward third parties, including confidentiality, data return, and payment for work already done.

7. No employment relationship

7.1. The Parties state that this Agreement does not create an employment relationship, a partnership, or an association. The Contractor performs with the independence of a service contractor. The Client is not liable for labor, social-security, workplace-accident, or tax obligations that belong to the Contractor because of these services.

8. Intellectual property, confidentiality, and non-solicitation

8.1. The Contractor, and anyone connected with it, will not disclose and will keep secret the Client’s business, transactions, processes, methods, techniques, the Client’s brand, and the Client’s clients and partners, except with the Client’s prior written permission.

8.1.1. “Confidential Information” means information, documents, and knowledge received by any means from the signing date, including information learned inside the Client’s systems, except information the Client expressly allows to be disclosed, and except information that is already public through no fault of the Contractor.

8.1.2. The Contractor will not publish photos, documents, images, or other information from the engagement, including on social media, to promote the Contractor, except when the Client asks for that in writing.

8.1.3. The Parties will also keep confidential the negotiations of this engagement, including meetings and calls.

8.2. The work gives access to strategic information: offers, prices, clients, and suppliers. That information belongs to the Client. The Contractor uses it only to perform this Agreement.

8.3. Copyrights, intellectual property, contact lists, client lists, creative work, content, know-how, manuals, projects, and other materials the Contractor develops in the engagement belong to the Client, or to the Client’s customer when the work was for that customer. The Contractor keeps no economic or intellectual-property right in those materials. The Contractor’s pre-existing skill stays the Contractor’s. The fee in Annex I is the payment for this assignment.

8.4. Within 5 (five) days after a written request, the Contractor will sign the documents reasonably needed for the Client to register or exploit the rights in clause 8.3. The daily fine for delay is R$ 1.000,00 (one thousand reais).

8.5. During this Agreement the Contractor will not, in Brazil, directly or indirectly:

a) solicit or serve a client of the Client, or

b) use the Client’s Confidential Information, access, or materials to perform services for a company that competes with the Client’s mortgage-marketing business.

The fine is R$ 12.000,00 (twelve thousand reais), plus proven losses.

8.6. After this Agreement ends, the Contractor remains bound by Law 9,279/1996 on unfair competition. A violation can bring civil and criminal liability.

8.7. Confidentiality survives the end of this Agreement for as long as the information is not public. A breach is subject to the fine in clause 8.5, plus proven losses.

8.8. If the Contractor uses employees or subcontractors, the Contractor tells them of these duties and limits access to people who need it to do the work.

8.9. The Contractor knows these are duties not to act, and that a breach can bring civil and criminal liability in addition to the fine. The Contractor will stop the breach and compensate the Client.

9. Data protection

9.1. The Contractor will follow applicable data-protection law, including Law 13,709/2018, whenever it handles personal data for this Agreement.

9.2. The Client remains the owner of third-party data, including personal data, shared with the Contractor. The Contractor treats that data as confidential.

9.3. The Contractor follows the Client’s instructions on personal data and is liable for loss caused by an unlawful use.

9.4. The Contractor will correct, complete, delete, or block personal data when the Client asks, and will pass on requests and complaints from data subjects.

9.5. The Contractor treats personal data as confidential unless it is already public, and uses reasonable technical care in storing and sending it.

9.6. The Contractor keeps a simple record of personal-data operations it performs for the Client, and protects the data against destruction, loss, alteration, and unauthorized access.

9.7. The Contractor watches its own compliance. If it has no employees, this duty is personal.

9.8. The Contractor does not take ownership of client data. Data generated or collected under this Agreement belongs to the Client. The Contractor will not sell or share it.

9.9. Processing personal data outside Brazil requires the Client’s prior written approval.

9.10. If a change in law makes the services impossible to continue lawfully, this Agreement ends without a penalty, and the Contractor returns the personal data.

9.11. Within 30 (thirty) days after the end, the Contractor returns the personal data it received and does not keep it longer than this Agreement or the law requires.

9.12. Anyone the Contractor allows to see the data is bound by confidentiality. The Contractor does not use the data for any purpose other than this Agreement.

10. Social responsibility

10.1. Each Party states that it complies with the labor rules of its country, does not discriminate, respects human dignity, and follows applicable health, safety, and environmental rules.

11. Anti-corruption

11.1. The Contractor knows and will respect Brazil’s Anti-Corruption Law (Law 12,846/2013) and Competition Law (Law 12,529/2011), and will conduct this engagement ethically.

12. The Parties have read this

12.1. The Parties state that:

a) They received this draft in advance, with time to review it, including with a lawyer of their choice, and they approve it.

b) They understand the clauses. Neither Party may later say it was surprised by them.

c) They will act in good faith in performing and ending this Agreement.

12.2. This Agreement replaces any earlier verbal or written agreement between the Parties on the same object. It does not waive an invoice for work performed under this Agreement.

13. Other terms

13.1. Neither Party is liable for loss caused by an act of God or force majeure (Article 393 of the Civil Code), if proven. The affected Party tells the other immediately and cures the failure within 30 (thirty) days, unless they agree a longer period.

13.10. Changes are valid only in a written amendment signed by both Parties. Verbal side deals are not part of this Agreement.

Annex I

Price and payment

This Annex is part of the Service Agreement between the Client and the Contractor named above. If this Annex and the Agreement conflict on price or payment, this Annex controls. The rest of the Agreement stays in force.

1. Price and payment

1.1. Stub, before the first full month. From the start date through the last day before the 1st of the training month, the Client pays R$ 25,00 (twenty-five reais) per hour worked. The rate is the monthly fee at a 40-hour week: R$ 4.000,00 divided by 160 hours (40 hours times 4 weeks). The Contractor keeps an hour record. The Client approves it. If the start date is the 1st, there is no stub.

1.2. First full training month. The 1st through the last day of that month is R$ 4.000,00 (four thousand reais), not hourly.

1.3. Each calendar month is paid on the 9th of the following month, against a service invoice (nota fiscal). Example: stub hours worked in October are paid on the 9th of November. A November training month is paid on the 9th of December. If the 9th is not a banking day, payment is on the next banking day. Payment is by PIX to the key the Contractor states in writing: ____________________.

1.4. The Contractor sends the invoice by the 5th of the payment month. The stub invoice lists the hours. If the invoice is missing, the Client may hold the payment until it arrives. The hold is not a waiver of the fee.

1.5. If the full training month ends before the last day under clause 6.1, that month’s fee is pro-rated: R$ 4.000,00 divided by the days in that month, times the days worked. The stub stays hours times R$ 25,00. Neither Party owes an extra indemnity for ending without cause.

1.6. Extra expenses are reimbursed only against a tax receipt, and only if the Client approved the expense in writing first.

2. Other payment terms

2.1. The stub hours and the training-month fee in this Annex are the only amounts due for this Agreement. They cover the Contractor’s taxes, labor charges, social charges, tools the Contractor chooses to use, and other costs of performing. They do not cover Client systems the Client provides.

2.2. A later written agreement may set a different monthly fee. That writing is required. A conversation is not enough. Clause 1.5 of the Agreement still applies: a new level is an amendment, not an implied raise.

2.3. Each Party bears the taxes the law assigns to it. Withholding required by law will be made on the invoice.

2.4. The Parties negotiated this Annex in good faith.
