## Purpose

Document the **intended** pay redesign so the team and ops can plan — without going live
before attribution and capacity gates clear. Canonical dollars and cost impact:
[Product Margin Model §5b](../../company/product-margin-model.md).

## Non-negotiable sequence

1. **Tracking gates G1–G2 green** for one full month (`agent_name`, `calendar_name`) —
   see [Q3 analysis §7](../../company/reflections/2026-q3-pricing-retention-analysis.md).
2. Publish show/transfer-weighted setter structure (below).
3. Fund raises from DFY ARPA/margin stack: spend kickers → stop Invisible dials on the
   DFY book → reclaim capacity if the call-center line freezes/winds down — not by
   cutting blended DFY delivery GM below **60%**. Do not fund raises by growing the
   call-center performance book (see [CC margin model](../../company/call-center-margin-model.md)).
4. CS / CCM raises only after CS max-logo floor respected (~35–40) and ≥80% DFY book at
   min spend.

## Setter target structure (gated)

| Component | Current | Target |
|---|---:|---:|
| Base salary | $400/mo | $500–600/mo |
| Booking (commissioned calendar) | $1 | **$0** |
| Show | $4 | $6 |
| Live transfer | $5 | $10 |

**Why:** Pay only for what clients buy (shows / transfers). Transfers are the human-only
premium. Bookings pay **$0** — AI already commoditizes that unit; paying for them
rewards calendar spam over show quality.

**Do not announce launch dates to setters until G1–G2 pass.** People must trust the counter.
