## VA Performance Bonus: Booking & Show Rate Tiers
## Base Salary: R$3,000
This document outlines the performance bonus structure for the Virtual Assistant (VA) role, tied directly to our team's key performance indicators (KPIs) for lead conversion. This bonus is separate from and in addition to your base salary of R$3,000 and any other performance-based compensation.

## The Philosophy: Your North Star KPIs
Your primary objective is to ensure the health and efficiency of our entire client acquisition system. This means you are a key stakeholder in making sure that leads are handled correctly, appointments are booked successfully, and those appointments show up. Your work directly impacts two critical metrics:

Booking Rate: The percentage of total leads that successfully book an appointment.

Show Rate: The percentage of booked appointments that are successfully held (the client shows up).

We operate with two distinct client types — HE and RM — each with its own performance benchmarks. Because these client types behave differently, the tier thresholds are calibrated separately to reflect realistic and fair targets for each.

This bonus structure is designed to reward you for improving the performance of the entire system, not just for individual actions. When the team wins, you win.

## The Bonus Structure: How It Works
The bonus is calculated monthly based on the overall team's performance. It is split into two independent components — one for Booking Rate and one for Show Rate — for each client type. You can earn a bonus for one, the other, or both. Furthermore, a Synergy Bonus is awarded when you successfully hit a target for both KPIs within the same client type in the same month, recognizing the skill it takes to balance both quality and volume.

## Rules & Clarifications
Data Source: All calculations will be based on the official data from our CRM and reporting dashboards. These will be reviewed and shared with you at the end of each month.

Payout: Bonuses are calculated at the end of each calendar month and will be paid out with your following paycheck.

Fair Play: This bonus is designed to reward systemic improvement. Any actions taken to artificially inflate these numbers at the expense of lead quality or team integrity will result in the forfeiture of the bonus and a performance review.

Dynamic Targets: These KPI targets are based on current performance and may be adjusted in the future as our systems and team evolve. Any changes will be communicated in writing with at least 30 days' notice.

This structure gives you a clear, measurable path to significantly increase your earnings by focusing on the most important outcomes for the business. We will track this together, and we are here to support you in hitting these goals.
