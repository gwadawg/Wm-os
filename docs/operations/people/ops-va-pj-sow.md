# Ops VA — PJ Statement of Work

## Purpose

What the Ops contractor owes at each level through **L5**. Proof bars and pay
rules are in the [payment agreement](ops-va-pj-payment-agreement.md).

## Mission

Own ops execution for client tech setup, tickets, and, as levels unlock,
automations and internal systems. Work inside Waiz Media OS. The founder
keeps the offer, pricing, client promises, spend, headcount, and the master
GHL snapshot publish.

## Engagement

- Brazil **PJ**. Reports to the founder. Paid per the [payment agreement](ops-va-pj-payment-agreement.md).
- **L2** is the Ops VA tier. **L3** and above are the same engagement, held without the founder in the daily work.
- Nothing above **L5** is in this statement of work.

## All levels

1. Follow the [priority ladder](../systems/ops-manager-priority-ladder.md): fires, then live onboarding tech, then assigned system work, then team requests on those systems, then new builds.
2. Write blockers, deadline slips, and the daily EOD as the payment agreement requires.
3. Leave media-buying strategy, budgets, client KPI calls, Funnel, and Media Buying with the media buyer.
4. Leave stage moves with the CSM. Tech QA is the tech lane’s done-definition. Marketing QA is the buyer’s.

## By level

| Level | What she owes |
|---|---|
| Training | Learn the tools on real tickets and one supervised Tech Buildout. |
| Holding | Do the work while the founder is still diagnosing, rewriting, or reassigning it. |
| **L2** | VA seat, guided. Tickets in GHL, Hot Prospector, and Closebot. A2P and Tech Buildout with founder review before Tech QA. Team requests that are those systems. Practice a full onboarding, Closebot and the AI agent, and an SOP with the founder. |
| **L3** | That seat without the founder: **3 to 5** tech onboardings to Tech QA, Closebot bugs and AI-agent fixes, SOPs, and updated broken processes. She is the named VA on A2P and Tech Buildout. She helps on automations with the founder still available. |
| **L4** | L3 stays hers. Deep client bugs inside snapshots (**3 to 5**). Improvements to workflows that already exist (**3 to 5**), including when nothing is broken. Client-subaccount edits she ships. Master snapshot edits she shows the founder once before publish. Larger development tasks on the internal software while the back end keeps running. |
| **L5** | **3 to 5** systems she specs, builds, and ships on one light review. L4 back end stays hers. If the founder has approved another VA, she runs that person. |

## Month 1

Systems access, OS walkthrough, shadowing, supervised reps. Check-in at the
end of the month. No automatic raise.

## Not in this SOW

- Levels above L5
- Publishing the master GHL snapshot without the founder seeing it once
- Moving a client’s onboarding stage (CSM)
- Funnel, Media Buying, account strategy, budgets, and client KPI calls
