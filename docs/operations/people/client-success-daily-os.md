# Client Success Daily OS (Laura)

## Purpose

Keep Laura focused on relationship, account health, KPI **commitment follow-up**, on-time OB, payroll, and high-level team/people management — not craft direction for Christian/Pedro or first-line stack bugs.

## Scope

Laura (Client Success + payroll / high-level team management). Day-to-day call center is **Pedro**. Ads/funnels/onboarding tech is **Christian**. You capture their KPI commitments and follow up that work landed; you help on stack bugs only after Pedro is stuck. Canonical: [Role Clarity Lane Map](../../plans/2026-07-15-role-clarity-lane-map.md).

## Mission

Every assigned client feels handled. Red accounts have a **named commitment** from Christian and/or Pedro. You follow up that it was executed. OB/launch only happens after Christian’s checklist is certified and on time. Team gets paid on time. Escalate to CEO only when something is truly important.

## Not Your Job

- Inventing Christian’s or Pedro’s craft tasks (they own what to do to hit KPIs)
- Telling them *how* to buy ads or coach setters — unless a **client nuance** requires a specific ask
- Day-to-day call center / setter floor management (Pedro)
- First-line AI bot / Hot Prospector / booking·GHL bugs (Pedro — you help only after he is stuck)
- Building ad accounts / tech onboard / funnels (Christian)
- Creating ads (CEO)
- Being the default fixer for every Slack ping — queue it or assign it

## Day Structure

| Block | Focus |
|-------|--------|
| Start of day (15–20 min) | Calendar + client Slack/email triage · mark anything that needs Christian (tech blocks) or Pedro |
| Morning | Scheduled check-ins / updates / OB-launch calls |
| Midday | Account health scan (red/yellow/green) · follow up open KPI commitments · help Pedro only if he is stuck on a stack bug |
| As scheduled | **Payroll** (run/confirm on pay cycle) · high-level team/people items (hiring support, time-off, role clarity handoffs, escalated people issues) |
| Mon / Thu | **KPI check** — bring rollup; leave with **named commitments** + due dates from Christian/Pedro. Cadence: [KPI Review Meeting SOP](kpi-review-meeting-sop.md) · [Under-KPI Ladder](under-kpi-diagnosis-ladder.md) |
| Mon (after KPI) | **Ops Planning** with CEO — OB board + system gaps |
| Fri | **Exec Q&A** — only pre-submitted questions for CEO |
| End of day (10 min) | Update rollup · confirm tomorrow’s calls · no open “who owns this?” items |

## Priority Stack (work in order)

1. **Live client calls** — check-ins, updates, OB/launch (only if checklist certified)
2. **Payroll / time-sensitive people ops** — pay cycle deadlines, urgent team issues that block someone from working
3. **Light launch gate** — verify Christian self-cert before scheduling/running launch
4. **Red accounts** — confirm Christian (CPL/CPQL/Opt-in) and/or Pedro (Booking/Show/dials) have a named commitment + due date; follow up
5. **Queue hygiene** — OB/setup breaks land on Christian’s **Tue AM / Wed AM** tech blocks (P0/P1 only as interrupts; P2 waits)
6. **Proactive check-ins** — healthy accounts on cadence, not only fires
7. **High-level team management** — hiring support, onboarding new hires (people side), PTO/coverage, performance notes to CEO when needed
8. **Stack-bug help (after Pedro)** — AI bot / Hot Prospector / GHL automation only when Pedro is stuck; then Christian if still stuck
9. **Admin / notes** — CRM + handoff notes so nothing lives only in your head

## KPI accountability (your scorecard lens)

You do **not** own CPL / CPQL / Opt-in % or Booking % / Show % / dial goals. Those belong to Christian and Pedro.

You own:

- Which accounts are red / yellow / green
- On Mon/Thu KPI: every red has a **named commitment** (what Christian / Pedro will do) + due date
- **Follow-up** that those commitments were executed (without redesigning their craft)
- On-time OB / launch cadence (Christian certified → your light gate → call runs)
- Client updated when something material changes
- Client-health narrative for Planning
- Escalating to CEO only when stuck, churn/legal risk, or seat underperformance

## Payroll + high-level team management

| Owns | Does not own |
|------|----------------|
| Running/confirming payroll on cycle | Designing final comp % without CEO (comp structure is CEO; she executes payroll) |
| Tracking who’s owed what for the period (hours/salary inputs as your process defines) | Day-to-day coaching of Bernardo/Lukka (Pedro) |
| High-level people issues: coverage, PTO conflicts, hiring logistics, escalating performance concerns to CEO | Replacing Pedro as CCM or Christian as MB; inventing their craft tasks |
| Keeping CEO off routine people admin | Strategic hire/fire decisions alone (bring to Fri Q&A / Planning) |

## Escalation

Canonical map: [Role Clarity Lane Map](../../plans/2026-07-15-role-clarity-lane-map.md).

| Situation | Who |
|-----------|-----|
| CPL / CPQL / Opt-in % (their fix) | Christian — you follow up on the named commitment |
| Booking % / Show % / dial goals (their fix) | Pedro — you follow up on the named commitment |
| AI bot / Hot Prospector / booking·GHL automation (Pedro stuck) | **You help next** → then Christian → then CEO |
| Clear OB setup / checklist error | Christian **direct** (queue; Tue/Wed AM unless P0) |
| Ads P0 (same-day launch / not delivering) | Christian interrupt |
| Payroll exception, hire/fire, comp change, or only CEO can decide | Mon Planning or Fri Q&A intake |
| Checklist/system broken twice, churn risk | Mon Planning or Fri Q&A intake |

## Done For The Day Means

- [ ] All scheduled client touches completed or rescheduled with notice
- [ ] No launch ran without checklist cert
- [ ] Reds have named commitments + due dates; open follow-ups chased
- [ ] Stack bugs are on the right rung (Pedro → you → Christian), not vague Slack
- [ ] Payroll/people deadlines for today handled or scheduled
- [ ] Tomorrow’s calls are on the calendar
