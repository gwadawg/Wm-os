## MB: Onboarding Steps
## Complete Step-by-Step Process for VA Implementation
## OVERVIEW
This SOP provides a complete onboarding process for new Media Buyrs following the License & Scale methodology. The process ensures new hires receive proper clarity, confidence, and connection to perform at a high level from day one.

Timeline: 10-day comprehensive onboarding process Roles: VA (executes), Manager (oversees), New Hire (completes training)

## PHASE 1: PRE-ONBOARDING SETUP
## Day 0: Contract & Initial Setup
## Step 1: Send Job Offer & Contract
VA Responsibility:

## Move candidate to “Hired" pipeline stage in GoHighLevel
## Verify automated job offer email was sent
## If no: Send Job Offer Email Template
## Send customized Media Buyer Independent Contractor Agreement
## Media Buyer Contract
## Send Slack notification to team about new hire (if not automated)
## Add them to our P&L sheet
## Step 2: Company Email Setup
VA Responsibility:

## Create company email: [firstname.lastname]@licenseandscale.com
## Send login credentials via secure method
## Send to personal email with instructions for first login
## Email Format: firstname.lastname@licenseandscale.com
## Password Requirements: Minimum 12 characters, include symbols
## Save credentials in NORDPASS
## Step 3: Skool Community Access
VA Responsibility:

## Navigate to Skool Internal Team Community
## Go to Members > Add Member
## Send invitation to company email address (NEVER personal)
## Grant access to "Intro & Onboarding" module (automatic)
## Grant access to “Media Buyer” bootcamp module
## Access Path: Members > Their Profile > Membership > Courses > Media Buyer Bootcamp
## PHASE 2: SYSTEM ACCESS & TOOL SETUP
## Day 0-1: Platform Integration
## Step 4: Slack Workspace Setup
VA Responsibility:

## Add to Internal team Slack workspace
Grant access to relevant Slack channels:

## Main Folder
## #announcements
## #better everyday
## #wins
## #coreteam-general-chat
With these channels make sure to join them yourself as they are public!

## Department Main
## #media-general
## #ads-for-approval
## #media-announcements
## #eods-mediabuyer
## Step 5: Welcome Message & Onboarding Call Booking
VA Responsibility:

Send welcome message with key information:

"Hey [NAME]! Welcome to COMPANYNAME! Please check your company email for an invitation to all platforms you need to join to get officially onboarded. Then start going through our Intro & Onboarding company bootcamp ASAP. Once done, please book your onboarding call here. Also, please read through our new team member onboarding doc. Welcome in!"

## Book their onboarding call with Manager ASAP (ideally same day)
## Invite to EVERY media buyer team meeting + core-team meeting
## Core team calls (daily)
## 1-1 Bi-weekly w/ manager
## Client performance calls (M,W,F)
## Step 6: Platform Access Setup
VA Responsibility:

Invite them to:

GoHighLevel: As "member" with ONLY these permissions:

## Calendars
## Contacts
## Conversations
## Opportunities
## Dashboard
## Funnels
## That's it! Nothing else
## Important: Do NOT grant access to automations, payments, products, or reporting
Monday.com with all board access as below:

## Client onboarding
## Ads onboarding
## Client management
## Ads management
## All ticketing boards
## Facebook Business Manager → Ad as EMPLOYEE
## Loom
## Hubstaff → Create their schedule under the People>Calendars section
## Client Media Drive (Media Directory)
## Canva (for creative templates & editing)
From here we’re all set! Next step is the onboarding call with the MB to initiate the ramp up process…
