## Media Buyer Onboarding Call SOP
## Introduction
This is the step by step guide to conducting an onboarding call with a Media Buyer

## Onboarding Call
4 main goals of this call:

## Set Clear Expectations
## Define Success
## Align Personal Goals with Company Vision
## Create a Clear Action Plan
## Step 1: Company Expectations
L&S | New Team Member Culture Call - we do this by running them through our “culture call” presentation which sets expectations on what it’s like to work at the company, what the core values are, and who they’ll be working with

Here we explain who everyone is in the company they’ll be directly working with and what our mission/vision is for the next 3 years so they’re aligned

## Step 2: Success Alignment
Here is where we run them through 2 key resources:

## Mission
## Media Buyer Mission
## Ramp Up Checklist
## Media Buyer 30 Day Ramp Up Checklist
We make it CRYSTAL CLEAR what success looks like in the role with tangible figures, and then explain to them the ramp up process to help them achieve “success”

We always get temp checks at this stage much like you’d do on a sales call with the following questions

Does this all make sense?

So before we move on NAME, I want you to understand that we don’t settle for anything less than exceptional here, and it’s important for me personally that I give you everything you need and make it as clear as possible what exceptional actually looks like in this role. Do you feel like you understand what that looks like now or is there anything you’d like me to clear up for you?

If clear: Awesome, that being said, is there any reason why you WOULDN’T be able to hit those targets we’ve spoken about today?
