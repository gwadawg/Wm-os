This is the full break down on how to prepare the report for clients when its time to bill them for the pending amount.

The way this reporting works is that each client has their own reporting sheet. Every month you will go in a make scenario and pull the data from the main sheet onto their own sheet.

## 0: Understanding Billing Tools
Two primary tools are used in this process: ClickUp for task management and the Waiz Board for generating reports.

## 0.1 : Click up Billing List
The ClickUp Billing List is used to track client billing cycles and invoice due dates. It is organized into two main tabs:

1. Accounts Tab: This tab contains a list of all clients, considered "Parent Tasks." The "Billing Day" field in this tab indicates the start date of each client's billing cycle.

2. The Billing Tab: This tab contains individual, recurring "tasks" that indicate when a client's invoice is due. The generated reports and any relevant details are uploaded and logged within these tasks to maintain a complete history.

## 0.2: Waiz Board
The waiz board is where we pull the reports. And Check the client KPI's and can pull the reports.

## Step 1: Prep
On this step, all you're doing is looking for the clients billing date. For this, you go to the billing list in the Client folder.

Navigate to the "Client" folder in ClickUp and open the billing list.

2. Go to the Accounts Tab to identify the client's billing start date, which is listed in the "Billing Day" column.

3. Determine the correct date range for the report. The start date will be the "Billing Day" of the previous month, and the end date will be the day before the current month's "Billing Day".
