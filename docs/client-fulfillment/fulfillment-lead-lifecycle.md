📡  Stage 1: Awareness & Retargeting

What's Happening

Everything starts when a prospect sees one of your ads on Facebook or Instagram. But here's what separates us from everyone else: we don't just run one ad and hope for the best.

Our campaigns are built to follow the prospect. If someone watches your video, pauses on your ad, or clicks but doesn't submit — they get retargeted. We stay in front of them until they're ready to take action.

Why We Do This

Most prospects don't convert on their first exposure to anything. That's just human psychology. By the time a homeowner decides to click and submit their information, they've likely seen your brand 3, 5, maybe 7 times. That repetition builds trust before the first conversation even happens.

🧠  The Psychology at Work

Repetition builds familiarity — familiarity builds trust.

Trust means less resistance on your sales call.

A prospect who has seen your face 5 times picks up the phone differently than a cold lead.

This is why our leads convert at higher rates — the education has already started before they ever speak to you.

What You Control at This Stage

At Stage 1, Waiz owns this entirely. Your job is to show up as the face of the brand in the video content we create together. The targeting, placement, retargeting logic, and ad spend management — that's on us.

🎯  Stage 2: Funnel Entry & Lead Qualification

What's Happening

When a prospect clicks your ad, they land on a custom landing page built specifically for you. This isn't a template we recycled from another client — it's branded to you, tested for conversion, and designed to do one job: filter serious prospects from tire-kickers.

Before they can submit their contact information, they go through a series of qualifying questions. This is intentional friction.

Why We Make Them Jump Through Hoops

The rule is simple: the harder it is to become a lead, the better the lead.

Anyone can fill out a one-click form. The leads that come from that? They're low-intent, often fake, and will ghost you on the phone. We've seen it hundreds of times.

When a prospect has to answer real questions — about their home, their situation, what they're looking for — they've made a micro-commitment. They've invested effort. And effort creates intent.

What You Control at This Stage

Waiz builds and manages the funnel. You review and approve the landing page content so it represents you accurately. Any significant life changes (new phone, new focus area) — let us know and we update it.

⚡  Stage 3: CRM Delivery & First Contact

What's Happening

The moment a lead submits their form, two things happen simultaneously: they're pushed into your CRM, and the contact process begins. There is no waiting period. Speed-to-lead is one of the most important variables in conversion, and we treat it that way.

📐  Speed-to-Lead: The Numbers

Leads contacted within 5 minutes are 9x more likely to convert than leads contacted after 30 minutes.

After 10 minutes, conversion probability drops by over 400%.

Our goal: first contact attempt within 5 minutes of form submission — every time.

How Contact Works by Service Tier

Higher-Tier Programs (SDR-Supported): Our live, reverse-mortgage-trained SDR team handles all outreach. They call, text, and qualify the lead on your behalf. You don't touch the lead until there's a booked appointment on your calendar.

Standard Programs: You receive the lead notification directly and are responsible for executing the first contact. We provide you with tested scripts, timing sequences, and follow-up frameworks to maximize your pickup rate.

What Happens in the CRM

Lead is auto-created with all submitted information

Initial text and email sequences are triggered automatically

Call tasks are assigned with specific timing windows

All activity is logged and trackable in real time

What You Control at This Stage

Tier-dependent. On SDR-supported programs — nothing. Sit back and wait for the booking notification. On standard programs — you execute the outreach using our provided frameworks.

📅  Stage 4: Appointment Booking

What's Happening

Once a lead is reached and qualifies, the next step is simple: get them on your calendar. This transition — from prospect to booked appointment — is the moment your system converts potential into pipeline.

How Bookings Happen

SDR-Supported Programs: Our team handles the full booking conversation. They confirm the prospect's interest, qualify their situation, and place the appointment directly onto your calendar. You receive a detailed notification that looks like this:

📲  Example Booking Notification

Call Center Booked ✅

Name: [Lead Name]

Date & Time: [Appointment Date/Time]

Phone: [Lead Phone Number]

Email: [Lead Email]

Notes: [Key qualifying details from the SDR conversation]

Standard Programs: You or your team schedule the appointment directly during your outreach call. The CRM provides the booking infrastructure and calendar sync.

Why This Matters

By the time a prospect reaches your calendar, they've been pre-qualified, they know who you are, and they've confirmed they want to speak with you. This is not a cold call. This is a warm, booked conversation — which is why our show rates and close rates consistently outperform industry averages.

🔥  Stage 5: Pre-Appointment Nurture & Show-Rate Maximization

What's Happening

Booking an appointment is only half the battle. The gap between booking and the actual call is where most systems lose. Prospects get busy, forget, or lose confidence. Our pre-appointment nurture sequence is designed to close that gap.

What Happens After Booking

Automated confirmation message sent immediately after booking

Reminder sequences triggered at 24 hours, 4 hours, and 30 minutes before the call

Personalized messaging that reinforces the value of the upcoming conversation

5-minute pre-call notification sent directly to you so you're ready when they are

🥅  The Goal of This Stage

Every percentage point improvement in show rate directly translates to more conversations, more pipeline, and more closed loans.

We have dedicated show-rate training for loan officers — you'll find it in a separate module. Don't skip it.

The Disposition System

After every scheduled appointment, you'll receive a simple text with two links: Yes or No. Did they show? Click the link. That's it.

This isn't just for record-keeping. When a no-show is dispositioned immediately, our system triggers a re-engagement sequence to get them back on the calendar as fast as possible. The faster we know, the faster we act.

Critical rule: Never leave an appointment undispositioned. It slows down the re-engagement process and costs you potential bookings.

What You Control at This Stage

Beyond dispositions, Waiz handles the automated sequences. Your job is to show up on time, review the lead details you were notified about, and come to the call prepared. We cover maximizing your personal show-rate practices in dedicated training.
