## Operating Content

Welcome to the WaizMedia AI Bot training. In this video, you’re going to learn exactly how your AI assistant works, what it does, and how to use it correctly so you get the best results possible.

Our AI bot was built for one main purpose:

To make your life easier and book you more appointments.

It does this by responding to your leads when you can’t — evenings, weekends, busy days, whenever. It knows everything about you, your business, and the home-equity product you offer. It handles questions, educates your prospects, and does all communication as your assistant.

This is intentional. If it ever makes a mistake, you can simply say: “Sorry about that — my assistant handled that message.”

## What the AI Bot Actually Does
1. Its main job: book appointments.

It is not designed to give financial advice, underwrite loans, or close deals. Its ONLY mission is to move conversations forward and get prospects onto your calendar.

2. It responds instantly when you’re unavailable.

If a lead texts at 10pm, lunch time, or while you’re with another client — the AI handles it.

3. It has full knowledge of your offer.

We train it on:

## Who you are
## Reverse Mortgages
## The FAQs
## The appointment calendar rules
So it can confidently answer general questions and guide the lead toward booking.

## How to Know When the AI Is Chatting
Inside your CRM, when you open a lead, you’ll see messages coming from your assistant. These are automatically generated by the AI unless you manually turn it off.

## If You Want to Interfere or Jump In
This part is VERY important.

The AI does NOT know when you’re about to interrupt it. So if you want to take over a conversation, you MUST:

1. Toggle “AI Off” on the top left of the lead.

This stops the AI from replying while you type.

2. After typing your message, PRESS SAVE.

If you don’t press save, the AI may continue talking over you or contradict you.

3. Best practice: let the AI book the appointment.

Clients who let the AI run uninterrupted get the highest booking rates.

## If You Prefer to Call the Lead Instead
This is actually encouraged. If the lead is actively texting, it’s a perfect time to call.

But if you call the lead, you MUST:

Add the “CLAIM” tag on the bottom of the lead.

This tells the system:

## You took over the conversation
## You spoke to the lead
## The AI should turn off
## Reporting will track this correctly
Once you CLAIM the lead, it will move into your LO pipeline where you can disposition it normally.

This is essential for clean reporting, accurate KPIs, and a clear pipeline.

## What Happens After the Appointment Is Booked
Once the AI books an appointment:

The AI turns off completely.

It will NOT:

## Reschedule
## Cancel
## Update details
## Respond to the lead
At that point, follow-up reminders come from your drip campaign, not the AI.

If you need to:

## Cancel an appointment
## Move an appointment
## Turn reminders on or off
Please follow the SOP specifically designed for appointment management.

## If You Turn the AI Off, Don’t Forget This
You may turn the AI off to write a personal message — even though we don’t recommend it.

But if you choose to do this:

If the lead does NOT reply to you… turn the AI back ON.

Why?

Because if you forget to turn it back on:

## The AI stops working
## We stop following up
## The lead goes cold
## You lose the opportunity
## The lead gets buried and you forget about it
This is one of the biggest reasons clients miss appointments — they turned the AI off and forgot.

So the rule is:

If you interrupt the AI, you are responsible for turning it back on.

## Final Summary
Here’s everything you need to remember:

1. The AI’s #1 job is to book appointments.

Let it run — don’t over-interfere.

2. If you want to jump in, turn the AI OFF first and press SAVE.

3. If you call the lead, add the CLAIM tag so reporting stays accurate.

4. After an appointment is booked, the AI is OFF — that’s drip campaigns, not AI.

5. If you turn the AI off to write a message, TURN IT BACK ON if the lead doesn’t reply.

Follow these simple rules and the AI will reliably book appointments, nurture your leads, and make your day-to-day workload lighter.
