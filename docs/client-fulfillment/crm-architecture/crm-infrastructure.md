## Operating Content

## 1. Overview
This CRM is designed to help you:

## Stay organized
## Manage conversations with leads
## Track all appointments and calendars
## Filter and work your contacts
## Manage your pipeline and dispositions
## Utilize marketing tools (optional)
## Access lead information and notes in one place
This document breaks down the full CRM infrastructure into simple, easy-to-follow sections.

## 2. Dashboard
Purpose:

The Dashboard shows an overview of data inside your CRM.

Key Notes:

Most of the dashboard data will not be accurate because we use our own reporting systems.

You can explore it, but you won’t use it often.

Some of the widgets may appear messy or irrelevant to your workflow.

Client Takeaway:  You will rarely need to be in the dashboard. Move on to the operational tabs.

## 3. Conversations Tab
Purpose:

To manage all message communication with your leads.

How to Use It:

View and respond to all inbound lead messages.

See all unread messages clearly marked.

Tap through conversations to clear unread notifications.

Search for contacts directly inside the Conversations tab.

Why It's Important:

This is where you will respond to leads, follow up, and keep communication organized. It’s one of the top 2–3 tabs you’ll use daily.

## 4. Calendar Tab
Purpose:

View and manage all your appointments and availability.

What You’ll See:

## Your appointment calendar
## AI-booked appointments (if you’re in the upper-tier package)
## Extra calendars like “Calls,” “AI Calendar,” etc.—these are just ways of organizing bookings
Key Functions:

## 4.1 Block Off Time
If you want to block time outside of what we’re booking for you:

## You can block time inside Outlook/Google Calendar  OR
## Block time directly inside the CRM calendar
## Choose your exact time frame
## 4.2 Create Custom Calendars
You can create additional calendars for:

## 1:1 bookings
## Round robin calendars
## Referral partner calendars
## Any other custom use case
Steps:

## Go to Calendar Settings
## Select Create Calendar
## Customize according to your workflow
## 5. Contacts Tab
Purpose:

To access and filter all your leads and contacts.

Why It's Powerful:

You can create filters and smart lists to isolate specific groups of leads.

How to Use It:

## 5.1 Filtering Leads
Examples:

## Filter leads with property value < $200,000
## Filter leads based on any criteria (income, LTV, tags, status, etc.)
## 5.2 Saving Smart Lists
Once filtered:

## Click Save as Smart List
## Name it appropriately (e.g., “Property Value < 200k”)
## The smart list appears at the top for easy access
This allows you to revisit saved segments instantly.

## 6. Opportunities Tab (Pipeline)
Purpose:

To manage your leads through your sales pipeline.

This is one of the most important tabs.

Main Pipelines You'll Use:

## Waiz Booking System
Everything that happens before you speak to the borrower.

## Loan Officer Pipeline
Everything that happens after you’ve spoken with the borrower.

This is where you’ll disposition leads after contact.

How It Works:

## When a lead shows up to an appointment
## Or when there’s a live transfer
## Or when a lead is claimed
These events will automatically populate into your pipeline inbox.

You will then:

## Review leads
## Update dispositions
## Move leads through stages
## Keep yourself fully organized
This tab will be part of your daily workflow.

## 7. Marketing Tab (Optional)
Purpose:

To manage outbound marketing such as social media posts, automated emails, and campaigns.

Tools Within This Tab:

## 7.1 Social Media Scheduling
## Connect Instagram, Facebook, and other channels
## Create and automate posts for 30–90 days at a time
## Useful if you want automated brand presence
## 7.2 Email Marketing
You can:

## Create custom email campaigns
## Use blank templates
## Use pre-built templates already provided
Templates include:

## Financial health
## Home equity topics
## Objection removers
## “Can you outlive a reverse?” style topics
## And many more
## 7.3 Blast Campaigns
You can send:

## One-time blast emails
## Automated sequences
## Educational sequences
Important Note:

Marketing tools are optional. They’re available, but you’re not required to use them.

## 8. Automations Tab
Important Warning:

Do NOT touch automations unless you are experienced.

Why:

## There are dozens of complex automations baked into your system
## Changing anything can break the workflow
## Our team manages all automations for you
When you’re ready to build your own:

## Join the support group
## Ask questions
## Use online resources
## More training will be released for this
But for now: Leave automations alone.

## 9. Settings → Custom Fields
Purpose:

To add new custom fields to track additional information about contacts.

Examples of Custom Fields You Can Add:

## Monthly income
## Residual income
## Specific financial metrics
## Any data point you want to capture from your leads
How to Add Custom Fields:

## Go to Settings
## Select Custom Fields
## Choose Contact Fields
## Add the field type (text, number, dropdown, etc.)
## Save and use it inside any lead’s record
Now you have a new field available in all contact profiles.

## 10. Lead Contact View (Profile)
Purpose:

This is where you view everything about a specific lead.

Sections Inside the Contact Profile:

## 10.1 Custom Fields Panel (Left Side)
Shows:

## All your custom fields
## All information added or collected on the lead
## 10.2 General Information Tab
Shows:

## Borrower information
## What they want to accomplish
## Debts
## Property info
## LTV
## Marital status
## Ages
## Full context for the loan conversation
## 10.3 Notes
You can:

## Add notes
## Document calls
## Track lead behavior
## 10.4 Booking Appointments
Book appointments directly from the contact record.

## 10.5 Tasks
You can:

## Create follow-up tasks
## Schedule reminders
## Get notified on your phone when tasks are due
This helps you stay on top of every conversation.

## 11. Daily Workflow Summary (What You’ll Use MOST)
While the CRM has many features, the majority of your workflow will be inside:

## Conversations – responding to leads
## Opportunities – managing the pipeline
## Contacts – filtering or checking data
Optional:

## Marketing tab
## Calendar customization
## Task management
The system is designed to keep things simple and organized.

## 12. Final Notes
The CRM is straightforward once you get familiar with it.

Each tab has a purpose, but you won’t use everything every day.

Focus on mastering Conversations + Opportunities first.

Use Contacts and Smart Lists to deepen your filtering and organization.

Leave Automations alone unless guided.

Always update lead dispositions so your reporting stays accurate.
