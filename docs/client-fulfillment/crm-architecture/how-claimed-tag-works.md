## Operating Content

Overview

This document explains the purpose, function, and correct usage of the Claimed Tag inside your Waiz Media CRM. This SOP is part of your client training and ensures full clarity on how to manage leads, organize your pipeline, and communicate effectively with both the system and our team.

Using the Claimed Tag properly helps:

Turn off the AI when you take over a lead

Remove the lead from the call queue

Ensure our team does not continue contacting someone you've already reached

Feed accurate reporting back to Meta for optimization

Keep your CRM pipeline clean, organized, and aligned with your workflow

What Is the Claimed Tag?

The Claimed Tag is a manual tag inside your CRM used to signal that you have personally spoken with a lead or taken control of the contact.

When applied, it tells the system (and our team):

You made contact with the lead

You are now handling the conversation

Our call center and AI should stop all outreach

When to Use the Claimed Tag

You should apply the Claimed Tag any time you personally speak with a lead, whether the contact happened through:

Your own manual dialing

An inbound call

A returned call

A follow-up you initiated

Any scenario where you, not the AI or call center, spoke to the lead

Use it in these key cases:

1. Manual Dialing – You Reached the Lead

If you're calling your leads directly and you connect with someone, add the Claimed Tag immediately.

2. Inbound Call From a Lead

If a lead calls you back and you talk to them, apply the tag.

3. Appointment Booked Outside the AI Process

If you book the appointment directly in real time, apply the Claimed Tag and then set the appropriate appointment status.

What Happens When You Add the Claimed Tag

Applying this tag triggers 3 important automations:

1. AI Turns Off for This Lead

The system immediately stops all AI engagement so your messages don't overlap.

2. Lead Is Added to Your Personal Pipeline

The contact will move into your pipeline so you can:

Manage dispositions

Track progress

Log outcomes

Move them through your sales process

3. Our Team Stops Calling the Lead

Your ISA team will no longer:

Call this person

Follow up

Attempt live transfers

This prevents duplicate contact and ensures professional communication.

Why Use the Claimed Tag

Using this tag correctly is essential for a clean, efficient, and high-performing system.

1. Clean Reporting & Accurate Data

Our system needs to know which leads you've engaged with so the reporting is accurate. This impacts:

Lead quality analysis

Appointment-setting performance

Your ROI metrics

2. Clear Communication With the Team

When the tag is applied, our team knows:

The lead is spoken to

You are handling them

No further outreach is required

3. Helps Meta Improve Your Ad Quality

Meta learns from behaviors like:

When a lead is claimed

When they convert to a call

When they progress in your pipeline

Accurate feedback → better targeting → better CPL over time.

4. Keeps Your CRM Organized

This prevents:

Duplicate follow-up

AI interruptions

Missed opportunities

Example Scenarios

Scenario 1 – You Call a Lead and Speak With Them

Steps:

Open the lead profile

Apply the Claimed tag

Add notes

Move to your pipeline stage

Scenario 2 – Lead Calls You Back

Steps:

Apply the Claimed tag

Remove them from any open call tasks

Set appointment or follow-up disposition

Scenario 3 – You Book an Appointment Live

Steps:

Apply the tag

Mark appointment as Booked or Show if immediate

Add details

Using the Claimed Tag ensures:

AI won’t interrupt you

Our team won’t double contact the lead

Your data stays clean

Your CRM remains organized

Meta receives accurate conversion signals

This single tag keeps your entire system aligned. Use it every time you speak with a lead.
