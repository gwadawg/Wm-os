# RM Conceptual Beliefs Playbook

> **North star:** Install seven prospect beliefs through discovery — **objection prevention**, not objection fighting — so by the pitch they enroll themselves and the close becomes logistics.

**Universal framework:** [Conceptual Beliefs Framework](../../acquisition/sales/conceptual-beliefs-framework.md). **Objection word tracks** (when pushback still surfaces): [RM Borrower Objections](../reverse-mortgage-dna/rm-borrower-objections.md) — diagnose which belief was missed, then handle there.

## Purpose

Give LOs a belief-installation map for live RM calls: what must be true in the borrower's mind, how to check each belief with questions (Socratic — they say it, not you), and when you're cleared to pitch.

Not LO mindset (→ [LO D2C Sales Foundations](playbook-lo-d2c-sales-foundations-rm.md) · nurture follow-up beliefs → [Nurture Framework §2](../client-marketing/playbook-nurture-framework.md#2-limiting-beliefs-to-break)). Not objection scripts (→ objections doc above).

## Scope


| Included                                                          | Excluded                                              |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Belief ladder, limiting vs empowering beliefs, check-off criteria | "When they say X" objection tables (→ objections doc) |
| RM discovery lines, pitch transition gate, archetype weighting    | Call-center setter scripts                            |
| Ferrari / resourcefulness frame for Money + Cost                  | Guaranteed savings or pressure tactics                |


## Owner

Client Success.

## Trigger

- Training discovery / qualifying (not product dumps)
- Coaching premature pitches or post-discovery ghosting
- CRM belief worksheets and call QA

## Inputs

- Live borrower; form intent (use of funds) if D2C Meta lead
- Financial picture: income, debts, mortgage, equity, property charges
- [LO D2C Sales Foundations](playbook-lo-d2c-sales-foundations-rm.md) + [RM Borrower ICP](../course-material/rm-borrower-icp-education.md) recommended

## Outputs

- Each belief: **checked**, **partial**, or **gap** — logged on worksheet
- Anchor pain documented (predicates the rest of the call)
- Go/no-go: more discovery vs education vs pitch vs joint call vs BAMFAM
- **Done signal:** prospect shifts from *"should I?"* to *"how do I?"*

## The belief ladder

Seven beliefs = seven rungs. **Miss one → hesitation at close.** Your job is to know which rung is missing and stay there until it's checked.


| #   | Belief               | Limiting (unchecked)                    | Empowering (checked)                           |
| --- | -------------------- | --------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **Pain**             | "It's fine / not urgent"                | "This is real and costing me"                  |
| 2   | **Doubt**            | "I'll figure it out / other paths work" | "This path fits; I need proper guidance"       |
| 3   | **Cost of inaction** | "Waiting is safer"                      | "Waiting costs more than moving"               |
| 4   | **Desire**           | "Extra cash would be nice"              | "I know what life looks like fixed"            |
| 5   | **Support**          | "I'll tell family later"                | "Decision makers aligned or scheduled"         |
| 6   | **Trust**            | "What's the catch?"                     | "This process makes sense for me"              |
| 7   | **Money**            | "Not worth it / can't afford friction"  | "Outcome justifies cost — I'll be resourceful" |


**Checked** = they articulated the empowering side in **their words** (Socratic), not you lecturing it.

## Operating principles

1. **Objection prevention** — Hard objections at the end mean a belief wasn't installed. Fix the belief, not the rebuttal. (Response tracks: [RM Borrower Objections](../reverse-mortgage-dna/rm-borrower-objections.md).)
2. **Socratic dialogue** — Ask until *they* say it. Consistency bias: people believe their own words.
3. **The Gap** — Pain is current state vs desired state. Start with problem (move away); Desire holds the move-toward side.
4. **Predicated conversation** — Once Pain is anchored, every question and education point ties back to *that* gap. Don't drift to product.
5. **Focus on their problem** — Not rates, not you-as-salesperson, not HECM features until beliefs support the pitch.
6. **Sprinkle, don't interrogate** — Beliefs emerge through normal qualifying.
7. **Dignity always** — Shame language confirms failure and kills Trust ([Doctrine RM Marketing](../reverse-mortgage-dna/doctrine-rm-marketing.md)). Validate without pity.
8. **Multi-call reality** — RM borrowers decide over weeks ([ICP](../reverse-mortgage-dna/intelligence-icp-rm.md)). Call 1 may not check all seven — log gaps and nurture.

## Belief gaps, ghosting, and nurture

Borrowers don't only ghost because you didn't call enough. Often they **disengage from nurture** when a conceptual belief was never installed — follow-up alone won't fix a Trust or Cost gap.

| Unchecked belief | Typical nurture symptom |
|------------------|-------------------------|
| Pain | Low urgency — "we're fine" — curiosity fades |
| Doubt | Compares options forever; goes silent mid-research |
| Cost | "I'll wait" — defers without a booked next step |
| Support | Stalls until spouse/kids align — never books joint call |
| Trust | One scary detail → stops returning calls |
| Desire / Money | Polite interest but no emotional win or value clarity |

**What to do:** Log checked vs gap on every live call. Each nurture touch should **move one belief** (education, joint-call booking, personalized follow-up) — not repeat the same pitch. Operational follow-up rules: [Nurture Framework §2](../client-marketing/playbook-nurture-framework.md#2-limiting-beliefs-to-break) and [§4 aged reactivation](../client-marketing/playbook-nurture-framework.md#4-aged-lead-reactivation).

**Reactivation angle:** Aged leads may now be ready because a belief finally shifted (equity grew = Cost; spouse aligned = Support). Segment and personalize per [Aged Lead Reactivation Script](../client-marketing/script-aged-lead-reactivation-rm.md).

## Archetype — which belief to weight first

Listen in the first few minutes; weight discovery toward the belief most likely unchecked.


| Signals                            | Likely archetype        | Weight first                |
| ---------------------------------- | ----------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Minimizes — "fine, just tight"     | Security-Seeker         | Pain + Trust                |
| "Did everything right" frustration | Financially Squeezed    | Pain + Doubt (validation)   |
| Compares options, asks detail      | Strategic Retiree       | Doubt + Trust (methodology) |
| Kids, inheritance, help family now | Legacy Planner          | Support + Doubt (heirs)     |
| Early retire, longevity worry      | Pre-Retiree             | Cost (long horizon) + Doubt |
| Pride, military, resists "help"    | Veteran (ICP)           | Trust + Desire (dignity)    |
| Widowed, alone, depleted savings   | Widowed homeowner (ICP) | Pain + Support + Trust      |


Archetype detail: [Doctrine RM Marketing §4](../reverse-mortgage-dna/doctrine-rm-marketing.md) · [Intelligence ICP RM §6](../reverse-mortgage-dna/intelligence-icp-rm.md).

## Core framework — seven beliefs


| #   | Belief               | One-line test                              | Primary phase         |
| --- | -------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | --------------------- |
| 1   | **Pain / Problem**   | The **gap** is real and felt               | Discovery             |
| 2   | **Doubt**            | Can't solve alone; this path + advisor fit | Education             |
| 3   | **Cost of inaction** | Waiting > acting (**action threshold**)    | Education → pitch     |
| 4   | **Desire**           | Emotional win behind the dollars           | Discovery → pitch     |
| 5   | **Support**          | Decision makers known / on call            | Discovery → pre-close |
| 6   | **Trust**            | Methodology + advisor credible             | Discovery → education |
| 7   | **Money**            | Value clear → resourceful about friction   | Pitch / close         |

## 1. Pain / Problem belief

**What it is:** The **gap** between where they are and where they want to be — **felt**, not minimized. Two sides: **problem** (move away from stress) and **unfulfilled desire** (move toward — Desire belief captures the toward side). **Establish problem first.**

**ICP pains to probe** ([Intelligence ICP RM §2–3](../reverse-mortgage-dna/intelligence-icp-rm.md)):

- Paycheck-to-paycheck on fixed income; savings draining
- **House-rich, cash-poor** — equity locked, life doesn't show it
- Credit cards / minimum payments creeping up
- **Property taxes, insurance, HOA** rising faster than income
- **Medical** bills or fear of one big expense
- **Deferred repairs** (roof, HVAC, accessibility)
- **Burden on children** — guilt, won't ask for help
- Choosing between groceries, utilities, or medications

**D2C Meta:** Anchor to **form intent** — "You reached out about [use of funds] — is that still the main thing?"

### Check-off criteria

- They name the problem with emotional weight in their own words
- You can state their gap back and they agree
- Minimizers ("just looking") have been gently challenged once — still unchecked until depth shows

### Lines to use

1. "What's the main thing you're looking to accomplish with this?"
2. "How long have you been dealing with [mortgage / tight cash flow / medical bills / minimum payments]?"
  - "Roughly what does that cost you per month?"
3. "With [paycheck-to-paycheck / borrowing from family], how has that affected your peace of mind or retirement?"
4. "Are property taxes or insurance going up faster than your income?"
5. "Anything on the house you've had to put off — roof, AC, repairs?"
6. "If something big hit tomorrow — medical, home repair — what would that do?"
7. "You've built a lot of equity — does it feel usable, or locked up while month-to-month stays tight?"

**Follow-ups when surface:**

- "What happens if next month is tighter?"
- "When you're lying awake about money, what's on your mind?"
- "What would change in your week if that pressure wasn't there?"

## 2. Doubt belief

**What it is:** A **spectrum** — they must believe (a) a solution is **possible**, and (b) doing it **alone or via the wrong path** is impractical vs **reverse + you**. The **buying pocket:** possible, but not without the right guide and product.

**Layers:**

- **DIY / alternatives exhausted** — refi denied or unaffordable, HELOC payment too high, sale unacceptable, waiting doesn't work
- **Product fit** — HECM fits vs alternatives for *their* situation (months/years out)
- **Advisor fit** — you vs shopping LOs; past bad experiences reframed
- **Debt Frame** — still see reverse as last resort? → education before features ([Doctrine §2](../reverse-mortgage-dna/doctrine-rm-marketing.md))

**Property charges:** Willingness/ability to keep taxes, insurance, maintenance current — qual signal ([RM High-Quality Lead Acquisition](../client-marketing/rm-high-quality-lead-acquisition.md)).

### Check-off criteria

- They explain why alternatives didn't or won't work *for them*
- They accept this path as plausible (not "just shopping rates")
- Debt Frame shifting toward retirement-tool frame (not full close on call 1)

### Lines to use

1. "Before today, were you looking at other ways to [outcome] — refi, line of credit, selling?"
2. "What have you tried or considered? What happened?"
3. "What would you do if we didn't figure something out on this call?"
4. "Based on what you shared, here's why some people in your situation look at this vs [alternative they named]…"
5. **HECM vs HELOC:** "Big difference for a lot of folks — required monthly payments vs none on the mortgage, and whether unused line can grow."

**Advisor frame:** "My job is the full picture so **you** decide — including not moving forward."

---

## 3. Cost of inaction belief (action threshold)

**What it is:** Pain of **status quo** > pain of fees, timing, or change. They believe waiting is the more expensive choice.

**RM cost anchors:**

- **Inflation** — income flat, costs up for years
- **Interest paid** on cards if minimums continue (backward cost)
- **Deferred repairs** — worse and pricier later
- **Timeline** — "You've been in this [X months/years] — what has waiting already cost?"
- **Future pace:** "If nothing changes in 2–3 years, what's harder?" then bring to present *(natural, not scripted doom)*
- **Qualification window** — reviewing options while eligible vs uncertainty later *(no guarantee of future eligibility)*

**Calm urgency** (not pressure): "Most folks wish they'd had this conversation before it felt urgent — so they had more options."

**Willingness tie-in:** "I can afford it but it's not worth it" usually means **Cost** unchecked, not Money — revisit action threshold before debating fees.

**Deferral patterns:** If-when ("when rates drop," "after I save for appraisal") and sunk cost ("already in the HELOC") usually mean **Cost** or **Doubt** still unchecked — stay in Socratic discovery on that belief; don't lecture labels on a live call.

### Check-off criteria

- They verbalize what gets worse if nothing changes
- Waiting feels costlier than acting — in their words
- Values linked (home, credit, independence, family)

### Lines to use

1. "What has waiting already cost you — money, stress, opportunities?"
2. "If nothing changes in the next 12–24 months, what gets harder?"
3. "What matters most that you'd hate to lose or see get worse?"
4. "Is this the home you plan to stay in long term?"
5. "How long has your credit been in this range — has it always been like this?"

**Values probes:** forever home · credit · savings/reputation · not burdening kids · spouse security if widowed

## 4. Desire belief

**What it is:** The **compelling payoff** — emotional outcome when the gap closes. Not "I want $30K" but peace, dignity, independence, family, fixing the home.

**ICP outcomes to listen for:**

- Stop worrying every month · breathe again
- **Not ask kids for help**
- **Stay in the home** for life · fix what's breaking
- Survive → **enjoy** retirement · travel · grandkids
- **Help family while alive** (Legacy Planner)
- **Spouse protected** if something happens to them
- Confidence they **won't outlive** the plan (Pre-Retiree)

### Check-off criteria

- You can complete: "When this works, they'll ___ so they can ___."
- Emotional driver captured in CRM for pitch weave

### Lines to use

- "What would that do for you ultimately?"
- "What does that look like day to day once it's handled?"
- "What would it mean if you didn't have to lean on [kids/family] for this?"

**Pitch weave:** mechanism → monthly impact → **their** emotional outcome (weave naturally — don't recite their story back).

> Example: "Removing the $1,220 payment frees ~$14,600/year toward the cards you described — so you're not choosing between bills and the trip you wanted."

---

## 5. Support belief

**What it is:** Stakeholders identified — and ideally **on the call** or a joint call scheduled. Support is not only "who decides" but **will influencers back the decision** (adult children often skeptics, not blockers).

**RM rules:**

- Influencers not present → **joint call** before full pitch
- **Legacy Planner:** involve heirs as educated allies early
- **Widow / secret mission:** adult kids may need their own call — don't burn pitch on proxy
- **Non-borrowing spouse** on title → include in process

### Check-off criteria

- Names and roles known
- Aware of the call or joint session booked
- You know whether to limit detail until everyone is present

### Lines to use

1. "As far as household finances — mostly you, or someone else involved?"
2. "Does [spouse / partner / adult children] know you're exploring this today?"
3. "Do your kids have strong opinions about home equity — would it help to have them on a call with me?"

---

## 6. Trust belief

**What it is:** Two layers — **relational** (advisor, not predator) and **methodology** (HECM process works, different from horror stories and past bad LO experiences). Trust in **method** matters as much as liking you.

**Relational trust:** agenda with no pressure · curiosity · dignity · options including "no" · long-term advisor frame

**Methodology trust:** FHA-insured · counseling requirement · transparent fees · why past attempts failed · Debt Frame → Retirement Tool Frame

**D2C:** Confirm form intent · consistent identity · offer verification if scam fear (details → objections doc)

### Check-off criteria

- They share sensitive financial detail
- They accept education without arms crossed
- They see you as guide, not "another salesperson"
- Product stigma acknowledged and met with facts, not dismissal

### Lines to use

- Open: "Goal today is understanding your situation and options — zero pressure to proceed."
- "Even if our costs aren't the lowest, you're hiring someone in your corner after closing."
- "What did you hear that made you skeptical — old program, bad experience, something else?"
- "I'll walk through costs and obligations straight — including when this isn't a fit."

---

## 7. Money belief

**What it is:** **Resources + willingness** — they believe the outcome is worth out-of-pocket friction (appraisal, costs not rolled in) and will be **resourceful** when value is clear.

Most RM costs roll into the loan; Money belief still matters for appraisal and "fees too high" moments.

### The Ferrari frame (resourcefulness)

If someone with almost no cash were offered a **Ferrari for $10,000**, they'd find a way within 24 hours — borrow from family, sell something, figure it out — because the **value is obvious** relative to the price.

Same psychology on your call: when **Pain, Cost, and Desire** are checked, a $500–$700 appraisal (or similar friction) is not a resource problem — it's a **value clarity** problem. They haven't fully internalized what they're getting yet. Don't argue affordability; **deepen Cost and Desire** until the tradeoff feels obvious.

**Compliance:** Never guarantee net savings, proceeds, or that appraisal "pays for itself" in a fixed timeline. Frame as their judgment on value vs friction using **their** numbers only.

**Diagnose before Money work:**


| Signal                         | Usually means             | Action                                |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| "Can't afford appraisal"       | Cost / Desire / value gap | Future pace + outcome vs one-time fee |
| "Have equity but not worth it" | **Cost** unchecked        | Action threshold, not fee haggling    |
| Genuine no path / no equity    | Qual                      | Honest exit — don't force             |


### Check-off criteria

- They agree outcome justifies friction once value is laid out in their terms
- Resourcefulness emerges ("I could ask my son" / "I can cover that") — not you pressuring

### Lines to use

1. Walk through proceeds vs out-of-pocket — what's paid at closing vs now
2. "The appraisal is a one-time [X] to unlock [Y monthly relief you validated] — given everything you've shared about [their pain], does that tradeoff make sense?"
3. "All regulated fees disclosed upfront — nothing surprises you at the table."
4. After Cost + Desire are solid: "When the numbers make sense for your situation, the friction piece is usually workable — does that match how you're thinking?"

## Pitch transition gate

Do **not** pitch until Pain is anchored and you know which beliefs are still open.

1. **Summarize the gap** in their words — problem + what they want on the other side
2. **Permission bridge:** "Based on what you shared, would it help if I walked through how this could work for your situation — including costs and when it wouldn't make sense?"
3. If yes → education (Doubt, Cost, Trust methodology) → personalized pitch
4. If no → more discovery or BAMFAM for joint call

**Self-enrollment signal:** They ask what's next, how to apply, what you need from them — close is logistics (app, docs, counseling, joint call).

## Call flow

```text
OPEN     → Trust (agenda, form intent, no pressure)
DISCOVER → Pain (The Gap), Desire signals, Support
EDUCATE  → Doubt (spectrum), Cost of inaction, Trust (methodology)
GATE     → Summarize gap + permission to show solution
PITCH    → Weave Desire + Money (value/resourcefulness); confirm Support
CLOSE    → Logistics — they ask how to move forward
```

## Decision rules


| Condition                  | Action                                                  |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Pain not checked           | Stay in discovery; no full pitch                        |
| Doubt weak                 | Alternatives + product comparison; Debt Frame education |
| Cost not checked           | Future pace, inflation, repair delay, timeline          |
| "Not worth it" with equity | **Cost**, not Money — action threshold                  |
| "Can't afford appraisal"   | Cost + Desire + Ferrari frame — value clarity           |
| Desire unknown             | Branch on outcomes they mention                         |
| Support gap                | Joint call; limit pitch                                 |
| Trust low                  | Slow down; methodology + dignity; follow-up             |
| All checked                | Pitch → logistics close + BAMFAM                        |
