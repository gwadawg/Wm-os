# LO D2C Sales Foundations Playbook — RM

> **North star:** Show up on paid leads as a **retirement mortgage consultant** — earn trust through curiosity and congruence, guide borrowers through emotional decisions, and install beliefs before you pitch.

Consolidates LO mindset + call approach for **direct-to-consumer** reverse mortgage sales. Two course lessons map to **Part 1** (market + beliefs) and **Part 2** (call approach).

## Purpose

Give loan officers the internal beliefs and opening mechanics they need **before** running discovery on Meta/D2C leads — so they don't treat paid traffic like referrals, product-dump on the first minute, or quit when borrowers push back.

## Scope

| Included | Excluded |
|----------|----------|
| Referral vs paid-lead context | Full seven-belief discovery ladder (→ [Conceptual Beliefs Playbook](playbook-rm-conceptual-beliefs.md)) |
| Five LO operating beliefs | Word-for-word objection tables (→ [RM Borrower Objections](../reverse-mortgage-dna/rm-borrower-objections.md)) |
| Marketing–sales congruency rules | Waiz ad strategy internals (→ client kickoff / application playbooks) |
| Identity shift + opening frame | BAMFAM booking mechanics (→ [BAMFAM Playbook](playbook-bamfam-rm.md)) |
| Anti-patterns, decision rules, self-check | Nurture cadence / dial blocks (→ [Nurture Framework](../client-marketing/playbook-nurture-framework.md)) |
| Preview: "I don't want a reverse mortgage" | Full belief-installation ladder (→ [Conceptual Beliefs Playbook](playbook-rm-conceptual-beliefs.md)) |

## Owner

Client Success (playbook maintenance). Community/Education owns course video + checklist.

## Trigger

Use this playbook when:

- Onboarding an LO who will take **paid social / D2C** appointments
- Coaching low show-to-close, early hang-ups, or "leads don't want reverse" complaints
- QA on first 60 seconds of consultation calls
- Before Conceptual Beliefs training (this is the prerequisite)

## Inputs

- Booked borrower on calendar (ideally with form intent / CRM notes)
- Basic qual awareness (age, equity, property charges) — not a full underwrite on minute one
- Recommended: [RM Borrower ICP — Client Education](../course-material/rm-borrower-icp-education.md)

## Outputs

- LO internalizes five operating beliefs (self-check passed)
- Opens calls with outcome-first consultant frame (not product dump)
- Matches ad promise / form intent on the call (congruency)
- Knows when to bridge to Conceptual Beliefs discovery vs pitch
- **Done signal:** borrower stays in conversation past minute five without arms-crossed product resistance

## Part 1 — Market context & LO beliefs

*Course Lesson 1 — target ~8–10 min*

### Two lead buckets

| Bucket | Trust at hello | What wins the call |
|--------|----------------|-------------------|
| **Referral / sphere** | Transferred from someone they trust | Context + efficiency; still needs discovery |
| **Paid ad (D2C Meta, etc.)** | Zero — they saw an ad, may not remember your name | **Connection + consultant frame** before product |

D2C reverse mortgage leads need **more** relationship work than any other product in the space because of who the ICP is and the industry's trust deficit. Detail: [RM Borrower ICP](../course-material/rm-borrower-icp-education.md).

**Rule:** Never diagnose lead quality on touch one using referral expectations.

### Why mindset comes first

Without the right mindset, LOs burn out, blame leads, and run the system like 2021 refi order-taking. Paid leads **opted in, talked to your team, and booked** — that is earned intent. Guarded behavior is protection, not rejection.

---

## Five LO operating beliefs

### 1. Earned intent — these people need help

- They clicked, opted in, and **showed up** — that is not accidental.
- Skepticism and guard-up are **defense mechanisms**, not proof the lead is bad.
- You are not bothering them when you follow up or stay on the call with patience.
- Compassion + curiosity beat retreat when they seem closed off.

> **Mindset:** They came for help even if they don't know how to ask for it.

**Tie to nurture:** Persistence on opted-in leads is ethical when done with dignity — [Nurture Framework §1](../client-marketing/playbook-nurture-framework.md#1-why-nurturing-matters-for-reverse-mortgage).

### 2. Advisor market — not an order-taker market

- This is not 2021 low-rate refi. Terms are often **imperfect** vs what they imagined.
- Example: they want $430K cash-out; you may deliver $10K cash-out + payment change — still a fit if outcome solves their gap.
- **Emotional sales, not spreadsheet sales.** Perfect math with no emotional alignment often dies on the table.
- Emailing a proposal and hoping they read numbers is not a close strategy in this market.

> **Mindset:** This market belongs to advisors — not order-takers.

**Clarify:** You are not manipulating when you walk them through the decision in their best interest. You are failing them when you throw numbers and disappear.

### 3. Curiosity before pitch

- Your most powerful tool is **questions**, not product features.
- Surface answers ("$10K debt") hide emotional drivers — layer down (minimum payments → duration → no game plan).
- **Education dump ≠ discovery.** Case-study lectures don't earn D2C trust; curiosity-led dialogue does.
- Full layering drills and Pain belief checks → [Conceptual Beliefs Playbook §1](playbook-rm-conceptual-beliefs.md#1-pain--problem-belief).

> **Mindset:** Be more interested than interesting.

**Preview drill (Pain belief):**

| Layer | Example question | What you learn |
|-------|------------------|----------------|
| Surface | "How much debt are we talking about?" | "$10K" |
| Structure | "Mostly cards? What are minimums adding up to?" | "$1K/mo, minimum only" |
| Timeline | "How long have you been paying minimums?" | "Over a year" |
| Stakes | "What's your game plan to get out?" | Often: none — sell, hope, wait |

That single thread becomes pitch ammo **and** helps them hear their own problem.

### 4. Doctor, not waiter

- You are a **consultant**, not a vendor taking orders.
- If there is financial benefit and they don't understand it yet, letting them walk is a **disservice** — not respect.
- **"Not interested"** usually means not interested **now** — disposition and nurture, not trash.
- When they ask for the wrong fix (antibiotics instead of root canal), your job is to show **cost of inaction** calmly — not pressure.

> **Mindset:** Selling done right is protection from the wrong decision.

**Professional responsibility** (not legal "fiduciary" overclaim):

1. Understand their full situation — financial and emotional
2. Surface unstated fears and misconceptions
3. Help them decide aligned with true needs
4. Say clearly when a reverse mortgage is **not** the right fit

Compliance: [RM Compliance Guardrails](../reverse-mortgage-dna/rm-compliance-guardrails.md).

### 5. Borrower psychology — you're rewriting a story

- Seniors carry **shame, guilt, and "giving up"** baggage around the product name.
- They often fight the **tribe's narrative** (kids, peers, media) — not you personally.
- "No" frequently means no to **embarrassment**, not no to relief.
- Debt frame vs retirement tool frame: [Doctrine RM Marketing §2](../reverse-mortgage-dna/doctrine-rm-marketing.md) — don't re-teach full doctrine here.

> **Mindset:** They're protecting the story they've been told. Help them rewrite it with dignity.

## Part 2 — Call approach

*Course Lesson 2 — target ~10–12 min*

### Marketing–sales congruency

Marketing and sales are one motion. Extreme failure mode: ads promise one thing (timeshare vacation), LO sells another — total misalignment.

**Waiz standard:** We market **outcomes** (payment relief, staying home, debt freedom) — not "buy a reverse mortgage." On the call:

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Read CRM / form notes — match their stated intent | Open with product jargon they didn't ask for |
| Mirror outcome language from the ad promise | Jump to balance + value interrogation |
| Ask what they're trying to accomplish | Assume they remember it's a reverse mortgage |

If they wanted to **remove a mortgage payment**, start there — not HECM feature lists.

### Identity shift — retirement mortgage expert

Subconscious bias kills trust: if every answer is "reverse," you sound like a product rep — even when you're right.

| Frame | Borrower hears |
|-------|----------------|
| Reverse mortgage specialist | "They only sell one thing" |
| **Retirement mortgage consultant** | "They'll find what's right for me" |

**Pivot language:** "I work in the retired mortgage space — I help homeowners access equity in the most effective way for **their** situation. I don't know the right program until I understand you."

You will still sell reverses when fit — but the **consultant position** unlocks deeper questions and neutralizes broker-skepticism.

### Education dump vs discovery

| Education dump (fails on D2C) | Discovery (wins on D2C) |
|-------------------------------|-------------------------|
| Feature monologue | Outcome + situation questions |
| "Let me tell you about reverse mortgages" | "What are you trying to accomplish?" |
| Case studies before rapport | Layers on **their** pain |
| Closing on logic | Closing on emotional gap + beliefs |

Borrowers want to feel they **decided** — not that they were sold. Belief installation → [Conceptual Beliefs Playbook](playbook-rm-conceptual-beliefs.md).

### Opening the call

**Wrong open (common):**

> "Have you heard of a reverse mortgage? What's your mortgage balance? What do you want to cash out?"

**Right open — consultant frame:**

> "Great to connect — I work in the retired mortgage space and help homeowners like you access home equity in the most effective and affordable way possible. I'd love to learn what you're looking to accomplish and what's going on in your situation so I can figure out the best way to serve you."

**First-five-minutes checklist:**

1. Warm greeting + name confirmation
2. Consultant identity (retirement mortgage / equity guide)
3. Permission to ask questions — no pressure
4. Anchor to **form intent** if available ("You mentioned [use of funds] — is that still the main thing?")
5. One open question before any product mention

Qualify early enough to avoid dead ends (e.g. clearly unqualified LTV/age) — but **through conversation**, not interrogation.

### When they say "I don't want a reverse mortgage"

They usually reject **what they think** it is — not what it does for them.

**Preview reframe:**

> "You're talking about the program where the bank takes your house and leaves nothing for the kids? Yeah — that's **not** what this is."

Then return to discovery — do not argue. Full tracks: [RM Borrower Objections](../reverse-mortgage-dna/rm-borrower-objections.md). Optional neutral labels (optional payment mortgage, etc.) — only with compliance awareness.

**Ethical line:** If you know the product fits their situation and they misunderstand the name, letting them hang up without reframing the misconception is negligence — not courtesy. Same doctor frame as Belief #4.

## Call flow (high level)

```text
OPEN       → Consultant frame + form intent (Trust start)
DISCOVER   → Curiosity layers — Pain signals (→ Conceptual Beliefs)
EDUCATE    → Only after gap is felt — Debt Frame → Retirement Tool
GATE       → Beliefs checked? Permission to show solution
PITCH      → Weave their outcome — not generic features
NEXT STEP  → BAMFAM / joint call / nurture — never dead end
```

## Anti-patterns

| Order-taker / product rep | Advisor / consultant |
|---------------------------|----------------------|
| Opens with "reverse mortgage" | Opens with outcome + service |
| Stops at balance / DU fields | Layers questions for emotional gap |
| Emails proposal, hopes they read it | Walks emotional decision on the call |
| "Not interested" → discard | Nurture + reframe misconception |
| Blames lead when guarded | Reads guard-up as protection |
| Every solution is reverse | Retirement consultant — right tool for situation |
| Marketing says relief, call sells product | Congruent with ad promise and notes |

## Decision rules

| Condition | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Paid ad lead, first consultation | Part 2 open — never product-first |
| Borrower guarded / minimal answers | Belief #1 — patience; build Trust before pitch |
| Form says "pay off mortgage" | Congruency — lead with payment relief outcome |
| They reject "reverse mortgage" label | Preview reframe → return to Pain discovery |
| Perfect terms impossible | Belief #2 — sell outcome at achievable terms |
| Pain not articulated | Stay in discovery — [Conceptual Beliefs §1](playbook-rm-conceptual-beliefs.md#1-pain--problem-belief) |
| Influencer not on call | Support gap — joint call before full pitch |
| Clear non-fit | Honest exit — still professional, offer resource |
| Call ending without next step | [BAMFAM](playbook-bamfam-rm.md) — book or nurture hold |
