# BAMFAM Playbook — RM

> **BAMFAM** = *Book a meeting from a meeting* (or from any live call). Never hang up without the next step on the calendar.  
> **Parent:** [Nurture Framework §3](../client-marketing/playbook-nurture-framework.md#3-cadence-science) · **Dial context:** [LO Lead Dialing SOP — RM](../client-marketing/sop-lo-lead-dialing-rm.md)

## Purpose

Give LOs and assistants the **rules and word tracks** to secure the next appointment while the borrower is on the phone — eliminating "phone tag" that kills RM show rates.

## Scope

- Client-side B2C calls (borrower ↔ LO team).
- Booking **consultation / education call** with the LO — not full loan origination on the assistant line.
- Complements [Framework §2](../client-marketing/playbook-nurture-framework.md#2-limiting-beliefs-to-break) ghost-prevention (save contact + BAMFAM).

## Owner

Loan officer. Assistant books; LO owns calendar accuracy.

## Trigger

Every live borrower conversation that does **not** end in:

- Live transfer to LO **right now**, or
- Funded / hard disqualify with no future path

---

## Rules (non-negotiable)

| Rule | Why |
|------|-----|
| **No "I'll call you back" without a hold** | Motivation dies in hours; phone tag = ghost |
| **Schedule inside 72 hours** | Sooner shows higher; prefer same day or next day |
| **Confirm while on the phone** | Read back date, time, timezone, and callback number |
| **Send confirmation immediately** | Text/email with LO name, time, and what to expect |
| **Pull forward when possible** | "Since I have you — could we do 10 minutes now with [LO]?" |
| **Between booked slot and LO call** | No multi-day gaps without confirmation touch — motivation fades |

If they resist 72-hour window, treat it as an **obstacle to solve on the call** — not permission to defer weeks.

---

## Five outcomes (every scheduling call)

| # | Outcome | Action |
|---|---------|--------|
| 1 | No answer | Dial + VM per [LO Lead Dialing SOP](../client-marketing/sop-lo-lead-dialing-rm.md) — not a BAMFAM moment |
| 2 | Unqualified | Politely exit; offer helpful resource if appropriate; update CRM |
| 3 | **Qualified + free now** | Live transfer to LO or LO joins line — **best show rate** |
| 4 | **Qualified + pulled forward** | Move existing appointment earlier while on phone |
| 5 | **Qualified + confirmed** | Book soonest realistic slot; confirm contact info |

## Word tracks

### They don't have time right now

> No stress at all — let's get something on the calendar so we're not playing phone tag. [LO FIRST NAME] has a quick opening **[tomorrow morning / tomorrow afternoon]** — which works better for you?

📋 Offer **two concrete slots** — not "when are you free sometime next week?"

### Pull appointment forward (they already booked)

> Hey [NAME], this is [ASSISTANT] from [LO NAME]'s office — I'm calling about your appointment on [DAY]. I actually had a spot open **[earlier today / tomorrow morning]** if you'd rather knock this out sooner. Would that be easier?

### Anchor to something they already said (personalization)

> You mentioned you were trying to figure this out **[before the holidays / before tax season / this month]** — if we put 15 minutes on the calendar for **[day they named]**, [LO FIRST NAME] can walk you through what that would actually look like for your situation. Fair?

### Confirm + save contact

> Perfect — you're set for **[DAY, TIME, TIMEZONE]** with [LO FULL NAME]. I'm going to text you a confirmation right now. And while I have you — can you save this number in your phone as **[LO NAME] — Reverse Mortgage]** so you know it's us when we call?

### They want to "think about it"

> Totally fair — that's exactly why [LO FIRST NAME] keeps these first calls short. No pressure, just answers. Let's hold **[DAY/TIME]** — if you decide it's not for you, you can cancel. Would morning or afternoon work better?

📋 Objection depth: [RM Borrower Objections](../reverse-mortgage-dna/rm-borrower-objections.md). Compliance: [RM Compliance Guardrails](../reverse-mortgage-dna/rm-compliance-guardrails.md).

## Anti-patterns

| Don't | Do instead |
|-------|------------|
| "Call me next week" (no hold) | BAMFAM — book next week **now** |
| Send calendar link and hang up | Walk them to the slot or confirm you sent it **while on phone** |
| Book 2+ weeks out by default | Pull to ≤72 hours; extend only if they insist and note why |
| Book then go silent until appointment | Confirmation text + day-before reminder; respond fast if they reply |

---

## Speed of response (after they book)

Between booking and the LO call, borrowers decide whether you'll show up for them.

| Window | Action |
|--------|--------|
| Immediately after book | Confirmation text with LO name, time, and what to expect |
| If they text a question | Reply within the hour — treat as hot |
| 24 hr before | Reminder with callback number and LO name |
| Day of | Short confirm text or call if high no-show risk |

Configure automated reminders in your CRM calendar workflow.

---

## Quality bar

- Assistant identifies in first 10 seconds — borrower should not dump full financial story before LO is on the line.
- One clear ask per message — book the call, don't underwrite on the phone.
