# Call Center — Representation

## Purpose

Fix who the rep is on the call, so every offer uses the same identity.

## Scope

Every fulfillment call-center conversation. Product scripts may change the product words. They do not change this identity.

## Who the rep is

The rep is the client's assistant, calling on the client's behalf.

The rep says they are calling from **the client's office**, using the client's name. Example shape: "calling from [Client Name]'s office."

The rep is not an employee of the client's company and does not speak as that company.

## Who the client is on the call

The client is the expert. The client answers the questions. The rep's job is to make that obvious and to get the lead into a conversation with them.

## Where the lead came from

The lead opted in on Facebook to an ad asking for more information about the product being marketed for that client.

## Facts the rep may state

Only facts that are on the **client file**. The rep does not guess.

Allowed from the client file:

- The states the client is licensed in
- The company the client works for
- Whether this client is approved for live transfer
- The client's working hours

Also allowed, as positioning:

- The client works with many products and will look for the one that fits the lead's situation
- The client has helped other people in similar situations and is strong at this work

Stating the company the client works for is a fact about the client. It is not the rep representing that company.

## Where identity gets installed

In stage 3 of the [call framework](call-framework.md#3-reason-for-call), before any discovery question. The lead has to know they are talking to the assistant before they start telling their story.

## What this page does not decide

Word-for-word openings, the deflection sentence, and objection tracks. Those belong to the script pass. The limits around those lines are in [Compliance guardrails](compliance-guardrails.md).
