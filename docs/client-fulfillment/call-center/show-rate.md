# Call Center — Show Rate on the Call

## Purpose

Record what reps do on the live call so a booked lead shows up. These are rules for the conversation. They are not reminder texts. Reminders are the layer after the call.

## Scope

The booking path in [Call flow](call-flow.md). A connected live transfer does not need these, because the conversation already happened.

These are the reasons behind the stages. The stages themselves are in the [call framework](call-framework.md).

## Interest first

Some leads do not remember opting in. Some are aged and have lost the motivation. Some are already talking to other loan officers.

The rep rebuilds interest before treating the calendar as the whole call. At a high level, learn what the lead wants from the transaction, and the pain or desire behind it. Then connect that to the client: the client works with people in that situation and can walk them through options.

## The client call, framed

The call with the client is a strategy conversation. The lead does not have to decide anything on it. They get the information they need to decide for themselves.

Give it a size, too. Around fifteen minutes. A lead who expects a sales close finds a reason to be busy.

## Concerns

If the lead raises a worry, the rep addresses it. Leaving it on the table is how the show gets lost.

## Proof the rep is real

Confirm the information already on file for that lead. A stranger would not have it. The lead gave it when they opted in.

## Time on the phone

Longer calls tend to show better, because the rapport is real. Length is not a target by itself. A short call that booked a firm, soon time is still a good call. A long call that never got a commitment is not.

## Timing and commitment

Sooner appointments show better. The commitment rules are in [Booking rules](booking-rules.md).

## Close the call properly

The wrap is show-rate insurance: confirm the number, ask them to save it, tell them reminders are coming, offer the reschedule, restate the time with the zone. Steps are in [call framework stage 7](call-framework.md#7-tie-down-and-wrap).
