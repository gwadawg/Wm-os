# Call Center — Dial Cadence

## Purpose

One place for how a new lead gets dialed. Contact-rate and booking-rate bands stay in [Fulfillment Constraint Diagnosis and KPI Standards](../client-success/fulfillment-constraint-diagnosis-kpi-standards.md). This page is the operating rule those docs check against.

## Scope

Outbound dials on client leads. What happens once someone answers is [Call flow](call-flow.md).

## Standard

- First attempt within **5 minutes** of form submission. After 10 minutes, pickup falls off hard.
- At least **one attempt per day for 5 days** before the lead leaves the active dial pool for cold nurture.
- Dial in the prospect's local **8–10am** and **4–6pm** windows.
- Text and email from the AI follow-up run alongside the voice dials.

## Number health

A spam-flagged outbound number is the usual reason contact rate collapses. Check the dialer number. If it is flagged, rotate to a new number in the prospect's local area code. Rotate proactively when a number has carried heavy volume for 6 months or more.

Reps also ask the lead to save the number during the wrap ([call framework stage 7](call-framework.md#7-tie-down-and-wrap)). That is the conversational half. It is not a substitute for rotating a flagged number.
