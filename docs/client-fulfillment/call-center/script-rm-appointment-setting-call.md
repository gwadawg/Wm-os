# Call Center Appointment-Setting Call Script (Reverse Mortgage)

## Purpose

Word-for-word language for fulfillment call-center agents booking reverse-mortgage borrower appointments on behalf of a client loan officer (e.g., Jesse Beard, Community First). Covers fresh outbound dials, callback follow-ups, and AI-booked appointment confirmation calls.

## Scope

Client-side B2C calls only. Excludes acquisition setter scripts in `docs/acquisition/sales/`. See [Script Boundary Rules](script-boundary-rules.md).

## Owner

See [domain owners](../../_inventory/domain-owners.md): **client-success** (until a dedicated call-center lead is assigned).

## When To Use

- New fresh lead from a client LO's pipeline (speed-to-lead, day 1–2 priority)
- Re-dial on an unreached lead
- Morning-of confirmation call for an AI-booked appointment
- Callback to a borrower who said "now isn't a good time"

If the conversation is about selling Waiz to an LO prospect, stop and route to [Acquisition Sales](../../acquisition/sales/script-intro-call-basic.md).

## The One Job of This Call

Get the borrower **live-transferred to the LO** or **booked on the LO's calendar** without making them tell their whole story to the wrong person. You are the front desk — warm, human, and ready to hand off.

---

## Core Operating Frames (read before every shift)

1. **You are the assistant — say it in the first 10 seconds.** If you don't, the borrower will dump their full situation to you and resent repeating it to the LO. The LO will feel like they're paying you to waste their time.
2. **Be human first, business second.** "How are you?" is not a script line — it's where 80% of call-center reps sound like robots. Smile, joke, relate, then pivot with "anyways…"
3. **Match energy.** Soft 78-year-old gets soft. Loud, no-time guy gets quick and direct.
4. **Never end an objection with "okay" and hang up.** Always pull one more thread (right person's number, callback time, etc.).
5. **Try the live transfer first. Always.** Booking is the fallback, not the goal. See [Live Transfer Warm Hand-off Script](script-live-transfer-warm-handoff.md).

## STAGE 1 — CONNECTION (first 15 seconds)

### Greet

> Hey, [BORROWER NAME]?
>
> Hey [NAME], this is [AGENT NAME] calling from [LO NAME]'s office — how are you doing?

📋 Notes:
- Say "from [LO NAME]'s office" — NOT "from Community First National Bank" or any bank name as the headline. You are calling **on behalf of** the LO, not as the institution.
- Wait for them to answer. Don't talk over the answer.

### Break the Robot Response

Pick one. Use a different one each shift so it stays natural:

> Doing great — better than I deserve, I'll tell you that. Thanks for asking, though. But anyways…

> Not bad — it's [DAY], how can I complain? But anyways…

> Doing really good. Beautiful day outside, nothing to complain about. Anyways…

> Doing pretty good — getting one day closer to the weekend, right? Anyways…

📋 Rules:
- Always end with **"anyways…"** — that's the pivot back to business. Without it the small talk drags.
- Don't overshare. ~5 seconds max.
- Don't reference a specific city or weather you can't verify ("snowing in Kansas" → trap). Stay generic.
- If you stutter or fumble — laugh about it. "Sorry, didn't know why I said that." Sound human. That's the point.

### Bonus Relate-Lever (if it fits)

If the borrower says anything you can mirror — relate it. From the founder: **"I lie all the time. Whatever I have to say to relate."**

Examples:
- They say their kid spends all their money → "Ha, my brother is the same way with my dad. Anyways…"
- They mention a state or weather → "Oh, I've heard great things about Michigan, never been. Anyways…"
- They sound tired → "Long day already, huh? Anyways…"

## STAGE 2 — FRAME (the non-negotiable)

This is the section the May 21 coaching call was built around. **Do not skip it.** Setting this frame is what prevents the borrower from dumping their full story to you and then repeating it to the LO.

### Set the "Assistant" Frame

> So [NAME] — just so you know up front, I'm not the loan officer. My name is [AGENT NAME], I'm the assistant for [LO NAME] over at [COMPANY]. They saw your inquiry come in for tapping into your home equity, and they asked me to give you a quick call.

> The reason I'm calling is just to get a little bit of clarity on your situation, so I can pass clean notes over and introduce you to [LO NAME] directly. Is this a good time for you to chat for a couple minutes?

📋 Why this works:
- Establishes you are **NOT** the decision-maker / loan officer.
- Tells them what's coming next: a transfer / introduction.
- Gives them permission to keep it short. They won't over-explain.

### If they ask "Who is this with?" / "What company?"

> So I'm calling on behalf of [LO NAME] — they're a reverse mortgage specialist at [COMPANY]. They got your information when you inquired about [TAPPING INTO HOME EQUITY / SOURCE]. I'm part of their intake team.

## STAGE 3 — QUICK QUALIFICATION (notes for the LO — NOT a full loan interview)

You are gathering **enough to brief the LO**. Not enough to underwrite the loan. Keep it tight.

### Open

> Beautiful. So [LO NAME] asked me to grab a few things real quick before I introduce you — what are you looking to accomplish here? How much were you hoping to access?

Listen. Don't interrupt. Capture in their words.

### Follow-Up Questions (ask only what's needed)

> And what's that going to be for — anything specific?

> Got it. And just so I can put it in the notes — about how much is the home worth, ballpark?

> And do you still have a balance on the mortgage, or is it paid off?

### Hard Disqualifiers (Community First / strict-program LO clients)

If you hear any of these — **do not promise anything**. Acknowledge, write it down, and escalate to your manager before transferring or booking. Per Gabe: send every one of these to the manager so reporting stays clean.

| Condition | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Manufactured home on leased land | Stop the qualification. Politely close: "Got it — let me check with the team and we'll follow up." Send to manager. |
| Home value clearly below LO's program minimum (e.g., $50K–$80K when program needs $150K+) | Don't transfer. Note it. Escalate to manager — they decide whether to call back. |
| Borrower not on title / not the homeowner | Note. Get the right person's contact. Escalate. |
| Borrower says they already have a loan officer / it's being handled | Pivot to Wrong-Person Objection (below) or get the right contact info. |

📋 **Do NOT silently disqualify a lead in the dialer.** Send every borderline lead through to the manager.

## STAGE 4 — TRANSITION TO TRANSFER OR BOOK

### Soft Pre-Frame Before the Transfer Attempt

> Awesome — that's actually exactly the kind of situation [LO NAME] specializes in. What I'd love to do, if you've got a minute, is just hop you on a quick intro with them right now so you don't have to repeat all this back to me later. They're [in the office / nearby / right at their desk] — let me see if I can grab them.

### Try the Live Transfer First (always)

→ Go to [Live Transfer Warm Hand-off Script](script-live-transfer-warm-handoff.md) for the exact hand-off language and what to say to the LO.

### If Live Transfer Is Not Possible

(LO unavailable, borrower not at a computer, borrower wants a scheduled time)

> No worries — let me get you on [LO NAME]'s calendar. What works best — sometime later today, or first thing tomorrow?

### Pull the Appointment Closer (always try)

- **Today first.** "Anything later today work? They've got a [TIME] open."
- **Then tomorrow morning.** "What about tomorrow morning? They're usually free first thing."
- **Then tomorrow afternoon.**
- **Then 2 days out.**
- **Last resort: 3+ days.** "They're a little packed — let me see if I can fit you in. Is there any reason [DATE/TIME] wouldn't work for you?"

📋 Per founder: every day further out drops show rate. Pull the appointment up wherever you can.

### Book the Calendar Invite NOW

> Cool — putting you in for [DATE/TIME]. You'll get a text and an email with the calendar invite in just a second. Can you do me a quick favor and confirm it on your end so nobody gets double-booked?

📋 Hard rule:
- **Book through GoHighLevel (or the LO's CRM) while they're on the line.** Do not promise to book later.
- **Confirm the borrower received the text + email before you hang up.**
- Save their number context: "Also — if you don't recognize the number that calls you, that'll be [LO NAME]. Save this number so you don't miss them."

## STAGE 5 — WRAP-UP & EXPECTATIONS

> Perfect — you're all set for [DATE/TIME]. I'll pass [LO NAME] the notes from our conversation so they're ready when you guys connect.

> If anything changes on your end, just shoot us a text at this number. Sound good?

> Have a great rest of your day, [NAME].

## CALL VARIANT A — AI-Booked Appointment Confirmation

Use when the lead was booked by AI (no human contact yet) and you're calling to confirm the morning of.

> Hey [NAME], this is [AGENT NAME] calling from [LO NAME]'s office — how are you doing? [break the robot — see Stage 1]
>
> So I saw you've got an appointment scheduled with [LO NAME] for [TIME] today — just wanted to give you a quick call to confirm you're still good for that, and grab a few notes so [LO NAME] can hit the ground running when you guys talk.

Then run Stage 3 (Quick Qualification — light version). Then:

> Awesome. Real quick — any chance you've got time before [SCHEDULED TIME]? [LO NAME] actually has a window open at [EARLIER TIME] — would save you the wait.

📋 Per founder: if you successfully **pull the AI-booked appointment forward** by phone, that counts as a credited booking. Worth doing.

---

## CALL VARIANT B — Callback After "Not a Good Time"

> Hey [NAME], this is [AGENT NAME] calling back from [LO NAME]'s office — last time we connected you mentioned now wasn't great and to try [TIME]. How are you doing? [break the robot]
>
> Good news, [LO NAME] is actually around right now and has a quick window. Got a couple minutes for me to introduce you?

## OBJECTION HANDLING — Common Lines (front-desk version)

These are call-center quick responses. For deeper objection handling on the loan itself, that is the LO's job — not yours. Your job is to keep the conversation alive long enough to hand off.

### "I'm not the one handling this — my wife / kid / friend is."

> Oh, got it — totally understand. Beautiful. So they're the one coordinating with you on this. What's the best way to reach them? I'd love to get them on a quick call with [LO NAME] directly.

📋 Per Laura: this is like saying "talk to my secretary." Don't accept it as a no — ask for the right person's number. The borrower didn't say they aren't interested; they said the wrong person picked up.

### "I'm not really interested right now."

> Totally fair — can I ask what changed? Last time we heard from you, you were looking into [USE OF FUNDS]. Did that situation resolve, or is it just bad timing?

📋 If they say bad timing → schedule a callback. If situation resolved → polite close, mark in CRM, do not book.

### "I already have a loan officer working on this."

> Got it — no problem. Just so I update our notes, are you actively closing with them right now, or is it still in the early conversation stage?

📋 If actively closing → polite close. If just shopping → "Got it — would it hurt anything for [LO NAME] to give you a quick second opinion? Won't take more than 10 minutes." Then book.

### "Where did you get my number?"

> So you came in through one of our inquiry forms / [LEAD SOURCE]. I've got your info as [NAME] looking at [USE OF FUNDS] — does that ring a bell?

📋 Never get defensive. Stay calm and factual.

### "Is this a scam?"

> Totally hear you — there's a lot of bad calls out there. I'm [AGENT NAME] from [LO NAME]'s office at [COMPANY], and I can give you their direct line and website so you can call them back if it makes you more comfortable. Want me to text that to you?

### "Just send me information by email."

> Of course — what I'll do is have [LO NAME] send you something directly so it's coming from the right place. Best email for that is the one you submitted, [EMAIL] — that good?

📋 Don't accept "send info" as a substitute for an appointment if you can avoid it. Pivot back: "And just so they can send you the right info — what was the use of funds you were looking at?"

### Kid / prank caller / unsure if it's a kid

> All right, no worries — sorry to bother you. Have a great day.

📋 Don't push. Hang up politely. Mark in CRM.

## QUALITY BAR

- **First 10 seconds**: agent self-IDs as assistant, names the LO, sounds human, ends greeting with "anyways…"
- **Frame is set before any qualification questions.** Borrower understands they will be handed off.
- **No mini-loan-interview.** Maximum 3–5 qualification questions before transfer attempt.
- **Live transfer attempted first** on every qualified lead.
- **Booking confirmed via text/email** before hang-up.
- **Every borderline lead is sent to the manager**, not silently dispositioned.
- **Notes captured in CRM** within 60 seconds of hang-up: name, use of funds, balance, home value, vibe.
