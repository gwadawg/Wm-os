SCENARIO 1: Creating a Brand New Image Ad from Scratch

Use this when you're starting fresh and don't have an existing winner to work from.

Step 1: Generate Your Image Prompt Using the Custom GPT

Go to the Ideogram Ad Assistant (Custom GPT link above)

The GPT will ask you two questions:

Question 1: Choose your marketing angle (1-5)

1 = The "Burden" Angle (independence, not being a burden to family)

2 = The "Trapped Asset" Angle (house-rich, cash-poor)

3 = The "Surviving vs. Living" Angle (enjoying retirement, not just surviving)

4 = The "Safety Net" Angle (protection from unexpected expenses)

5 = The "Stay in Your Home" Angle (aging in place)

Question 2: Choose your emotional focus

A = Problem focus (thoughtful, concerned expression)

B = Solution focus (relieved, happy expression)

The GPT will generate a complete, detailed prompt in a code block

Copy the entire prompt

Step 2: Generate the Image Using Ideogram

Go to Ideogram

Paste your prompt into the image generation field

Select your aspect ratio:

4:5 (Portrait) - Best for Facebook feed, Instagram Stories (RECOMMENDED)

1:1 (Square) - Best for Instagram feed posts

16:9 (Landscape) - Best for Facebook desktop feed

Click Generate

Wait 30-60 seconds for Ideogram to generate 4 variations

Step 3: Review and Download

Review the four generated images

Check for:

Text accuracy (spelling, grammar)

Visual composition (clean, centered, professional)

Emotional tone (matches your angle choice)

Select the best output and download it

Step 4: Finalize in Canva

Upload the image to Canva

Make any final adjustments:

Add client logo

Adjust text if needed

Ensure pixel-perfect alignment

Export the final ad

Step 5: Upload to Ads Manager

Go to your client's Facebook Ads Manager

Create a new ad or duplicate an existing one

Upload the image as your creative

Add your ad copy and CTA

Launch the ad

SCENARIO 2: Creating a Variation of a Winning Image Ad

Use this when you have an image ad that's performing well and you want to create variations to test against it.

Step 1: Screenshot the Winning Ad

Go to your client's Facebook Ads Manager

Find the winning image ad

Take a screenshot of the image (or download it directly if you have access)

Save the screenshot to your desktop

Step 2: Upload to Ideogram and Generate Variation

Go to Ideogram

Click Upload Image (or Image-to-Image option)

Upload your screenshot of the winning ad

In the prompt field, describe what you want to change:

"Same image but change the background color to light blue"

"Same concept but make the person's expression more confident"

"Same layout but change the headline to [NEW HEADLINE]"

"Same style but add a different person with glasses"

Select your aspect ratio (match the original ad's aspect ratio)

Click Generate

Wait 30-60 seconds

Step 3: Review and Download

Review the variation to make sure it's different enough to test but similar enough to the winner

If you need to make adjustments, tweak your prompt and regenerate

Once you're happy with it, click Download

Step 4: Finalize in Canva (Optional)

In the event you want to add additional text or change something, move to Canva to:

Add or adjust text

Add brand elements

Fine-tune colors or positioning

Export the final version

Step 5: Upload to Ads Manager

Go to your client's Facebook Ads Manager

Duplicate the winning ad

Replace the image with your new variation

Keep the same ad copy and CTA (so you're only testing the image)

Launch the ad

SCENARIO 3: Creating a Custom Ad for a Specific Client

Use this when creating a personalized ad featuring a specific client's headshot.

Steps 1-3: Follow Scenario 1

Complete Steps 1 through 3 from Scenario 1 above. This will give you a fully generated ad with a stock image.

Step 4: Create Enhanced Client Images in Ideogram

Obtain a high-quality headshot of the client

In Ideogram, upload the client's headshot

Use the following prompt to create professional variations of the client in different poses that match our ad style:

A cutout photograph of the referenced person, shown waist-up. The subject should have a [Insert Desired Expression, e.g., warm and friendly smile, or a thoughtful and professional expression]. The image should be captured with professional studio lighting and have clean cutout edges with a subtle soft shadow behind them. The background should be a solid, neutral color to make it easy to remove.



Generate several variations and select the one that best fits the ad's emotional tone

Download the selected client image

Step 5: Composite the Final Ad in Canva

Upload both images to Canva:

The base ad (from Step 3)

The enhanced client image (from Step 4)

Use Canva's "Background Remover" tool on the client image to ensure it has a transparent background

Delete or hide the original stock person image from the ad

Place the client image cutout in the same position, anchoring it to the bottom edge

Adjust the size and position to match the original composition

Add any final touches (like a client logo)

Export the final ad

Step 6: Upload to Ads Manager

Go to your client's Facebook Ads Manager

Create a new ad or duplicate an existing one

Upload the customized image as your creative

Add your ad copy and CTA

Launch the ad

Quality Control Checklist

Before finalizing any ad, run through this checklist:

Text Accuracy: Is all text spelled correctly and grammatically sound?

Brand Consistency: Does the ad adhere to our brand's color palette, typography, and overall tone?

Composition: Is the layout clean, centered, and visually balanced?

Compliance: Does the ad meet all compliance guardrails (no prohibited language, no financial advice, no age in copy)?

Image Quality: Is the person's image professional, well-lit, and properly positioned?

Pro Tips for Better Image Ads

Use the Custom GPT consistently. It's pre-loaded with our brand voice, compliance rules, and proven messaging frameworks. Don't skip it.

Test multiple angles. Don't just stick with one marketing angle. Create 3-5 variations using different angles (Burden, Trapped Asset, Safety Net) and test them all.

Match the emotional focus to the stage. Use "Problem" focus (A) for cold audiences who need to feel understood. Use "Solution" focus (B) for warm audiences ready to see the outcome.

Always check text accuracy. Ideogram is excellent but can occasionally misspell words. Always review before downloading.

Use the right aspect ratio. 4:5 (Portrait) is the gold standard for Facebook and Instagram feeds. Don't default to square unless you have a specific reason.

Keep client headshots high-quality. The better the original headshot, the better the AI-generated variations will be. Aim for well-lit, professional photos.

Finding Competitor Ads for Inspiration

How to Find Competitor Winners in the Ads Library (Step-by-Step)

STEP 1 — Open Meta Ads Library

Go to: facebook.com/ads/library

STEP 2 — Search the Niche Keywords

Example queries:

"reverse mortgage"

"home equity"

"retirement planning"

"senior homeowners"

STEP 3 — Sort by "Active Ads"

If the ad has:

More than 1 variation → the advertiser is investing money

Running 2+ weeks → it's likely a winner

Running across multiple placements → evergreen creative

This means the pattern + positioning is performing.

STEP 4 — Screenshot Every Promising Creative

You don't care about the brand. You care about:

Angles

Structures

Color layouts

Poses

Lighting

Emotional states

Offer formats

STEP 5 — Analyze and Replicate

Ask yourself:

What pattern is this? (testimonial, problem-first, aspirational?)

What positioning is being used? (fear-based, solution-focused, educational?)

What structure is it using? (centered text, person bottom-third, CTA button?)

Use these insights to inform your choices when using the Custom GPT, or upload the winning ad to Ideogram to create variations (Scenario 2).
