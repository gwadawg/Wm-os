## Operating Content

New Client Campaign Launch

This document outlines the standardized process for launching a new client account on Meta. Following this structure ensures consistency, simplifies troubleshooting, and sets the foundation for scalable success.

🧠 Why This Matters

Launching a new account is simple, but not easy. A sloppy setup creates a cascade of problems: unreliable data, wasted ad spend, and an inability to diagnose what’s working. This SOP provides a clean, repeatable framework—The "1-1-3" Method—to get it right from day one.

The goal is not just to launch ads, but to build a predictable system for generating qualified leads.

⚙️ The "1-1-3" Launch Framework

The "1-1-3" framework is our standard for all new client launches. It is designed for maximum clarity and learning, focusing our initial efforts on creative testing within a controlled environment.

1 Campaign: Special Ad Category (Housing)

1 Ad Set: State-level targeting only.

3 Ads: Each with a different angle.

📝 Step-by-Step Setup Guide

1️⃣ Step 1: Campaign Setup

The campaign level sets the overall objective and budget strategy.

Objective: Select Leads. We are focused on generating direct, measurable interest.

Special Ad Category: You must declare the Housing category. This is non-negotiable for our industry and ensures compliance, even though it limits some targeting options.

Budget: Use Ad Set Budget Optimization (ABO). For a new client launch, we will set the budget at the ad set level to maintain control and ensure each ad set spends its allocated amount. A typical starting budget is $35/day.

2️⃣ Step 2: Ad Set Setup

The ad set level defines who we are targeting and where we are sending them.

Conversion Location: Select Website. We want to drive traffic to our funnels to capture leads.

Pixel: Choose the correct Meta Pixel. This will typically be named something like "reverse data". This is critical for tracking conversions and optimizing for more of them.

Location: Target the entire State the client operates in (e.g., California). Do not layer in any other location targeting at this stage.

Audience: Leave the audience broad. Do not add any detailed targeting (interests, behaviors). The creative will do the targeting for us. The AI is smart enough to find the right people based on the ad copy and creative.

3️⃣ Step 3: Ad Setup

The ad level is where we test our different angles and messaging. If you’re making custom ads for the clients, you can reference to guide here. How to Use AI to Create Reverse Mortgage Ad Images

Facebook Page: Select the client’s official Facebook Page.

Website URL: Paste the link to the client’s funnel. This link should be available in the client’s setup documentation.

Creative: Upload the three different ad creatives. Each ad should represent a distinct angle.

For ad creatives, reference our library:Library

Primary Text: The main ad copy. This should be pulled from our copy library, matching the angle of the creative.

For ad copy, reference our library: Ad Copy & Angle Library (RM)

Headline: The headline for the ad. This should also be pulled from our library.

✅ Pre-Launch Checklist

Before publishing the campaign, you must complete these two final steps:

Install Meta Pixel: Ensure the Meta Pixel is correctly installed on the client’s funnel. Without this, we cannot track leads.

Run a Test Lead: Go through the entire funnel as a user would and submit a test lead. Verify that the lead is captured correctly in our system (e.g., Go High Level) and that the conversion event fires in Meta Events Manager.
