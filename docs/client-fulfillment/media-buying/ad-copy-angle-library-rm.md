## Operating Content

SOP: Reverse Mortgage Ad Copy & Angle Library

A Production-Ready Guide for Media Buyers & Creative Teams

Author: Manus AI Date: February 20, 2026

Introduction

This document contains the complete, ready-to-use ad copy for all 12 prioritized advertising angles. Each angle is designed to give Meta's algorithm a distinct psychological signal to find different segments of your target audience.

How to Use This Document:

Select an Angle: Choose an angle from the library based on your current testing phase (start with Tier 1).

Copy and Paste: Use the provided Primary Text, Headline, and Body Copy directly in your Meta Ads setup.

Match Creative: Ensure the creative (video or static) you use aligns with the chosen angle's core emotion and message.

Test and Rotate: Follow the testing cadence outlined in the Angle Library document, rotating new angles in and pausing underperformers.

Compliance Notes

Because reverse mortgages fall under Meta's Special Ad Category (Housing & Financial Products), all copy must adhere to the following rules at all times.

Do not include any language that implies guaranteed approval, specific loan amounts, or specific interest rates. Phrases like "you will qualify" or "get $200,000" are not permitted.

Do not use age-specific language in a way that excludes protected classes. The "62+" qualifier is acceptable as a program eligibility requirement, not as an exclusionary targeting mechanism.

Always include a compliant disclosure in the ad's primary text where required by your client's licensing agreement. A standard example: Borrowers must qualify for the mortgage loan program. This is not a commitment to lend.

Do not use the "Breaking News" or "LIVE" format if your compliance team or client's lender has flagged it. Some lenders prohibit news-style creative formats.
