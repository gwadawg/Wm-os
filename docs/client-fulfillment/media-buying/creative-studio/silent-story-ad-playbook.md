# Silent Story Ad Playbook (Caption-Engine Video)

Canonical playbook for **silent text-overlay story ads** (Format **T2** in
[higgsfield-format-modules.md](higgsfield-format-modules.md)): caption-driven narrative over
B-roll, **mute-first**, confession + hidden secret + proof. Distilled from the reference
pattern (“$50K house / $5K down” silent storyteller), validated RM outputs, and the 2026-06
creative sprint (rate-protection pack + mortgage-elimination variants).

## Purpose

Give media buyers and AI agents a **repeatable system** to brainstorm, script, and produce
high-converting RM story ads without re-deriving structure each time.

## Scope

- **In:** TOF/MOF silent caption ads for generic RM fulfillment (composite dramatization).
- **Out:** Spoken UGC (prompt builder), static images, long-form LO talking head — see sibling docs.
- **Format module:** T2 — production prompts still live in [higgsfield-format-modules.md](higgsfield-format-modules.md).

## Trigger

New story ad concept, hook batch, caption script, B-roll plan, or editor handoff for silent format.

## Inputs

- Archetype + angle from [rm-archetypes-canonical.md](rm-archetypes-canonical.md)
- VOC/objections from [intelligence-icp-rm.md](../../reverse-mortgage-dna/intelligence-icp-rm.md)
- [frameworks-reference.md](frameworks-reference.md) (F7 hook taxonomy, DNA-of-winner)
- [compliance-gate-checklist.md](compliance-gate-checklist.md) Part E (T2)

## Outputs

- Caption frame table (hook → CTA)
- Optional **multi-hook pack** (3–4 videos, shared body)
- B-roll sheet (REAL / AI / STILL / EDITOR tags)
- Editor Notes (captions, music, disclosure, end card)
- Compliance gate block

## Quality bar

- Works **on mute** — captions carry 100% of meaning.
- **One idea per frame** — short reads (see Caption engine).
- **Midpoint doubt** mandatory — story feels earned, not salesy.
- **Lead-quality discipline** — hook frames attract the right psychographic (see Lead quality).
- Pass [compliance gate](compliance-gate-checklist.md) before publish.

## What this format is

| Property | Rule |
|----------|------|
| **Name** | Silent story ad · caption-engine · T2 punchy variant |
| **Runtime** | ~42–56s · **16–19 frames** at 2–2.5s each (preferred) or 12–14 frames at 3–4s (legacy) |
| **Audio** | Melancholic indie/piano → bittersweet; **no VO**; music in post only |
| **Talent** | Couple/homeowner as **proof** — never speaks to camera, documentary observe |
| **Text** | Burned-in captions in editor — **never** AI-rendered in Higgsfield frames |
| **Story type** | Composite dramatization — disclosure required (Part E) |

**Core insight:** The viewer reads micro-story beats like a screenplay; B-roll supplies credibility
and emotion. No talking head = no friction.

## Why it converts (reference DNA)

Reuse these layers from proven silent storytellers (F7 DNA-of-winner); change wording and RM compliance, not structure.

| Layer | Mechanic | RM adaptation |
|-------|----------|---------------|
| **Hook** | Pattern interrupt · number or age contrast · “stupid / risky” self-awareness | Paradox (house-rich/cash-poor), age + betrayed expectation, rate protection |
| **Caption engine** | Short factual beats · ellipsis open loops | 6–12 words/frame · Zeigarnik `…` |
| **B-roll** | Raw phone energy · social proof of life | REAL + STILL docs · AI couple scenes · dignity standard |
| **Audio** | Melancholic bed vs wins | Bittersweet, not triumphant |
| **Conversion** | Specificity · aspirational ladder · self-deprecation | Numbers on **struggle side only** · proof ladder · midpoint doubt |

**Validated RM siblings:** [rm-testimonial-trapped-asset.md](outputs/rm-testimonial-trapped-asset.md) ·
[rm-testimonial-inflation-hedge.md](outputs/rm-testimonial-inflation-hedge.md)

## Three story layers (always stack)

Every strong silent story combines:

1. **Emotional** — names their world (“I thought retirement would be stress-free”).
2. **Hidden secret** — overlooked option (“what smart retirees do / bank never mentioned”).
3. **Proof** — “here’s what we actually did” + before/after ladder (not bragging).

**Confession frame** lowers guard: *“We almost didn’t share this…”* · *“I wish someone told us at 65.”*

---

## Caption engine (punchy standard — preferred 2026-06+)

| Rule | Spec |
|------|------|
| **Length** | **6–12 words** per frame · one idea |
| **Read time** | **2–2.5 seconds** per frame in edit |
| **Ellipsis** | Lines that continue end with `…` · resolution lines end clean |
| **Hook** | Frame 1 (sometimes 1–2): **ALL-CAPS** · specific anchor (age, rate, $ contrast) |
| **Mechanism** | **Once**, plain English, outcome-first · TOF = no product name |
| **Numbers** | Struggle/asset side only · **no result $/month** unless `[TO FILL]` + HUMAN REVIEW |

**Bad (too long):**  
`Refinancing meant giving up that rate. A HELOC meant another monthly payment. Neither worked for us…`

**Good (split):**  
`Refinance? We'd lose our rate…` → `HELOC? Another monthly payment…`

## Narrative arc → frame map

Maps the [5-part script generator](rm-script-generator.md) onto **16–19 caption frames**.

| Act | Frames (typical) | Job | Must include |
|-----|------------------|-----|--------------|
| **I — Hook + trap** | 1–5 | Stop scroll · name stuck situation | Hook · empathy · bad options (refi/sell/payment) |
| **II — Secret + doubt** | 6–10 | Third option · skepticism | Friend/research · soft mechanism · “last resort” doubt · horror stories |
| **III — Proof** | 11–14 | What we did | Title · kids · leave first alone / payment gone / cash out |
| **IV — Results** | 15–16 | Outcomes ladder | **Debt/credit here if used — never in hook** |
| **V — Payoff + CTA** | 17–19 | Identity shift · qualify · action | “Same home, living again” · equity/credit/rate quiz CTA |

**Midpoint conflict (non-negotiable):** frames ~9–10 — *almost didn’t look* · *heard horror stories*.

## Multi-hook pack workflow

Produce **3–4 videos** from one body to isolate hook performance on Meta.

| Part | Unique per video | Shared |
|------|------------------|--------|
| **Frames 1–2** | Hook + bridge | — |
| **Frames 3–19** | — | Identical captions + B-roll |
| **Edit** | ~4–6 unique seconds | ~46–50 shared seconds |

**Process:**

1. Lock **story spine** (one revelation).
2. Write **shared body** (frames 3+).
3. Draft **4 hooks** (different F7 types: confessional, paradox, question, insider).
4. Write **one bridge line** per hook (frame 2).
5. One B-roll timeline · swap F1–F2 assets per export.

## Hook formula bank (TOF)

Mix variables — do not copy-paste one ad’s hook into another angle without changing the inciting incident.

| Formula | Example | Product |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **At [age], I thought [expectation]. [Reality].** | At 67, I thought retirement was stress-free. It wasn't. | Either |
| **[Rate asset]. We refused to touch it. Still needed cash.** | 3% mortgage. We weren't touching it. Still needed cash from the house. | **HomeSafe Second only** |
| **[Good thing] + [bad trap].** | Our first rate was too good to give up. We still needed equity cash. | **HomeSafe Second only** |
| **How do [audience] [desired outcome] without [fear]?** | How do retired homeowners get cash out without a new monthly payment? | Either |
| **Only options? A or B. [Constraint].** | Only options? Refinance or sell. Another payment? Not on the table. | **HomeSafe Second** if "refinance" is the bad option |
| **We almost didn't share this…** | I'm 67. We almost didn't post this — wish someone told us at 65. | Either |
| **Smart [audience] do X. We didn't know how.** | Smart retirees don't refinance a great rate. They do this instead. | **HomeSafe Second only** |

**Split-screen hooks:** pair **document still** (rate, receipt, statement) with **human still/video** (couple, home).

## Story spine library (angle → revelation)

Use one spine per ad. Hook batches vary; body follows spine.

| Spine | Inciting pain | Revelation | Results (late frames) | Product |
|-------|---------------|------------|------------------------|---------|
| **Mortgage elimination** | Still paying mortgage in retirement | Eliminate payment · stay in home | Bills easy · grandkids · living not surviving | **HECM** (first lien) |
| **Leave first rate** | Low first mortgage · need cash · no new payment | Access equity **without** refinancing first | Balances cleared · home upgraded · cash flow | **HomeSafe Second only** — not HECM |
| **Surviving → living** | Retirement shrunk · not leaving house | Equity unlock · dignity | Travel · grandkids · identity shift | Either (TOF-agnostic) |
| **Trapped asset** | House-rich · cash-poor paradox | Soft equity access · no sell | Relief · concrete life wins | Either |
| **Inflation / fixed income** | Flat income · rising costs | Monthly cash flow from equity | Prepared · not scared monthly | Either |
| **Growing LOC / hedge** | Plan incomplete · idle equity | Standby line · optionality | Expense hits · portfolio preserved | **HECM LOC** primary — confirm if HS2 LOC |

> **Product fit:** Full matrix in [rm-product-lines.md](../../reverse-mortgage-dna/rm-product-lines.md). Do not run **Leave first rate** or refi-vs-keep-rate comparison hooks for HECM-only clients.

## Proof ladder (aspirational escalation)

Like reference ad $650 → $7K → $50K — RM uses **feeling + concrete life wins**, not income promises.

| Rung | Example caption beat | B-roll |
|------|---------------------|--------|
| 1 | First bill cycle without juggle | Organized mail · exhale |
| 2 | Deferred repair done | Contractor · roof/kitchen |
| 3 | Family / travel | Grandkids · packing |
| 4 | Identity line | “Living it, not surviving it” |
| 5 | Same home · better plan | Porch bookend · same exterior |

## Lead quality — hook vs results

| Lead with (hook frames) | Hold for results frames (15+) |
|-------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Rate protection · cash need · no new payment | Debt paid · credit improved |
| Equity · retirement expectation · strategic trap | Specific card payoffs |
| Age + confessional · hidden secret | Dollar savings claims |

**Debt-first hooks** pull debt-rescue clickers. **CTA qualify** (`equity` + `good credit` + quiz) filters further.

## B-roll sourcing matrix

Extend T2 with explicit **REAL / AI / STILL / EDITOR** tags (borrowed from E1).

| Tag | Use for | Notes |
|-----|---------|-------|
| **REAL** | Home exterior, porch, contractor, hands on door | Bookend same house |
| **AI (Higgsfield)** | Consistent couple · kitchen · friend+phone · skeptic · relief | Never render text/music |
| **STILL** | Mortgage statements, mailers, calendars, PAID IN FULL mocks | Legible text · Canva/Figma mocks |
| **EDITOR** | Captions, disclosure, split-screen, quiz end card, rate circles | Ken Burns on stills 2–2.5s |

**Rules:**

- Couple **never** addresses lens.
- **No** cash stacks, crying, distress-porn, distressed-senior stock.
- **Documents** = stills (AI warps text).
- **Mood arc:** cooler/tighter F1–5 → warm F6–14 → calm CTA.

See frame-level examples in rate-protection sprint (June 2026) — template below.

## Editor Notes template

Paste into every T2 output:

```text
Disclosure: Dramatization. Composite of real homeowner experiences. Individual results vary.
Placement: persistent small super OR frames 1 + final frame.

Caption style: Bold sans-serif · white + dark stroke · center or lower-third · 62+ legible.

Music: Slow melancholic indie/piano → bittersweet by proof section · low volume · no sting.

Pacing: 2–2.5s/frame · 1–2 cuts per frame · montage OK on results frames (15–16).

End card: Quiz mock on phone/tablet · 1.5s hold.
```

## Compliance overlay (T2 + RM)

- Composite dramatization — never real named client without approval.
- No product name in TOF hook/body/CTA.
- No guaranteed outcomes · no result `$`/month invented.
- Property-charge qualifier when stating no required monthly payment (taxes, insurance, occupancy).
- Age in copy: default RM guardrail is **retired homeowners** — client may approve age-in-hook tests; flag HUMAN REVIEW when used.
- Frame 12+ debt/credit outcomes = illustrative composite, not universal promise.

Full gate: [compliance-gate-checklist.md](compliance-gate-checklist.md).
