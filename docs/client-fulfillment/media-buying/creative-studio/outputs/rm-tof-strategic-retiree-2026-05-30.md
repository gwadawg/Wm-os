# Script: IDEA-003 — Strategic Retiree / Surviving vs Living / TOF

Format: Video (45-90s, long-form variant) | Talent: relatable retired homeowner to camera, warm home interior

## Script
HOOK (0-5s):  [option A below]

EMPATHY (5-20s):
"If you worked your whole life and still feel like retirement is more about surviving than
living... you're not doing anything wrong. You did everything right. You own your home. You
played by the rules. And somehow money is still tighter than you ever expected. That feeling
— that's the part nobody warns you about."

FRAME SHIFT (20-45s):
"Here's what smart retirees realize: the home you already paid for can do more than just sit
there. There's a way for retired homeowners to turn the value they've already built into
steady monthly cash flow — without selling, without moving, and without taking on a monthly
payment. It's not a last resort. For a lot of people it's just smarter planning they were
never told about."

PROOF (45-70s):
"I know — it sounds too good to be true. But this isn't free money or some gimmick. It's a
federally insured program, and you stay the owner of your home. You can't owe more than the
home is worth, and you can't be forced to leave as long as you keep up the basics like taxes
and insurance. The homeowners who look into it early tend to say the same thing: 'I wish I'd
understood this sooner.'"

CTA (70-90s):
"If you're a retired homeowner who'd rather plan ahead than react later, there's a quick quiz
below. Answer a few questions and see if this is even a good fit for you. No pressure, no pitch,
just clarity instead of confusion."

## Hook options (0-5s)
- A. (curiosity gap — recommended) "There's a move smart retirees make to stop just surviving and start actually living — and almost no one tells you about it."
- B. (paradox, from IDEA-001) "Your home might be the most valuable thing you own — and the one asset you've never been allowed to use."
- C. (rhetorical) "What if the smartest financial move left in your retirement is one you already own?"

## Frameworks Applied
- Hook: curiosity gap (F7) + Zeigarnik open loop (F2)
- Empathy: VOC (F4 §5 "I worked my whole life — this isn't how it was supposed to be") + Liking/Similarity (F2) + pain→impact (F4 §3 surviving vs retirement)
- Frame Shift: mechanism in one sentence — turn already-built home value into monthly cash flow without selling, moving, or a monthly payment (F1) + Framing/Contrast Debt→Retirement-Tool (F2) + benefit-over-feature (F3) + "not a last resort" reframe (F4 persona-4 angle)
- Proof: objection "this sounds too good to be true" + "I don't want more debt" (F4 §7) + Loss Aversion reframed + Authority/Social Proof (F2) + FHA non-recourse / retain-ownership reassurance (F4 §7)
- CTA: approved CTA bank — casual quiz / self-qualification, "see if this is a good fit, no pressure" (F5, ICP §8 step 5) + Commitment & Consistency small-step (F2)
