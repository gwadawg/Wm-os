# RM Testimonial Ad — "Inflation Hedge" (Silent Text-Overlay Story)

Built in the **silent sequential text-overlay** format (reference: "17 Year Old Tries to Buy a
$50,000 House With Only $5,000"). The story is told entirely through on-screen captions over
casual, dignified B-roll of a retired couple. **Works on mute. The text is the content; the people
are the proof, not presenters.**

- Archetype: **Financially Squeezed** · Persona: **Married Couple** · Stage: **TOF**
- Angle: **Inflation Hedge / fixed income** · Hook type: **Specific pain + paradox**
- Story type: **Composite** (illustrative; on-screen disclosure required — see Compliance Gate)
- Powered by the [RM script generator](../rm-script-generator.md) 5-beat arc, mapped onto the
  reference's 13-frame caption rhythm.

> Reference-structure reuse (per [frameworks-reference §F7](../frameworks-reference.md)): ellipsis
> open-loop engine, specific numbers, objection pre-handle, midpoint conflict, mute-first delivery,
> melancholic-then-bittersweet music. RM-compliance changes: **no product name at TOF** (the
> "mechanism reveal" is a soft outcome reframe), and **numbers stay on the struggle side** — the
> payoff is feeling prepared, never a promised "$X/month."

---

## Hook frame (0:00–0:02) — the most important 2 seconds

Split-screen still, bold ALL-CAPS overlay:

```
OUR INCOME HASN'T CHANGED SINCE 2019. EVERYTHING ELSE HAS.
```

| Element | What it signals |
|---|---|
| Left: a 2019 receipt / old calendar page pinned to the fridge | "This is a real, specific moment in time" |
| Right: today's receipt — same cart, far higher total | "The squeeze is real and measurable" |
| Specific year (2019) | Grounds it in reality, not a timeless fantasy |
| ALL CAPS, burned-in, two-beat line | "This matters — stop scrolling" (works on mute) |
| Small disclosure super, bottom: "Dramatization · composite of real homeowner experiences" | Compliance: clearly illustrative, not a fabricated testimonial |

- **Pacing:** ~1 caption every 3–4s; 1–2 cuts per frame. Slow, warm, calm — never fast-UGC energy.
- **Caption style:** bold, high-contrast, burned-in, center or lower-third; legible for an older audience; one short line at a time, mute-first.
- **Music:** slow, melancholic indie/piano at low volume; turns bittersweet (not triumphant) by the payoff.
- **First frame (0:00):** the frozen-since-2019 line — instant recognition for anyone on a fixed income; stops the scroll without sound.
- **No talking head, no sales pitch on camera.** The couple never addresses the lens.

## Frameworks Applied

- **Hook:** specific-pain + paradox (F7) + Zeigarnik open loop (F2); first words are FOR the prospect.
- **Empathy:** VOC "Everything costs more, but my income never increases" (F4 §5) + Liking/Similarity (F2) + inflation pain→impact (F4 §3).
- **Frame Shift:** soft mechanism "turn equity into monthly cash flow, no monthly payment" stated once, outcome-first (F1) + Framing/Contrast (F2); **product unnamed (TOF)**.
- **Proof:** objections "I've heard horror stories" + "I don't want more debt" (F4 §7 #4, #6) dismantled via myth-bust (old unregulated vs today's regulated) + retained-title / no-payment reassurance; Loss Aversion reframed (F2).
- **CTA:** approved info-access CTA bank (F5) + Commitment small-step (F2); low friction for TOF.
- **Format/DNA:** silent text-overlay, ellipsis engine, person-as-proof, midpoint conflict (F7 DNA-of-winner — reuse the layer, not the wording).

## Compliance Gate

```
A Hard:    PASS — no product name in TOF; "retired homeowners," no age; no guarantee
           ("monthly cash flow," no promised amount); no tax claim; no false urgency; story is a
           disclosed composite (dramatization super), dignity not pity.
B Stage:   PASS — TOF leads with the prospect's world; product unnamed; frame-shift by outcome.
           Note: frames 9–10 use myth-bust/reassurance (more MOF-flavored); acceptable inside a
           story arc because no product is named. If strict-TOF is required, soften frames 9–10.
C Quality: PASS — VOC present; mechanism named once (outcome-first); proof specific (retained title,
           no-payment condition, regulated-vs-old myth-bust, concrete after-state); awareness matched.
D Hygiene: PASS — hook passes "prospect or speaker?"; works on mute; CTA low-friction info-access.
RESULT: READY (generic composite) — see numbers/HUMAN-REVIEW note before publishing.
```

### Numbers + HUMAN REVIEW note

- On-screen numbers (frozen-since-2019 income, climbing receipts) are on the **struggle side** —
  compliant, no promised payout.
- **Do NOT add an on-screen RESULT dollar figure** ("we now get $X/month"). A specific outcome number
  must be a **real, approved client result** marked `[TO FILL]` and routed to **HUMAN REVIEW**.
- Frame 9 ("old, unregulated programs") and frame 10 ("no monthly payment required — as long as you
  live here and keep up taxes and insurance") state program facts. Confirm the client's compliance team
  approves this phrasing for the target stage; otherwise mark `[TO FILL]` and defer to the LO's
  approved language. "Monthly cash flow" must never imply a guaranteed amount.
