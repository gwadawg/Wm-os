# RM Testimonial Ad — "Trapped Asset" (Silent Text-Overlay Story)

Built in the **silent sequential text-overlay** format (reference: "17 Year Old Tries to Buy a
$50,000 House With Only $5,000"). The story is told entirely through on-screen captions over
casual, dignified B-roll of a retired couple living their life. **Works on mute. The text is the
content; the people are the proof, not presenters.**

- Archetype: **Financially Squeezed** · Persona: **Married Couple** · Stage: **TOF**
- Angle: **Trapped Asset** · Hook type: **Paradox / number-contrast**
- Story type: **Composite** (illustrative; on-screen disclosure required — see Compliance Gate)
- Powered by the [RM script generator](../rm-script-generator.md) 5-beat arc, mapped onto the
  reference's 13-frame caption rhythm.

> Reference-structure reuse (per [frameworks-reference §F7](../frameworks-reference.md)): we keep the
> ellipsis open-loop engine, specific numbers, objection pre-handle, midpoint conflict, mute-first
> delivery, and melancholic-then-bittersweet music. We change two moves for RM compliance: **no
> product name at TOF** (the "mechanism reveal" is a soft outcome reframe), and **numbers stay on the
> struggle/asset side** — the payoff is relief and dignity, never a promised "$X/month."

## Hook frame (0:00–0:02) — the most important 2 seconds

Split-screen still, bold ALL-CAPS overlay:

```
WE OWNED A $400,000 HOUSE AND ARGUED OVER AN $80 GROCERY BILL
```

| Element | What it signals |
|---|---|
| Left: ordinary, lived-in paid-off home (not glamorous) | "This is real, not a highlight reel" |
| Right: inset of a retired couple at the kitchen table with a calculator + small stack of bills | "These are real people like me" |
| Number contrast ($400,000 vs $80) | "There's a mechanism here I don't understand — how?" |
| ALL CAPS, burned-in | "This matters — stop scrolling" (works on mute) |
| Small disclosure super, bottom: "Dramatization · composite of real homeowner experiences" | Compliance: clearly illustrative, not a fabricated testimonial |

- **Pacing:** ~1 caption every 3–4s; 1–2 cuts per frame. Slow, warm, calm — never fast-UGC energy.
- **Caption style:** bold, high-contrast, burned-in, center or lower-third; legible for an older audience; one short line at a time, mute-first.
- **Music:** slow, melancholic indie/piano at low volume; turns bittersweet (not triumphant) by the payoff — mirrors the reference's audio doctrine.
- **First frame (0:00):** the $400K vs $80 contrast — instant math-curiosity, stops the scroll without sound.
- **No talking head, no sales pitch on camera.** The couple never addresses the lens.

## Frameworks Applied

- **Hook:** paradox / number-contrast (F7) + Zeigarnik open loop (F2); first words are FOR the prospect.
- **Empathy:** VOC "My house is worth a lot, but I can't touch any of it" (F4 §5) + Liking/Similarity (F2) + house-rich/cash-poor pain→impact (F4 §3).
- **Frame Shift:** soft mechanism "use the equity already built, no monthly payment" stated once, outcome-first (F1) + Framing/Contrast (F2); **product unnamed (TOF)**.
- **Proof:** objections "the bank will take my house" + "my kids will inherit debt" + "too good to be true" (F4 §7 #1, #2, #5) dismantled; FHA non-recourse / retained-title reassurance; Loss Aversion reframed (F2).
- **CTA:** approved info-access CTA bank (F5) + Commitment small-step (F2); low friction for TOF.
- **Format/DNA:** silent text-overlay, ellipsis engine, person-as-proof, midpoint conflict (F7 DNA-of-winner — reuse the layer, not the wording).

## Compliance Gate

```
A Hard:    PASS — no product name in TOF; "retired homeowners," no age; no guarantee
           (relief language, no promised $/month); no tax claim; no false urgency; story is a
           disclosed composite (dramatization super), dignity not pity.
B Stage:   PASS — TOF leads with the prospect's world; product unnamed; frame-shift by outcome.
           Note: frames 9–10 lean into reassurance/myth-bust; acceptable inside a story arc
           because no product is named. If strict-TOF is required, soften frame 10 wording.
C Quality: PASS — VOC present; mechanism named once (outcome-first); proof specific
           (retained title, non-recourse, concrete after-state); awareness matched (Problem-aware).
D Hygiene: PASS — hook passes "prospect or speaker?"; works on mute; CTA low-friction info-access.
RESULT: READY (generic composite) — see numbers/HUMAN-REVIEW note before publishing.
```

### Numbers + HUMAN REVIEW note

- All on-screen numbers ($400,000 home, $80 groceries) are on the **struggle/asset side** — compliant,
  no promised payout.
- **Do NOT add an on-screen RESULT dollar figure** (e.g., "we got $X/month"). If the client wants a
  specific outcome number, it must be a **real, approved client result** marked `[TO FILL]` and routed
  to **HUMAN REVIEW** — never invented.
- Frame 10 ("never personally responsible," "keep the title") states FHA/non-recourse facts. Confirm the
  client's compliance team is comfortable with this phrasing for the target funnel stage; if not, mark
  `[TO FILL]` and defer to the LO's approved language.
