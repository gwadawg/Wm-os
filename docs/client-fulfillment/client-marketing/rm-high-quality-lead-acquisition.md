# RM High-Quality Lead Acquisition

## Purpose

Operational playbook for attracting **higher-intent reverse mortgage leads** — homeowners with meaningful home equity and stable property-charge history — without sacrificing compliance or the education-first methodology.

## Scope

- Meta creative strategy, funnel qualification, and metrics for media buyers and AI creative generation
- Archetype selection when **lead-to-qual %** or **cost per qualified conversation (CQPCONV)** is the goal
- Complements [RM Ad Playbook](rm-ad-playbook.md), [Doctrine RM Marketing](../reverse-mortgage-dna/doctrine-rm-marketing.md), and [Constraint Troubleshooting SOP](../client-success/constraint-troubleshooting-sop.md)

## Owner

Media buying lead (Gabriela primary); Client Success and Operations use for diagnosis.

## Trigger

- New client onboarding when LO wants better closable leads, not just volume
- Lead-to-qual % below 45% or rising disqual reasons (no equity, wrong segment)
- Creative refresh after stable CPL but weak downstream conversion

## Inputs

- Client market and service area
- GHL/ClickUp disqual reason export (weekly)
- Funded-loan or booked-call cohort for lookalikes (when available)
- [RM Compliance Guardrails](../reverse-mortgage-dna/rm-compliance-guardrails.md)
- [Intelligence ICP RM](../reverse-mortgage-dna/intelligence-icp-rm.md)

## Outputs

- Archetype-weighted creative brief
- LP qual question set
- Video script starters (see team draft for copy-paste)
- Weekly disqual → creative fix loop

## Quality Bar

- Never guarantee eligibility, amounts, or rates — use *may qualify*, *could access*, *in many cases*
- Never lead cold creative with "reverse mortgage" — outcome first per [Doctrine RM Marketing](../reverse-mortgage-dna/doctrine-rm-marketing.md)
- Optimize **CQPCONV** and **lead-to-qual %** before raw CPL when downstream is healthy

## 1. Define “quality” for HECM (avoid wrong optimization)

| What teams often mean | HECM reality |
|----------------------|--------------|
| "Good credit" / 700+ FICO | No traditional FICO floor. Lenders assess **willingness and ability** to keep property charges current (taxes, insurance, HOA). |
| "Lots of equity" | Proceeds depend on **age, home value, existing liens**, and program limits — not click intent alone. |
| "Warm referral lead" | Paid leads need **education + friction + speed-to-lead** to match referral seriousness. |

**Quality lead (operational definition):** Homeowner 62+, primary residence, **meaningful equity** (paid-off or low mortgage balance relative to value), **intent aligned with ad promise**, able to discuss property charges honestly, completes qual steps on LP.

**Highest-fit archetypes for equity + stability:** [Strategic Retiree](../reverse-mortgage-dna/doctrine-rm-marketing.md) and [Legacy Planner](../reverse-mortgage-dna/doctrine-rm-marketing.md) — smaller volume, higher close rate once engaged. **Security-Seeker** drives volume; thinner equity and heavier charge-risk mix — deprioritize when quality is the goal.

## 2. North star metrics (quality over volume)

| Metric | Target (Waiz funnel w/ friction) | Meaning |
|--------|----------------------------------|---------|
| Lead-to-qual % | 55–65% | LP + angle self-selection |
| CQPCONV | Per KPI doctrine | True quality signal |
| CPL / CPQL | Secondary if CQPCONV healthy | Higher CPL can mean self-selection |

**Golden rule** (from constraint SOP): If **CQPCONV is under $149**, do not cut CPL by broadening angles — campaign may already be selecting quality.

**Theory of constraints:** If ads fill the pipe but LOs report no equity, fix **angle + LP qual**, not dialer scripts first.

## 3. Lever hierarchy (what to pull first)

### Tier A — Highest leverage (do before new hooks)

1. **Weekly disqual analytics** — top reasons from GHL/ClickUp: under 62, renter, no equity, already has RM, misunderstood ad.
2. **Funded-loan / booked-call lookalikes** — seed Meta from closes, not all form fills.
3. **Intentional qual friction** — home value band + mortgage balance + primary residence (see §5).
4. **Message match** — ad promise = LP headline (e.g. payment elimination ad → payment elimination LP).
5. **Archetype mix shift** — 30–40% net new creative toward Strategic Retiree + Legacy Planner when quality is the goal.

### Tier B — Meta creative (Andromeda era)

- **New concepts**, not hook swaps on same body (see [Meta Andromeda RM Rules](meta-andromeda-rm-rules.md)).
- **Video for quality, static for volume** — LO-led education lowers skepticism pre-call.
- **BOF retarget** — VSL 50%+ viewers → estimate / educational next step.

### Tier C — Channel mix (outside Meta; coordinate with ops)

| Channel | Quality profile |
|---------|-----------------|
| Google Search (HECM / equity / retirement mortgage intent) | Higher research intent |
| YouTube long-form | Pre-education filter |
| Financial advisor / CPA / estate attorney referrals | Planner mindset |
| Sphere / past client reactivation | Known equity |

Repo does not replace channel strategy here — flag to founder when Meta-only plateaus.

### Tier D — Geo proxy for equity

- ZIP/radius in higher median home value submarkets (within LO license).
- Separate state/county creative where home values differ — realistic **anchor ranges** in copy (may qualify language only).

## 4. Archetype angles for equity + financial standing

### Strategic Retiree (primary for quality)

**Frame:** Optimization tool — Retirement Tool frame, not Debt frame.

| Use | Avoid |
|-----|-------|
| Portfolio preservation, sequence-of-returns | "Help," "assistance," desperation |
| Growing standby line of credit | Urgency, last-resort |
| Access equity without liquidating investments in down markets | Generic "get cash now" |
| Delay Social Security / reduce portfolio withdrawals | Patronizing simplification |

**Psychology:** Authority, unity ("financially savvy retirees"), contrast (401(k) drawdown vs. home equity).

**Sample outcome hooks (compliant):**

- "Pulling from retirement accounts in a down market? There's a federally-insured way homeowners may access equity they've already built — without a required monthly mortgage payment."
- "Many retirees use a growing standby line of credit as a buffer — so they're not forced to sell investments at the wrong time."

### Legacy Planner (strong equity, family motivation)

**Frame:** Deploy equity in service of family; answer inheritance directly.

| Use | Avoid |
|-----|-------|
| Living legacy, help adult children/grandkids while alive | Vague inheritance dismissals |
| Spouse protection, non-recourse for heirs | Pure survival / charity framing |
| "Equity you've already built" | Pressure tactics |

### Paid-off / low-balance (equity signal)

- Eliminate the **last** mortgage payment
- "Decades of payments — now use what you built for retirement"
- Aging-in-place / roof / HVAC (asset upkeep = ownership pride)

### Deprioritize for quality goals

- Pure "stop the bleeding" Security-Seeker volume plays
- Generic cash-out without equity/planning context
- Fake urgency, guaranteed amounts, "you qualify"

## 5. Funnel qualification (intentional friction)

Per [Doctrine Reverse Mortgage](../reverse-mortgage-dna/doctrine-reverse-mortgage.md) Pillar 4: friction creates commitment; optimize **lead-to-qual %**, not form volume.

### Recommended LP questions (non-advisory wording)

| Question | Why |
|----------|-----|
| Homeowner vs. renter | Hard filter |
| Age 62+ / retired homeowner | Hard filter |
| Approximate home value band | Equity self-selection |
| Mortgage balance (paid off / low / moderate / high) | Paid-off ≈ more proceeds |
| Primary residence Y/N | Product fit |
| Primary goal (payment elimination / LOC buffer / legacy / repairs) | Routes LO conversation |

**Tune one field at a time** if opt-in rate collapses (target opt-in 10–15%+ with friction).

### FAQ disqualifiers (reduces junk clicks)

- "This information is for **homeowners 62+** exploring options on their **primary residence** — not renters."
- Educational tone; no shame language.

### Post-submit

- Speed-to-lead **under 5 minutes**
- Opener: home equity **outcome**, not "reverse mortgage callback"

## 7. Psychology map (ethical influence)

| Goal | Models |
|------|--------|
| Serious applicants | Commitment/consistency, IKEA effect (qual effort), foot-in-the-door |
| Planner segment | Confirmation bias (align with "smart use of equity"), status-quo bias (optimize what you built) |
| Fewer tire-kickers | Regret aversion (no-obligation education), pratfall/transparency (costs, counseling) |
| Wrong segment repelled | Paradox of choice (one outcome per ad), inversion (list who you don't want) |

## 8. Financial Assessment alignment (marketing ↔ underwriting)

Pre-align language without giving tax/legal advice:

- "Homeowners who keep property taxes and insurance current"
- "Planning for property costs, not only cash out"
- Residual income / life-expectancy set-asides are LO topics — marketing sets expectation of **honest financial conversation**

See [Intelligence RM Product](../reverse-mortgage-dna/intelligence-rm-product.md) — myths on credit and property charges.

## 9. 30-day experiment stack

1. Shift **30–40%** of new creative to Strategic Retiree + Legacy Planner.
2. Add or enforce **home value + mortgage balance** qual fields; track lead-to-qual % weekly.
3. Launch **one BOF retarget** campaign: 50%+ video viewers → estimate/education LP.
4. **Disqual review every Monday** → one creative fix + one LP fix.
5. Judge success on **CQPCONV**, not CPL alone.

## 10. Diagnosis quick reference

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---------|--------------|-----|
| High CTR, high CPL, low opt-in | Message mismatch / slow mobile | Match headline; page speed |
| Good opt-in, low lead-to-qual | Wrong archetype or missing friction | Disqual export; add equity questions |
| Good qual, low contact | Ops / spam flag / speed | Constraint SOP §3A |
| Good contact, low book | Opener says "reverse mortgage" | Outcome framing |
