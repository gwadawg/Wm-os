### Section 1: Platform Context

All ads we produce for reverse mortgage loan officer clients run on Meta — Facebook and Instagram. Understanding how Meta users behave directly determines how every ad must be structured.

The Scroll Environment

Meta users are not searching for information. They are scrolling — passively consuming content. An ad appears uninvited. The prospect has no prior relationship with the loan officer and no reason to stop. Their default behavior is to keep scrolling.

The first job of every ad is to stop the scroll. Not to sell. Not to explain. Not to build trust. To stop the scroll. Nothing else happens if that fails.

The 62+ Demographic on Meta

The reverse mortgage prospect is an active Meta user. This demographic uses Facebook more than any other social platform. They engage with content that feels personal and relevant. They are highly skeptical of anything that feels like a financial sales pitch — an ad that looks like a typical mortgage ad will be ignored. The approach must feel warmer, more educational, and more human than what they have learned to scroll past.

Two Formats — One System

Static image ads — A single image or graphic paired with headline, body copy, and CTA. Primary format for cold audiences and ongoing retargeting.

Video ad scripts — Written scripts for educational video content delivered by the loan officer or spokesperson. Highest-performing format for trust-building and frame-shifting.

⭐  Why Video Outperforms Static in This MarketEducational video content does the one thing static ads cannot do at scale: it builds a human connection before the first call.A prospect who has watched a 60-second video of a loan officer explaining something clearly and calmly arrives at the qualification call with a fundamentally different relationship than one who clicked a static image. The skepticism is lower. The trust is higher. The conversation is easier.Static ads drive volume. Video ads drive quality. Both are necessary. Neither replaces the other.

### Section 2: The Tof / Mof / Bof Framework

Every ad we produce belongs to one of three funnel stages. The stage is not just a label — it determines the rules, the language, and the job the ad is doing. Producing the wrong type of content for the wrong stage is one of the most common reasons campaigns underperform.

### Tof

Top of Funnel — Cold Audience

Audience: Prospect has never seen our content. May have no knowledge of reverse mortgages. Default view is neutral to skeptical.

Never mention reverse mortgage. Lead exclusively with the prospect's problem, frustration, or desired outcome. Pure education and value.

Goal: Stop the scroll. Shift the Debt Frame. Build the first layer of trust.

🚨  TOF Non-NegotiableThe words 'reverse mortgage' never appear anywhere in a TOF ad — not in the headline, not in the body copy, not in the CTA.TOF content leads with the prospect's world. Their problem. Their frustration. Their desired outcome. The product does not exist yet at this stage — only the person does.

### Mof

Middle of Funnel — Warm Audience

Audience: Prospect has engaged with TOF content. Aware a solution exists. Trust is building but not complete. Objections and myths are present.

Trust is built through education, social proof, and myth-busting. The product category can now be introduced — but never as an opener.

Goal: Deepen familiarity. Handle objections before they are spoken. Earn the right to convert.

💡  What Changes at MOFThe term 'reverse mortgage' may now appear — but only after the Retirement Tool Frame has been established within that piece of content. Never as the opening. Never as the hook.MOF content proactively dismantles objections and myths before the prospect raises them. It does not wait to be asked.

### Bof

Bottom of Funnel — Hot Audience

Audience: Prospect knows what a reverse mortgage is. Has seen multiple touchpoints. Is close to a decision. The question is no longer 'what is this' — it is 'why this loan officer.'

Position the loan officer as the leading expert and trusted choice. Direct, confident, and conversion-focused.

Goal: Convert intent into a booked conversation.

⭐  What BOF Is DoingBOF is not educating. It is positioning.The loan officer's expertise, track record, and approach are the product at this stage. BOF content earns the right to ask for a conversation — because the trust required to say yes has already been built.CTAs at BOF are the most direct of all three stages: 'Let's talk.' 'Book a call.' 'Get your personalized estimate.'

Format B: Video Ad Script

Video scripts for Meta run between 45 and 90 seconds and follow a five-part structure without exception.

Part

Job and Guidance

1. Hook (0–5 sec)

Stop the scroll and earn the next 10 seconds. One sentence spoken directly to the prospect's fear, frustration, or desired outcome. No slow build. No introduction. No 'Hi, my name is...' — the first words must be for the prospect, not about the speaker.

2. Empathy & Validation (5–20 sec)

Show the prospect you understand their world before saying anything about the product. Name their situation specifically. Validate the fear or frustration. If skipped, the prospect assumes they are being sold to and disengages.

3. Frame Shift (20–45 sec)

Begin moving the prospect from the Debt Frame to the Retirement Tool Frame. Introduce the product concept by outcome first — not by name. Keep it simple. One concept at a time. No feature lists.

4. Proof & Reassurance (45–70 sec)

Address the most common fear or objection for this archetype. Reference the FHA-insured nature of the product. Use social proof if available. Earn the right to ask for action.

5. CTA (70–90 sec)

One clear, low-pressure next step framed as access to information. No urgency language. No 'act now.' The speaker delivers it directly to camera with calm confidence.

🚨  The First 5 Seconds — Non-NegotiableOn Meta, the viewer can scroll past a video instantly. The hook must work in the first five seconds or the ad has failed — regardless of how good the rest of the script is.The hook is never: 'Hi, I'm [Name] from [Company]...' That is five seconds about the speaker. The prospect does not care who the speaker is yet.The hook is always: a statement or question that makes the prospect think 'that's exactly my situation.'

### Section 5: High-Performing Bof Approaches

BOF content speaks to prospects who already know what a reverse mortgage is and have been exposed to multiple touchpoints. The question they are now asking is not 'what is this' — it is 'why this loan officer.' BOF ads are direct, expertise-forward, and conversion-focused.

The following approaches consistently convert at the bottom of the funnel for reverse mortgage campaigns. As with the angles in Section 4, treat these as proven frameworks to build from — not scripts to copy. Adapt every concept to the specific loan officer's voice, market, results, and client profile.

BOF Approach 1 — Direct Expert Positioning (Video)

The loan officer speaks directly to camera — no script artifice, no educational framing. They speak as an established authority who has helped many homeowners in exactly this prospect's situation. The video is short (30–45 seconds), confident, and closes with a direct invitation to a conversation.

BOF Video — Expert Positioning

Hook (0–5s)

"I've helped [X] homeowners in [State] access their home equity and eliminate their mortgage payment. Here's what most of them told me afterward."

Body (5–30s)

"They said they wished they had done it sooner. They spent months — sometimes years — second-guessing a decision that, once they understood it fully, was one of the best financial moves of their retirement. My job is to make sure you have the information you need to make that call with confidence — not pressure."

CTA (30–45s)

"If you're a homeowner 62 or older and you want to understand exactly how this works for your specific situation — click below and let's talk. No obligation. No sales pitch. Just a real conversation."

BOF Approach 2 — The Objection Killer (Static or Video)

Directly addresses the single most common objection for the target archetype. Names the objection explicitly, validates it, and dismantles it with calm, specific authority. Works because it demonstrates the loan officer understands exactly what the prospect is thinking — which is the fastest path to trust at this stage.

### Section 6: What Never Appears In A Waiz Ad

These are absolute prohibitions across all three funnel stages. No exception.

Prohibited Element

Why

'Reverse mortgage' in any TOF headline or hook

Activates the conditioned rejection response before the content can land.

Age references in ad copy (62+, seniors, elderly)

Compliance requirement and dignity standard. Use 'retired homeowners' or 'homeowners in retirement.'

Guaranteed outcomes (you will qualify, you will receive)

Compliance violation. Always use 'may qualify,' 'could access,' 'in many cases.'

Urgency or scarcity language (act now, limited time)

Triggers the skepticism reflex this demographic has spent a lifetime developing.

Pity, desperation, or crisis imagery

Activates shame and causes immediate withdrawal.

Stock photography of distressed seniors

Reinforces the Debt Frame and the last-resort association.

Tax advice claims (tax-free, no tax consequences)

Compliance violation. Tax treatment depends on individual circumstances.

Fabricated testimonials or statistics

Legal and ethical violation. Social proof must be real or clearly hypothetical.
