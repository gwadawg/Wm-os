# D2C Sales Foundations — Course Material

> **LO course module** (`shareability: lo-course`) — mindset and call approach for paid leads.  
> **Canonical playbook:** [LO D2C Sales Foundations Playbook](../client-sales/playbook-lo-d2c-sales-foundations-rm.md) (read for full rules).  
> **Prerequisite:** [RM Borrower ICP](rm-borrower-icp-education.md) · **Next:** [Conceptual Beliefs Playbook](../client-sales/playbook-rm-conceptual-beliefs.md)

## What you'll understand after this module

1. **Why** paid ad leads are not referral leads — and what that changes on the call
2. **Five beliefs** every LO needs before pitching on D2C appointments
3. **How** to open as a retirement mortgage consultant (not a product rep)
4. **Congruency** — match the ad promise and form notes on every call
5. **Where to go next** — belief installation, BAMFAM, objections

## Lesson 2 — How to open & run the call

**Watch:** Best approach to sales calls training *(add Loom URL when published)*

Covers playbook **Part 2**:

- Referral vs paid lead buckets
- Marketing–sales congruency — read the notes
- Identity shift: **retirement mortgage expert**
- Consultant open (not "let me tell you about reverse mortgages")
- Preview: "I don't want a reverse mortgage"

Full teaching: [Playbook Part 2](../client-sales/playbook-lo-d2c-sales-foundations-rm.md#part-2--call-approach)

### Opening script (use or adapt)

> "Great to connect — I work in the retired mortgage space and help homeowners like you access home equity in the most effective and affordable way possible. I'd love to learn what you're looking to accomplish and what's going on in your situation so I can figure out the best way to serve you."

## Before your first D2C consultation

- [ ] I read [RM Borrower ICP](rm-borrower-icp-education.md) — trust deficit and slow cycles
- [ ] I know **referral warmth ≠ paid ad trust** — I will earn the relationship
- [ ] I will **read form notes** and match outcome language from the ad
- [ ] I open as a **retirement mortgage consultant**, not a reverse rep
- [ ] I treat guard-up as **protection**, not a bad lead
- [ ] I won't let a fit borrower walk on a **product name misconception** alone
- [ ] I'm ready for [Conceptual Beliefs](../client-sales/playbook-rm-conceptual-beliefs.md) — seven beliefs to install on the call
