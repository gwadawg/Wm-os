## 2. Limiting beliefs to break

The biggest threat to your pipeline is often **your own conclusion** — not the lead's answer.

| If you catch yourself thinking… | Replace with |
|--------------------------------|--------------|
| "They ghosted — they're gone" | Ghost ≠ gone. Blocked numbers, slow RM cycles, and life interruptions are normal. |
| "I've called enough" | Most reps quit after one try; your job is volume with purpose ([§1](../client-marketing/playbook-nurture-framework.md#1-why-nurturing-matters-for-reverse-mortgage)). |
| "That objection means no" | It's often a **missing belief** — diagnose the gap, then respond. |

**Three habits that prevent unnecessary ghosting:**

1. **Save your number** — on the first live call, ask them to save your name and callback number.
2. **Multi-channel** — if calls don't land, use text and email; use alternate lines when blocks are likely.
3. **BAMFAM** — never hang up without the next step booked (*full cadence in [Framework §3](../client-marketing/playbook-nurture-framework.md#3-cadence-science)*).

**Borrower belief gaps:** When borrowers ghost or stall, an unchecked conceptual belief is often the cause — not lack of follow-up. Use [RM Conceptual Beliefs Playbook](../client-sales/playbook-rm-conceptual-beliefs.md) to diagnose which of the seven beliefs is missing; then [RM Borrower Objections](../reverse-mortgage-dna/rm-borrower-objections.md) if pushback still surfaces.

Full rules: [Nurture Framework §2](../client-marketing/playbook-nurture-framework.md#2-limiting-beliefs-to-break).

## 3. Cadence science

Three speeds drive show rates: **how fast you first contact**, **how soon you book**, and **how fast you reply** when they engage.

| Speed | Your standard |
|-------|----------------|
| **First contact** | Same day on fresh leads; ≤5 min when possible |
| **First appointment** | Book inside **72 hours** — sooner is better |
| **Response** | Same hour when they text back |

**Dial discipline:** Work a queue (fresh first). Double-dial. Voicemail + text. Front-load Week 1.

**BAMFAM:** Never hang up without the next step booked — [BAMFAM Playbook](../client-sales/playbook-bamfam-rm.md).

| Execution doc | Use |
|---------------|-----|
| [LO Lead Dialing SOP](../client-marketing/sop-lo-lead-dialing-rm.md) | Call order, cadence, VM/text |

## 4. Reactivating aged leads

**Old leads are often deferred decisions — not dead leads.**

When someone opted in months ago and went quiet, their situation may have changed: equity grew, bills increased, family aligned, or another option fell through. Your job is a **new opportunity check-in** — not an apology for following up.

| Mindset shift | |
|---------------|--|
| "They're dead" | **Deferred** — timing and circumstances change |
| "I'd be annoying" | They asked to be contacted; a respectful check-in is service |
| "I already tried" | Most competitors quit after Week 1 — reactivation is volume with purpose |

**Before you dial:** segment the list (never reached, no book, no-show, engaged no close) — angle changes per segment.

| Execution doc | Use |
|---------------|-----|
| [Aged Lead Reactivation Script](../client-marketing/script-aged-lead-reactivation-rm.md) | Openers, VM/text, cadence |
| [BAMFAM Playbook](../client-sales/playbook-bamfam-rm.md) | Rebook when they engage |

Full rules: [Nurture Framework §4](../client-marketing/playbook-nurture-framework.md#4-aged-lead-reactivation).

## Appendix — Waiz DFY clients only

> **Do not include in prospect LO course exports.**  
> `shareability: paying-client` and `internal-fulfillment` docs stay in the client portal and team OS.

If you are a **paying Waiz client** on the Meta stack, your team runs automated nurture on your behalf. Your job is the four pillars below — not rebuilding GHL or drip copy.

Universal pillar definitions: [Framework — Four pillars](../client-marketing/playbook-nurture-framework.md#the-four-pillars-universal).

| Pillar | What you do |
|--------|-------------|
| **Availability** | Calendar open; booking links work |
| **Speed** | Reply fast when they text you directly |
| **Personalization** | Approve LO voice; we match form intent |
| **Volume** | Trust the 90-day arc — don't panic on Day 3 silence |

High-level flow (no internal build specs here):

```text
Meta form → speed-to-lead → education drip → reply → booked call
                ↓ no reply yet
         long-term nurture continues
```
