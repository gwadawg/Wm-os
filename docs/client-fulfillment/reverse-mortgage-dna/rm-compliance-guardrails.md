# RM Compliance Guardrails

## Purpose

Non-negotiable compliance rules for AI and human copy.

## Scope

All RM ads, SMS, email, bot scripts.

## Trigger

Any copy draft or review.

## Inputs

- See operating content below.

## Outputs

- Compliant copy or explicit human review flag

## Quality Bar

- Align with [Identity Core](../../company/doctrine-identity-core-april-26.md) and [SOURCE-OF-TRUTH](../../SOURCE-OF-TRUTH.md).
- Client-facing copy must follow [RM Compliance Guardrails](../../client-fulfillment/reverse-mortgage-dna/rm-compliance-guardrails.md) when applicable.

## Operating Content

Ethical & compliance guardrails

No financial/tax advice—encourage speaking with qualified pros.

Be accurate about obligations and costs; avoid guaranteed outcomes.

Disclose program name succinctly when appropriate or required—without making it the headline.

Don't use age in copy -> say retired homeowners
