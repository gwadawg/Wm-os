# RM Product Lines — Catalog & Ad-Angle Fit

## Purpose

Single canonical map of **reverse-mortgage product types** Waiz clients may offer — and which **ad angles, hooks, and story spines** are valid for each. Prevents creative from mixing HECM mechanics with proprietary second-lien messaging (a common failure mode).

**Load before:** any RM ad concept, script, static brief, or comparison ad (refi vs keep rate, HELOC vs no payment, etc.).

## Scope

- Client fulfillment — B2C ads, nurture, and bot copy that references product mechanics
- Generic Waiz fulfillment defaults assume **HECM** unless the client brief specifies otherwise

## Trigger

- Brainstorming or scripting RM ads
- Client onboarding when product mix is unknown
- Reviewing copy that mentions refinancing, keeping a first mortgage rate, HELOC alternatives, or proprietary program names

## Inputs

- Client offer sheet / LO product menu (which lines they originate)
- Licensed states for the client
- [Intelligence RM Product](intelligence-rm-product.md) — HECM facts and myths
- [RM Compliance Guardrails](rm-compliance-guardrails.md)

## Outputs

- Confirmed **primary product** for the campaign
- Angle × product fit check before creative goes to compliance gate

## Quality bar

- **One primary product per campaign** when mechanics differ (same rule as one archetype per campaign)
- Never imply a client offers HomeSafe Second unless verified
- Proprietary products are **not HECM** — do not call them FHA-insured or cite HECM-only rules unless accurate for that line
- State availability and age floors vary by product and state — use "may qualify" / "in eligible states" language

---

## Why this matters for ads

Reverse mortgage is a **category**, not a single product. Different lines solve different problems:

| Situation | Typical fit |
|-----------|-------------|
| Homeowner wants to **eliminate their existing mortgage payment** (first lien paid off at close) | **HECM** (or proprietary first-lien reverse) |
| Homeowner has a **great first mortgage rate** and needs **cash without refinancing** | **HomeSafe Second** (second-lien proprietary) |
| Homeowner wants a **standby line** that can grow (HECM rules) | **HECM line of credit** |
| Homeowner wants **cash alongside an existing mortgage** without a new monthly payment on the second lien | **HomeSafe Second** (lump sum or proprietary second LOC) |

**Critical rule:** Angles that promise **"keep your low rate"**, **"don't refinance"**, **"access equity without touching your first mortgage"**, or **"refinance vs keep rate"** comparison ads are **HomeSafe Second only**. Standard **HECM replaces the first lien** — using those hooks for a HECM-only client is **misleading**.

## Product catalog (operating summary)

### 1. HECM — Home Equity Conversion Mortgage

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Type** | Federally insured first-lien reverse mortgage (FHA) |
| **Typical age floor** | 62+ (all states) |
| **First mortgage** | Existing first lien is typically **paid off at closing** with proceeds |
| **Payments** | No required monthly mortgage payment on the HECM |
| **Counseling** | HUD-approved independent counseling required before close |
| **Key differentiator** | Growing **HECM line of credit** on unused portion (program formula) |
| **Waiz default** | Yes — most fulfillment copy and funnels assume HECM unless noted |

**Plain-English job:** Turn home equity into cash flow or a lump sum; often used to **remove the monthly mortgage payment** and stay in the home.

**Canonical product facts:** [Intelligence RM Product](intelligence-rm-product.md)

---

### 2. HomeSafe Second — second-lien reverse (proprietary)

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Type** | Proprietary **second-lien** reverse mortgage (Finance of America; **not HECM**) |
| **Typical age floor** | 55+ in many states (varies by state — confirm per client) |
| **First mortgage** | **Stays in place** — borrower keeps existing rate and terms on first lien |
| **Second lien** | Access equity via lump sum and/or proprietary second **line of credit** (product expanded 2026) |
| **Payments** | No required monthly mortgage payment on the **second** lien (must stay current on first lien + property charges) |
| **Counseling** | Not HECM counseling — proprietary disclosures and LO process |
| **Availability** | **State-limited** — confirm client license and current FOA availability |
| **Momentum** | Growing awareness in market; strong fit for **rate-lock retirees** who will not refinance |

**Plain-English job:** Get cash (or a standby line) from equity **without refinancing away a low first mortgage rate** and **without** a HELOC-style monthly payment on the second lien.

**Official reference (external):** [Finance of America — HomeSafe Second](https://www.financeofamerica.com/solutions/homesafe-second/) — verify current terms before client-specific claims.

---

### 3. Other proprietary reverse lines (awareness only)

Waiz clients may also originate **proprietary first-lien** reverse products (jumbo / non-FHA) from various lenders. Treat each as its own row in the client brief:

- Confirm age floor, state list, lien position (first vs second), and whether first mortgage is paid off at close
- Do **not** assume HECM growing-LOC rules or FHA counseling apply
- Do **not** reuse HomeSafe Second "keep your rate" angles unless the product is explicitly a **second lien**

When in doubt, ask the LO: **"Does this product pay off my first mortgage or sit behind it?"**

## Ad angle × product matrix

Use this at concept stage. **FAIL** = do not run that angle for that product without rewriting.

| Ad angle / hook / story spine | HECM | HomeSafe Second | Notes |
|------------------------------|------|-----------------|-------|
| **Eliminate your mortgage payment** | ✅ Primary | ⚠️ Partial | HS2: first lien payment remains; angle must clarify **second lien** has no required payment — not "mortgage gone" |
| **No monthly payment** (generic, TOF) | ✅ | ✅ | Valid for both; mechanism differs — HS2 = no payment on **second** lien only |
| **Keep your low rate / don't refinance** | ❌ **BLOCK** | ✅ **Exclusive** | HECM pays off first lien — this is the #1 mix-up to prevent |
| **Refinance vs keep rate** comparison static | ❌ **BLOCK** | ✅ | Reference comparison-ad format (red/green columns) |
| **HELOC vs no monthly payment** | ✅ (HECM LOC) | ✅ | HS2 often positioned as HELOC alternative; HECM uses different LOC mechanics |
| **Growing line of credit / standby hedge** | ✅ Primary | ⚠️ Check product | HECM LOC growth is the flagship story; HS2 LOC has separate terms (e.g. draw rules) — LO confirms |
| **Trapped asset / house-rich cash-poor** | ✅ | ✅ | Product-agnostic TOF; introduce correct product in MOF |
| **Surviving vs living** | ✅ | ✅ | Product-agnostic |
| **Burden / independence / heirs** | ✅ | ✅ | Product-agnostic; use correct non-recourse / heir language per product |
| **Breaking news / state bulletin** | ✅ | ✅ | Must match **real** product availability in that state |
| **Silent story spine: Mortgage elimination** | ✅ Primary | ❌ Misleading | "Payment gone" implies first lien — HS2 only if copy is precise about **which** payment |
| **Silent story spine: Leave first rate** | ❌ **BLOCK** | ✅ **Exclusive** | See [silent-story-ad-playbook.md](../media-buying/creative-studio/silent-story-ad-playbook.md) |
| **"Say goodbye to refinancing"** (headline) | ❌ **BLOCK** | ✅ | See [mb-rm-ad-copy-standards.md](../media-buying/mb-rm-ad-copy-standards.md) HS angle |

## Creative workflow checklist

Before Step 1 (Concept) in the creative studio:

1. **Confirm client products** — HECM only, HomeSafe Second, or both?
2. **Pick one primary product** for the campaign (or split into separate ad sets per product)
3. **Run the matrix above** — if angle is ❌ for that product, pick a different angle or change product
4. **Tag the concept row** with `product: hecm` | `product: homesafe-second` | `product: mixed` (mixed requires MOF clarification — avoid for TOF)
5. **Pass compliance gate Part F** — [compliance-gate-checklist.md](../media-buying/creative-studio/compliance-gate-checklist.md)

## Common mistakes (avoid)

| Mistake | Why it fails | Fix |
|---------|--------------|-----|
| "Keep your 3% rate" on a HECM-only client | HECM retires the first lien | Use **payment elimination** or **HECM LOC** angles instead |
| Calling HomeSafe Second "HECM" or "FHA-insured" | Wrong program | "Proprietary second-lien program" + LO disclosure |
| "No mortgage payment" for HS2 without qualification | First lien payment still due | "No required monthly payment **on the second lien**" / "keep your current mortgage" |
| Comparison ad: Refi column vs Smart column for HECM | Implies refi is the alternative to HECM when HECM *is* a refi/replacement | Reserve refi-vs-keep-rate for **HomeSafe Second** clients |
| One ad set selling two incompatible mechanics | Confuses algorithm and LO calls | Separate campaigns per product line |

## Client brief field (recommended)

When onboarding or briefing creative, capture:

```yaml
products:
  hecm: true | false
  homesafe_second: true | false
  other_proprietary: []  # list lender + product name
primary_campaign_product: hecm | homesafe-second | dual-track
licensed_states: []
```
