# DSCR Cash-Out Drip — LO First Person (12 touches + long tail)

> **PAYING-CLIENT · CASH-OUT ANGLE · NUMBER-FREE · LO FIRST PERSON.** For DSCR clients who **do not** use the Waiz call center or Waiz CRM. The loan officer is the sender. Paste into the client's own CRM. Deliver this file in the Launch Kit Drive pack (`05-Playbooks/`) with the [DSCR Prospecting Playbook PDF](assets/playbook-self-serve-nurture/DSCR-Prospecting-Playbook.pdf).

Copy-paste ready. Sent from the loan officer to Facebook ad leads who haven't replied or booked yet.

**Client-delivery copy (same body):** [assets/playbook-self-serve-nurture/DSCR-Cash-Out-Drip.md](assets/playbook-self-serve-nurture/DSCR-Cash-Out-Drip.md)

## Before you paste

**Tokens to map in your CRM**

| Token | What it is |
|---|---|
| `{{contact.first_name}}` | Lead's first name |
| `{{contact.state}}` | State the rental is in (from the form) |
| `{{custom_values.lo_first_name}}` | Your first name |
| `{{custom_values.booking_link}}` | Your calendar link |

**Schedule**

Twelve touches over 30 days, not 12 days in a row. Nobody texts a stranger every day for two weeks. Front-load while the lead is warm, then spread out.

| Touch | Send on | Touch | Send on |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Day 1 (as soon as lead lands) | 7 | Day 12 |
| 2 | Day 2 | 8 | Day 15 |
| 3 | Day 3 | 9 | Day 18 |
| 4 | Day 5 | 10 | Day 22 |
| 5 | Day 7 | 11 | Day 26 |
| 6 | Day 9 | 12 | Day 30 |

Some touches are two texts. Send the second one 1–3 minutes after the first — that's how a person texts. Don't send them as one block.

**Rules**

- 8am–7pm lead's local time. Skip Sundays if your CRM allows it.
- Any reply → stop the sequence, you take over.
- Booked call → stop the sequence.
- STOP / unsubscribe → stop immediately.
- Touch 12 with no reply → lead moves to the long tail (Touches 13–19, then quarterly). Don't re-enroll in Touches 1–12.
- Assumes the ad was about **cash-out / pulling equity**. If your ad angle is different, swap that phrase on Touches 1, 3, 7, and 12.
- Only enroll leads in states you're licensed in.
- No numbers (rates, LTV, days-to-close), no guarantees, no "best rates." Everything below stays clear of that. Keep it that way when you edit.
- Touch 2 says "Done all three." Only send that as-is if it's true. If not, change it to "I do all three."

## The texts

### Touch 1

From your own cell:

```
Hey {{contact.first_name}}, it's {{custom_values.lo_first_name}}. Saw you were looking at pulling some equity out of your rental in {{contact.state}}. Is it just the one property or a few?

Txt STOP to opt out
```

From a company / CRM number:

```
Hey {{contact.first_name}}, it's {{custom_values.lo_first_name}}, I'd be the one looking at this. Saw you were looking at pulling some equity out of your rental in {{contact.state}}. Is it just the one property or a few?

Txt STOP to opt out
```

### Touch 2 (two texts)

```
Btw not a one size fits all thing. Most investors I do these for are either pulling cash out to buy the next one, paying off a hard money loan, or getting out of a bank loan with a balloon coming up
```

```
Done all three. Which one sounds like you?
```

### Touch 3

```
Seeing a lot of this in {{contact.state}} right now, investors sitting on a rental they've paid way down and the equity's just parked. Feels safe, but it's not doing anything. Sound familiar?
```

### Touch 4

```
In case the ad didn't make it clear, this goes off what the property rents for, not your tax returns. That's usually what gets self employed investors shot down at a bank. You run into that?
```

### Touch 5

```
Quick thing people don't realize. You don't need a mortgage on the place to do a cash out. If it's paid off that's actually an easier file, loan just gets sized off the rent. Is yours free and clear or still got a note on it?
```

### Touch 6 (two texts, no ask)

```
Heads up before you talk to anyone, prepay penalties and seasoning rules on these are all over the place depending on the lender
```

```
That's the main reason I don't just use one. I shop it across a few and grab the terms that fit what you're doing, not whatever one bank happens to have
```

### Touch 7

```
Still thinking about the cash out on the {{contact.state}} rental or did that get handled?
```

### Touch 8 (no ask)

```
Had this come up again last week. Investor with 4 or 5 rentals wants the next one, bank tells him he's capped on financed properties. That's the bank's cap, not a real rule. DSCR each property stands on its own rent. Doesn't matter how many you've already got.
```

### Touch 9

```
One thing I'd rather tell you now than later. Rate on these is usually higher than conventional. That's the trade for going off the property instead of your income. Whether it's worth it depends on what that equity's doing sitting there. Happy to walk through it with you
```

### Touch 10 (two texts)

```
If it's easier to just talk it through, 15 min. You tell me roughly what it rents for and what you owe, I'll tell you if it's in range. No application for that
```

```
{{custom_values.booking_link}}

Or just shoot me a time that works
```

### Touch 11

```
Other reason people fill that form out, they're on a bridge or hard money note and the maturity's coming up. If that's you lmk and I'll be straight with you on whether the timeline works
```

### Touch 12

```
Last one from me, don't want to blow up your phone. If you ever want to pull the equity out of the {{contact.state}} rental I'm easy to find {{custom_values.booking_link}}
```

## Long tail (after Touch 12)

Spacing widens to every 2–4 weeks, then monthly, then quarterly. Same rules: any reply kills it, STOP kills it.

| Touch | Send on | Touch | Send on |
|---|---|---|---|
| 13 | Day 45 | 17 | Day 120 |
| 14 | Day 60 | 18 | Day 150 |
| 15 | Day 75 | 19 | Day 180 |
| 16 | Day 90 | Quarterly | Day 270, 360, 450... |

### Touch 13

```
Hey, rates have moved around a bit since you first reached out. If that's what you were waiting on lmk and I'll tell you where these are landing right now
```

No number in the text. Give actual pricing live, never in a drip.

### Touch 14

Only send as written if it's true. Otherwise open with "One I see a lot:"

```
Just wrapped one up for an investor in a similar spot, paid down rental, wanted cash for the next one, bank had passed on him. Whole thing went off the rent. Made me think of yours
```

### Touch 15

```
Did you end up doing anything with the {{contact.state}} rental?
```

### Touch 16 (no ask)

```
Random one but comes up a lot. If the rental's in an LLC or you want it in one, most DSCR lenders will close it that way. Banks usually make you put it in your name. Just so you know it's not a blocker
```

### Touch 17

```
If you've raised rent on the place since you first filled that out, that changes what you can pull out. Worth a fresh look if so
```

### Touch 18

```
Still got the rental?
```

### Touch 19

```
It's been a while since you reached out. Not going to keep texting, but if the {{contact.state}} rental's still sitting on equity I'm around. {{custom_values.booking_link}}
```

### Quarterly (rotate, one per quarter)

```
Still got the {{contact.state}} rental?
```

```
Anything change with the rental? Happy to take a fresh look
```

```
Quick one, still thinking about pulling equity out at some point or did you move on from that?
```

## Why it's built this way (read once, then ignore)

- Every ask is a one-word or yes/no reply. Nobody is asked for documents or numbers until Touch 10, and there it's framed as what the call is.
- Each touch moves one belief and says one new thing. The product mechanism ("rent, not tax returns") is explained once, on Touch 4. Repeating it is what makes a drip feel like a drip.
- Touches 6 and 8 have no question. Touch 7 is one line. All three are pattern breaks.
- "Other investors" shows up as a pattern, never as a stat.
- Links only appear on Touch 10 and 12. A link on every text reads as automated.
- State is used on 1, 3, 7, and 12 — enough to feel known, not enough to feel mail-merged.
- Touch 8 opens with "Had this come up again last week." Only send that if it's true. Swap to "See this one a lot." if not.
- No em dashes, loose punctuation on purpose. It's supposed to look typed on a phone. Don't "clean it up."

## What to watch

| Signal | What it means |
|---|---|
| Low Touch 1 replies | Ad angle doesn't match the text, or the sender ID looks like a company |
| Touch 7 outperforms everything | Expected — the one-liner usually does. Don't turn every text into one. |
| STOP spike after Touch 6 | Too many touches for this list; cut Touches 3 and 11 |
| Booked from Touch 10 vs 12 | Whether the calendar link helps or reads as automated |
