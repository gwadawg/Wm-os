## A — Product overview

### What is a DSCR loan?

A **DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio) loan** is an **investment-property refinance** that qualifies on the **property's rental income covering the payment** — not the borrower's W-2s, tax returns, or DTI.

- **DSCR** = whether rent covers the loan payment (rent ÷ payment).
- Built for **real estate investors** refinancing **rentals they already own**.
- **Business-purpose** financing — non-owner-occupied, income-producing property.

### One-line summary (use often)

> "We refinance your rental based on what the property earns — not your tax returns."

### What investors are really solving

| Problem | DSCR angle |
|---------|------------|
| Equity trapped in a rental | Cash-out refinance on the property's income |
| Conventional won't refi them | Qualify on the property, not personal income |
| Balloon / hard money coming due | Exit into a longer-term, property-based loan |
| Write-offs kill their DTI | Rental cash flow matters; returns often don't |
| Need to refi in an LLC | Entity vesting is common |

## B — Qualification basics

### How qualification works (high level — no numbers)

Lenders evaluate the **property**, not the person's income docs:

1. **Rental income** — actual lease or market rent (program-dependent).
2. **Payment** — loan payment plus taxes, insurance, HOA (PITIA).
3. **Ratio** — does rent cover the payment? (That's DSCR.)
4. **Plus** — credit, equity in the property, reserves, and program guidelines.

**Key point:** No W-2s, pay stubs, or tax returns in most DSCR programs.

### What the LO reviews on the call (defer here — never quote over text)

- Property rent vs. payment
- Approximate equity / balance
- Credit band
- Property type and state
- Goal: cash-out, rate/term, or balloon exit
- Entity vs. personal vesting

### What you never collect over text

SSN, full financials, exact property address, or underwriting decisions. Form already captured basics.

## E — DSCR vs conventional

| | Conventional refi | DSCR refi |
|--|-------------------|-----------|
| **Underwrites** | The person (income, DTI, tax returns) | The property (rent vs. payment) |
| **Income docs** | W-2s, returns, pay stubs | Generally none |
| **Property count** | Often capped (~6–10 financed) | Generally no conventional cap |
| **Entity** | Often personal name | LLC / entity common |
| **Rate** | Typically lower | Typically higher — trade for flexibility |
| **Best for** | W-2 borrowers, simple files | Investors, self-employed, portfolio owners |

### Why a bank said no

> "Conventional lenders look at **your** tax returns and DTI. DSCR looks at **the rental's income**. Different product, different question — worth a second look on the property."

### Why work with {{user.first_name}} vs one bank

> "A bank has one guideline set. {{user.first_name}} shops **100+ lenders in all 50 states** to find what fits **your** property — not one box."

## F — Common myths

Use **only when the lead states the myth**. One correction, then book.

| Myth | Truth |
|------|-------|
| "I need to show tax returns." | No — DSCR qualifies on the property's rent, not personal income. |
| "My write-offs disqualify me." | Write-offs hurt conventional DTI — DSCR looks at rental income instead. |
| "I'm capped at 10 properties." | DSCR generally doesn't have the conventional financed-property cap. |

| "I can use this on my primary home." | No — investment / rental property only. |
| "This is for buying a property." | No — refinance on property you already own. |
| "I can cash out almost all my equity." | Cash-out leaves equity in the property — exact amount depends on the deal (on the call). |
| "Interest-only is free money." | IO lowers payment now but has a reset — {{user.first_name}} walks through it on the call. |
| "DSCR is always slower than conventional." | Often doc-light and can move faster — timeline depends on the file (on the call). |
| "All DSCR rates are the same." | Rates vary by property, credit, and lender — {{user.first_name}} shops 100+ options. |
