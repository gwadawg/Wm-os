# DSCR GTM And Positioning Brief

> **Strategy / test waves — not default AI creative context.**
> Who + what to say → [intelligence-icp-dscr.md](intelligence-icp-dscr.md).
> Buckets / angles / naming → [dscr-creative-taxonomy.md](dscr-creative-taxonomy.md).

## Purpose

Beachhead, big idea, channel order, and creative test rules so marketing
isn't scattered.

## Scope

DSCR refinance go-to-market for the test and early scale.

## Quality Bar

- Refinance only · business-purpose · one **bucket** per ad
- Judge on refi-ready CPL → book → show, not vanity CTR

## Operating Content

### 1. The one big idea

**"The property qualifies itself."** Lead with the reframe, then name DSCR.

- "Qualify on the property, not yourself."
- "Refinance on the rent, not your tax returns."

We're not selling a cheaper loan — a *different basis of approval*.

### 2. Beachhead (constraint buckets)

Canonical definitions: [dscr-creative-taxonomy.md](dscr-creative-taxonomy.md).

| Rank | Bucket | Old persona labels (aliases) | Role |
|------|--------|------------------------------|------|
| **1** | **DENIED** | Write-Off / Self-Employed (+ STR, FN, property-count modifiers) | Always-on beachhead |
| **2** | **DEADLINE** | Bridge / Hard-Money / Balloon | Urgency engine |
| 3 | **IDLE** | Portfolio Scaler / idle equity | First expansion (belief-break lane) |
| — | **IN-MARKET** | Terms / rate-card shoppers | Cold harvest when terms are approved |

### 3. Message-market match

| Bucket | Pain | Lead hook direction |
|--------|------|---------------------|
| DENIED | Write-offs kill DTI / conventional says no | Property qualifies, not tax returns |
| DEADLINE | Balloon due | Exit into long-term DSCR before the note |
| IDLE | Idle equity / "safer sitting" | Scale by moving equity (ICP §4) |
| IN-MARKET | Already shopping DSCR | Publish terms / checklist — specificity is the filter |

### 4. Positioning vs alternatives

| They're comparing | Frame that wins |
|-------------------|-----------------|
| Conventional refi | They underwrite *you*; we underwrite the *property* |
| Staying in hard-money / doing nothing | Trapped equity / balloon cost usually beats the rate |
| Selling the door | Cash-out keeps the asset working |

### 5. Channels

1. **Meta** — primary test engine (Special Ad Category)
2. **Google Search** — high-intent "DSCR loan / refinance"
3. BiggerPockets / REIA / YouTube / LinkedIn — after paid core shows signal

### 6. Creative test rules (from former ads playbook)

- **New concepts > recolors.** Distinct bucket/angle each concept.
  Andromeda: creative *is* the targeting.
- **Idea-first**, not ✅ feature checklists (except intentional IN-MARKET
  terms cards).
- **Own under-served lanes:** refinance pain, equity velocity, balloon
  urgency, proof — not louder "no income / fast."
- **~76% of category ads die in two weeks** — budget concept volume;
  refresh on cadence.
- **Low-friction CTA:** "See if your property qualifies" before hard quote.
- **Wave 1:** Meta on DENIED + DEADLINE.
- **Wave 2:** add Google Search on same beachhead.
- **Wave 3:** expand IDLE → STR/FN modifiers → more IN-MARKET terms tests.
- **Judge:** refi-ready CPL → booked → showed (see
  [KPI scorecard](dscr-kpi-and-test-scorecard.md)).

### 7. Decisions locked

- Beachhead: DENIED + DEADLINE
- Big idea: property qualifies itself
- Cold angle order when starting: taxonomy DENIED → DEADLINE → IDLE (proven 3 → 2 → 1)
- Channels: Meta + Google Search first
