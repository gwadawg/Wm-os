# DSCR GTM And Positioning Brief

> **DRAFT — REFINANCE ONLY.** The strategic clarity layer above the DSCR assets: which investor to attack
> first, the one big idea, message-market match per persona, channel priority, and the test sequence. This
> is the "how we win" decision doc — read it before building or judging any creative. Anchored to
> [DSCR Offer And Funnel Map](dscr-offer-and-funnel-map.md); personas from
> [Intelligence ICP DSCR](intelligence-icp-dscr.md); frames from [Doctrine DSCR Marketing](doctrine-dscr-marketing.md).

## Purpose

Turn the pile of DSCR assets into a clear go-to-market: one beachhead, one big idea, a message-market match per persona, a channel order, and a test plan — so marketing is deliberate, not scattered.

## Scope

DSCR refinance marketing strategy (the test and early scale). Not legal/tech (separate gates).

## Quality Bar

- Align with [Identity Core](../../company/doctrine-identity-core-april-26.md) and [SOURCE-OF-TRUTH](../../SOURCE-OF-TRUTH.md).
- Refinance only · business-purpose · number-free copy · one persona per ad.

## Operating Content

### 1. The One Big Idea (the core reframe)

**"The property qualifies itself."**

Every conventional lender judges *the investor* — tax returns, DTI, income docs, property-count caps. DSCR moves the burden of proof from the person to the asset: **if the rental's income covers it, it qualifies.** That single shift is what makes DSCR feel *new* to an investor who's been told "no" by a bank that was looking at the wrong thing.

Everything ladders to this. Lead with the reframe, then name DSCR.

**Umbrella lines (number-free):**
- "Qualify on the property, not yourself."
- "Your property has its own income — borrow against that."
- "Stop qualifying yourself. Let the property do it."

> Why it works (per [ads playbook](dscr-ads-playbook.md), "new > better"): we're not selling a cheaper loan, we're selling a *different basis of approval*. Investors who've been rejected on personal income don't need a better bank — they need a different question asked.

### 2. The Beachhead Decision (who we attack first)

For a test you do **not** market to all five personas. Rank by: **reachability** (addressable volume), **urgency** (speed to convert), **message clarity** (how easily it lands), and **deal quality**.

| Rank | Persona | Reach | Urgency | Msg clarity | Verdict |
|------|---------|-------|---------|-------------|---------|
| **1** | **Self-Employed / Write-Off** | High | Medium | **Highest** | **Primary beachhead** — biggest evergreen pool, purest expression of the big idea |
| **2** | **Bridge / Hard-Money Refinancer** | Medium | **Highest** | High | **Urgency engine** — best conversion, deadline-driven; pair with #1 |
| 3 | Portfolio Scaler / Cash-Out | High | Medium | High | **First expansion** — high value, big TAM; overlaps heavily with #1 |
| 4 | STR / Airbnb Operator | Niche | Medium | Medium | Expansion once core proven; passionate, vocal community |
| 5 | Foreign National | Smallest | Low | Low | Last — program-gated, confirm eligibility first |

**Decision:** launch the test on **#1 Self-Employed / Write-Off** as the always-on beachhead, with **#2 Bridge / Hard-Money** as the high-urgency second angle. Why this pair:

- **#1 is the clearest, broadest "yes."** "Refinance on the rent, not your tax returns" *is* DSCR in one line. Massive, always-on pool of self-employed investors with strong real cash flow and "messy" paper. Lowest creative risk, highest volume — ideal for generating signal fast.
- **#2 converts fastest.** A maturing balloon is a bleeding-neck deadline; these prospects *must* act. Lower volume and timing-dependent, but excellent conversion and perfect for urgency creative + retargeting.
- Hold #3–5 until the core message and funnel economics are proven, then expand (3 → 4 → 5).

### 3. Message-Market Match (the matrix that drives every asset)

One persona per ad. Match the pain to the reframe to the proof they need.

| Persona | Core pain (their words) | Reframe (the big idea, applied) | Lead hook | Proof they need | Top channel |
|---------|-------------------------|----------------------------------|-----------|-----------------|-------------|
| **Self-Employed / Write-Off** | "My write-offs kill my DTI." | The property qualifies, not your tax returns | "Your tax returns say you're broke. Your rentals say otherwise." | "Investors with strong cash flow but messy paper do this all the time" | Meta (broad), Google Search |
| **Bridge / Hard-Money** | "My balloon's coming due." | Exit into a long-term loan that qualifies on the rent | "That hard-money loan was never meant to be permanent." | A real exit/escape example | Meta + retargeting; Google Search ("DSCR refinance fast") |
| **Portfolio Scaler / Cash-Out** | "My equity's trapped; I hit the cap." | Each property qualifies on its own income — no cap | "You own the doors. You built the equity. It's just sitting there." | Portfolio cash-out example | Meta, BiggerPockets, REIA |
| **STR Operator** | "Conventional won't credit my Airbnb income." | Refinance on the property's actual revenue | "Your Airbnb performs. Refinance it on the income." | STR-specific funded example | STR communities, Meta, YouTube |
| **Foreign National** | "No US tax returns/credit." | Qualify on the US property, in an entity | "Own a US rental, no US tax returns? Refinance on the property." | Program eligibility clarity | Niche/referral; confirm program |

Pull copy from the [angle library](ad-copy-angle-library-dscr.md) and scripts from [ad creative batch 01](dscr-ad-creative-batch-01.md); this matrix tells you *which* to run for *whom*.

### 4. Positioning Against the Alternatives

An investor isn't choosing DSCR vs. nothing — they're comparing it to three things. Win each comparison (number-free):

| They're comparing against… | The frame that wins |
|----------------------------|---------------------|
| **Conventional refinance** | "Conventional underwrites *you* — your DTI, returns, property count. DSCR underwrites the *property*. The door conventional closed is the one we open." |
| **Staying in hard-money / doing nothing** | "Trapped equity is dead money — it doesn't compound while it sits. And a balloon doesn't wait. The cost of waiting usually beats the rate." |
| **Selling the property** | "You don't have to sell a door to free up capital. Cash-out on the income and keep the asset working." |

### 5. Channel Priority (where these investors actually are)

| Order | Channel | Why / role |
|-------|---------|------------|
| **1** | **Meta (FB/IG)** | The funnel we built. Creative-led, broad, scalable volume — the primary test engine. (Special Ad Category.) |
| **2** | **Google Search** | Captures **high-intent** "DSCR loan / refinance" searches — different, hotter intent than Meta. Cheap signal on bottom-funnel demand. |
| 3 | BiggerPockets / REIA / investor communities | Highest-intent organic; credibility + referral. Compounds, lower cost, slower. |
| 4 | YouTube / investor podcasts | Where investors *learn* before they call — long-form competence + retargeting fuel. |
| 5 | LinkedIn | B2B referral surface (investor-realtors, CPAs, attorneys) — partnerships, not direct response. |

**Test engine = Meta (volume) + Google Search (intent).** Everything else is compounding/relationship and comes after the paid core shows signal.

### 6. The Test Sequence

1. **Wave 1 — prove the core.** Meta, beachhead **#1 (Self-Employed/Write-Off)** + urgency **#2 (Hard-Money)**. Run the two matching landing variants. Judge on **refi-ready CPL → book → show** (see [KPI scorecard](dscr-kpi-and-test-scorecard.md)), not vanity CTR.
2. **Wave 2 — add intent.** Layer **Google Search** on the same beachhead to capture people already looking.
3. **Wave 3 — expand personas.** Once core economics work, add **#3 Portfolio/Cash-Out**, then **#4 STR**, then **#5 Foreign National**.
4. **Always:** more *concepts* (Andromeda), not recolors; let winners reveal the next batch's emphasis.

### 7. What this brief decides (so downstream stops guessing)

- **Beachhead:** Self-Employed/Write-Off (primary) + Bridge/Hard-Money (urgency).
- **Big idea:** "The property qualifies itself." Lead with the reframe, then name DSCR.
- **First creative emphasis:** angles 1, 3, 7 (property-qualifies / escape hard-money / no tax returns) over the niche angles.
- **First channels:** Meta + Google Search.
- **Judge on:** refi-ready CPL → booked → showed, by angle × persona.
