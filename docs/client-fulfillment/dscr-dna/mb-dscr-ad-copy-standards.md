# MB DSCR Ad Copy Standards

> **DRAFT — REFINANCE ONLY.** Quick-reference headlines + primary text for DSCR **refinance** Meta ads.
> **No pricing or specific figures** (rates, LTV, payments, amounts) appear in any variant — DSCR copy
> never quotes numbers. Full angle development lives in the
> [Ad Copy And Angle Library (DSCR)](ad-copy-angle-library-dscr.md); this is the copywriter/media-buyer pick list.

## Purpose

Fast pick list of approved headline + primary-text variants with angle tags and risk levels.

## Scope

DSCR refinance ad copy only (Meta primary; reusable on Google).

## Trigger

Writing or reviewing ad copy at the media-buyer level.

## Inputs

- [Ad Copy And Angle Library (DSCR)](ad-copy-angle-library-dscr.md), client offer + approved pricing sheet

## Outputs

- Selected, compliance-passed copy variant per ad

## Quality Bar

- Align with [Identity Core](../../company/doctrine-identity-core-april-26.md) and [SOURCE-OF-TRUTH](../../SOURCE-OF-TRUTH.md).
- Every variant passes [DSCR Compliance Guardrails](dscr-compliance-guardrails.md). Refinance only. No pricing/numbers in copy.

## Operating Content

**Angle key:** `PQ` property-qualifies-itself · `TE` trapped-equity / cash-out · `HM` escape hard-money/balloon · `WO` write-offs / no tax returns · `PMT` lower-the-payment · `STR` short-term-rental income · `LLC` close-in-entity · `FN` foreign national.
**Risk level:** 1 = safe/educational · 5 = aggressive (heavier compliance review, weaker substantiation tolerance).

### Primary Text

| Angle | Primary text | Risk |
|-------|--------------|------|
| PQ | Refinance your investment property on the income it produces — no W-2s, no tax returns, no personal DTI. Qualify on the property. See if yours does. ⬇️ | 1/5 |
| TE | You've built equity in rentals you already own. A DSCR cash-out refinance can put it back to work — qualified on the property, not your personal income. | 2/5 |
| HM | That bridge/hard-money loan was never meant to be permanent, and the balloon is coming. Refinance into a long-term DSCR loan that qualifies on the rent — before the note comes due. | 3/5 |
| WO | Smart investors write down their income — then conventional lenders punish them for it. A DSCR refinance qualifies on the property's cash flow instead. Keep the write-offs and the financing. | 2/5 |
| PMT | Refinance the rental you already own into a stable, long-term DSCR loan. Qualify on the property, close in an LLC, keep the flywheel turning. | 1/5 |
| STR | Conventional underwriting barely credits short-term-rental income. A DSCR refinance can use the property's actual performance. Refinance your STR on what it really earns. | 2/5 |
| LLC | Refinance your investment property into your LLC and qualify on its rent — built for how investors actually operate. | 1/5 |
| FN | Own income-producing US property but have no US tax returns? A DSCR refinance can qualify on the property itself. Eligibility varies — see if yours fits. | 3/5 |

> **No numbers in copy.** DSCR ads do not quote rates, LTV caps, payments, or cash-out amounts. Keep
> copy outcome-focused ("pull your equity out," "lower the payment") without asserting any figure.

### Compliance reminders (do not skip)

- Refinance only — no purchase/acquisition copy.
- Business-purpose / investment property — no primary-residence or personal-use framing.
- No pricing or specific figures of any kind in copy.
- No guarantees — "may qualify," "could," "in many cases."
- No tax/legal advice — route depreciation/entity questions to a CPA/attorney.
- Substantiate all proof.
