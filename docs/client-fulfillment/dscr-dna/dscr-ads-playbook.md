# DSCR Ads Playbook

> **DRAFT — REFINANCE ONLY.** Execution playbook for building Meta/Google ads for DSCR **refinance**
> campaigns targeting real-estate investors. **No purchase angles.** Every ad must pass
> [DSCR Compliance Guardrails](dscr-compliance-guardrails.md). **No invented pricing** — rate/LTV/payment
> figures are bracketed placeholders to fill from the approved pricing sheet, never asserted.

## Purpose

Strategic + execution playbook for DSCR refinance ad creative, funnel-stage logic, and campaign structure.

## Scope

Meta (Facebook/Instagram) and Google ads for DSCR clients; creative strategy + media-buyer execution.

## Trigger

New campaign, creative refresh, or constraint layer-1 (creative) fixes.

- Funnel-stage-mapped creative briefs (static + video), campaign structure, pre-delivery QA

- Align with [Identity Core](../../company/doctrine-identity-core-april-26.md) and [SOURCE-OF-TRUTH](../../SOURCE-OF-TRUTH.md).

- One persona/angle per ad. Refinance framing only. No invented pricing, no guarantees.

## Operating Content

### How DSCR ads differ from RM ads (read first)

This playbook is cloned from the RM ad system and **inverted**. The RM rules that do **not** apply here:

| RM rule | DSCR reality |
|---------|--------------|
| Never name the product at TOF ("reverse mortgage" is a liability) | **Name "DSCR" openly** — investors search it; the name signals competence |
| Audience is 62+; target/hook on age | **No age** — target on investor intent; business-purpose, no age framing |
| Lead with emotional relief/security | Lead with **numbers/outcomes** — equity unlocked, payment fixed, fast close |
| De-stigmatize a misunderstood product | **Demand already exists** — prove competence and execution |
| Special Ad Category: Housing | Special Ad Category still applies — **confirm Financial Products & Services (Credit)** treatment per client |

**Non-negotiable for every ad:** refinance only (no purchase), business-purpose/investment property, no invented pricing, no guarantees ("may qualify"), no tax/legal advice.

### The true goal of an ad

The ad has one job: **get the click** from a qualified investor. It does not close the loan. Funnel jobs:

| Stage | Job |
|-------|-----|
| Ad | Get the click (qualified investor) |
| Landing page / VSL | Get the lead + qualify on refi-readiness (see [DSCR Funnel Setup SOP](dscr-funnel-setup-sop.md)) |
| Setter | Book the call |
| Loan officer | Run the numbers, structure the refinance, close |

A **qualified DSCR lead** is an investor who **already owns** an income property, has a refinance reason (trapped equity, high rate, maturing hard-money/balloon, can't qualify conventionally), and enough equity to work with. That is it — it does not mean an easy close.

### The 3 core pillars (90% of performance)

1. **The Angle (the offer)** — the specific refinance outcome you present. Most important.
2. **Ad Copy** — hook → body → CTA.
3. **Ad Creative** — stops the scroll; the algorithm reads it as your targeting.

**The Value Equation (use on every concept):**

- **Dream outcome (↑):** equity pulled out and redeployed, a lower/stable payment, an exit from bad debt, a scalable portfolio.
- **Perceived likelihood (↑):** proof — funded outcomes, "closed in `[CLOSE_DAYS]`," investor reviews, DSCR fluency.
- **Perceived time delay (↓):** "close in `[CLOSE_DAYS]`," fast term sheet — beat the balloon clock.
- **Perceived effort (↓):** "no W-2s, no tax returns — qualify on the property."

**New > better.** Don't run "Get a DSCR loan." Frame the outcome: "Pull the equity out of the doors you already own." Sell the destination, not the vehicle (but you *can* name the vehicle here).

### Platform context — the Andromeda era (creative is the targeting)

Meta's AI (Andromeda) reads every element of the creative — image, text, copy sentiment — to decide who sees it. **The creative is the targeting.** Implications for DSCR:

- **Go broad.** No interest/behavior stacking. Investor intent is carried by the creative and copy, not by audience layers. (No age targeting.)
- **New concepts > variations.** Map angles (below) into 30–50 distinct concepts for scaled accounts (8–12 for smaller client budgets). Diversify the **messaging** first, the look second.
- **Angle mapping = every reason an investor refinances:** trapped equity, balloon/hard-money maturity, write-offs blocking conventional, high rate eating cash flow, STR income not credited, can't refinance past the property cap, move into an LLC, foreign-national with no US tax returns.
- **Exploration then exploitation:** test new concepts first; once a winner emerges, spin variations (hooks/headlines/creatives) around that proven angle.

### Funnel framework — TOF / MOF / BOF (DSCR version)

Because the product name is an asset and demand exists, the funnel shifts from "de-stigmatize" (RM) to **"prove competence and qualify."**

| Stage | Audience | Name DSCR? | Primary job | Tone |
|-------|----------|------------|-------------|------|
| **TOF** | Cold investors | **Yes, fine** | Hook the refinance friction; demonstrate you get investors | Peer operator, numbers-forward |
| **MOF** | Engaged | Yes | Mechanism + proof; pre-handle objections (rate, close speed, equity, entity) | Educational, specific |
| **BOF** | Ready | Yes, directly | Position the LO as the investor-specialist; book the call | Confident, direct |

- **TOF** leads with the investor's world: "Conventional won't refinance your rental?" / "Balloon coming due?" Naming DSCR is fine — it builds credibility, not rejection.
- **MOF** walks the DSCR math (rent ÷ PITIA), shows funded outcomes, and dismantles the top objections before they're spoken.
- **BOF** is "why this loan officer" — investor-specialist who closes fast, handles entities/STR/cash-out. CTA: "Get a DSCR refinance quote" / "Let's run your numbers."

### Ad anatomy

**Static image:** Headline (≤10 words, name the refinance friction/outcome, may name DSCR) · Image direction (investor/property context, not distressed-senior imagery) · Body (2–4 sentences: friction → qualify on the property → CTA) · CTA (low-friction, see bank below).

**Video (45–90s):** (1) Hook 0–5s — name the friction, no "Hi I'm…"; (2) Relevance 5–20s — show you understand how investors operate; (3) Mechanism 20–45s — DSCR refinance in plain terms (qualify on the rent); (4) Proof 45–70s — funded outcome / DSCR fluency / reviews (substantiated); (5) CTA 70–90s — run-your-numbers invite.

**Hook bank by persona** (refinance-only):

| Persona | Opening hook |
|---------|--------------|
| Portfolio Scaler | "You've got equity sitting in doors you already own — doing nothing." |
| Self-Employed / Write-Off | "Your tax returns say you're broke. Your rentals say otherwise." |
| STR / Airbnb Operator | "Conventional barely credits your Airbnb income. There's a better way to refinance it." |
| Bridge / Hard-Money Refinancer | "That hard-money loan was never meant to be permanent — and the balloon is coming." |
| Foreign National* | "Own a US rental but have no US tax returns? You can still refinance." |

\*Program-dependent — confirm eligibility.

**Approved CTA bank:** Get a DSCR Refinance Quote · See If Your Property Qualifies · Run Your Numbers · Check Your Rate Options · See How Much Equity You Can Pull Out · Get a Term Sheet · Talk to a DSCR Specialist. (No guarantees; no "instant approval.")

### The angles

Build every ad on **one** angle for **one** persona. Use the 12 refinance-only angles + headline grid in the [Ad Copy And Angle Library (DSCR)](ad-copy-angle-library-dscr.md) — do not duplicate them here. Tiers: core drivers (property-qualifies-itself, trapped equity, escape hard-money) → cash flow/structure (lower the payment, into your LLC, interest-only) → myth-busting (no tax returns, write-offs aren't a liability, higher-rate-better-math) → niche (STR income, portfolio cash-out, foreign national).

### High-performing BOF approaches (investor version)

1. **Direct expert positioning (video):** "I've helped investors in `[STATE]` refinance rentals on the property's income — here's what they say." Confident, 30–45s, book-a-call close.
2. **Objection killer (static/video):** name the top objection — "Worried the rate is higher than conventional?" — and answer it with the math (trapped equity / balloon cost). 
3. **The comparison (static):** DSCR refinance vs. staying in hard-money, or vs. a conventional refi they can't get. The comparison does the work.
4. **The numbers/term-sheet offer (static):** "Send the address, rents, and payoff — get a term sheet." Most direct conversion offer.
5. **Investor testimonial / case study (substantiated):** a real, specific funded refinance ("refinanced out of a 12% hard-money loan into a 30-year DSCR"). Specific and real, never fabricated.

### What never appears in a DSCR ad

| Prohibited | Why |
|-----------|-----|
| Purchase / acquisition framing | Waiz DSCR line is **refinance only** |
| Primary-residence / personal-use framing | Business-purpose only |
| Invented or guaranteed rates/LTV/amounts | No invented pricing; UDAP/compliance |
| Guaranteed approval / "you qualify" / locked rate or timeline | Compliance — use "may qualify," "in many cases" |
| Tax / legal advice ("tax-free," depreciation guidance) | Route to CPA/attorney |
| Fabricated testimonials or stats | Legal/ethical violation; substantiate everything |
| Running into unlicensed states | Advertise only where the client is authorized |

### Campaign structure (Chad media buyer — radical simplification)

- **Special Ad Category:** declare it (confirm Financial Products & Services / Credit treatment for the investor refinance offer per client). Non-negotiable for compliance.
- **Broad, creative-led:** one consolidated campaign; CBO for scaled budgets; broad targeting (no age, no interest stacks); the creative is the targeting.
- **Volume:** $100+/day → CBO, one ad set, 30–50 distinct concepts; sub-$100/day (most clients) → 8–12 creatives, ~5 copies × 5 headlines as individual ads.
- **Geo:** licensed states only (see compliance + funnel SOP). 
- **Learn/iterate:** let it spend ~2–3× target CPL before judging; kill losers, spin variations off winners.

### Pre-delivery checklist (run on every ad)

- [ ] One persona + one angle only?
- [ ] Refinance framing — zero purchase/acquisition language?
- [ ] Business-purpose / investment property — no primary-residence framing?
- [ ] Funnel stage assigned (TOF/MOF/BOF) and obeys its job?
- [ ] Hook stops the scroll (names the friction/outcome); video hook works in 5s with no self-intro?
- [ ] No invented pricing — placeholders sourced from the pricing sheet, framed illustrative?
- [ ] No guarantees — "may qualify," not "you qualify"?
- [ ] No tax/legal advice; entity/depreciation routed to a CPA/attorney?
- [ ] CTA from the approved bank; licensed-state geo; Special Ad Category set?
- [ ] All proof/testimonials real and substantiated?

If any item fails, the ad is not ready.
