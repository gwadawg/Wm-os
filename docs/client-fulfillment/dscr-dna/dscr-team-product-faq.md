# DSCR Team Product FAQ — Internal Training

> **DRAFT — REFINANCE ONLY · INTERNAL WAIZ TEAM.** Read this before your first DSCR shift — enough to sound
> literate on calls and know when to route to the LO. Distilled from the [DSCR DNA](README.md) pod; go deeper
> via links in §7.

## Purpose

Give every internal Waiz team member a single, scannable primer on the DSCR refinance product: who the borrower is, what the product is, how it differs from other financing, vocabulary for calls, and top objections — without underwriting, invented pricing, or tax/legal advice.

## Scope

Internal Waiz team only (setters, client success, bot handlers, media buying context). Not the client LO's external shop. Refinance of investment property the borrower already owns — **no purchase, no primary residence.**

## How to use by role

| Role | Read for | Do NOT do |
|------|----------|-----------|
| Setter / intake | ICP, vocabulary, booking objections | Quote rates, LTV, or approval |
| CS / bot handler | Product in plain English, top myths, booking path | Underwrite or give tax/legal advice |
| Media buying | ICP personas, phrases they say, outcome framing | Invent pricing in copy |
| Everyone | Refinance-only scope, business-purpose rule | Purchase or primary-residence angles |

📌 **Waiz DSCR line = refinance only.** Purchase leads are out of scope.

## 1. Who you're talking to (ICP snapshot)

Your caller is **not** an anxious consumer shopping for relief. They are a **real-estate investor / operator** who treats property as a business. They think in cash flow, leverage, cap rates, and capital velocity.

**Core profile**

- **Already owns** the rental they want to refinance (cash-out, rate/term, or escape a balloon / hard-money loan)
- Often **self-employed or write-off-heavy** — conventional DTI fails despite real income
- Often holds property in an **LLC**; skeptical of lenders who don't understand investors
- Credit is often strong, but **tax returns make them look broke on paper**
- Values **speed and competence** over hand-holding

**What they're buying (not what they're buying)**

| They are NOT buying | They ARE buying |
|---------------------|-----------------|
| A mortgage product | Equity unlocked from property they own |
| Income-doc underwriting | Qualifying on the property, not their W-2 |
| Relief or security | A better payment, capital velocity, exit from bad debt |

### Five personas (recognize the caller type)

| Persona | One-line signal |
|---------|-----------------|
| **Portfolio Scaler** | 5–30 doors; equity trapped; conventional won't cash-out refi because of property count |
| **Write-Off Investor** | Self-employed; strong cash flow, "messy" tax returns; DTI kills conventional refi |
| **STR / Airbnb Operator** | Short-term rental; conventional doesn't credit STR income well |
| **Bridge / Hard-Money Refinancer** | Short-term note maturing; needs long-term exit before balloon hits |
| **Foreign National** *(program-dependent)* | Owns US rental; no domestic income/credit history |

### Phrases you'll hear on calls

Use these to recognize intent — investors often say them verbatim:

- "Conventional won't refinance my rental."
- "My tax write-offs are killing my DTI."
- "My balloon's coming due — I need to refinance fast."
- "Can I refinance this in my LLC?"
- "Does the property cash-flow enough to refinance?"
- "I'm tired of documenting my whole life every time I refinance."
- "I've got the equity — I just need to pull it out and redeploy it."
- "I need a lender who actually understands investors."

## 2. What DSCR is (layman's terms)

A **DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio) loan** refinances an **investment property you already own**. The lender qualifies you based on **whether the property's rent covers its payment** — not your W-2, pay stubs, or tax returns. It is usually **business-purpose** financing and is often closed in an **LLC**.

**The one big idea:** *"The property qualifies itself."*

Conventional lenders judge *the investor* — tax returns, DTI, income docs, property-count caps. DSCR moves the burden of proof to the asset: **if the rental's income covers the payment, it qualifies.**

🚨 **Not eligible:** primary residence, personal use, or purchase financing. Waiz DSCR line is **refinance only.**

## 3. Why DSCR vs other products (qualification comparables)

This table is about **how qualification works** — not rates or pricing. Route all specific numbers to the LO.

| | Conventional investment refi | DSCR refi | Hard-money / bridge |
|--|------------------------------|-----------|---------------------|
| **Qualifies on** | Borrower's income, tax returns, DTI | Property rent vs. payment (DSCR ratio) | Asset/equity, exit strategy |
| **Income docs** | Full doc — W-2, returns, DTI | None — property income only | Minimal |
| **Property count cap** | Yes (~6–10 financed) | Generally no conventional cap | N/A |
| **Entity (LLC)** | Often difficult | Commonly allowed | Yes |
| **Typical use** | Straightforward investor with clean docs | Write-offs, property-capped, STR, entity refi | Short-term hold/rehab |
| **Term** | 15–30 yr fixed | 30 yr fixed, IO, ARM | 6–24 mo balloon |
| **Trade-off** | Lower rate if you qualify | Higher rate; qualifies when conventional won't | Fast but expensive; must exit |

### When investors choose DSCR

- Conventional refi stalled on tax returns, DTI, or property count
- Equity trapped — need cash-out to redeploy into the next deal
- Balloon or hard-money note maturing — need a long-term exit
- STR income not credited conventionally
- Want to refinance in an LLC

💡 **Frame for team:** DSCR is the trade for no income docs and entity flexibility. Investors don't pick it for the lowest rate — they pick it when conventional won't close the refi or when trapped equity / a maturing note costs more than waiting.

## 4. Vocabulary glossary (call-ready)

Alphabetical quick-reference. Define terms; do not quote specific rates or LTV percentages on calls.

| Term | Plain definition |
|------|------------------|
| **Balloon** | Loan with full balance due at maturity — common urgency driver on calls |
| **Business-purpose** | Investment/commercial intent — not a primary-residence loan |
| **Cash-out refi** | Refinance and pull equity out of the property |
| **DSCR** | Debt Service Coverage Ratio — whether the property's rent covers its payment |
| **DTI** | Debt-to-income — conventional metric DSCR bypasses |
| **Hard-money / bridge** | Short-term, high-cost loan investors use temporarily — DSCR is often the long-term exit |
| **LTV** | Loan-to-value — how much of the property value is borrowed |
| **Non-QM** | Non-qualified mortgage — DSCR sits in this bucket |
| **Rate/term refi** | Refinance to lower payment or change structure — no cash out |

## 5. DSCR vs Reverse Mortgage (orientation for Waiz team)

The team already knows RM. DSCR is the **near-inverse** — do not cross-contaminate tone or framing.

| | Reverse Mortgage | DSCR |
|--|------------------|------|
| Borrower | Retiree homeowner | Real-estate investor |
| Product name in marketing | Hidden / careful | **Named openly** — investors search it |
| Tone | Education, trust, relief | Competence, speed, numbers |
| Qualifies on | Age, equity, occupancy | Property cash flow |
| Waiz scope | HECM / RM | **Refinance only** |

📌 **Golden rule:** RM sells security and education. DSCR sells **outcomes** (equity unlocked, payment fixed, bad debt escaped) to operators who already understand leverage.

## 6. Top objections + rebuttals (team-safe)

Pattern: **validate → reframe → route to LO.** Setters and CS stay high-level; the LO runs real numbers live. Full LO word tracks: [DSCR Objection-Handling Guide](dscr-objection-handling-guide.md).

### 1. "The rate's higher than conventional."

- **What's really going on:** Weighing cost vs. trapped equity or a maturing balloon.
- **What you say:** "That's the trade for qualifying on the property instead of your income, and often closing in your entity. [LO] can run your actual property and compare — that's what the consultation is for."
- **Route to LO when:** They want numbers, payment comparison, or "is it worth it."

### 2. "Will it close before my balloon is due?"

- **What's really going on:** Fear of a blown refi at maturity.
- **What you say:** "DSCR is doc-light by design — we underwrite the property, not your whole financial life, so the timeline is usually tighter than conventional. I can't promise a date, but [LO] can look at your timeline today."
- **Route to LO when:** They have a specific maturity date or hard deadline.

### 3. "Do I have to show income / tax returns?"

- **What's really going on:** Doc fatigue; wants confirmation DSCR is real.
- **What you say:** "No — that's the whole point. We qualify on the property's rent versus its payment, not your W-2, pay stubs, or returns."
- **Route to LO when:** They want to know what docs they *will* need (appraisal, lease, etc.).

### 4. "Does my property even qualify?"

- **What's really going on:** DSCR uncertainty — don't know if rent pencils.
- **What you say:** "It comes down to whether the rent covers the property's payment. [LO] can walk your rough rents and current loan on the call."
- **Route to LO when:** Always — this is an LO question.

### 5. "Can I refinance in my LLC?"

- **What's really going on:** Liability and entity structure.
- **What you say:** "In most programs, yes — entity vesting is normal for investors. Tax and structuring specifics, though, you'll want to confirm with your CPA or attorney."
- **Route to LO when:** They need program confirmation; route tax/structure to CPA/attorney.

### 6. "Conventional won't refinance me / I hit my property cap."

- **What's really going on:** Blocked by conventional limits, not deal quality.
- **What you say:** "Right — that's a conventional limitation, not a DSCR one. DSCR qualifies each property on its own income and generally doesn't have that financed-property cap."
- **Route to LO when:** They want to know if their specific portfolio fits.

### 7. "How much equity do I need to leave in?"

- **What's really going on:** Capital efficiency — how much can they pull out.
- **What you say:** "Cash-out leaves some equity in the property — exactly how much depends on the property and program. [LO] can run yours and show what you could pull and what stays in."
- **Route to LO when:** Always — no fixed figures from setters or CS.

### 8. "I'm a foreign national."

- **What's really going on:** Eligibility uncertainty without US income/credit history.
- **What you say:** "Some programs are built for foreign-national investors refinancing US rentals — qualifying on the property, often in an entity. It's program-dependent, so [LO] will confirm what fits your situation."
- **Route to LO when:** Always — do not promise eligibility.

### 9. "Interest-only sounds risky."

- **What's really going on:** Payment shock fear later.
- **What you say:** "IO lowers the payment now and maximizes cash flow, but you're right to think ahead. [LO] can model the reset so you go in clear-eyed."
- **Route to LO when:** They want IO vs. amortizing comparison.

### 10. "How are you different from my bank?"

- **What's really going on:** Lender competence — tired of explaining investors to a generalist.
- **What you say:** "Your bank underwrites you. [LO] underwrites the property. DSCR, entities, STR income, and refi speed — that's the focus, so you're not teaching your loan officer how investors work."
- **Route to LO when:** They want proof of specialization or process detail.

### Universal guardrails (every objection)

🚨 **Never:**

- Guarantee approval, rate, LTV, amount, or timeline — use "may qualify," "in many cases," "subject to underwriting"
- Give tax, legal, or financial advice — route entity, depreciation, and 1031 questions to CPA/attorney
- Market purchase or primary-residence use — refinance + investment only
