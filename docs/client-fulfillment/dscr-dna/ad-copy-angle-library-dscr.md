# Ad Copy And Angle Library (DSCR)

> **DRAFT — REFINANCE ONLY.** The Waiz DSCR line covers **refinance transactions only** (rate/term +
> cash-out) on rentals the investor already owns. **No purchase angles.** Every angle here must pass
> [DSCR Compliance Guardrails](dscr-compliance-guardrails.md). **No pricing or specific figures**
> (rates, LTV, payments, amounts) appear in any copy — keep it outcome-focused and qualitative.

## Purpose

Canonical angles, headlines, and primary text for DSCR **refinance** Meta/Google ads targeting real-estate investors.

## Scope

Media buyers and AI creative generation for the DSCR vertical.

## Trigger

New client creative build, creative refresh, constraint layer 1 fixes.

## Inputs

- Client offer + voice/brand notes
- [DSCR Compliance Guardrails](dscr-compliance-guardrails.md)
- Approved personas + objections: [Intelligence ICP DSCR](intelligence-icp-dscr.md)
- Product mechanics: [Intelligence DSCR Product](intelligence-dscr-product.md)

## Outputs

- Ad copy variants mapped to persona + angle
- UTM patterns

## Quality Bar

- Align with [Identity Core](../../company/doctrine-identity-core-april-26.md) and [SOURCE-OF-TRUTH](../../SOURCE-OF-TRUTH.md).
- Every variant passes the pre-flight check in [DSCR Compliance Guardrails](dscr-compliance-guardrails.md).
- One persona/angle per ad set. Lead with the outcome, then name "DSCR." No purchase framing.

## Operating Content

Read [Doctrine DSCR Marketing](doctrine-dscr-marketing.md) first. Each angle below gives a distinct
psychological signal to the algorithm and maps to one approved persona. Copy is operator-to-operator
(numbers-literate, peer tone) — never the senior/relief tone used for RM.

**No numbers in copy.** DSCR ads never quote rates, LTV, DSCR ratios, payments, close times, or amounts.
Keep every variant qualitative and outcome-focused. The only allowed token is `[STATE]` for geo localization.

**Standing disclosure (append where the client's licensing requires):** *Business-purpose investment
financing. For investment (non-owner-occupied) property only. Not a commitment to lend; all loans subject
to underwriting and property qualification.*

### Tier 1: Core Investor Drivers

#### Angle 1: The "Property Qualifies Itself" Angle  ·  Persona: Self-Employed / Write-Off

- **Primary Text:** Your tax returns say one thing. Your rentals say another. A DSCR refinance qualifies on the property's rent — not your W-2, pay stubs, or tax returns. If your rental cash-flows, your write-offs stop working against you.
- **Headline:** Refinance on the Rent, Not Your Tax Returns
- **Body:** No income docs. Qualify on the property's cash flow. See if your rental qualifies.

#### Angle 2: The "Trapped Equity" Angle  ·  Persona: Portfolio Scaler

- **Primary Text:** You've got equity sitting in doors you already own — doing nothing. A DSCR cash-out refinance lets you pull it out and put it back to work, qualifying on the property's income instead of yours.
- **Headline:** Your Equity Is Just Sitting There. Put It to Work.
- **Body:** Cash-out refinance on the property's cash flow. No income verification. See what you can pull out.

#### Angle 3: The "Escape Hard-Money" Angle  ·  Persona: Bridge / Hard-Money Refinancer

- **Primary Text:** That bridge or hard-money loan was never meant to be permanent — and the balloon is coming. Refinance into a long-term DSCR loan that qualifies on the rent, before the note comes due.
- **Headline:** Balloon Coming Due? Refinance Before It Hits.
- **Body:** Exit hard-money into a 30-year DSCR loan. Qualify on the property, not your income.

---

### Tier 2: Cash Flow & Structure

#### Angle 4: The "Lower the Payment" Angle  ·  Persona: any (rate/term)

- **Primary Text:** If your rental is carrying a high rate or an unstable structure, it's quietly eating your cash flow every month. A DSCR rate/term refinance can reset the payment — qualified on the property, not your tax returns.
- **Headline:** Stop Letting a Bad Rate Eat Your Cash Flow
- **Body:** Refinance your rental to a steadier payment. No income docs. See if it pencils.

#### Angle 5: The "Refinance Into Your LLC" Angle  ·  Persona: any (entity)

- **Primary Text:** Holding a rental in your personal name commingles your finances and exposes your personal assets. Most DSCR programs let you refinance into your LLC — asset protection and clean entity treatment, qualified on the property's income. (Talk to your CPA about your structure.)
- **Headline:** Refinance the Rental Into Your LLC
- **Body:** Entity-friendly DSCR refinance. Qualify on the property. Ask us how it works.

#### Angle 6: The "Interest-Only Cash Flow" Angle  ·  Persona: STR / cash-flow focused

- **Primary Text:** Want to maximize monthly cash flow on a rental you already own? Some DSCR refinances offer interest-only options that lower the payment now. (Plan for the reset — we'll model it with you.)
- **Headline:** Maximize Cash Flow on the Doors You Own
- **Body:** Interest-only DSCR refinance options. Qualify on the property's income. See the numbers.

---

### Tier 3: Myth-Busting & Education

#### Angle 7: The "No Tax Returns" Angle  ·  Persona: Self-Employed / Write-Off

- **Primary Text:** Most investors think they need two years of clean tax returns to refinance a rental. You don't. A DSCR refinance verifies the property's rent and the lease — not your personal income. That's the whole point.
- **Headline:** You Don't Need Tax Returns to Refinance a Rental
- **Body:** Property qualifies, not your paperwork. Learn how a DSCR refinance works.

#### Angle 8: The "Write-Offs Aren't a Liability" Angle  ·  Persona: Self-Employed / Write-Off

- **Primary Text:** The write-offs that lower your tax bill also wreck your DTI on a conventional refinance. With DSCR, they don't matter — the property's cash flow does the qualifying. Smart on taxes shouldn't mean stuck on financing.
- **Headline:** Your Write-Offs Shouldn't Block Your Refinance
- **Body:** DSCR qualifies on the property, not your AGI. See if your rental qualifies.

#### Angle 9: The "Higher Rate, Better Math" Angle  ·  Persona: rate objection

- **Primary Text:** Yes, DSCR rates run higher than conventional. But compare that to equity trapped doing nothing, or a balloon you can't refinance. For a lot of investors, the math on freeing capital or escaping bad debt wins. Run your numbers.
- **Headline:** A Higher Rate Can Still Be the Smarter Move
- **Body:** Weigh the rate against trapped equity or a maturing note. Let's run your scenario.

---

### Tier 4: Persona & Niche

#### Angle 10: The "STR Income" Angle  ·  Persona: STR / Airbnb Operator

- **Primary Text:** Conventional lenders barely credit short-term rental income. DSCR programs can underwrite the refinance off your STR's actual performance (some use AirDNA projections). Refinance your Airbnb on what it really earns.
- **Headline:** Refinance Your Airbnb on What It Actually Earns
- **Body:** STR-friendly DSCR refinance. Qualify on the property's revenue. See what's possible.

#### Angle 11: The "Portfolio Cash-Out" Angle  ·  Persona: Portfolio Scaler

- **Primary Text:** You don't have to sell a door to free up capital. A DSCR cash-out refinance across the rentals you already own lets you unlock equity without a property-count ceiling — each property qualifies on its own income.
- **Headline:** Free Up Capital Without Selling a Single Door
- **Body:** Cash-out refinance across your portfolio. No conventional cap. See what you can unlock.

#### Angle 12: The "Foreign National Refinance" Angle  ·  Persona: Foreign National *(program-dependent — confirm eligibility)*

- **Primary Text:** Own a US rental but don't have US tax returns or credit history? Some DSCR programs let foreign national investors refinance on the property's income, in an entity. (Eligibility varies by program — we'll confirm.)
- **Headline:** Refinance Your US Rental — No US Tax Returns
- **Body:** Foreign-national DSCR refinance options. Qualify on the property. Check your eligibility.

### Compliance Notes

DSCR refinance ads are **business-purpose**, but advertising still carries real constraints. Every variant must follow [DSCR Compliance Guardrails](dscr-compliance-guardrails.md). Specifically:

- **Refinance only.** No purchase/acquisition language. Assume the investor already owns the property.
- **Business-purpose / investment property only.** Never primary-residence or personal-use framing.
- **No pricing or numbers in copy.** Never quote rates, LTV, DSCR ratios, payments, close times, or amounts. Keep every variant qualitative and outcome-focused.
- **No guarantees.** Use "may qualify," "could," "in many cases." Never "you qualify," "guaranteed approval," or a locked rate/timeline.
- **Meta Special Ad Category.** U.S. mortgage ads still require the Financial Products & Services Special Ad Category (targeting restrictions; proof of authorization) even though the loan is business-purpose.
- **Licensed states only.** Run only where the client is authorized to lend/broker for the relevant collateral; confirm `[STATE]` list per client.
- **No tax/legal advice.** Route entity, depreciation, and structuring questions to a CPA/attorney.
- **Append the standing disclosure** where the client's licensing agreement requires it.
