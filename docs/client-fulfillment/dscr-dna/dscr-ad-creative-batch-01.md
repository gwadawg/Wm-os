# DSCR Ad Creative — Batch 01

> **DRAFT — REFINANCE ONLY · NUMBER-FREE.** Ready-to-produce DSCR refinance ad creative: video scripts +
> static image briefs. Built from [Intelligence ICP DSCR](intelligence-icp-dscr.md),
> [Ad Copy And Angle Library (DSCR)](ad-copy-angle-library-dscr.md), and the
> [DSCR Ads Playbook](dscr-ads-playbook.md); anchored to the offer/CTA in
> [DSCR Offer And Funnel Map](dscr-offer-and-funnel-map.md). Every asset passes
> [DSCR Compliance Guardrails](dscr-compliance-guardrails.md).

## Purpose

A produce-ready batch of DSCR refinance ads (3 video scripts, 6 static briefs) spanning the four core personas plus two BOF angles.

## Scope

Generic DSCR loan-officer voice (brand/LO speaks in ads; **Laura is the follow-up voice, not the ad voice**). Meta primary; reusable on Google/YouTube.

## Trigger

New client creative build or creative refresh — adapt to the client's voice/market, then run the pre-flight check.

## Inputs

- Personas + objections: [Intelligence ICP DSCR](intelligence-icp-dscr.md)
- Angles + headlines: [Ad Copy And Angle Library (DSCR)](ad-copy-angle-library-dscr.md)
- Offer/CTA ladder: [DSCR Offer And Funnel Map](dscr-offer-and-funnel-map.md)

## Outputs

- 3 video ad scripts (45–90s) + 6 static image briefs, persona- and angle-mapped.

## Quality Bar

- Align with [Identity Core](../../company/doctrine-identity-core-april-26.md) and [SOURCE-OF-TRUTH](../../SOURCE-OF-TRUTH.md).
- One persona/angle per ad · refinance only · business-purpose · **no pricing/numbers** · no guarantees.
- **Proof beats must be filled with real, substantiated results before running** — never fabricate. Where no proof exists yet, keep the structural framing provided and omit specifics.

## Operating Content

### How to use this batch

- **Adapt, don't copy verbatim.** Match the client's voice, market, and the one persona each ad targets.
- **Voice:** ads are the LO/brand speaking (or an actor/spokesperson). Warm, numbers-literate, peer-to-peer — operator-to-operator, never the senior/relief RM tone.
- **Compliance is non-negotiable:** name "DSCR" openly; never quote a rate/LTV/payment/amount; "may qualify," not "you qualify"; refinance/investment only; no tax/legal advice; substantiate every proof claim.
- **Production:** shoot video vertical (9:16) for feed/Reels; statics 1:1 and 4:5. Hook must land in the first 5s with no self-intro.

## Video Scripts (45–90s)

### Video 1 — Persona: Portfolio Scaler · Angle: Trapped Equity / Portfolio Cash-Out

| Beat | Script |
|------|--------|
| **Hook (0–5s)** | "You own the doors. You built the equity. And right now, it's just… sitting there." |
| **Relevance (5–20s)** | "If you're an active investor, you already know the trap — conventional lenders cap how many properties you can finance, your tax returns make you look broke on paper, and the equity you've built across your rentals is stuck where you can't touch it." |
| **Mechanism (20–45s)** | "A DSCR refinance qualifies on the property's rental income — not your W-2, not your tax returns, not your DTI. So you can do a cash-out refinance on a rental you already own, pull that equity out, and put it back to work. Each property stands on its own income — no property-count ceiling." |
| **Proof / Reassurance (45–70s)** | "It's how a lot of serious investors keep their capital moving instead of letting it sit. You're qualifying on the asset — the way investors should be underwritten — and you can typically close in your LLC. *[Insert a real, substantiated investor result here before running, or keep this structural framing.]*" |
| **CTA (70–90s)** | "If you've got equity trapped in doors you already own, let's see what they qualify for. Tap below and run your numbers — no tax returns required." |

## Static Image Briefs

### Static 1 — Portfolio Scaler · Trapped Equity · TOF

- **Headline:** Your Equity Is Just Sitting There. Put It to Work.
- **Image direction:** Confident investor (35–55) in front of a small multifamily or a row of rental doors, mid-day, arms crossed — operator energy, not consumer. Alt: clean graphic of a house unlocking into a flow of capital. No distressed/senior imagery.
- **Body:** You built the equity across the rentals you already own. A DSCR cash-out refinance lets you pull it out and redeploy it — qualifying on the property's income, not your tax returns.
- **CTA:** See If Your Property Qualifies

### Static 2 — Self-Employed / Write-Off · Property Qualifies Itself · TOF

- **Headline:** Refinance on the Rent, Not Your Tax Returns.
- **Image direction:** Split visual — left: a stack of tax documents stamped "DENIED"; right: a clean rental with a "QUALIFIES" tag. Alt: an unbothered investor at a laptop. Sharp, modern, number-free.
- **Body:** Great write-offs shouldn't get your refinance denied. A DSCR refinance qualifies on your rental's cash flow — no W-2s, no pay stubs, no tax returns.
- **CTA:** See If Your Property Qualifies

### Static 3 — Bridge / Hard-Money · Escape Hard-Money · TOF/MOF

- **Headline:** Balloon Coming Due? Refinance Before It Hits.
- **Image direction:** Clean metaphor — a calendar with a circled date beside a small balloon/pin, or an investor calmly reviewing a property file. Authoritative, lightly time-sensitive, never alarmist.
- **Body:** That bridge or hard-money loan was never meant to be permanent. Refinance into a long-term DSCR loan that qualifies on the rent — before the note comes due.
- **CTA:** Map Your Exit

### Static 4 — STR / Airbnb Operator · STR Income · TOF

- **Headline:** Refinance Your Airbnb on What It Actually Earns.
- **Image direction:** Bright, well-styled short-term-rental interior, or a host reviewing bookings on a tablet. Aspirational-operator, not a vacation ad.
- **Body:** Conventional underwriting barely credits short-term-rental income. A DSCR refinance can use the property's actual revenue — so your STR qualifies on its performance, not your W-2.
- **CTA:** See What Your STR Qualifies For

### Static 5 — Rate Objection (any persona) · Higher Rate, Better Math · BOF

- **Headline:** A Higher Rate Can Still Be the Smarter Move.
- **Image direction:** Minimal two-column comparison graphic, **no numbers** — "Equity trapped · balloon ticking" vs. "Equity working · stable long-term loan." Clean, financial, confident.
- **Body:** Trapped equity and a maturing balloon cost more than a slightly higher rate. A DSCR refinance gets your capital moving and your loan stable — qualified on the property, not your income.
- **CTA:** Run Your Numbers

### Static 6 — Specialist Positioning (any persona) · Investor-Competence · BOF

- **Headline:** A Lender Who Actually Gets How Investors Operate.
- **Image direction:** The loan officer direct-to-camera (approachable, professional) or a clean "DSCR Specialist" badge treatment. Trust/competence cue, not salesy.
- **Body:** No income docs. Qualify on the property. Close in your LLC. If you're refinancing a rental you already own, let's talk like investors.
- **CTA:** Talk to a DSCR Specialist

### Pre-flight check (run on every asset before it ships)

- [ ] One persona + one angle?
- [ ] Refinance framing — zero purchase/acquisition language?
- [ ] Business-purpose / investment property — no primary-residence framing?
- [ ] No pricing or specific figures anywhere?
- [ ] No guarantees — "may qualify," not "you qualify"?
- [ ] No tax/legal advice; entity/depreciation routed to a CPA/attorney?
- [ ] Proof beats filled with real, substantiated results (or structural framing only)?
- [ ] CTA from the approved ladder; DSCR named openly is fine?
