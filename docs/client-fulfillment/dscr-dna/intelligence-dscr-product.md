## Plain English

A **DSCR loan** qualifies on **property rental income ÷ PITIA** (principal +
interest + taxes + insurance + HOA) — not personal income. Waiz line =
**refinance** of income-producing property already owned, often in an LLC.

## Ratio (illustrative)

| DSCR | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Below ~1.0 | Rent doesn't cover payment — most programs won't; specialty/no-ratio exists at worse terms |
| ~1.0 | Break-even |
| ~1.25+ | Stronger cushion / better pricing tiers (program-dependent) |

Income source: market rent (1007/1025) or actual lease — per lender.

## Terms (illustrative — confirm pricing sheet)

| Variable | Typical range (illustrative) |
|----------|------------------------------|
| Max LTV | Rate/term ~80%; cash-out ~75% (lower for weaker credit / FN) |
| Credit | Floors ~620–660; better pricing often 700+ |
| Reserves | ~3–6 months PITIA |
| Structures | 30-yr fixed, IO options, some ARMs |
| Property | SFR, 2–4, condo/TH — **not** owner-occupied |
| Vesting | LLC / entity common |

**Do not put these numbers in TOF ads.** MOF/BOF may use tokens from
[Campaign Master Angles](dscr-campaign-master-angles.md) filled from the
**client's** sheet only.

## What investors use it for

- Cash-out → redeploy equity
- Rate/term → lower/stabilize payment
- Exit bridge / hard-money / balloon
- Refinance when write-offs kill conventional DTI
- STR income (program-dependent)
- Refinance into an entity (CPA for tax specifics)

## Quick reality checks

- Rates higher than conventional — trade for no-doc access + flexibility
- Property must cash-flow (or close)
- Refinance only for Waiz line; investment property only
- Cash-out leaves equity in the property (not 95% unlock myths)
