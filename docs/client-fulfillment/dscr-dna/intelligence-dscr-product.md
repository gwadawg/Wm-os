### What It Is (Plain English)

A **Debt-Service-Coverage-Ratio (DSCR) loan** is an investment-property mortgage that qualifies on the **property's rental cash flow — not the borrower's personal income.** No W-2s, pay stubs, or tax returns. For the Waiz line it is used to **refinance** income-producing property the investor already owns — typically as **business-purpose** financing and frequently closed in an **LLC/entity**. It is **not** a primary-residence loan and **not** purchase financing.

### How Qualification Works

- **The ratio:** `DSCR = Gross Rental Income ÷ PITIA`, where **PITIA** = Principal + Interest + Taxes + Insurance + Association (HOA) dues.
- **What it means (illustrative):**
  - **Below 1.0** — rent does not fully cover the payment. Most lenders won't fund here; some specialty "no-ratio" / ~0.75 programs exist at lower LTV and higher rate.
  - **1.0** — break-even; rent exactly covers debt service.
  - **1.25+** — strong cushion; typically unlocks the best pricing tiers.
- **Income source:** market rent (appraiser 1007/1025) or actual lease — per lender program.

### What Investors Use DSCR Refinances For

- **Cash-out refinance** to pull trapped equity out of existing doors and redeploy it (the refi leg of BRRRR).
- **Rate/term refinance** to lower the payment, improve cash flow, or move to a more stable structure.
- **Exiting bridge / hard-money / balloon debt** into a long-term (30-yr) DSCR loan before the short-term note comes due.
- **Refinancing self-employed / write-off-heavy** rentals that won't pass conventional refi DTI.
- **Short-term rental / Airbnb** refinances (some programs use STR revenue projections, e.g. AirDNA).
- **Moving a property into an entity** via the refinance for liability protection and tax structuring (route specifics to their CPA/attorney).

### Where It Helps Most

- Self-employed investors whose tax returns show heavy write-offs that sink a conventional refinance DTI.
- Investors who can't get a conventional refinance because of property count or income documentation.
- Investors stuck in **bridge / hard-money / balloon** debt who need to refinance into a long-term loan.
- STR operators and investors in secondary/tertiary cash-flow markets refinancing existing doors.
- Foreign nationals refinancing US rentals without domestic income/credit history (program-dependent).
- Investors who value **speed and low documentation friction** over the lowest possible rate.

### Quick Reality Checks

- DSCR rates run **higher** than conventional investment-property loans — the trade-off for no income docs and entity flexibility.
- The **property must cash-flow** (or come close) — a property that doesn't pencil won't qualify at most lenders.
- It is **business-purpose / investment** financing — not for a primary residence, and (for the Waiz line) **refinance only, not purchase.** See [DSCR Compliance Guardrails](dscr-compliance-guardrails.md).
- A refinance leaves equity in the property (cash-out caps near ~75% LTV); reserves are higher than a typical primary-residence loan.

### Myths and Debunks

**Qualification**

- *Myth: I need to show my income / tax returns.* No — qualification is based on the **property's rent vs. PITIA**, not personal income. (Confirm program docs.)
- *Myth: My write-offs disqualify me.* The opposite advantage of DSCR — heavy write-offs that hurt conventional DTI are irrelevant when the property cash-flows.
- *Myth: I'm capped at 10 properties.* DSCR programs generally don't impose the conventional financed-property cap.

**Cost & Structure**

- *Myth: The rate is the same as a primary-residence loan.* DSCR is investment, business-purpose financing and prices higher; weigh it against speed, doc-light underwriting, and freeing trapped equity.
- *Myth: I can cash out 95% of my equity.* Cash-out DSCR typically caps near **~75% LTV** — the refinance leaves equity in the property.
- *Myth: Interest-only is free money.* IO lowers the payment now but can create payment shock later; model it.

**Entity & Use**

- *Myth: I must close in my personal name.* Most DSCR programs **allow LLC/entity vesting** — common for liability and tax structuring (talk to your CPA/attorney).
- *Myth: I can use it for the house I live in.* No — owner-occupied is not eligible; DSCR is for **income-producing investment property** you already own.

**Process**

- *Myth: It's slower than conventional.* Doc-light underwriting often closes **faster**, which is why investors racing a balloon maturity or pulling equity quickly use it.
