# DSCR Setter / Appointment-Setting Call Script

> **DRAFT — REFINANCE ONLY · NUMBER-FREE.** Word-for-word language for a setter/intake agent booking
> DSCR **refinance** consultations on behalf of a loan-officer client. Investor (B2B-ish) tone — not the
> senior/relief RM tone. Anchored to [DSCR Offer And Funnel Map](dscr-offer-and-funnel-map.md); passes
> [DSCR Compliance Guardrails](dscr-compliance-guardrails.md).

## Purpose

Get a refi-ready investor **live-transferred to the LO** or **booked + confirmed** on the LO's calendar, with clean notes — without making them re-tell their story.

## Scope

Client-side investor calls only. Excludes acquisition setter scripts in `docs/acquisition/sales/`.

## When To Use

- Fresh lead from the funnel (speed-to-lead, day 1–2 priority)
- Re-dial on an unreached lead
- Confirmation call for a Laura-booked (AI) appointment
- Callback after "now isn't a good time"

> **Persona note.** Laura (AI) handles text/email booking. This phone setter is a **human agent**. If the
> business wants one consistent "Laura" assistant identity across channels, the same buffer-persona and
> AI-disclosure caveats in the [funnel map §5](dscr-offer-and-funnel-map.md) apply — **confirm with counsel.**

## The one job of this call

Live-transfer to the LO, or book + confirm. You are the LO's intake/assistant — fast, competent, investor-literate. You are **not** the loan officer and you do **not** quote numbers.

---

## Core operating frames (read before every shift)

1. **Self-ID as the assistant in the first 10 seconds** — or they dump the whole deal on you and resent repeating it to the LO.
2. **Match investor energy: quick, direct, competent.** These are busy operators. No small-talk drag, no hand-holding. Respect their time and they'll respect the call.
3. **Speak investor.** "Cash-out," "rate/term," "the property's cash flow," "in your LLC," "balloon." If you sound like you get it, you earn the transfer.
4. **Never quote numbers.** No rate, LTV, payment, or approval. "[LO] runs your exact numbers live — that's the call."
5. **Live transfer first, always.** Booking is the fallback.

---

## STAGE 1 — CONNECTION (first 10 seconds)

> Hey, [LEAD NAME]? — this is [AGENT NAME] from [LO NAME]'s office. You reached out about refinancing one of your rentals — got a quick minute?

📋 Notes:
- "From [LO NAME]'s office" — you call **on behalf of** the LO, not as the bank.
- Keep it brisk. Investors don't need the weather. One beat of rapport max, then into it.

## STAGE 2 — FRAME (the non-negotiable)

> Real quick so you know who you're talking to — I'm not the loan officer, I'm [LO NAME]'s assistant. They saw your DSCR refinance request come in and asked me to grab a couple quick details so they can look at what your property qualifies for before you two talk. Cool if I ask you like three quick things?

📋 Why: establishes you're not the decision-maker, tells them what's next (intro/transfer), and gives them permission to keep it short.

### If they ask "who's this with / what company?"

> I'm with [LO NAME]'s team — they're a DSCR/investor-financing specialist at [COMPANY]. You came in through our refinance inquiry. I'm just intake — I tee you up for [LO NAME].

---

## STAGE 3 — QUICK QUALIFICATION (refi-readiness — NOT underwriting)

Pull only from the canonical [refi-readiness questions](dscr-offer-and-funnel-map.md) — enough to brief the LO. 3–5 questions, max.

> Perfect. So — the property you're looking to refinance, you currently own it, right? … And is it a rental / producing income?

> Got it. And what are you trying to do — pull cash out, lower the payment, or get out of a short-term or balloon loan?

> Makes sense. Is it held in your name or an LLC? … And what state's the property in?

📋 Capture in their words. Don't interrupt. You're briefing the LO, not pre-approving anything.

### Hard disqualifiers (acknowledge, note, escalate — don't promise)

| Condition | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Doesn't currently **own** the property (it's a purchase) | Not a fit — this line is **refinance only**. "Got it — we focus on refinancing property you already own. Let me flag it and we'll follow up if that changes." |
| **Primary residence / personal use** (not investment) | Not business-purpose. Note, don't transfer, escalate to manager. |
| Property in a **state the LO isn't licensed/authorized** for | Note. Don't promise. Escalate — manager decides. |
| Owner-occupied / consumer-purpose signals | Stop qualification. Escalate. |

📋 **Never silently disqualify in the dialer.** Send every borderline lead to the manager so reporting stays clean.

---

## STAGE 4 — TRANSITION TO TRANSFER OR BOOK

### Soft pre-frame

> Awesome — that's exactly the kind of refinance [LO NAME] does all day. If you've got a sec, let me just hop you on a quick intro with them right now so you're not repeating all this — they can tell you what the property looks like on its income. Let me grab them.

### Live transfer first (always). If not possible:

> No problem — let me lock a time with [LO NAME]. They'll run your actual numbers live. What's better, later today or first thing tomorrow?

### Pull the appointment forward (every day out drops show rate)

- Today → tomorrow AM → tomorrow PM → 2 days → (last resort) 3+ days.
- "They've got a window at [TIME] today — want me to grab it before it's gone?"

### Book the invite NOW

> Cool — putting you in for [DATE/TIME]. You'll get a text and email with the invite in a second — do me a favor and confirm on your end so nobody double-books. And if a number you don't recognize calls you at [TIME], that's [LO NAME] — save it.

📋 Book in the CRM **while they're on the line**. Confirm they got the text + email before hanging up.

---

## STAGE 5 — WRAP-UP

> Perfect, you're set for [DATE/TIME]. I'll pass [LO NAME] the notes so they're ready to talk numbers when you connect. Anything changes, just text this number. Talk soon, [LEAD NAME].

---

## CALL VARIANT A — Laura-booked (AI) appointment confirmation

> Hey [LEAD NAME], [AGENT NAME] from [LO NAME]'s office — you've got a refinance review booked with [LO NAME] for [TIME] today. Just confirming you're good, and grabbing a couple notes so they can hit the ground running.

Then run Stage 3 (light), then try to pull it forward: "Any chance you've got time before [TIME]? They've got an earlier window — saves you the wait."

## CALL VARIANT B — Callback after "not a good time"

> Hey [LEAD NAME], [AGENT NAME] calling back from [LO NAME]'s office — you said to try you around [TIME]. Good news, [LO NAME]'s around right now with a quick window. Couple minutes to get you introduced?

---

## OBJECTION HANDLING (front-desk version — keep the call alive, hand off)

Deep loan objections are the LO's job — see [DSCR Objection-Handling Guide](dscr-objection-handling-guide.md). Your job is to keep it alive long enough to transfer/book.

**"What's the rate?"**
> Totally fair question — and honestly it depends on the property, so [LO NAME] runs your exact numbers live rather than me guessing. That's the whole point of the call. Got a few minutes today or tomorrow?

**"I'm just shopping / comparing lenders."**
> Smart — you should. Would it hurt to have [LO NAME] run your property too, so you've got a real comparison? Takes 15 minutes. What works?

**"Is this a refinance only? I wanted to buy."**
> Ah — we focus on refinancing property you already own, not purchases. If you've got a rental you want to refi, that's our wheelhouse. Do you?

**"Send me info by email."**
> Of course — I'll have [LO NAME] send something directly. But the fastest read on your specific property is a quick call — want me to grab 15 minutes so it's worth your time?

**"Where'd you get my number?"**
> You came in through our DSCR refinance inquiry — I've got you down looking to [GOAL] on a [PROPERTY TYPE]. Ring a bell?

**"Is this a scam?"**
> Hear you — lots of junk calls out there. I'm [AGENT NAME] from [LO NAME]'s office at [COMPANY]; I can text you their direct line and site so you can verify. Want me to?

---

## QUALITY BAR

- Self-ID as assistant + name the LO in the first 10 seconds; investor-paced.
- Frame set before any qualification.
- Max 3–5 refi-readiness questions; **no underwriting, no numbers quoted**.
- Live transfer attempted first on every qualified lead.
- Booking confirmed via text + email before hang-up.
- Every borderline/DQ lead escalated to manager, never silently killed.
- Refinance/investment framing only; purchase/primary-residence = escalate.

## METRICS

Live-transfer rate · book rate (unreached→reached) · show rate · pull-forward rate · AI-booked confirmation success · manager-escalation rate. See [DSCR KPI And Test Scorecard](dscr-kpi-and-test-scorecard.md).
