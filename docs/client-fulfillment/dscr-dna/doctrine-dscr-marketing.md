### What This Document Is

This is the operating doctrine for every piece of fulfillment work produced for a **DSCR / real-estate-investor** loan officer client. It establishes the market reality, investor psychology, the two frames, the personas, and the non-negotiable rules governing every ad, email, script, and piece of content. Read this before producing any output. Read it **alongside** [Doctrine DSCR](doctrine-dscr.md) (strategic) and [DSCR Compliance Guardrails](dscr-compliance-guardrails.md) (gates all copy).

### Section 1 — The Market Reality

**Demand already exists.** DSCR is one of the fastest-growing segments of US mortgage lending (≈30% of record non-QM securitization in 2025; growing into 2026). The investor knows they want leverage — so unlike RM, we are **not** doing de-stigmatization work. We are competing for the investor's trust on **competence and execution.**

**What this means for every output:** assume the reader is a numbers-literate operator who has likely seen "DSCR loan" before, already **owns** the property, is skeptical of generalist lenders, and cares most about *will this refinance close, on my property, on time, in my entity.* We start from "prove you understand investors" and build toward "book the conversation."

**The competitive reality (live ad intel).** A 2026-06 scrape of the Meta Ad Library
([DSCR Competitor Ad Intelligence](dscr-competitor-ad-intelligence.md)) shows the category has
collapsed into a **sea of sameness**: ~60% of active DSCR ads shout the same two things — "no income /
no tax returns" and "fast" — and ~76% of ads die within two weeks. The field is loud on **purchase**
and **feature lists**, and quiet on the things that actually move investors: **refinance pain**
(trapped equity, maturing hard-money/balloon), **proof** (funded outcomes / investor stories), and a
**low-friction first step**. That gap *is* our opening. We do not win by saying the same claim slightly
better — we win on a sharper **idea** ("the property qualifies itself"), the under-served **refinance /
cash-out / urgency** lanes, and **proof**. Our number-free posture is an advantage here, not a handicap:
the top human-led competitor wins on outcome framing, not quoted rates.

### Section 2 — The Two Frames

Every prospect approaches DSCR through one of two frames. Every output either builds the right one or reinforces the wrong one.

**The Commodity-Lender Frame — What We Work Against:** "This is just another loan officer who'll fumble my entity, won't credit my STR income, will bury me in doc requests, and will blow my closing timeline." This is the default skepticism of a burned operator.

**The Investor-Partner Frame — What We Build:** "This team actually understands how investors operate — DSCR math, LLCs, STR revenue, cash-out/BRRRR, hard-money/balloon refinances — and they close fast and reliably." This is the frame that converts.

| Commodity-Lender Frame (against) | Investor-Partner Frame (building) |
|---|---|
| Generic "we do loans" | Investor-specialist who speaks the language |
| Income-doc underwriting | Qualifies on the property's cash flow |
| Slow, uncertain closings | Fast, reliable, deadline-aware execution |
| Doesn't get entities/STR/cash-out refi | Built for entities, STR income, refi timelines |
| Sells rate | Sells equity unlocked, a better payment, and certainty |

**Critical Rule — Credibility Before Conversion.** An investor won't book with a lender they suspect is a generalist, no matter the rate. Prove competence (mechanism, specificity, real outcomes) before asking for the conversion.

### Section 3 — Investor Psychology

**The Core Drivers** (present across personas; weight varies):
- **Velocity of capital** — pull trapped equity out and redeploy; don't let money sit idle.
- **Better position** — a lower/more stable payment, or an exit from hard-money/balloon debt.
- **Speed & certainty** — beat a balloon/maturity deadline; refinance closings that don't slip.
- **Control & optionality** — entity vesting, structure, redeploy equity.

**The Competence Filter.** Investors filter every lender through one question: *do you actually get how I operate?* The mechanism that passes the filter is **specific, mechanism-forward proof** — DSCR math, real funded outcomes, fluency in entities/STR/cash-out refi — not reassurance.

**How decisions are made.** Analytically and fast. Investors run the numbers, compare lenders, and move quickly on whoever demonstrates competence and can close. Trust is built through demonstrated expertise and social proof from other investors, not emotional nurture.

**Beliefs we work around:**
- "Most lenders don't understand investors."
- "The rate is higher, so it has to be worth it in speed and freed-up equity."
- "If they can't talk DSCR/entity/refinance fluently, they'll fumble my deal."
- "Show me it closed for someone like me."

### Section 4 — The Personas

**One persona per campaign. Always.** Full profiles + objections: [Intelligence ICP DSCR](intelligence-icp-dscr.md).

| Persona | Core Driver | Primary Friction | Conversion Language | Frame |
|---------|-------------|------------------|---------------------|-------|
| Portfolio Scaler | Capital velocity | Trapped equity; can't cash-out refi conventionally | "No property-count ceiling," "cash out on each property" | Equity engine |
| Self-Employed / Write-Off | Frustration w/ DTI | Write-offs sink a conventional refi DTI | "Refinance on the property, not your tax returns" | Vindication |
| STR / Airbnb Operator | Yield per door | STR income not credited | "Refinance on what it actually earns" | Revenue-based |
| Bridge / Hard-Money Refinancer | Certainty & speed | Short-term note maturing; conventional too slow | "Exit into a 30-year DSCR before the balloon hits" | Reliability |
| Foreign National* | Access to US RE | No US income/credit | "Refinance on the property, in an entity" | Access |

\*Program-dependent — confirm lender eligibility.

### Section 5 — The Operating Rules

These apply to every output. Non-negotiable.

**Rule 1 — Business-Purpose, Refinance-Only Framing.** Always market refinances of investment/income-producing property the investor already owns. Never primary-residence, personal/household use, or purchase financing. (Compliance foundation — see guardrails.)

**Rule 2 — Lead with Outcome, Then Name DSCR.** Open with the investor outcome (equity unlocked, a better payment, qualify on the property, close fast), then name the product. **Unlike RM, naming "DSCR" is encouraged** — investors search and trust it.

**Rule 3 — Competence Over Reassurance.** Prove investor expertise with specific mechanism and real outcomes. Never the senior/relief/fear tone from RM.

**Rule 4 — One Persona Per Output.** Write to one operator's friction, language, and frame.

**Rule 5 — Speed/Certainty Is the Offer, Compliantly.** Foreground reliable, fast closing — but never *guarantee* approval, rate, or timeline. "In many cases," "may qualify."

**Rule 6 — No Invented Pricing, No Guarantees, No Advice.** Approved pricing sheet only, framed illustrative; no guarantees; route entity/tax/depreciation questions to a CPA/attorney; respect licensed-state geography and platform ad policy (Meta Special Ad Category).

**The Language Check — run before every output:** Does it lead with an investor outcome (then name DSCR)? Does it build the Investor-Partner Frame, not the Commodity-Lender Frame? Does it speak to one persona? Does it prove competence rather than reassure? Is it business-purpose, compliant, guarantee-free, and free of invented pricing? If any answer is no, the output is not ready.

### Section 6 — Language Reference

| Always Use | Never Use |
|------------|-----------|
| Qualified investor conversations | Leads (commodity volume) |
| Acquisition system / engine | Lead-gen service |
| Real-estate investors / operators | Seniors / homeowners / consumers framing |
| Refinance / cash out / lower your payment | Buy / purchase / acquisition framing (refinance only) |
| Qualify on the property's cash flow | Qualify on your income (it's the opposite point) |
| DSCR loan (name it) | Vague "funding" when DSCR is the point |
| Investment / income-producing property | Primary-residence / personal-use framing |
| May qualify / in many cases / subject to underwriting | Guaranteed approval / you qualify / locked rate |
| Close fast / reliable timeline | "Instant approval" / fabricated countdowns |
| Investor-specialist, operator-to-operator tone | Hand-holding, fear-based, or relief tone |
