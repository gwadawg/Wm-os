## Purpose

Expand a proven ICP angle into a full ad set. Hold stage logic + program-fact
tokens so numbers stay evergreen per client. New angles invented in testing
don't need a writeup here until they win.

## Scope

Meta primary (Special Ad Category); reusable on Google/YouTube. Adapt to
client voice — don't run verbatim.

## Trigger

Human expands a chosen ICP angle, or fills MOF/BOF tokens from a client's
approved pricing sheet.

## Operating Content

### Stage logic (TOF / MOF / BOF)

| Stage | Job | Numbers? |
|-------|-----|----------|
| **TOF idea** | Stop scroll with an **idea or pain** | Prefer **no** — filters optional later |
| **Filter / spec card** | Self-qualify junk out (loan range, FICO, LTV, APR, appraisal) | **Yes** — confirmed tokens only |
| **MOF** | Competence: mechanism + proof | Soft tokens / substantiated proof |
| **BOF** | Qualification stack + low-friction CTA | **Yes** — tokenized program facts |

### Lead-filter token sheet (confirm before creative)

**Waiz working defaults for DSCR filter copy** — overwrite per client /
lender program every refresh. Do not ship numbered creatives until this
row is filled and checked.

| Token | Confirm default | Use in copy | Notes |
|-------|-----------------|-------------|-------|
| `[NO_APPRAISAL]` | Yes on **qualifying products only** | "No appraisal on qualifying products" | Never imply every DSCR loan; omit if program doesn't allow |
| `[MIN_LOAN]` | $75,000 | "Financing from [MIN_LOAN]" | Pair with max |
| `[MAX_LOAN]` | $5,000,000 | "… to [MAX_LOAN]" | |
| `[FICO_FLOOR]` | 660 | "FICO [FICO_FLOOR]+" / "as low as …" | Never "you qualify at" |
| `[APR]` | **Must confirm — changes** | "From [APR] APR" only if client approves rate-in-ads | Highest FTC MAP risk |
| `[MAX_LTV]` | 80% | "Up to [MAX_LTV] LTV" | Program-dependent (cash-out vs rate/term may differ) |

**Other tokens (as needed):**

| Token | Framing when filled |
|-------|---------------------|
| `[MAX_LTV_CASHOUT]` / `[MAX_LTV_RT]` | Split LTV if cash-out ≠ rate/term |
| `[MIN_DSCR]` | "Properties cash-flowing at …+ may qualify" |
| `[CLOSE_RANGE]` | "Many close in …" — never guaranteed |
| `[STATE]` | Licensed-state list only |
| `[PROOF_OUTCOME]` | Real, documented, representative funded outcome |

**Hard rules:** never invent tokens from memory. Soften with "as low as /
up to / from / may qualify." Standing disclosure where licensing requires.
Re-verify `[APR]` and `[NO_APPRAISAL]` every refresh.

### Angle 1 — The Idle-Equity Tax · Portfolio Scaler · TOF

**Big idea:** Leaving equity parked isn't "playing it safe" — it's an expense.
Every month it sits, it costs the next deal. Loss framing attacks the
status-quo bias that keeps investors from acting.

**Angle mode:** [Behavioral modeling / equity velocity](intelligence-icp-dscr.md)
— reveal how scaling investors already operate; break the "leave equity alone"
belief; sell identity and habit before naming DSCR.

**Hooks:**

1. Your equity is just sitting there. Put it to work.
2. The most expensive thing you own is the equity you're not using.
3. You don't have to sell a door to get your capital back.
4. Real investors don't wait for capital. They take it from what they already own.
5. If you already have a portfolio, you've done the hardest part.

**Primary text:**

> Every month your equity sits locked inside doors you already own, it quietly costs you something — the down payment on the next deal, the rehab you keep postponing, the capital that should be compounding.
>
> A DSCR cash-out refinance pulls it out and puts it back to work. The property qualifies on its own rent — not your W-2, not your tax returns, not your DTI. No property-count ceiling, and you can typically close in your LLC.
>
> See what the doors you already own may qualify for.

**Body + CTA:** Cash-out refinance, qualified on the property's cash flow. → **See If Your Property Qualifies**

**Visual:** Row of rental doors, one rendered as a locked vault; or "equity sitting still vs. equity moving" two-panel graphic. Operator energy, no consumer/family imagery.

**BOF variant (tokenized):** Cash out up to `[MAX_LTV_CASHOUT]` — FICO as low as `[FICO_FLOOR]`, no tax returns, close in your LLC, many close in `[CLOSE_RANGE]`. + standing disclosure.

### Angle 2 — The Deadline · Hard-Money / Balloon Refinancer · TOF

**Big idea:** The only DSCR lane where urgency is real, not manufactured — compliant *and* scroll-stopping. Voice: an operator calmly mapping an exit, never panic.

**Hooks:**

1. Balloon coming due? Refinance before it hits.
2. Hard money got you in. It was never the plan to stay.
3. The worst time to refinance a balloon is the month it matures.

**Primary text:**

> That bridge loan did exactly what it was supposed to do — got you the property, funded the rehab, got it rented. But it has a date on it, and every month past stabilization you're paying short-term rates on a long-term asset.
>
> A DSCR refinance is the standard exit: a long-term loan that qualifies on the property's rent, not your income. No W-2s, no tax returns — and you map the closing against your note's maturity, not after it.
>
> Don't price your exit the week the balloon hits.

**Body + CTA:** Exit hard-money into a 30-year DSCR loan, qualified on the rent. → **Map Your Exit**

**Visual:** Calendar with a circled maturity date beside a property file; or "short-term rate vs. long-term loan" timeline. Calm and deadline-aware — never alarmist red-warning aesthetics.

**BOF variant (tokenized):** Still on hard money? FICO as low as `[FICO_FLOOR]`, property cash-flowing at `[MIN_DSCR]`+, many close in `[CLOSE_RANGE]` — mapped against your maturity date. + disclosure.

### Angle 3 — "Did You Know?" / Program Call-Out · Self-Employed / Write-Off · TOF

**Big idea:** Most investors literally don't know this program exists for them. The hook is the reveal itself — curiosity plus the "wait, that's me" call-out. Exclusivity reads as *specific access* ("a program built for investors like you"); vague "exclusive offer" language is what burned operators scroll past.

**Hook family A — "Did you know?":**

1. Did you know there's a loan program for real estate investors that doesn't look at your income — at all?
2. Did you know you can refinance a rental with zero tax returns? Most investors don't.
3. Did you know your rental can qualify for a refinance all by itself — no W-2, no 1040, no DTI?

**Hook family B — ICP call-out + program:**

4. Real estate investors: there's a refinance program where your income docs stay in the drawer.
5. If you own rental property, there's a program that qualifies the property — not you.
6. Attention rental owners: the program most lenders never mention — no tax returns, no income verification.

**Primary text (pairs with either family):**

> There's a loan program built specifically for real estate investors — and most have never been offered it.
>
> It's called a DSCR refinance, and it works the opposite of every mortgage you've ever applied for: no tax returns, no W-2s, no pay stubs, no personal DTI. The property's rent does the qualifying. If the rental covers its own payment, it may qualify — even if your write-offs make your 1040 look tiny.
>
> Cash-out or rate/term, on rentals you already own, typically closeable in your LLC.
>
> Most investors find out about this program two denials too late. Now you know.

**Body + CTA:** A refinance program where the property qualifies — not your paperwork. → **See If Your Rental Qualifies**

**Visual:** Big native-text "DID YOU KNOW?" card, or direct-to-camera LO opening with the question (first 3 seconds of a talking-head video). Call-out family: bold "REAL ESTATE INVESTORS:" eyebrow line over a rental backdrop.

**Compliance note:** avoid the literal word "exclusive" paired with unavailability claims (DSCR is widely offered). "A program most investors have never been offered" is the safe construction. "May qualify" preserved.

### Angle 4 — The Checklist · Warm / Retarget · BOF

**Big idea:** While the category hides the ball behind "book a call," we publish the qualification stack up front. Specificity reads as competence; the numbers self-filter lead quality upward. This is the campaign's dedicated BOF engine.

**TOF teaser (number-free, optional):**

> Here's exactly what it takes to refinance a rental. No mystery, no "book a call to find out."
>
> Most lenders make you sit through a pitch before telling you whether you'd even qualify. We'd rather just show you the checklist. Qualify on the property's rent — see where you stand in two minutes.
>
> CTA: **See the Checklist**

**BOF / filter-spec core asset (tokenized — confirm sheet first):**

> **See if your rental qualifies — the stack, up front:**
> ✅ FICO `[FICO_FLOOR]`+
> ✅ Financing `[MIN_LOAN]`–`[MAX_LOAN]`
> ✅ Up to `[MAX_LTV]` LTV
> ✅ `[NO_APPRAISAL]` *(qualifying products only — omit line if not offered)*
> ✅ From `[APR]` APR *(omit entire line if rate-in-ads not approved)*
> ✅ No tax returns / W-2s — qualify on the rent
> ✅ Close in your LLC
> *Program-dependent; subject to underwriting and property qualification. Business-purpose investment financing — non-owner-occupied property only.*
>
> CTA: **Check Your Property in 2 Minutes — No Credit Pull, No Obligation**

**Visual:** Clean checklist / rate-card, single accent, zero stock photography.
Specificity *is* the creative — and the junk-lead filter.

### Angle 5 — "What You Could Do With It" · Equity-Holding Investor · TOF/MOF

**Big idea:** Stop selling the mechanism, sell the *uses*. Call out the
investor, then paint the concrete moves this refinance unlocks. Outcome
vividness + the velocity-of-capital driver.

**Angle mode:** same
[behavioral modeling](intelligence-icp-dscr.md) lane as Angle 1 —
peer strategy reveal ("here's how operators scale") rather than loan pitch.
Ego stroke for portfolio owners; aspiration for smaller landlords who already
own doors.

**Hooks:**

1. Real estate investors: here's what a DSCR cash-out refinance actually lets you do.
2. If you own rentals, this one refinance can fund your next three moves.
3. Your rental already earned you this — most investors just never collect it.
4. Real investors don't wait for capital. They take it from what they already own.
5. If you already have a portfolio, you've done the hardest part.

**Primary text:**

> If you own rental property, a DSCR cash-out refinance is one of the most flexible tools in the game — because the cash that comes out is yours to deploy. Investors use it to:
>
> 🔑 Pay off a hard-money or balloon note before it matures
> 🔨 Fund the rehab on the next door — without touching savings
> 📊 Build the reserves that let you move fast when a deal appears
> 🔁 Free up capital for the next project instead of waiting years on rent alone
> 🏠 Replace a high-rate loan and improve monthly cash flow
>
> And the qualifying? The property handles it. Rent covers the payment — no tax returns, no W-2s, no DTI. Typically closeable in your LLC.
>
> The equity is already there. The question is what you'd do with it.

**Body + CTA:** Cash-out refinance qualified on the property's rent — your capital, back in motion. → **See What Your Property Qualifies For**

**Visual:** Carousel — one use-case per card, CTA on the closing card. Static: a "1 refinance → 5 moves" branching graphic. Video: LO ticking through the uses on fingers, direct to camera.

**STR variant:** swap hook 3 for "Airbnb hosts: your bookings can fund your next move," and lead the use-case list with "refinance on what the STR actually earns, then redeploy the equity."

**Compliance note:** use-of-proceeds list stays business-purpose (rehab, reserves, note payoff, next project — never personal/household uses like tuition or debt consolidation). "Fund the next project" is proceeds-use, not purchase-loan marketing; the loan itself is always framed as a refinance of property already owned.
