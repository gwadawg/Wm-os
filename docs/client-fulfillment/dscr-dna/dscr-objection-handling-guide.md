# DSCR Objection-Handling Guide

> **DRAFT — REFINANCE ONLY · NUMBER-FREE IN COPY.** Conversational word tracks for the loan officer (and
> setter) on the DSCR refinance consultation. Expands the objections in
> [Intelligence ICP DSCR §7](intelligence-icp-dscr.md). The LO may discuss the borrower's **own** real numbers
> live; **marketing/scripts never assert pricing**. Passes [DSCR Compliance Guardrails](dscr-compliance-guardrails.md).

## Purpose

Give the LO/closer calm, investor-credible responses to the objections that actually surface on DSCR refinance calls — without guarantees, invented pricing, or tax/legal advice.

## Scope

The sales conversation (consultation/close). For front-desk/booking objections, see the [setter script](dscr-setter-appointment-script.md).

## How to use

- **Validate → reframe → route to the property's numbers.** Investors decide on math, not reassurance.
- **Number rules:** the LO may walk the **borrower's own** scenario live (their rents, their balance) and current lender guidelines. Never *guarantee* a rate/approval/timeline; use "in many cases," "subject to underwriting." Never put pricing in marketing or written scripts.
- **Route tax/legal** (entity vesting, depreciation, 1031) to the borrower's CPA/attorney — every time.

## Operating Content

### The 10 core objections (word tracks)

**1. "The rate's higher than conventional."**
- *Concern:* cost vs. alternatives.
- *Reframe:* "It is — that's the trade for qualifying on the property instead of your income, and closing in your entity. The real question isn't the rate in a vacuum, it's what your trapped equity (or that maturing balloon) is costing you while you wait. Let's run your actual property and compare." → route to their numbers.
- *Compliance:* no guaranteed rate; compare on their real scenario.

**2. "Will it close before my balloon is due?"**
- *Concern:* a blown refi at maturity.
- *Reframe:* "DSCR is doc-light by design — we're underwriting the property, not your whole financial life, so the timeline is usually tighter than a conventional refi. I can't promise a date, but in many cases this is exactly the move people make to beat a maturity. Let's look at your timeline today." 
- *Compliance:* "in many cases," never a guaranteed close date.

**3. "Do I have to show income / tax returns?"**
- *Concern:* doc fatigue.
- *Reframe:* "No — that's the whole point of DSCR. We qualify on the property's rent versus its payment, not your W-2, pay stubs, or returns. Your tax strategy stays your business."

**4. "Does my property even qualify?"**
- *Concern:* DSCR uncertainty.
- *Reframe:* "Easy to check — it comes down to whether the rent covers the property's payment. Give me your rough rents and current loan and I'll show you where you land." → walk their real ratio live (no invented benchmarks in writing).

**5. "Can I refinance in my LLC?"**
- *Concern:* liability/structure.
- *Reframe:* "In most programs, yes — entity vesting is normal for investors. The specific tax and structuring side, though, I'll have you confirm with your CPA/attorney so it's set up right for you."
- *Compliance:* route structuring to CPA/attorney.

**6. "Conventional won't refinance me / I hit my property cap."**
- *Concern:* blocked refi.
- *Reframe:* "Right — that's a conventional limitation, not a DSCR one. We qualify each property on its own income, and DSCR generally doesn't have that financed-property cap. The door conventional closed is the one we open."

**7. "How much equity do I need to leave in?"**
- *Concern:* capital efficiency.
- *Reframe:* "Cash-out leaves some equity in the property — exactly how much depends on the property and program. Let's run yours and I'll show you what you could pull and what stays in." → their numbers, no fixed figure in writing.

**8. "I'm a foreign national."**
- *Concern:* eligibility.
- *Reframe:* "Some programs are built for foreign-national investors refinancing US rentals — qualifying on the property, often in an entity. It's program-dependent, so let me confirm what fits your situation."
- *Compliance:* program-dependent — confirm, don't promise.

**9. "Interest-only sounds risky."**
- *Concern:* payment shock later.
- *Reframe:* "IO lowers the payment now and maximizes cash flow, but you're right to think ahead — let's model the reset so you go in clear-eyed. It's a tool, not a trap, when you know the timeline."
- *Compliance:* transparent on resets; no overpromising.

**10. "How are you different from my bank?"**
- *Concern:* lender competence.
- *Reframe:* "Your bank underwrites you. We underwrite the property. We do DSCR, entities, STR income, and refi speed all day — so you're not explaining how investors work to your loan officer. That's the difference."

### Universal guardrails (every objection)

- No guarantees of approval, rate, LTV, amount, or timeline — "may qualify," "in many cases," "subject to underwriting."
- No tax/legal/financial advice — route to CPA/attorney.
- Refinance/investment framing only — never purchase or primary-residence.
- Numbers discussed live are the **borrower's own**; nothing fabricated, nothing put into marketing.
