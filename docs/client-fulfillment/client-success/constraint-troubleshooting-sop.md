## Golden Rules
Start at Layer 1. Work downstream. Never blame a downstream layer without clearing those above it.

Check external factors before touching anything.

If CQPCONV is healthy, do not chase CPL. The campaign is working.

Every action must be logged in ClickUp before it is executed.

Any GHL change requires Founder approval. No exceptions.

Most problems trace back to the offer or the creative. Start there.

## CHECK FIRST: External Factors
Before touching any campaign setting, creative, or sequence, run through this checklist. Many performance dips are temporary and caused by conditions outside the funnel. Acting on a campaign during an external disruption wastes time and can break things that were working.

⚠️  If ANY external factor is confirmed or suspected — observe for 48–72 hours before making changes. Document the factor in ClickUp and note it on the weekly account review.

## Environment & Timing
Is there a US federal holiday this week? (Christmas, Thanksgiving, Memorial Day, July 4th, Labor Day) — seniors dramatically reduce phone engagement during these windows.

Is there a major national event impacting ad performance? (Super Bowl Sunday, election day, major news cycle) — Meta CPMs spike and audience attention shifts.

Is the weather severely disrupted in the client's geography? Extreme weather reduces engagement and appointment attendance.

Has Meta released a recent platform update affecting ad delivery or tracking? (iOS privacy changes, pixel updates, policy changes)

## Client-Side Factors
Is the client's calendar fully open with at least 4+ slots available in the next 10 days? If slots are limited, leads physically cannot book — this looks like a booking problem but isn't.

Has the LO made any recent changes to their GHL pipeline, calendar settings, or CRM without notifying the team?

Does the client have an active Google My Business listing? If it is suspended or has a sudden influx of bad reviews, trust collapses and show rates tank.

Does the client have a functional, professional website? Seniors 62+ will Google the LO before their appointment. No website or a bad one = no-shows.

Is there a reputation issue? (bad reviews, no online presence, no professional photo) — run a quick Google search on the client's name and business.

Are there any known technical issues in GHL? (refer to the Technical Issues SOP before diagnosing any funnel metric)

## 🧠  The Offer Is Usually the Problem
Before exhausting every technical checklist below, ask one honest question: Is the offer compelling enough for a 62+ homeowner who is skeptical of financial products and has been conditioned to distrust the phrase 'reverse mortgage'? If the answer is uncertain, the offer is the first thing to fix.

⚠️  GOLDEN RULE: If CQPCONV is under $149 — do not touch the ad campaign. The leads are converting downstream. A higher CPL with a healthy CQPCONV means the campaign is self-selecting quality. Leave it alone.

If CPL / CPQL is high from campaign launch:

## Is the campaign set up correctly? (targeting radius, placements, pixel firing, calendar URL)
Verify radius covers a realistic service area. For reverse mortgage, targeting too narrow in a low-density area starves the algorithm of data.

Confirm the pixel is firing on the thank-you / confirmation page, not just the landing page.

Is the landing page live, loading correctly on mobile, and routing leads into GHL?

Test the full funnel on mobile: click ad → land on page → submit form → confirm lead appears in GHL pipeline.

Are we running at least 6–8 creatives covering different formats (video + image) and different archetype angles?

Each of the four ICP archetypes (Security-Seeker, Strategic Retiree, Financially Squeezed, Legacy Planner) should have at least one dedicated creative angle in the active set.

## Is the ad messaging consistent with the landing page headline? (message match)
The landing page headline must directly continue the promise made in the ad. Mismatch = people click then bounce instantly.

Is the daily budget at least $50/day? Is there a Meta spending cap on the account?

Is the audience size large enough? (too small = Meta charges premium CPMs and can't optimize)

If CPL / CPQL was healthy but has risen after 30+ days:

Have any recent changes been made to the campaign (targeting, budget, creative swaps)?

YES — Revert changes and monitor for 48 hours before diagnosing further.

Has ad frequency exceeded 3.0? (audience fatigue — the most common cause of rising CPL after a stable period)

Launch 3–5 new creatives immediately. Prioritize a new hook and new archetype angle. Do not just re-skin the same visual.

Has the winning creative been tested with variations? (different hook, different opening frame, different archetype)

Have both video AND image formats been tested against each other?

Has the offer angle been tested? (outcome-first framing, different pain point, different ICP archetype)

Creative Diagnostic — Are Ads Underperforming Because of the Content?

Does the ad lead with an emotional outcome — not the product name?

'Reverse mortgage' in the first line = conditioned rejection before the value lands. Lead with what it does: 'Eliminate your mortgage payment' / 'Stay in your home without financial stress'

Does the creative speak to ONE specific archetype, not a generic 'seniors 62+' audience?

Does the video hook stop the scroll within the first 3 seconds?

Is the ad tone calm, educational, and trustworthy — not sales-heavy or urgent?

Urgency language ('act now', 'limited time') actively harms performance in this space. It confirms the prospect's suspicion they're being sold to.

## 🔑  If all levers have been pulled and CPL remains above KPI
Check the offer itself. Is the consultation framed compellingly? Is the landing page copy speaking to the right 'before state' pain? Is the ICP archetype in the ad matched to the landing page copy? Mismatched archetype-to-page is one of the most common hidden causes of persistent high CPL.

The landing page is the most overlooked constraint in the funnel. High CTR + high CPC + high CPL almost always points here. The opt-in rate benchmarks are intentionally conservative because Waiz funnels use qualification friction by design — but friction can cross the line into obstruction.

If Opt-In Rate is below KPI (CTR is healthy but CPL is high):

Does the landing page headline directly match the ad's promise?

This is the single highest-leverage fix. A prospect who clicks on 'eliminate your mortgage payment' and lands on a page that says 'learn about reverse mortgages' will bounce immediately.

Is the page loading in under 3 seconds on mobile?

62+ demographic is less tolerant of slow-loading pages. Check GTmetrix or PageSpeed Insights. Slow load = immediate bounce.

If Lead-to-Qual % is below KPI (opt-in rate is fine but leads don't qualify):

Pull the disqualification reasons from GHL/ClickUp. What is the most common reason leads are failing qualification?

Common patterns: wrong age (under 62), renting (not a homeowner), no meaningful home equity, already has a reverse mortgage, not interested (misunderstood the ad).

## Are qualifying questions present on the landing page? (homeownership, age 62+, approximate equity)
Is the ICP archetype in the ad attracting the wrong segment? (e.g., curiosity-seekers vs. motivated homeowners in financial stress)

## 🔀  Diagnose the Split First
Before working through the checklists below, identify whether the constraint is a CONTACT problem (people aren't picking up) or a CONVERSION problem (people pick up but don't book). The fixes are completely different. Low contact rate = operational/technical fix. Low booking rate with healthy contact rate = script/conversation fix.

## Section 3A: Contact Rate is Below KPI (People aren't picking up)
Is the outbound phone number spam-flagged?

Check Hot Prospector number health. If flagged, rotate to a new number in the correct local area code immediately. A spam-flagged number is the #1 cause of contact rate collapse.

How long has this number been in use? (6+ months of heavy volume = rotate proactively)

Is the first contact attempt happening within 5 minutes of form submission?

Speed-to-lead is the single most controllable variable in contact rate. A lead contacted within 5 minutes is exponentially more likely to pick up than one contacted after 30 minutes. This is non-negotiable.

Is the dial cadence running correctly? (at least one attempt per day for 5 days)

Is the AI follow-up sequence (text + email) running alongside the voice dials?

## Are dials going out during optimal contact windows? (8–10am and 4–6pm prospect local time)
Is the phone number in the correct local area code for the prospect?

## Section 3B: Booking Rate is Below KPI (People pick up but don't book)
If contact rate is healthy but booking rate is not, this is a conversation quality problem — not a dialing problem. Pull call recordings before acting.

Pull the last 10–15 call recordings for this client. Feed them into Claude/ChatGPT with the prompt: 'What are the common patterns in why leads aren't booking an appointment?'

Are SDRs leading with the product name ('reverse mortgage') in the opening?

The opening must frame the outcome, not the product. 'I'm calling about your interest in accessing your home equity without monthly payments' — not 'calling about the reverse mortgage you inquired about.'

Are SDRs trying to educate the lead on the phone instead of booking the appointment?

The call center's only job is to get the appointment on the calendar. Education happens on the LO consultation call. If SDRs are explaining the product, they are losing the booking.

Are SDRs handling the top objections correctly? ('I need to think about it' / 'I'm not interested' / 'the bank takes your house')

These are reflexive responses driven by the Debt Frame — not real objections. They require reframing, not answering. Review objection handling scripts.

Is the AI bot (between SDR contacts) warming or cooling the lead?

Review AI message sequences — are they educational and trust-building, or are they leading with 'reverse mortgage' and triggering rejection?

Does the LO's calendar have at least 4 days open with multiple slots in the next 10 days?

No available slots = leads cannot book = booking rate collapses. This is the most frequently missed cause of booking rate drops.

Are disqualified leads being properly tagged and moved out of the active dial pool?

## ⚠️  If booking rate remains below KPI after script and cadence review
Pull a sample of disqualified leads and audit the qualifying reasons. If a high proportion of leads are fundamentally unqualified (wrong age, renting, no equity), the constraint has moved upstream to the ad layer — fix targeting and creative before expecting call center improvement.

Show rate is where booked appointments are lost between the calendar and the call. Every percentage point improvement here translates directly into more conversations and more closed loans with zero additional ad spend. This is the highest-leverage layer for LO performance.

## Reminder Sequence Audit — Check These First
Are all four automated reminder touchpoints firing correctly in GHL?

Required sequence: Immediate confirmation → 24-hour reminder → 4-hour reminder → 30-minute reminder. Pull a test lead through the sequence and verify every touchpoint.

Are reminder messages personalized with the LO's name and photo?

Generic reminder messages from unknown senders are ignored. The 62+ demographic needs to see the face and name they expect to speak with.

Is the AI bot correctly turning OFF after appointment booking? (per SOP, AI should stop; drip campaigns take over)

If the AI is still messaging after booking, it can confuse the lead or contradict the appointment context.

Booking Quality Audit — Are the Right Appointments Being Booked?

Are appointments being booked more than 7 days out?

The further out an appointment is booked, the more likely it is to no-show. Push for next-3-day availability wherever possible. Work with the LO to open more near-term calendar slots.

Does the lead clearly understand what the appointment is? (free no-obligation consultation with the LO, not a sales call)

Misalignment between what was promised on the SDR call and what the appointment actually is drives no-shows. Review the SDR booking script.

Is a commitment question present on the booking confirmation page?

Add: 'This is a no-cost, no-obligation consultation. Are you committed to attending at the time you selected?' with a hard YES/NO gate. It sounds small — it reduces no-shows.

No-Show Recovery — Is the Re-Engagement System Working?

Are no-shows being dispositioned in GHL immediately after the missed appointment time?

A no-show that isn't dispositioned doesn't trigger the re-engagement sequence. Every hour of delay = lower probability of getting the lead back on the calendar.

Is the re-engagement sequence firing for no-shows? (automated text + email + SDR callback within 30 minutes)

Is the LO reviewing lead notes before each appointment?

An unprepared LO = a cold opening conversation = the lead feels like they're talking to someone who doesn't know them. This drives last-minute cancellations and silent no-shows.

## 📋  Reporting Integrity Check
Before concluding show rate is a systemic problem, verify that show/no-show dispositions are being logged correctly and consistently. Incorrect or delayed dispositioning produces false show rate data and delays re-engagement sequences — making the problem look worse than it is.

Waiz Media does not control close rate — the LO does. But a persistently low close rate requires investigation before concluding it is purely a sales problem. Always verify lead quality first.

## Step 1: Verify It Is Not a Lead Quality Problem
Pull the last 10 attended appointments. Review the SDR qualification notes. Are the leads matching the ICP profile?

## Is Lead-to-Qual % at KPI? (50%+ means the funnel is delivering qualified leads)
Are the disqualification notes in GHL showing a pattern of unqualified leads slipping through to appointments?

If lead quality is confirmed as sound, proceed to Step 2.

## Step 2: Diagnose the LO's Consultation Process
Has the LO completed Module 3 (Waiz Sales Call training)?

Is the LO reviewing lead notes and SDR call summary before each appointment?

Coming into a consultation cold — not knowing the lead's archetype, pain point, or qualifying details — is the most common cause of low close rates for LOs who are otherwise competent.

Is the LO leading the consultation with education and trust-building, or jumping to product features?

The consultation must establish the Retirement Tool Frame before introducing the product. Leading with features into a prospect still in the Debt Frame = instant resistance.

Is the LO addressing the top objections correctly?

Top objections to train on: 'The bank will take my house' / 'My kids will inherit debt' / 'I need to think about it' / 'This sounds too good to be true.' Refer the LO to the ICP Objection Handling Guide (Section 7 of the ICP document).

Is the LO asking for a next step / commitment at the end of the consultation?

## Step 3: Check CQPCONV as the Full-Funnel Verdict
Even if close rate is below target, check CQPCONV. If CQPCONV is under $149, the funnel is working — the LO may simply need more volume to normalize the conversion rate.

If CQPCONV is above $225 AND close rate is below 10%, the funnel has a multi-layer problem. Work backward through Sections 1–4 before isolating close rate as the cause.
