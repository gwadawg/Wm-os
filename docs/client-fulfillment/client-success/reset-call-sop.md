The Reset Call

The Reset Call

Introduction

This SOP breaks down how to conduct reset calls with clients who are misaligned with our company expectations.

Red Flags To follow the ''Reset Process''

The client doesn't pay their invoices on time

The client always asks for more work than what they've paid for

The client just flat out doesn't respect us and they treat us like sh*t

The Reset Call

GOAL: To realign expectations with the client and understand the tension they are experiencing in either allowing us to have a healthier relationship, or ending the engagement

Part 1: Message Client

Hey ''NAME'' thanks for your message. I'd like to get on a call ASAP with you to find the best solution for you, this problem and moving forward.

Part 2: Clear Your Mind BEFORE The Call

Get out of your head + emotion

(Block off time on the calendar beforehand to go outside, get some air, workout, and remove all emotion)

Part 3: Preperation

Before the call, write out explicitly the ways in which the client has been unruly and/or broken boundaries. These will be used for re-alignment during the call together.

Examples:

Messaging after hours and expecting responses

Disrespectful to me / the team

Changing direction with projects consistently

Part 4: The Call

Intro:

Hey [NAME], thanks for joining this call. I'd like to jump straight into things. Is that alright with you?

(Client Responds)

Thanks, I feel like your expectations for this relationship are misaligned, and whether thats us completing this project / month of service and parting ways, or realigning expectations for our work together, I'd like to figure out together what's best for you.

Hear the client out:

To get things started, I want to understand the tension you're experiencing, what's going on? (or be explicit based on circumstances)

(Client Responds)

(Listen and accept blame if there was a disconnect with the team. Thank them for the feedback and communicate thoughts to the team after the call)

(If the client has been unruly or disrespectful in feedback + you do not wish to continue, politely tell them you'd like to complete this project and or finish out the rest of the month of services, and that this will be the final month working together)

If you do this, just be prepared for a refund request and be in a place to honour that.

Help the client understand you:

"I want to get on the same page with you for the boundaries of our working relationship

.

Our hours of operations are 9am - 5pm Monday - Friday EST meaning we won't respond unless it's an emergency.

We cannot keep changing directions on projects, It forces me and my team to work double the amount of time.

My team and I cannot complete our high quality standards when communication isn't in slack.

Will respecting these boundaries of our services work for your needs?

Decide Best Foot Forward: Based off your intuition of the conversation, choose how you'd like to proceed with the relationship + be explicit. If this call went great, make sure to thank the client for their time.
