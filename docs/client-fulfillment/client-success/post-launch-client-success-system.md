SOP: Post-Launch Client Success System (First 30 Days)

Owner: Laura Moco Last Updated: March 2026

Overview

This system is designed to help you easily track and manage high-ticket clients during their first 30 days post-launch. It ensures we proactively check in, track any bugs, and document all interactions without getting overwhelmed by complex systems.

Where to Find the System

Everything is located in ClickUp under: Workspace > Client Hub > Client Success - Post-Launch

Step 1: Setting Up a New Client

When a new client goes live, you need to create their tracking list:

Go to the Client Success - Post-Launch folder in ClickUp.

Find the list named CLIENT NAME - 30 Day Launch [TEMPLATE].

Click the three dots (...) next to the template list and select Duplicate.

Rename the new list to [Client Name] - 30 Day Launch (e.g., "Acme Corp - 30 Day Launch").

The new list will automatically contain the 4 Weekly Check-in tasks and the templates for Bugs and Interactions.

Step 2: Managing Weekly Check-in Calls

The core of this system is the 4 weekly check-in tasks. These tasks contain everything you need to prepare for, conduct, and follow up on your weekly calls.

Open the task for the current week (e.g., "Week 1 — Weekly Client Check-in Call").

Before the call: Complete the Pre-Call Preparation Checklist in the task description. Check the box for the "Materials Prepared" custom field when ready.

During the call: Complete the During the Call Checklist and fill out the Call Notes Template directly in the task description.

After the call: Complete the Post-Call Subtasks (found inside the task). This includes sending a Slack summary, logging any new bugs, and scheduling the next call.

Step 3: Tracking Bugs and Issues

If a client reports a bug or you find an issue during a check-in call:

Find the task named [BUG TEMPLATE] Issue: [Describe the issue here] in the client's list.

Duplicate this task.

Rename it to clearly describe the issue (e.g., "Issue: Calendar sync failing").

Fill out the Bug / Issue Report in the task description.

Set the Bug Severity custom field (Minor, Moderate, or Critical).

Assign the task to the appropriate team member to fix.

Step 4: Logging Client Interactions

If you have a significant interaction with the client outside of the weekly check-in (e.g., a long Slack thread, an ad-hoc call, or an important email):

Find the task named [INTERACTION TEMPLATE] Client Interaction: [Date - Topic].

Duplicate this task.

Rename it with the date and topic (e.g., "Client Interaction: March 15 - Billing Question").

Fill out the Client Interaction Log in the task description.

Step 5: The 30-Day Wrap-Up

The Week 4 task is slightly different—it's the final 30-day wrap-up call.

Follow the specific checklists in the Week 4 task to review all progress made over the 30 days.

Celebrate wins with the client and confirm all open issues are resolved.

Once the Week 4 task is complete, update the Client Health custom field to its final status.

Notify Gabriel that the 30-day post-launch period is complete.

Custom Fields Reference

You will see several custom fields in the list view to help you track progress at a glance:

Task Type: Shows if a task is a Check-in Call, Bug/Issue, or Client Interaction.

Client Health: A quick indicator (Green, Yellow, Red) of how the client is doing.

Week Number: Shows which week a task belongs to.

Next Call Date: The date of the next scheduled check-in.

Bug Severity: Prioritizes issues (Minor, Moderate, Critical).

Client Communication Log: For storing notes from/to the client.

Resolution Summary: For documenting how an issue was fixed.

Materials Prepared: A checkbox to confirm you are ready for a call.

CSM Notes: Private internal notes just for you.
