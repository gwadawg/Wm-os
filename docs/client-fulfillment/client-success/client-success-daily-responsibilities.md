## Operating Content

Client Success Daily Tasks, Responsibilities, and Priorities

Primary Metric for Client Success: Client Retention + Client Satisfaction

Client Success Priority Ladder

Purpose: If you are ever unsure what to work on, follow this order. Higher priorities always take precedence over lower ones.

Priority 1 — Client Communication

Anything involving direct communication with a client.

Examples:

Slack messages from clients

Support questions

Meeting preparation

Meeting follow-ups

Client check-ins

Client onboarding communication

Status updates to clients

Rule: Respond within 2 hours maximum during business hours.

Client communication always comes first because client trust and satisfaction are the foundation of retention.

Priority 2 — Client Fires / Urgent Issues

Anything that could negatively impact a client's account or performance.

Examples:

Broken automations

Ads not running or campaigns paused

Lead delivery issues

Calendar booking issues

Call center problems

CRM system errors

Incorrect reporting data

Rule: These issues should be identified and escalated immediately and resolved same day whenever possible.

If another department must resolve the issue (media buyer or ops), escalate immediately and track until resolved.

Priority 3 — Assigned Tasks

Tasks assigned in ClickUp or tied to weekly/monthly goals.

Examples:

Client reporting

Account updates

Project tasks

Client onboarding setup steps

Updating client dashboards

Internal project tasks related to accounts

Rule: Assigned tasks should move forward every day and not remain idle.

If blocked, communicate the blocker immediately.

Priority 4 — Internal Team Requests

Helping internal team members.

Examples:

Answering questions from team members

Providing account information

Supporting operations or marketing teams

Providing client feedback to other departments

Rule: Only address team requests when priorities 1–3 are handled.

Client-related work always takes precedence.

Priority 5 — Optimization & Audits

Improving systems, client accounts, and internal processes.

Examples:

Reviewing funnels

Checking automations

Auditing client accounts

Reviewing dashboards

Identifying performance improvements

Improving internal documentation

Creating SOPs

Rule: Focus on optimization only when everything else is stable.

This is proactive improvement work.

The Daily Rule

When deciding what to work on:

Check for client messages

Check for client issues or fires

Work on assigned tasks

Support team requests

Improve systems or accounts

Always move top → down through the list.

End-of-Day (EOD) Checklist

1. Client Communication Check

Confirm that no client messages are left unanswered.

Check:

Slack messages

Email messages

Client support channels

Meeting follow-ups

Questions to ask:

Has every client message received a response?

Are any responses waiting on information from another team member?

If waiting on another team member:

Notify the client that the issue is being handled

Tag the responsible team member internally

Rule: No client should feel ignored overnight.

2. Client Issue Check

Confirm that no active client issues are unresolved without visibility.

Check for:

Broken automations

Ads not running

Lead delivery issues

CRM errors

Call center issues

Reporting errors

If an issue still exists:

Ensure a ClickUp ticket is created

Assign the correct owner (Ops / Media Buyer)

Update the client if needed

Rule: Every problem must have an owner.

3. ClickUp Task Review

Review your ClickUp task list.

Check:

Tasks completed today

Tasks still in progress

Upcoming deadlines

Update:

Task statuses

Comments or notes

Blockers if present

Rule: ClickUp should accurately reflect the current status of work.

4. Client Account Monitoring Check

Quickly verify that client systems appear stable.

Examples:

Leads still coming in

Calendars functioning

No major anomalies

If anything looks unusual:

Report it immediately

Open a ticket

5. Tomorrow Preparation

Prepare for the next day.

Check:

Meetings scheduled tomorrow

Client reports due

Onboarding steps required

Any high-priority tasks

Prepare:

Meeting notes

Reports

Agenda items

Rule: Tomorrow should already be planned.

6. Internal Communication Check

Confirm that any important internal updates were shared.

Examples:

Tagging Ops about bugs

Updating media buyer about performance issues

Notifying leadership of client risks

This ensures no important information stays in your head overnight.
