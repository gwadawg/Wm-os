# Client Offers

## Purpose

This document defines the two offers Waiz Media sells to new clients:
what each one includes, what it excludes, and how to tell them apart.

## Scope

Use this when describing the product a client is buying. Pricing shape
and commercial rules live in the
[money model](../../company/overview-money-model.md). Do not copy
dollar amounts into this document.

This page does not reprice existing clients. They stay on their signed
agreement.

## Owner

Gabe owns which offer is shown and any exception to the inclusions
below. Operations keeps this document matched to the money model.

## When To Use

- Building a proposal, one-pager, or scope of work for a new client.
- Checking whether booking, live transfer, or CRM is in the deal.
- Briefing fulfillment on what was sold.

## Shared Facts

Both offers are built for one product lane: DSCR or reverse mortgage.
The lane changes who the ads and funnels speak to. It does not change
the offer list below.

Both offers include a marketing system that generates leads for that
client only. Leads are not shared with another client.

## Core Offer

The client buys conversations. Waiz Media generates the demand,
qualifies it, and puts a real introduction on the client's calendar
or phone.

### Included

| Component | What the client gets |
|---|---|
| Lead generation | Paid marketing that produces inbound leads for the client's lane. |
| WM Apex funnels | Custom landing pages built with Waiz Media's proprietary high-level funnel method. |
| CRM | A Waiz Media CRM configured for the client. |
| AI booking agent | Automated booking and follow-up on leads. |
| Call center | People prequalify leads before the client is involved. |
| Conversation delivery | A live transfer, or a booking on the client's calendar. |
| Playbooks | Operating playbooks for the system. |
| Support | Ongoing support across the stack. |
| Reporting | High-level performance reports. |
| Strategy | A monthly strategy session on results and how to scale. |

### Pricing shape

Startup fee, retainer, and pay per legitimate conversation. The
conversation fee is billed only when the introduction was real. If
the conversation was not legitimate, that fee is not charged. The
startup fee and the retainer are still due.

Rates are on the founder pricing sheet. See the
[money model](../../company/overview-money-model.md#pricing-rule).

### Still open on this offer

- Who chooses live transfer versus a calendar booking.
- The operational test for a legitimate conversation.
- Whether every core-offer funnel is a full WM Apex build. The offer
  includes WM Apex funnels. Treat that as included until Gabe narrows it.

## Leads-Only Offer

Working name. This offer does not have an official name yet.

The client buys the marketing system and the leads. Waiz Media does
not book appointments and does not live-transfer. The client, or the
client's team, works the leads.

### Included

| Component | What the client gets |
|---|---|
| Marketing system | Waiz Media's process for generating leads on the client's lane. |
| Funnel development | A funnel built for that lane. |
| Nurture playbooks | How to work the leads and raise contact rate. |

### CRM choice

The client picks one. Both are part of the offer.

| Choice | What Waiz Media does |
|---|---|
| Use the Waiz CRM | Provides the CRM and a basic drip campaign. |
| Keep their own CRM | Integrates a lead drop so leads flow into their CRM automatically. |

### Not included

- Appointment booking
- Live transfer
- Call-center prequalification
- The AI booking agent
- The core offer's reporting package and monthly strategy session

### Pricing shape

Startup fee, retainer, and a percentage of that month's ad spend.
Rates are on the founder pricing sheet.

### Still open on this offer

- The official name.
- Whether funnel development is a full WM Apex build or a lighter
  funnel. Do not promise WM Apex on this offer until that is decided.
  WM Apex is named on the core offer.

## Side By Side

| | Core offer | Leads-only offer |
|---|---|---|
| Ends in | A legitimate conversation | A lead in a CRM |
| WM Apex funnels | Included | Not promised |
| Waiz CRM | Included | Optional, with a basic drip |
| Client's CRM | Not the default | Lead-drop integration if they opt out of the Waiz CRM |
| Call center and prequalification | Included | Not included |
| AI booking agent | Included | Not included |
| Live transfer or calendar booking | Included | Not included |
| Nurture playbooks | Included with the system | Included, aimed at contact rate |
| Reporting and monthly strategy | Included | Not included |
| Third pricing leg | Pay per legitimate conversation | Percent of monthly ad spend |

## How To Choose

Use the core offer when the client wants Waiz Media to prequalify
leads and hand them a conversation.

Use the leads-only offer when the client wants leads and a system for
working them, and their own people will make contact.

Gabe chooses when a prospect could fit either offer. Do not invent a
downsell between them.

## Quality Bar

A proposal or script that describes an offer should match the table
for that offer. Booking and live transfer stay off the leads-only
offer. The conversation fee stays off any conversation that was not
legitimate. Dollar amounts come from the founder pricing sheet.
