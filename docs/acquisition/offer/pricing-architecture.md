# Waiz Media Pricing Architecture — Team Tiers

## Purpose

Define the pricing model for multi-LO team accounts. The Solo tier (current core offer) is documented in
[overview-money-model-april-26.md](../../company/overview-money-model-april-26.md). This document extends
that model with team tiers designed for 2-20+ LO accounts, including setup fees, monthly retainers,
ad spend kickers, bundled reporting, and performance fees.

## Scope

This document governs:

- Team tier definitions and price points
- Minimum ad spend requirements by tier
- Ad spend kicker formula by tier
- Dashboard provisioning and COGS guidance
- Performance kicker design and selling mechanics
- Performance rev-share option (proven accounts only)
- Grand Slam value stacks for sales proposals
- Upsell pathway from Solo → Team → Branch

It does not supersede the Solo offer or the Boot Camp downsell defined in the money model doc.

## Owner

Founder (Gabe) owns all pricing decisions. This document is the canonical reference for sales calls,
proposals, and AI-generated offer materials for team accounts.

## When To Use

- Preparing a proposal for a prospect with 2+ LOs
- Calculating what to charge a team that is already on the Solo tier and growing
- Building a sales deck, one-pager, or ROI calculator for a team pitch
- Training a closer or setter on team pricing logic

## Operating Rules: Performance Fees and Revenue Share

### Standard Kicker Model (Default)

The default for all team tiers is a base retainer + ad spend kicker + threshold-gated performance fee.
This is distinct from revenue share:

- It is a **flat fee per funded loan** (not a percentage of commission)
- It only activates **above a volume threshold** (e.g. loans 4+ in a month)
- It stacks **on top of a guaranteed base retainer** — it is never the primary compensation
- It is agreed upfront in the contract with a clear tracking mechanism

This structure does not conflict with the existing no-revenue-share operating rule. It is a volume
incentive stacked on guaranteed monthly revenue.

### Performance Rev-Share Option (Proven Accounts Only)

An optional alternative pricing model is available for accounts that have demonstrated consistent,
tracked results on the standard retainer for 6+ months. This model is not offered to new clients.

See the [Performance Rev-Share Option](#performance-rev-share-option-proven-accounts) section below
for full details.

## Minimum Ad Spend Requirements

A minimum monthly ad spend is required for each tier. Clients spending below the minimum do not have
enough budget for the system to generate reliable results. Minimums are contract terms, not suggestions.

| Tier | Minimum Ad Spend | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Solo | $2,000/month | Baseline for ~1 loan/month at $2k CAC |
| Team Starter | $4,000/month combined | $1,500–2,000 per active LO |
| Team Growth | $10,000/month combined | $1,500–2,000 per active LO across 5 LOs |
| Branch | $20,000/month combined | $2,000 per active LO across 10 LOs |

**What happens below the minimum:** Waiz Media cannot guarantee lead volume or pipeline performance
below these thresholds. If a client's spend drops below their tier minimum for two consecutive months,
the account can be renegotiated to the appropriate tier or exited at Gabe's discretion.

**The minimum is also a selling tool.** In discovery, framing the minimum as a requirement signals
that this is a serious acquisition system, not a $500/month experiment. Prospects unwilling to meet
the minimum are not qualified for team tiers.

### Team Starter (2–4 LOs)

| Line Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Setup fee | $3,500 |
| Monthly retainer | $3,200 |
| Ad spend kicker | 8% of combined team spend over $8,000/month |
| Dashboard | Provisioned and bundled (software cost absorbed into monthly) |
| Performance kicker | $500 per funded loan after team closes 4+ loans in a calendar month |
| Estimated COGS | $200–300/month software provisioning + ~15–20 hrs labor |

**What is included:**

- Multi-LO campaign management (separate angles, creative batches per LO)
- Custom lead routing — leads are attributed and distributed to the correct LO
- LO-level conversion tracking inside the shared CRM
- Consolidated reporting dashboard with one view across all LOs
- Pre-call nurture and SMS automation running across all leads
- Ongoing technical and strategic support

**Performance kicker threshold rationale:**
4 loans/month is the threshold where the team is operating at a healthy volume given standard reverse
mortgage close rates. Set low enough to be achievable by Month 2–3 of a healthy campaign. The kicker
should feel like a reward for the system working, not a penalty or stretch target.

## Ad Spend Kicker — Quick Reference Table

| Tier | Monthly Retainer | Kicker Rate | Kicker Threshold |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solo | $1,689 | 10% | Over $4,000 |
| Team Starter | $3,200 | 8% | Over $8,000 combined |
| Team Growth | $5,500 | 7% | Over $15,000 combined |
| Branch | $8,000–12,000 | 5–6% (negotiated) | Over $25,000 combined |

**How the kicker is calculated:** Monthly ad spend minus the threshold, multiplied by the kicker rate.

> **Example — Team Starter at $10,000 combined ad spend:**
> ($10,000 − $8,000) × 8% = $160 kicker
> Total invoice: $3,200 + $160 = $3,360

> **Example — Team Starter at $8,000 or below:**
> No kicker. Invoice is flat $3,200.

## The Performance Kicker as a Sales Mechanic

The performance kicker is not a back-of-proposal detail. It is the **risk-removal close**.

**How to present it:**

> "You pay the base retainer every month regardless. The performance fee only activates once your team
> is closing loans above the threshold. If the system isn't working and you're not hitting that number,
> you're not paying more. We only earn the kicker when you win."

**Why this works:**

- Reduces perceived risk for the buyer — aligns Waiz Media's incentive with their outcome
- Reframes the retainer as a floor, not a ceiling — implies upside for both sides
- Creates urgency to close because the kicker threshold is achievable and feels like a milestone, not a penalty

**Threshold design rules:**

- Set the threshold low enough that a healthy campaign hits it by Month 2–3
- If the client never hits the threshold, that is a performance signal, not a pricing win
- The kicker should feel like a celebration when it triggers, not a surprise on an invoice
- Always confirm the tracking mechanism in the contract before presenting this structure

## Dashboard Provisioning — COGS Guidance

Team tiers include dashboard access as a bundled service. Software provisioning costs $100–300/month
per client depending on the tool and seat configuration.

**Do not bill this as a separate line item.** Bundle it into the monthly retainer.

Reasons:
- Separate billing creates friction and erodes the "full-service" positioning
- At Team Starter ($3,200/month), absorbing $200 in software COGS is 6% of monthly revenue — well inside margin
- The dashboard is a retention asset: clients who can see their pipeline numbers stay longer
- If a client leaves, remove their dashboard access. The software cost goes away with them.

## Performance Rev-Share Option (Proven Accounts)

This model is **not offered to new clients**. It is available as an upgrade for accounts with a
documented track record of consistent performance on the standard retainer.

### Qualification Criteria

A client qualifies for the rev-share option when they meet all of the following:

- 6+ months on a standard retainer tier with Waiz Media
- Funded loan data is cleanly tracked and attributable (CRM event or client-confirmed monthly report)
- Consistent monthly loan volume with no attribution disputes in the prior 3 months
- Team size of 2+ LOs with active campaigns

### Structure

| Line Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Reduced base retainer | $1,500/month (Team Starter) or $2,500/month (Team Growth) |
| Per funded loan fee | $1,200–$1,500/funded loan (no volume threshold) |
| Ad spend kicker | Same formula as the standard tier — does not change |
| Minimum ad spend | Same as the standard tier — does not change |

### When It Pays More Than the Standard Model

The rev-share model only makes sense for Waiz Media when loan volume is high enough to exceed the
standard retainer value. Breakeven math:

- **Team Starter breakeven:** ($3,200 standard retainer − $1,500 reduced base) ÷ $1,300/loan avg fee = ~1.3 loans/month above threshold to break even on the reduced base
- At 5+ loans/month, rev-share generates $7,500–$9,000/month vs. $3,700–$3,800 on the standard model
- Do not offer this model to accounts closing fewer than 4 loans/month consistently

### How to Present It

> "You've been closing consistently and the attribution is clean. We can restructure your pricing so
> the base is lower and you pay us per funded loan instead of a flat monthly. That means you pay more
> when you're winning and less when things slow down — and we're fully aligned to push harder for your
> team because our income scales with yours."

### Tracking Requirement

Before switching any account to the rev-share model, confirm in writing:

- What constitutes a "funded loan" for billing purposes
- Who reports it and when (e.g., client sends funded confirmation within 5 business days of funding)
- What happens if a loan closes that the client attributes to a referral rather than Waiz leads
- The dispute resolution process before Gabe will approve the model switch

**Do not switch a client to rev-share without a signed addendum that specifies these terms.**

## Upsell Pathway

The performance kicker can be introduced at any tier transition as a loyalty upgrade:

> "You've been getting results on the base model. Here's a structure where you pay more only when
> you close more — and it lets us go harder for your team because we're aligned on outcomes."

**Recommended transition points:**

| Trigger | Offer |
|---|---|
| Solo LO hires a second LO | Upsell to Team Starter |
| Team Starter closes consistently for 3+ months | Introduce performance kicker if not already in contract |
| Team Starter adds 2+ more LOs | Move to Team Growth |
| Team Growth hits 10+ LOs or $15k+ consistent spend | Scope Branch contract |
| Any team tier at 6+ months with clean attribution | Eligible for Performance Rev-Share Option |

The existing client paying $4,500 + $800/funded loan (after 5 loans/month) was an upsell after
12 months on the Solo tier. That pathway is the template for the rev-share option above. For new team
acquisitions, do not lead with rev-share — use the standard tier structure first, then upgrade.

## Pricing Authority

Gabe is the only person authorized to quote, adjust, or negotiate pricing for team accounts. Setters
do not discuss team pricing. Pricing questions from prospects or existing clients escalate to Gabe
immediately.

## Quality Bar

Any sales script, proposal, or AI output referencing team pricing should:

- Quote only from this document or a founder-approved pricing sheet
- Present the performance kicker as a risk-removal benefit, not an add-on fee
- Anchor the conversation to client ROI (loan commission ÷ Waiz invoice), not to feature lists
- Never position team tiers as "more expensive than Solo" — frame them as scaled systems
- Include the value stack in any written proposal to show standalone value vs. the price
