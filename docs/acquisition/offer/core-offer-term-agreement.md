# Service Agreement

This Service Agreement is entered into on {{right_now.middle_endian_date}} between **Waiz Media LLC** ("Waiz Media") and the person who signs below (the "Client").

Waiz Media LLC is the only company party. The person who signs for Waiz Media does so as its founder and is not personally a party. The person who signs for the Client is the Client and owes the fees in this agreement.

## 1. The system

Waiz Media will install and run its proprietary mortgage acquisition system for the Client. Waiz Media developed this system. The version installed for the Client is built around the Client's business. The mortgage products to be advertised are the ones named on the onboarding form. The onboarding form is part of this agreement.

**Campaign management.** Campaigns run through Waiz Media's media-buying process, a process Waiz Media developed, on the Client's ad account. Waiz Media produces the creative for those campaigns, including statics, UGC, and video.

**WM Apex.** WM Apex is Waiz Media's proprietary funnel strategy. Waiz Media designs and builds the WM Apex landing pages for the Client's mortgage business. The pages capture and route prospects. They are written to educate prospects and to present the Client as a leading professional in the Client's market, and they are tailored to the Client's business.

**Virtual business card.** A custom virtual business card, built for the Client, to send to prospects.

**WM CRM.** WM CRM is Waiz Media's CRM, built on GoHighLevel. The nurturing sequences and the CRM automations are built for the Client, using Waiz Media's tested campaigns and follow-up process, built for high response rates. Waiz Media provides the phone number used to call and text prospects, and pays the call and text costs of that number. Those costs are included in the fees under this agreement. They are not billed separately. Ad spend remains the Client's cost under Section 7.

**Waiz AI Agent.** Waiz Media's custom agent, configured on the Client's business. It responds to prospects as the Client's assistant, under Section 4.

**WM Appointment Setters.** A trained sales team that prospects the Client's leads on the Client's behalf and books appointments or live-transfers them under Section 6. The team is open 10:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Eastern, 5 to 6 days a week, and dials new leads as they come in. The people on the team are humans, not AI. They are trained on the Client's business and on objections, and they identify themselves only as the Client's assistant, under Section 4. They remain Waiz Media's personnel. Waiz Media chooses the channel.

**Strategy.** At least one strategy session each service month while the work is on, covering KPIs, the reports, and scaling.

**Reporting.** A monthly report of the work and the amounts due.

**Team members.** Waiz Media works with the Client's team members when they are under one company identity and leads are distributed by round robin, each new lead to the next team member in turn. Messaging and systems built for each team member are not part of this agreement. A lead distribution built custom for each person is not part of this agreement.

**Higher-level work.** Social media management for the Client's company pages, custom software to track a team's KPIs and manage team performance, and filming and editing custom video content for ads are a higher-level package. They are not part of this agreement. The monthly report in this section is included. The campaign creative in this section, including statics, UGC, and video, is still included.

Leads from the Client's campaigns belong to the Client only. Waiz Media does not promise a number of leads, conversations, or funded loans. The media-buying process, WM Apex, the virtual business card build, the WM CRM build, the nurturing sequences, the automations, the Waiz AI Agent, the scripts, and the workflows are Waiz Media's property under Section 12.

Onboarding is expected to take 7 to 14 business days. That range is not a deadline.

## 2. Definitions

**Go-live date.** The first day the client's ads are live.

**Term.** {{contact.term}} months, measured from the go-live date, plus any days added under Section 9, and minus no days except as Section 9 states for an unresponsive pause.

**Service month.** The month that begins on the go-live date, and each month that begins on a monthly anniversary of the go-live date.

**Conversation fee.** ${{contact.cost_per_conv}}.

**Live transfer.** Waiz Media has the prospect on the phone and introduces the prospect to the Client while the Client is on the line.

**Booked appointment.** An appointment Waiz Media books between the Client and a prospect.

**Bail.** A booked appointment the Client does not attend, and for which the Client did not give at least 2 hours' notice to reschedule or cancel.

**Claimed.** The Client marks a lead with the claimed tag in WM CRM. The tag tells Waiz Media to stop outreach on that lead. Section 6 states when that lead carries a fee.

## 3. Launch

Waiz Media sends the Client the material that will run under the Client's name. This includes each ad, page, text, and script a prospect will see or hear, and any later change to that material.

Waiz Media does not launch until the Client approves that material. Waiz Media does not run later material under the Client's name until the Client approves it. Material the Client approves is the Client's responsibility for compliance.

Waiz Media may decline to run any rate, claim, or promise it will not run. Waiz Media may take down an asset that creates a legal or platform risk. Taking an asset down is not a pause under Section 9. Fees and the term do not change because an asset is declined or taken down.

The Client warrants that the Client is licensed to originate loans in the states being advertised. The Client's name and license number are the advertiser. Waiz Media's people and AI do not quote rates, promise approval, or give loan advice.

Waiz Media calls and texts leads who opt in through the Client's campaigns. The Client authorizes those calls and texts, and authorizes recording of them. Waiz Media honors a lead's opt-out and gives any recording notice the law requires. The Client will not give Waiz Media leads who did not opt in through those campaigns.

## 4. Representation

This section applies when Waiz Media reaches out to a lead, and when Waiz Media communicates with a lead. It applies to those calls and messages only.

On those calls and messages, Waiz Media's people and the Waiz AI Agent identify themselves only as the Client's assistant. They do not speak as the mortgage company. They do not present Waiz Media as that company. Waiz Media may do otherwise only with the Client's written approval.

When they name the company to a lead, they say the Client works for the company named on the onboarding form, unless the Client has given written approval to say otherwise.

This is the rule so a lead is not misled about who is speaking. It protects both the Client and Waiz Media from that misrepresentation.

"Assistant" is how they identify themselves to a lead. It does not make them the Client's employees.

## 5. Fees

On signing, the Client pays the startup fee of ${{contact.startup_fee}} and the first retainer of ${{contact.package_cost}}.

The startup fee is for setup. It is separate from the retainer. The first retainer is the first month of the term. That month begins on the go-live date.

On each monthly anniversary of the go-live date, the Client pays ${{contact.package_cost}} for the next month of the term, until the term is fully paid.

After the term, the Client pays ${{contact.package_cost}} on each monthly anniversary for as long as the agreement continues month to month.

The conversation fee is charged on top of the retainer, for each service month in which a chargeable conversation or bail occurs.

The Client authorizes Waiz Media to charge the payment method on file for amounts due under this agreement.

All fees are final. Waiz Media does not refund the startup fee, a retainer, or a conversation fee, in whole or in part. This includes a month the Client does not use, time left after notice, and a launch that never happens. If the ads never go live, the term does not start, and the startup fee and the first retainer stay paid.

A charge for a conversation, a bail, or a claimed lead that does not meet this agreement is removed from the invoice before the charge is made, under Section 8. After the charge is made, the fee stays paid.

## 6. Conversations

Waiz Media chooses whether a lead is a live transfer or a booked appointment.

A live transfer is charged the conversation fee when the Client is on the line and Waiz Media makes the introduction. The full fee is due if the prospect hangs up after that introduction. If the prospect hangs up before the Client is on the line, there is no fee.

If the Client does not accept a live transfer while the prospect is on the line, including a no-answer, a rejection, or a transfer to voicemail, there is no fee. In each service month the count of those missed transfers starts at zero. On the 10th miss in that service month, Waiz Media may end live transfers. From that notice forward, Waiz Media delivers appointments only, for the rest of the term and for any month-to-month period after it. Waiz Media may turn live transfers back on by writing to the Client. Sending another live transfer is not an agreement to keep sending them. The Client cannot require live transfers after Waiz Media has ended them.

A booked appointment is charged the conversation fee if the Client speaks with that prospect at the scheduled time, or within 7 days after that scheduled time because of the booking. If the Client gives at least 2 hours' notice to reschedule, the new time is the scheduled time, and there is no bail. Notice of at least 2 hours to cancel the appointment means there is no fee.

A bail is charged one half of the conversation fee. There is no fee when the Client attends and the prospect does not.

**Claimed leads.** This applies when the Client uses WM CRM and contacts leads on the Client's own. The Client marks that lead with the claimed tag. Waiz Media stops outreach on that lead.

If the tag is on the lead before Waiz Media books an appointment or live-transfers the lead, there is no fee.

If the Client had already spoken with the lead before that booking or transfer, there is no fee, even when the tag is late. A text, call, or email from before the booking is enough to show that.

If the Client first contacts the lead after the booking and then claims it, the conversation fee is due.

A prospect is charged once. The half fee is not added on top of the conversation fee. If the Client speaks with that prospect because of a booking or a completed live transfer, or the conversation fee is due because of a claim after the booking, the charge is the conversation fee. If the only amount due is a bail, the charge is one half of the conversation fee.

## 7. Ad spend

The monthly ad spend target is ${{contact.min_ad_spend}}. The Client pays the advertising platform on the Client's own ad account. That spend is separate from the startup fee, the retainer, and the conversation fee.

Spend may come in under the target. The target is not a promise that this amount will be spent. It does not change any fee in this agreement.

If the ad account is off because the platform declined the Client's ad payment, Waiz Media stops ad spend and does not keep trying to spend on that account. Dialing and booking of leads already in hand continue, unless a pause under Section 9 is on.

## 8. Monthly report

Within 2 business days after a service month ends, Waiz Media emails the Client a report of the conversations, the bails, the claimed leads, and the amount due.

The Client has 3 calendar days from that email to object by email. An objection is limited to a listed conversation, bail, or claimed lead that does not meet Section 6. If the Client does not object within those 3 days, Waiz Media charges the payment method on file, and that month is final.

## 9. Pause

A pause is a temporary stop of all work: campaigns, dialing, booking, live transfers, and strategy sessions. No new conversation fees are created while a pause is on. The agreement stays in place. Fees for conversations, bails, and claimed leads that already happened stay due.

**The Client asks to pause.** The Client pauses by email and may name a restart date. If no date is named, the pause is 30 days. A longer pause needs Waiz Media's agreement by email. Waiz Media turns the work back on when the Client asks to restart. Paid service days are not used during the pause. Waiz Media does not charge a new retainer while the pause is on. The end of the term moves back by the number of paused days. The startup fee does not become extra service days. Money already paid stays paid.

Paused days the Client asked for may add up to 60 during the term. After that, Waiz Media may end the agreement by email. The agreement continues until that email is sent. A restart date Waiz Media has agreed to by email stands. A pause that starts because a fee charge did not clear does not count toward those 60 days.

**A fee charge does not clear.** Waiz Media notifies the Client the day the charge fails and pauses all work. Paid service days are not used. Waiz Media does not charge a new retainer while the work is off. The end of the term moves back by those days. The work turns back on when the past-due balance is paid. A failed charge does not end this agreement.

**The Client is unresponsive.** One missed text or email does not count. The Client is unresponsive only when all of the following are true: Waiz Media has asked by email for a meeting or an update the account needs; the Client misses that meeting or does not answer that request; and 14 days then pass with no reply from the Client by email, text, or Slack. Work continues during those 14 days. The term keeps moving during those 14 days. When the 14 days end and the Client is still silent, all work pauses. The term keeps moving during that pause, and retainers that come due stay due. The work turns back on when the Client responds.

If a fee charge fails while an unresponsive pause is already on, the term still keeps moving.

## 10. Term

The term is {{contact.term}} months from the go-live date, adjusted only as Section 9 says.

The Client cannot end the agreement before the last day of the term. After the paused days the Client asked for reach 60 under Section 9, Waiz Media may end the agreement by email. That email ends the agreement when it is sent. Either party may send any other notice during the term. The agreement then ends on the last day of the term, or 30 days after that notice, whichever is later.

After the term, the agreement continues month to month at the same retainer and the same conversation fee. Either party may end that continuation with 30 days' written notice.

Notice by email is sufficient.

## 11. Leads and confidentiality

Leads from the Client's campaigns, and the prospect's personal information, are the Client's. Waiz Media does not give those leads to another client. Waiz Media does not share those records with anyone outside the work unless the Client gives permission, or the law requires the disclosure.

Call recordings of those leads, and the chat history with them, are part of the Client's lead records. While this agreement is in effect, the Client may ask by email to see them. Waiz Media provides the recordings and the chat history it has for those leads.

Waiz Media may share those records with its staff, its contractors, and the tools used to deliver the work, including WM CRM and the dialer.

The Client will not share Waiz Media's pricing, scripts, playbooks, workflows, or reports with anyone outside the Client's own business, and will not give them to another agency. This duty lasts during the agreement and for 3 years after it ends.

## 12. After the agreement ends

Waiz Media owns its media-buying process, WM Apex, the virtual business card build, the WM CRM configuration and automations, the Waiz AI Agent, the scripts, the workflows, and the phone number it provides under Section 1. The Client's lead records stay the Client's, including the call recordings and chat history in Section 11. If the Client asks within 30 days after the agreement ends, Waiz Media exports those records to the Client. Access to Waiz Media's systems ends when the agreement ends. The phone number stays with Waiz Media.

## 13. Name and results

Waiz Media may use the Client's business name, and performance figures that do not identify a prospect, in Waiz Media's own marketing. The Client may opt out of this by email. Prospect records stay under Section 11.

## 14. Responsibility and liability

The Client is responsible for the Client's license, the Client's loan product, material the Client approved, and any claim the Client told Waiz Media to run.

Neither party is liable for indirect damages or lost profits. Waiz Media's total liability under this agreement will not exceed the fees the Client paid Waiz Media in the 3 months before the claim. Amounts the Client owes Waiz Media are not capped.

## 15. General

Waiz Media is an independent contractor. Its people are not the Client's employees and may not bind the Client.

A failure or delay caused by an event outside a party's reasonable control excuses the affected work while the event continues. That event is not a pause under Section 9. Fees already paid stay paid.

This agreement and the onboarding form are the entire agreement. A change must be in writing and signed by both parties, except where this agreement allows notice by email. If a provision is unenforceable, the rest remains. This agreement may be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature.

California law governs this agreement. The state and federal courts in California are the venue.
