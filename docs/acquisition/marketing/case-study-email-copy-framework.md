# Case Study Email Copy Framework

## Purpose

Canonical framework for writing **case study emails** — nurture or follow-up messages that use a client result as proof while following Waiz’s standard **3-step copywriting formula**. Use this when you need copy for a case study email, testimonial email, or social-proof email (not Money Tales story emails).

## Scope

**In scope:** Email structure, tone, case study beats, video deep-dive framing, CTA placement.

**Out of scope:** Full case study production, thank-you page build, Wistia setup, Money Tales emails ([Money Tales Email Copy Framework SOP](sop-money-tales-email-copy-framework.md)), post-demo follow-up scripts (see Drive inventory).

## Owner

See [domain owners](../../_inventory/domain-owners.md): **marketing-lead**.

## When To Use

- Writing or reviewing a case study email for nurture, follow-up, or proof-led outreach
- Prompting an AI agent: *“Write a case study email using the Case Study Email Copy Framework”*
- Turning a client win into email copy without sounding like a desperate pitch

## Inputs

- Target reader segment and the **unspoken question or objection** they likely have
- Case study assets: client context, before/after, measurable result, proof (screenshot, number), client quote
- Optional: video testimonial or long-form case study (Wistia / thank-you page with chapters)
- Next-step CTA (e.g. book a call)

## Outputs

- Subject line + email body that completes all three framework steps
- Tight written case study (not a testimonial essay)
- Optional video link framed as deep dive
- Direct-response CTA

## Quality Bar

- **Helpful expert, not desperate pitch** — proof does the selling; tone is premium and authoritative
- **Client in the spotlight** — not Waiz self-promotion in the case study body
- **Show, don’t tell** — include numbers, screenshots, or other proof when claiming results
- **Concise case study block** — a few sentences per beat, not paragraphs
- Subject and opener address a real question in the reader’s head, not “Check out our case study!”

## Metrics

- Define or track in [KPIs](../../kpis/README.md) as this workflow is operationalized (e.g. reply rate, call bookings from case study sends).

## Core Principle

Case study emails use the **same 3-step copywriting formula** as other Waiz copy. The **case study is the vehicle** that delivers the answer — not a separate format.

| Step | Name | Role in a case study email |
|------|------|----------------------------|
| 1 | Acknowledge the unspoken question | Subject + opening hit a question or objection; case study result is the hook |
| 2 | Provide a concise, written answer | Short written case study (the proof story) |
| 3 | Offer the video as a deep dive + CTA | Optional longer proof; then direct next step |

---

## Step 1 — Acknowledge the Unspoken Question

The **subject line** and **opening sentence** should name a question or objection you know is in the reader’s head. Then use the **case study result** as the hook that addresses it.

**Do**

- Lead with specificity: *“Can this actually work for [their situation]?”* → then the case study answers it.

**Don’t**

- Generic opens: *“Check out our case study!”*

---

## Step 2 — Provide a Concise, Written Answer (The Case Study)

Shine the spotlight on the **client**, not Waiz. Keep this section **short**. Cover these beats in order:

1. **Who the client was** — relatable to the reader (role, situation, segment)
2. **What they struggled with before** — the constraint or pain they felt
3. **What happened / the result** — outcome with **proof** (number, screenshot, metric)
4. **A key quote from the client** — one strong line that reinforces the result

### Rules

- **A few sentences per beat** — not paragraphs
- **Show, don’t tell** — if they “hit X result,” include the proof
- **Avoid the testimonial essay** — the biggest mistake is letting Step 2 balloon into a long testimonial

---

## Step 3 — Offer the Video as a “Deep Dive”

Frame a **video testimonial** or **longer case study video** as an **optional** resource for readers who want more detail. Examples:

- Full recorded case study interview
- Thank-you page with Wistia video, chapters, and key quote pulled out underneath

Then close with your **direct-response CTA** (book a call, reply, or whatever the defined next step is).

---

## Tone Reminder

Sound like a **helpful, authoritative expert** delivering a **premium experience**. You are saying: *“This person was in your exact shoes; here’s what happened”* — and letting proof carry the persuasion.

---

## Quick Checklist (Before Send)

- [ ] Subject + first line = unspoken question/objection, not a case-study announcement
- [ ] Four beats present: who → struggle → result (with proof) → quote
- [ ] Case study body is tight (sentence-scale per beat)
- [ ] Video (if any) framed as optional deep dive, not required reading
- [ ] Clear CTA at the end
- [ ] Tone: expert + helpful, not pushy
