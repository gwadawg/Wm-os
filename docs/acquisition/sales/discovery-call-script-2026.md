## DISCOVERY CALL - SCRIPT
## DISCOVERY CALL SCRIPT
Use this as a guide, not a crutch.

If you are following a script, that means the script is leading the call, not you.

If the script is communicating to the prospect, that means you are not the one communicating to the prospect.

If the script is your crutch, that means you are handicapped. Use it as you need, but build your own strength so you can move freely.

Additionally, it is your responsibility to adapt in the moment as the need arises, to flush out all potential objections in Discovery (even if it’s not part of the script), and to ensure the prospect is a GOOD FIT (mindset) before making an offer.

## PAY-PER-CLOSE OBJ
## IF SOMEONE SAYS THEY WANT PAY-PER-CLOSE
### Gotcha, what makes you interested in a pay-after-close model?
The goal here is to understand if it’s a FINANCIAL thing or an UNCERTAINTY thing

You can even ask… “For most people looking for pay-after-close, it usually boils down to two things: they either do not have the actual funds to invest into their business, or they don’t feel they can AFFORD to lose money without knowing if there will be an ROI… which one do you think better describes your situation?”

### IF FINANCIAL:
Seek greater understanding by asking clarifying questions.

If you have a clear picture that this person would not be able to afford our service, do the following:

### Offer our $97/mo DIY program
## Send free trainings from our YouTube
## Have them follow our IG page
Refer them to Redfin (pay after close company with 77% client retention), but warn them of the cons of pay after close companies

Ask if you can follow-up in a couple months, and put them in “Long-Term Nurture”

Set a task to reach back out in 45-60 days and see how they’re doing, see if they’re ready then, offer coaching on how to get funds, or congratulate them on their newfound success

### IF BOTH:
Do everything above.

If they are financially DQ’d, end the call after you’ve completed the above steps.

If they are financially qualified, move on to the next step.

### IF UNCERTAINTY:
Do CIVR.
