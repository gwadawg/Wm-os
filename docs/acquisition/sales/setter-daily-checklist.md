# Setter Daily Checklist

A run-every-day checklist. Work top to bottom. Do not skip to a lower priority until the one above it is clear.

Full context for each priority: [Setter Daily Operations Playbook](setter-daily-operations-playbook.md)

---

## Start of Shift

- [ ] Review pipeline — check where every active lead is sitting
- [ ] Check your notes from yesterday
- [ ] Audit the next 3–4 days of the calendar — mark Grade 1–2 or unqualified bookings as FREE now, before the day starts
- [ ] Confirm your watchshift channels are live (GHL, Slack notifications on)

---

## Priority 1 — Inbound Sets (Respond Immediately)

New inbound notification = drop everything, call now. Do not wait for their booked time.

- [ ] Any new inbound booking notifications? Call immediately.
- [ ] Ran FUN qualification on the call (Financial, Urgency, Need)
- [ ] Captured their motivators — exact words, personal details, emotional drivers
- [ ] Framed the demo as a consultation, not a sales pitch
- [ ] Booked demo slot + passed notes to closer
- [ ] If failed FUN — routed to Boot Camp, not demo

_Script: [Intro Call Basic Script](script-intro-call-basic.md) | Framework: [Intro Call Qualification Framework](intro-call-qualification-framework.md)_

---

## Priority 2 — Watchshift: Speed-to-Lead Outbound Calls

Monitor channels for new leads. Call them outbound — use the intro call script.

- [ ] Watchshift channels actively monitored (GHL, Slack)
- [ ] All new leads from watchshift called immediately
- [ ] Used intro call script for outbound calls
- [ ] Ran FUN on each — booked demo or disqualified, no grey area
- [ ] Texting only after calling — if they text back, called immediately

_Script: [Intro Call Basic Script](script-intro-call-basic.md) | Framework: [Intro Call Qualification Framework](intro-call-qualification-framework.md)_

---

## Priority 3 — Confirm Appointments (Upcoming Demo Calls)

Call every booked prospect 24–48 hours before their demo. Do not rely on automations.

- [ ] Pulled list of all demos in the next 24–48 hours
- [ ] Called every booked prospect to confirm
- [ ] Double tied-down: confirmed date/time and got verbal commitment
- [ ] Attempted to push the call forward if sooner slot available
- [ ] Sent social proof assets after the call (case studies, testimonials)
- [ ] Anyone unresponsive 24hrs out? — WhatsApp text + voice note sent, Instagram DM sent
- [ ] Calendar audit done — unqualified slots marked FREE

_SOP: [No Shows and Maximizing Show Rates](no-shows-maximizing-show-rates-setter-levers.md)_

---

## Priority 4 — LinkedIn Responses + Outreach

Replies first. Then new outreach.

**Part A — Replies**
- [ ] All pending replies reviewed
- [ ] Any prospect replies handed off to Gabriel immediately

**Part B — New Outreach (30–60 min block)**
- [ ] 5–10 ICP post comments done first
- [ ] Mined lists, groups, or engagement for prospects
- [ ] 90 seconds of research per prospect before connecting
- [ ] Connect requests sent (blank or signal micro-note — A/B per copy-angles)
- [ ] First DM sent to accepted connections
- [ ] One no-reply bump sent to anyone at 48–72 hours — stopped after that

_Process: [LinkedIn Outbound Process](../outbound/linkedin/process.md) | Copy: [LinkedIn Copy Angles](../outbound/linkedin/copy-angles.md)_

---

## Priority 5 — Power Dialer: New Leads

Work cold leads via power dialer — newest to oldest only after P1–P4 are clear.

- [ ] Dialing new leads in order: newest → oldest
- [ ] Texting only after calling each lead
- [ ] If they text back — called immediately
- [ ] Objections via text — steered to booking, no long paragraph replies
- [ ] CRM dispositions updated after every call

_SOP: [Power Dialer New Leads SOP](sop-power-dialer-new-leads.md)_

---

## Priority 6 — No-Shows

This week's no-shows first, then archived. Never sit idle on a no-show — run protocol and keep dialing simultaneously.

**No-Show Protocol Checklist (run for each no-show):**
- [ ] Waited 3 min — then called
- [ ] No answer → double-dialed immediately
- [ ] No answer → sent first text
- [ ] Called front desk (if business hours) — framed as "they booked a client generation call"
- [ ] 7 min past: 3rd call from GHL + personal phone call + Jeremy Miner text sent
- [ ] 10 min past: final reschedule text sent
- [ ] Lead moved to no-show pipeline stage in CRM
- [ ] Archived no-shows worked after this week's are done

_SOP: [No Shows and Maximizing Show Rates](no-shows-maximizing-show-rates-setter-levers.md)_

---

## Priority 7 — Call Center Work (B2C Leads)

Only when P1–P6 are fully exhausted for the current block.

- [ ] P1–P6 confirmed clear before starting
- [ ] Working B2C leads per call center script system

_SOP: [Call Center Script Factory SOP](../../client-fulfillment/call-center/sop-call-center-script-factory.md)_

---

## Training Block (Sacred — Do Not Skip)

- [ ] Training completed today

---

## EOD Checklist (Complete at Clock-Out)

**Admin:**
- [ ] Setter KPI tracker filled out
- [ ] Calendar for next 3–4 days audited — unqualified/low quality bookings marked FREE
- [ ] Pipeline organized — fake leads moved to lost, stuck leads on call-book stages moved
- [ ] Hotlist followed up

**Metrics to report:**
- [ ] Total dials made
- [ ] Total conversations
- [ ] Booked demos (from outbound)
- [ ] Calls confirmed

**Roll-up:**
- [ ] Full call roll-up written — every call logged with prospect name, outcome, next step
- [ ] Objections / misaligned expectations from today noted

**EOD form submitted to sales channel.**
