# Setter Lead Messaging (SMS)

## Purpose

Give the setter **value-first** SMS patterns and word-tracking frames for loan officer prospects — without a rigid day-by-day automation calendar. Use with [Watchshift SMS rules](sop-watchshift.md#sms-responses-pre-demo) and [Power Dialer](sop-power-dialer-new-leads.md).

## Scope

- Pre-demo SMS (setter-owned unless [Watchshift escalation](sop-watchshift.md#sms-responses-pre-demo) applies).
- Follow-up after calls (dialer, watchshift, intro).
- B2B tone: peer-to-peer with **busy LOs / owners** — ROI and relevance fast; not consumer hype.

## Owner

See [domain owners](../../_inventory/domain-owners.md): **setter**.

---

## Before you send anything

**Every text** — reply, bump, or first touch:

1. Open the **lead record** in GHL (notes, pipeline stage, tasks).
2. Read **what they submitted** on the form (volume, market, pain, source).
3. Read **full SMS/call history** — do not repeat yourself or ignore what they already said.
4. Add **CRM notes** after every live conversation (call or meaningful text thread) — what was said, stage, next step. Calling without notes is incomplete work.

If the lead has **never been texted** after a call or inbound interest → **send a value-based follow-up** (do not leave them call-only). See [After a call](#after-a-call-no-prior-text).

---

## Core rules (non-negotiable)

| Do | Don’t |
|----|--------|
| Lead with **value, relevance, or a sharp question** about their business | “Did you get my message?” / “Just checking in” / empty bumps |
| Reference **their** form answers or prior messages | Generic blast copy |
| One clear idea per text; short paragraphs | Long objection essays over SMS |
| Steer toward **call or book** when momentum is there | Pitch full DFY offer over text |
| Log notes + stage after the touch | Move to next lead with no CRM update |

**Compliance:** No guaranteed results, no misleading claims. Proof = approved case studies / [pre-call videos](../marketing/pre-call-objection-videos.md) only.

---

## Value frameworks (pick one per message)

Use one frame per text. Personalize with their form data and market.

### 1. Micro case study

> We helped a [similar LO / shop] go from [problem] to [result] in [timeframe]. Reminded me of what you put on the form about [their detail]. Happy to share what we did if useful.

### 2. Industry insight

> Quick thought — seeing a lot of [HECM shops / LO teams] struggle with [specific bottleneck] right now while [trend]. Is that on your radar?

### 3. Free tool / audit (only if we actually offer it)

> Put together a short [audit / breakdown] for LOs doing [volume/stage]. Shows where [booking flow / lead quality] is leaking. Want me to send it?

### 4. Contrarian take (use carefully, must be true)

> Most LOs think [common belief]. What we’re seeing with teams on [channel] is [reality]. Happy to unpack on a quick call if you want.

### 5. Pattern interrupt question

> What’s the **one** thing in your [bookings / lead flow / calendar] you’d fix in the next 90 days if you could?

## Example text library

Ready-to-adapt SMS for **reverse mortgage LO prospects** (Waiz acquisition ICP). Sources: [Identity Core](../../company/doctrine-identity-core-april-26.md), [Money Model buyer profiles](../../company/overview-money-model-april-26.md), [WM Sales Intelligence Bible](../intelligence/wm-sales-intelligence-bible.md), [Objection Categories](wm-objection-categories.md), [LinkedIn copy angles](../outbound/linkedin/copy-angles.md).

**Rules:** Swap bracketed fields from the lead file. One framework per message. No pricing on SMS — escalate to Gabriel. No guaranteed unit counts unless ops approves exact wording.

### A. Speed-to-lead (0–5 min)

**Form opt-in / resource download**

> Hey [Name], saw you grabbed [resource]. Quick one — are you mainly trying to fix [referral volume / lead quality / show rate] right now, or something else? Want to point you the right way.

**Booked intro or strategy call**

> Hey [Name], [Rep] from Waiz — saw you booked [time]. Before we hop on: what made you reach out now — [slow referrals / bad agency experience / empty calendar]?

**30-min bump (only if no other value sent yet)**

> No pressure [Name] — a lot of reverse LOs I talk to right now are stuck on [referrals drying up / leads that don’t answer / can’t fill the calendar]. Is that the headache for you too?

### B. Micro case study (Framework 1)

> Hey [Name] — we’re working with a reverse shop that was basically living on referrals. They got consistent **qualified conversations** booked on the calendar without adding another “lead list” to babysit. Reminded me of your note about [form detail]. Want the breakdown?

> [Name] — LO in [state/region] similar size to you fixed the back end first (speed-to-lead + show rate), then scaled ads. They’re not chasing cold names anymore. Relevant to what you put on the form about [pain]?

> Saw your form — you mentioned [production goal]. Team we support went from feast/famine referrals to waking up to **appointments**, not spreadsheets of leads. Happy to share what changed on a quick call if useful.

### C. Industry insight (Framework 2)

> Quick thought [Name] — rates get blamed for everything, but the LOs still winning in reverse right now are the ones with **conversation volume** locked in, not the ones waiting on referrals. Is that on your radar?

> [Name] — seeing a split in the niche: shops still marketing “reverse mortgage” in the headline vs education-first (home equity / retirement security). The second bucket’s pulling better **intent**. You testing either side?

> A lot of producers tell us the market feels dead — but it’s often a **pipeline math** problem (not enough qualified convos), not zero demand. Does that match your world or is it something else?

### D. Audit / breakdown offer (Framework 3)

> [Name] — if you’re open to it I can have the team sketch where [booking flow / speed-to-lead / show rate] is leaking based on what you shared on the form. No pitch — just clarity. Want me to send it?

> Put together a short breakdown for LOs doing [X units / referral-heavy shops] on why leads die before the calendar. Takes ~5 min to review. Useful?

### E. Contrarian take (Framework 4)

> Most LOs think they need **more leads**. What we’re seeing is more shops drowning in names they never reach in time — fix contact + qualification first, then volume. Worth a 10-min call if that’s your situation?

> [Name] — common belief: “Facebook doesn’t work for mortgages.” Usually it’s **wrong product framing + no call center**, not the channel. Curious if that’s what burned you before or something different.

### F. Pattern interrupt (Framework 5)

> Random question [Name] — if you could fix **one** thing in the next 90 days (calendar fill, lead quality, or show rate), which would move the needle most?

> [Name] — yes or no: is building a **predictable** pipe outside referrals still a priority this quarter, or are you heads-down with what you have?

### G. After a call (no prior text on file)

> Hey [Name], [Rep] from Waiz — good talking through [their pain from notes]. You mentioned [motivator from call] — sending [pre-call video / case study] that hits that. Still good for [booked time] or want an earlier slot?

> [Name] — thanks for the few minutes earlier. Next step is [intro with me / demo with Gabriel] so you can see how the **full system** (not just ads) fits [their goal]. Does [day/time] work?

### H. Steer to book (short — use when thread is warm)

> Easiest path is a 15-min intro so we’re not playing text tag — I’ve got [time] or [time] open. Which works?

> Happy to send proof over text, but a quick call is faster to see if we’re even a fit for [state/volume]. Want me to ring you now or book a slot?

### I. No-reply bumps (new value only)

**Proof drop (pre-call video)**

> [Name] — thought of you. Short video on [objection they likely have — agency burn / lead quality / how it works]: [prospect_page_url from Pre-Call Objection Videos]. No strings — just relevant if [topic] is still live.

**Direct check**

> [Name] — still trying to solve [referral dependency / calendar gaps / lead follow-up] or did the market win for now? Either answer is fine — just helps me know if I should close the loop.

**Graceful close**

> Hey [Name] — I’ll get out of your hair. If **qualified conversations on the calendar** becomes a priority later, I’m here. Hope the pipeline treats you well this quarter.

### J. Objection-flavored (steer to call — do not full-reframe over SMS)

| They said (theme) | Example SMS |
|-----------------|-------------|
| Burned by agency ([Bible 01](wm-objection-categories.md)) | Makes sense you’re skeptical — most shops got **leads without infrastructure**. Worth 10 min to see if your situation was ads vs back-end? |
| “Need to think about it” | Totally fair — what part do you need to think through: **fit**, **timing**, or something else? Easier on a quick call than over text. |
| “Market’s slow / rates” ([Bible 03](wm-objection-categories.md)) | A lot of LOs say that — the ones still closing usually fixed **volume**, not waiting on rates. Is that worth exploring or off the table? |
| “I live on referrals” ([Bible 06](wm-objection-categories.md)) | Referrals are great until they’re not — most guys we talk to want **one predictable channel** alongside them. Is that a conversation you’d entertain? |

## After a call (no prior text)

If you spoke with them and there’s **no SMS on the file**:

1. Note the call in CRM (motivators, FUN snapshot, objection if any).
2. Send **one** value text within the same block or watchshift window — reference the call:

> Hey [Name], [Rep] from Waiz — good chatting about [their pain from call]. Sending [one proof/asset/link if appropriate]. Easiest next step is [book intro / hold slot] — does [time] work or want a different window?

3. If no reply, next texts use **new value** (framework table), not “following up again.”

---

## When to call instead of text

- Booking is one step away.
- Objection needs tone ([Objection Handling Hub](objection-handling-hub.md)) — text steers to call.
- Thread is heated or confused → call, then summarize in notes.

During power dialer: reply, then return to dialing per [checklist texting rules](setter-daily-checklist.md#texting-rules-all-priorities).

---

## Optional bump patterns (value required)

Use only when prior messages had **no** reply and you have **new** value (case study, video, insight) — not a fixed “Day 5 / Day 7” calendar.

| Situation | Copy angle |
|-----------|------------|
| Direct check | Short yes/no on whether [outcome] is still a priority this quarter — respect their time |
| Proof drop | Link to [pre-call video](../marketing/pre-call-objection-videos.md) or case study — “thought of you, no strings” |
| Graceful close | Timing matters; door open if [bookings/leads] becomes priority — professional sign-off |

Avoid fake scarcity (“only X spots”) unless ops provides real capacity language.

---

## Pipeline + handoff

- Strong pre-demo, not booked → stage **Setter quality lead** ([Watchshift SOP](sop-watchshift.md#sms-responses-pre-demo)).
- Needs Gabriel’s voice or high-value question → tag Gabriel; **do not** reply.
- Qualified → book intro or demo per [Intro Call Script](script-intro-call-basic.md).
- Demo on calendar → confirmations per [Demo Appointment Confirmation Script](script-demo-appointment-confirmation.md) (phone first; SMS patterns in § backup there).
