# Pre-Intro Follow-Up Messaging

## Purpose

Automated and manual follow-up copy for leads **before** the intro (discovery) call. Single job: **get them to show up** and **build trust** — not presale, not process education.

Pre-demo follow-up is a separate playbook (expectations, objections, process — see [Intro Call Qualification Framework](intro-call-qualification-framework.md) for demo framing).

## Scope

- Inbound and outbound leads with a **booked intro** on the calendar
- Thank-you / confirmation page messaging after booking
- Email, SMS, and setter manual touchpoints through show or no-show
- Asset mapping for Hammer Them / nurture videos (trust and proof only)

**Out of scope:** Full process walkthrough, pricing deep dives, demo pre-call guide, Hammer Them **presale** sequence (post-intro → pre-demo).

## Trigger

Lead books an intro call (GHL calendar, setter book, or self-book from ad/landing page).

## Inputs

- Lead first name, LO business name, intro date/time, timezone
- Setter name and callback number
- Links: pre-intro expectation video, 1–2 case studies/testimonials, social profiles
- Thank-you page Q&A blocks (differentiators, mission — belief only)

## Outputs

- Confirmed attendance (calendar accepted + verbal/text confirmation)
- Prospect arrives with basic trust in Waiz and clear intro expectations
- No-show → [No Shows And Maximizing Show Rates](no-shows-maximizing-show-rates-setter-levers.md) pipeline

## Strategic Frame (Psychology)

| Principle | Pre-intro application |
|-----------|------------------------|
| **Low pressure / qualification frame** | Intro is a short discovery conversation — see if there is fit for a deeper strategy session with the growth consultant |
| **Social proof / authority** | Case studies, LO stories, “why we’re different” — they have not spoken to your team yet |
| **Loss aversion (light)** | Missing the call = missing clarity on their pipeline problem, not “losing a deal” |
| **Regret aversion** | Confirmations reduce “I forgot” guilt; setter calls feel helpful, not chase-y |
| **Mere exposure** | Hammer content between book and call — familiarity before the setter speaks |
| **Do not use** | Process breakdown, pricing anchors, objection demolition (save for pre-demo Hammer) |

## Touchpoint Timeline

| When | Channel | Owner | Job |
|------|---------|-------|-----|
| Instant | SMS + email | Automation | Confirm book, set intro frame, link thank-you page |
| +2–4 hours | SMS or email | Automation | One proof asset (case study or story) |
| 24 hr before | Email + SMS | Automation | Reminder + “why show up” |
| 4 hr before | SMS | Automation | Short reminder (align with fulfillment reminder audit) |
| 2.5 hr before | Phone | Setter | Confirm + double tie-down |
| 60 min before | Phone or SMS | Setter / automation | Final confirm if no contact at 2.5 hr |
| 30 min before | SMS | Automation | Optional: “see you soon” + link |
| No-show | Calls + SMS + email | Setter + dialer | Rebook daily per no-show SOP |

## Page Copy — Pre-Intro Thank You / Confirmation

Use after calendar book. **Belief and trust only** — keep existing Q&A (“what makes us different,” “why we do this”).

### Headline

**You're booked — here's how to get the most out of your call**

**Alternatives**

- **Option A:** Your discovery call is confirmed — read this before we talk *(specific, low pressure)*
- **Option B:** You're in — quick prep for your call with Waiz Media *(qualification frame)*
- **Option C:** Before we connect: who we are and what to expect *(trust-first)*

### Subheadline

This is a short conversation to understand your business and see if it makes sense to book a deeper strategy session with our team. Watch the 3-minute prep video below so we can use your time well.

### Primary CTA (on-page)

**Watch the 3-minute prep video**

### Section: What to expect on this call (not the demo)

**Header:** What happens on your intro call

- 15–20 minutes with a Waiz Media team member
- Questions about your pipeline, what you've tried, and where you want to be
- No pitch deck — if there's a fit, we'll book a separate strategy session with a growth consultant
- You leave with clarity either way

### Section: Why show up

**Header:** Why this call is worth your time

You clicked because something in your business isn't consistent yet — leads, appointments, or both. This call is to see if we're the right partner to fix that, not to sell you on the first ring.

### Section: Q&A (keep on page)

- What makes Waiz Media different
- Why we only work with reverse mortgage LOs
- Who we are / founder story (credibility)
- Testimonials / case study embeds (rotate 2–3)

**Do not include:** Step-by-step fulfillment process, ad account structure, pricing tiers, or “how the system works” video — reserve for demo track.

## Automated Messaging — Full Copy

Replace `[NAME]`, `[DATE]`, `[TIME]`, `[TZ]`, `[SETTER]`, `[PHONE]`, `[LINK_*]`.

### T0 — Instant confirmation (SMS)

```
[NAME] — you're booked with Waiz Media for [DATE] at [TIME] [TZ].

This is a quick discovery call (not a sales pitch). We'll learn your situation and see if you're a fit for a deeper strategy session.

Confirm here: [CALENDAR_LINK]

Prep (2 min): [LINK_PRE_INTRO_VIDEO]
```

### T0 — Instant confirmation (email)

**Subject:** You're booked — quick prep for your Waiz Media call

**Body:**

Hi [NAME],

You're confirmed for **[DATE] at [TIME] [TZ]**.

**What this call is:** A short discovery conversation. We'll ask about your pipeline, what you've tried, and your goals. If it looks like a fit, we'll get you on with [CLOSER ROLE] for a full marketing strategy session. If not, you'll still leave with a clearer picture of your next move.

**What this call is not:** A demo or a pressure close.

**Before you hop on (3 minutes):** [LINK_PRE_INTRO_VIDEO]

**See who we are:** [LINK_THANK_YOU_PAGE]

Reply to this email if you need to reschedule.

— [SETTER]  
Waiz Media

### T1 — +2–4 hours (email or SMS — social proof)

**Email subject:** What happened when [SIMILAR LO] fixed [PROBLEM]

**Email body:**

Hi [NAME],

Quick story before your call on [DATE].

[LO NAME / anonymized] came to us with the same bottleneck you mentioned — [PROBLEM: inconsistent leads / bad lead quality / referral dependency]. [1–2 sentences of outcome — use approved case study only.]

That's the kind of result we look for when we take someone on. Not a promise for your call — just context for who we are.

See you [DATE] at [TIME].

[LINK_CASE_STUDY]

— Waiz Media

**SMS variant:**

```
[NAME] — before your call [DATE]: short win story from an LO like you. [LINK_CASE_STUDY] (2 min). Helps us not waste your time on the intro.
```

### T2 — 24 hours before (email)

**Subject:** Tomorrow at [TIME] — your Waiz Media intro call

Reminder: we're on for **tomorrow, [DATE] at [TIME] [TZ]**.

Prep video: [LINK_PRE_INTRO_VIDEO]

```

```

```

### T4 — 30 minutes before (SMS — automation backup)

```

## Setter Manual Scripts

### 2.5 hours before — confirmation call

**Voicemail:**

```
Hey [NAME], [SETTER] from Waiz Media. Quick confirm for your intro at [TIME] today — not a sales call, just making sure you're still good and have the Zoom link. Call me back at [PHONE] if you need to move it.
```

**Live answer:**

```
Hey [NAME], it's [SETTER] from Waiz Media — you still good for [TIME] today?

[Wait]

Perfect. Just a heads up, this isn't a big pitch call. It's 15–20 minutes so we can understand your business and see if you're a fit for a strategy session with [CLOSER]. Did you get a chance to watch the short prep video we sent?

[If yes:] Great, that'll save us time.

[If no:] No stress — I'll text it again. It's 3 minutes on what to expect.

So you're locked in for [TIME] — you'll be at your computer with the calendar open?

[Double tie-down per no-show SOP]

Awesome. See you shortly.
```

**If no answer:** Send SMS within 5 minutes:

```
[NAME] — tried you for your [TIME] intro confirm. Still on? Reply YES or call [PHONE]. Prep: [LINK_PRE_INTRO_VIDEO]
```

### 60 minutes before — second attempt

Only if **no contact** at 2.5 hr (no answer, no text reply).

**Call script:** Same as 2.5 hr, shorter.

**If still no answer — SMS:**

```
[NAME] — intro in 1 hour ([TIME]). Join link in your calendar. Text YES if you're in, or CALL if you need to reschedule.
```

Do not cancel the slot automatically unless your ops rule says so — goal is confirm, not purge.

## Hammer Them Asset Map (Pre-Intro Only)

Rotate assets so repeat touches do not feel identical. Tag each asset in your library:

| Asset type | Use pre-intro? | Example placement |
|------------|----------------|-------------------|
| Founder / why Waiz | Yes | Thank-you page, email 1 |
| What makes us different (no how) | Yes | Thank-you Q&A |
| LO testimonial (video or written) | Yes | T1, thank-you page |
| Case study / before-after story | Yes | T1 email, IG DM if unresponsive |
| Industry education (reverse stigma, etc.) | Yes | Optional email between T1–T2 |
| Social proof compilation | Yes | 24hr email P.S. |
| Process / fulfillment walkthrough | **No** | Pre-demo only |
| Pricing / investment breakdown | **No** | Demo only |
| Objection-handling deep dives | **No** | Pre-demo only |

**Hammer intensity:** 1 email + 1 SMS minimum between book and call; add 1 proof touch if booking is 48+ hours out. Do not exceed 4 automated touches day-of (plus setter calls).

## No-Show Messaging (Intro)

After live no-show protocol in [No Shows And Maximizing Show Rates](no-shows-maximizing-show-rates-setter-levers.md), use these templates in dialer + automation.

### Same day — SMS 1 (Jeremy Miner style)

```
Hey [NAME], it's [SETTER] from Waiz Media. We had your intro at [TIME] to talk through getting you more consistent [deals/conversations] — tried you a few times. Where should we go from here?
```

### Same day — SMS 2 (+10 min)

```
[NAME] — called a few times. Want to reschedule the intro, or should I close your file?
```

### Day 2–7 — daily setter call + rotating email subjects

- **Day 2:** Still want clarity on your pipeline? — [rebook link]
- **Day 3:** [CASE STUDY] — 3 min watch, then pick a new time
- **Day 4:** Quick question — [NAME]
- **Day 5:** Last open slot this week for intro calls
- **Day 6–7:** Long-term nurture handoff per CRM rules

**Email body (rebook):**

Hi [NAME],

We held a spot for you at [ORIGINAL_TIME] and didn't connect.

The intro call is still the right first step — 15 minutes, no pitch, just to see if a full strategy session makes sense.

Grab a new time here: [REBOOK_LINK]

Or reply with two times that work.

— [SETTER]

## Quality Bar

- Every message reinforces: **discovery, not demo**; **they may qualify for next step**
- Zero process reveals pre-intro
- Proof claims only from approved case studies (no fabricated stats)
- Setter confirmation calls logged in CRM
- Tone: professional, direct, reverse-LO insider language — see [Identity Core](../../company/doctrine-identity-core-april-26.md)

## Metrics

- Intro show rate (setter-attributed)
- % calendar invites accepted within 24 hr of book
- % reached on 2.5 hr confirmation call
- No-show → rebook rate within 7 days
