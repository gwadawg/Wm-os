## Demo Call Script
### Internal Use Only  |  Sales  |  Updated: April 2026
📌  The One Job of This Call: CLOSE the prospect on the core DFY retainer. Discovery is a means to that end — every question builds toward the pitch.

## CONNECTION
Hey, [NAME], how's it going? It's great to virtually meet you.

### Curious, whereabouts are you calling from?
## [ ENGAGE IN HUMAN SMALL TALK — CONNECT WITH THEM ]
### Cool, well are you ready if we dive right in?
## FRAME
## High-Level Agenda
I reviewed some of the notes [SDR] left me, so I've got a high-level overview of your situation. What I think makes the most sense here — if you're okay with it — is getting a clearer picture of what's going on in your business.

### Context (Why?)
For context, we're not your typical lead gen company. We're a boutique agency, so everything we do is custom to the particular LO we're working with and their unique market and needs.

## Clear Agenda
Really, this is a question of: what bottlenecks are you facing, where are you trying to get to — and then we can craft your game plan together. And if it's not a right fit, no harm no foul, I'll point you in a different direction.

### Buy-In (Permission)
### Is that fair? Is it cool if I ask you some questions?
## DISCOVERY
### Stage 1 — HELL (The Problem)
📋  Surface the challenge, why it's happening, and the impact. Get them talking — listen more than you speak. Do NOT offer solutions yet.

## Identify Problem
Cool, well I'll go ahead and put the ball back in your court...

### What's going on in your business that has us talking? How can I help?
### [ Listen & probe — get clear on the specific problem plaguing their business ]
## Context Questions
Okay, let's zoom out...

### For context, what are the loan amounts you're working with?
### And about how much do you make per deal? Two and a half percent? Three percent?
### You're working with a lender/broker?
### Okay, so you make roughly $[X] per deal, yeah?
And how many sales calls have you taken in the last 2 weeks with POTENTIAL clients?

### And out of those, how many proposals did you send out?
### Is that normal for you? About [X] proposals sent per month?
### [ X = total deals in last 2 weeks x 2 ]
## Timeline
### Got it... how long has that been the case?
## Impact
Where do you feel like you SHOULD have been by this point in your business/career?

### [ Alternative for low-volume LOs: ]
### When you became an LO, where were you expecting you'd be by now?
Man... so if you should be making $[X]/month, but you're currently only doing $[X]/month — that means we're missing out on $[X]/month...

### Gotcha, and do you think that discrepancy is due to [PROBLEM]?
### Stage 2 — BRIDGES (What They've Tried)
📋  Identify and eliminate all alternatives. Listen for frustrations — these are your future reframe ammunition.

### Okay, where are most of those leads coming from?
Is that scalable for you? Like, do you feel that lead source will get you where you wanna be?

### Okay, do you have any other lead sources?
### How are those working for you?
### [ Repeat as needed until all lead sources are covered ]
### What else have you tried?
How'd those go? / What's the reason you're not still doing it? / Why do you think it didn't work?

### Got it. What else?
### [ Repeat the above until ALL options are exhausted ]
### I mean, have you tried doing things yourself?
### Why not?
What else could you try? Say if we're not the right fit — what would be the next step?

### Why haven't you been doing that up to this point?
### [ Repeat until ALL future alternatives are also burned ]
### Stage 3 — HEAVEN (The Goal)
📋  Identify where they want to go. Anchor on a specific number. Get the personal 'why' behind it.

Okay, so you're currently at [X] — but we can't grow unless we get quality leads... so what's the goal if you do get a steady flow? If you had as many people to talk to as you wanted, how many loans would you want to be closing monthly — say, 90 days from now?

Okay, and what about long term? 12 months down the road, where do you wanna be? What do you wanna be doing?

### [ Capture: how much they make per transaction ]
Do you think you would be able to get there doing what you're doing? Or do you need something different?

Okay, so what exactly do you feel you need in a company to make it unreasonable for you NOT to hit [X] deals/month?

### Stage 4 — FUEL (Commitment & Urgency)
📋  Tie down their urgency using their own numbers. Make the cost of inaction real and concrete.

Okay, so I want to go back to the numbers we spoke about...

### You said you were losing $[X]/month to [PROBLEM], right?
### And that's been the case for [X] months, right?
So I want to make sure I have this right... that means in essence, you've 'spent' $[X] in opportunity cost because this problem hasn't been solved... do I have that right?

[ If needed: coach them that opportunity cost is a REAL cost in business — that's why it's called a cost ]

### Have you ever looked at those numbers before?
### What do you think about that?
### Are you committed to making a change to those numbers?
### How quickly?
Why is that? Some folks are content staying where they're at. I'm not getting that vibe from you — but help me understand why it's important for you.

⏸  PAUSE — Is there ANYTHING you noticed in this conversation that could prevent them from moving forward on this call? If YES, address the obstacle directly. If CONFIDENT they'd buy, move on.

## LABEL — CONFIRM YOUR UNDERSTANDING
📋  Reflect their situation back in their own words. This builds trust, confirms you listened, and gets them to re-affirm their own problem out loud.

Gotcha — I just want to make sure I'm understanding you correctly...

You need to go from [CURRENT DEALS + SITUATION] to [DESIRED DEALS + SITUATION]...

But haven't been able to because of [PROBLEM]...

And you've tried [PAST/CURRENT BRIDGES], but those don't work because [REASONS]...

And you can't [FUTURE BRIDGES] because [REASONS]...

So you just need [WHAT THEY NEED IN A COMPANY], and if you had that, you'd for sure hit [DESIRED DEALS]...

And you don't really wanna put it off anymore because [FUEL].

### Am I understanding you correctly, [NAME]?
### [ Wait for their response — do not rush past this ]
### Are you cool if I share my thoughts with you?
## OFFER — PRE-PITCH
## Certainty — Grant Confidence You Can Help
Honestly, it's around this point in the call where I would cut things off if this wasn't a good fit. There are many LOs we turn away because we're not the right fit — so we're selective, to maintain our own reputation.

But based on everything you've shared, I know for a fact that we can help you. Not just because of the listings thing — we give people listings for breakfast. It's because of the mindsets you've shown right here in our conversation.

## Reasons — Plant Mindsets for Success
Most of the LOs we don't move forward with have a couple things in common.

You ever met LOs that just aren't willing to put in the work? Well, we run into them too. They want something where they kick their feet up and money rolls in — some sort of get-rich-quick scheme. This is not that. To succeed in this industry requires hard work, learning new ways of doing business, taking uncomfortable leaps, and following a process. Would you agree?

And then there are LOs who think they know it all. They're not coachable. They wanna do everything their way and don't adapt to the market. And what do you think happens to those guys?

Exactly. They die off.

But in this conversation, I can see that's not you. You seem coachable, ready for change, and ready to put in the work — you just need the right people to talk to.

## Lean-Out — Let THEM Claim It
But I could be totally wrong here... you tell me.

## Permission — Buy-In to Move On
Well, like I said, I know exactly what we'd need to do to get you to [GOAL]. If you'd like, we can go through those details and talk about what that would look like.

## CONSULTATION — THE PITCH
📋  Adjust this section to who you're speaking to. Find what resonates with them. Emphasize the parts of the program that match their pain.

### Actually, have you got a pen and paper?
### Phase 1 — The Hook & High-Level Overview
Great. Go ahead and write the numbers one through four down the side of the page. We'll use this to map out the path to scaling your business.

So in a nutshell, our entire focus is on helping reverse mortgage loan officers like you build predictable, modern businesses. We do that by installing a complete client acquisition engine — a steady flow of qualified, educated prospects — and providing the infrastructure to convert them into closed deals.

For you, based on what you've shared, there are a few key areas we'd need to solve for.

## Phase 2 — The 4-Step Framework
## Step 1 — The Volume Imperative
The first thing we need to solve for — next to number one on your page — is Volume.

Think of your business like a pipeline. If there's no water coming through, or just a few random drops, nothing else matters. The single biggest constraint for any loan officer — especially in the reverse space — isn't your skill or the interest rates. It's the sheer volume of qualified conversations you're having each month.

You mentioned you've tried [mention what they tried, e.g., buying leads, referrals]. The problem with those methods is that they're unpredictable and don't address the real issue.

The real problem is that decades of misinformation have created a huge stigma around reverse mortgages. Most people who could benefit from one don't even know it — or they've been told it's a scam. So running a generic ad that says 'Apply for a Reverse Mortgage' is like shouting into the wind.

The solution is to change the first impression at scale. Instead of marketing the product, we market the solution to the homeowner's problems — like funding retirement or eliminating mortgage payments. This is our first core mechanism: Educational Video Content Marketing.

We create high-impact, educational video campaigns branded to you. These videos debunk myths and show real-world benefits, reaching a massive, untapped audience who would never respond to a traditional reverse mortgage ad. We're creating a new market of educated, interested prospects.

Do you see how that's fundamentally different from just buying a list of names or running a basic ad?

### [ Pause for questions. Confirm understanding. ]
## Step 2 — The Quality Filter
So now the key is making sure these leads are actually quality. Next to number two, write Quality.

You'd said you worked with [XYZ company], and the leads were [XYZ quality], right? It's arguably worse than having no one to talk to — because now you're wasting time and money on leads that aren't even qualified.

The issue is, those companies were missing a CRUCIAL piece of the puzzle.

ℹ️  Use what applies to their situation:• Native lead forms — low-intent, sometimes bots, autofill contact info• Lead aggregators — same lead sold to multiple LOs• Low-Intent Prospects — people who don't have real intent

Now that we have leads running through that pipe, we need to make sure you're only getting the people who are actually serious. So what do we do? We put a net at the end of the pipe to filter out the real leads from the trash. This is Step 2: qualifying.

We do this by taking each lead through a high-converting quiz funnel — intentionally creating friction before a lead enters your CRM, so only the serious ones make it through.

Most lead gen companies just ask for name, email, and phone. We take leads to a multi-step, manual-fill quiz — asking about age, home equity, existing mortgage, goals, timeline — all before you ever speak with them.

And for the leads that really want to connect quickly — about 1 in every 3 — our AI engages in a conversation with them, nurtures them, and books them directly onto your calendar.

So you wake up in the morning, look at your phone, and you've got 3 qualified homeowners booked and ready to speak with you.

Make sense? Do you think this is in line with what you'd need to hit [GOAL]? Why or why not?

### [ Pause for questions. Confirm understanding. ]
### Step 3 — The Response & Follow-Up Engine
But here's the thing — and this is critical — just because there's a high volume of quality leads doesn't guarantee they'll answer when you call. Next to number three, write Response Rates.

You can't possibly work leads like we can, with the team and software we have to contact them fastest and most efficiently.

Most companies just give you leads and say 'Good luck, go fish.' You're dialing numbers, leaving voicemails, sending texts — and you don't have the bandwidth to maximize every single lead.

That's where we're completely different. We have a dedicated team of reverse mortgage sales agents that dial and follow up with your leads for you.

This team knows the reverse mortgage product inside and out. They're trained on objection handling, building rapport with seniors, and getting people on the phone. They're calling your leads within minutes of them coming in, using proven scripts and sequences we've tested over and over.

This dramatically increases your response rates. Our team does the heavy lifting — they qualify them further, warm them up, and either book them onto your calendar or facilitate a live transfer when they're hot and ready.

You're not just getting leads — you're getting leads that have been worked by a professional team, maximizing the value of every single prospect.

Does that make sense? Can you see how having a team working your leads would completely change your conversion rates?

### [ Pause for questions. Confirm understanding. ]
## Step 4 — The Business Owner Model
So once you have a consistent flow of high-quality leads, and a team maximizing response rates and bookings, we get to the final step — next to number four: becoming a true Business Owner.

Right now, you're likely operating as a practitioner. You're doing everything yourself. But to truly scale and achieve the freedom you want, you can't rely on just your own efforts. You need to own the system — not just work in it.

This is where we shift from being a service to being your strategic partner. We help you take our acquisition engine and turn it into the foundation of your own 'mini-retail' operation. We provide the systems, the reporting, and the strategic guidance to help you dominate your market.

This is how you go from being dependent on referrals and brokers to being in complete control of your destiny. You own the direct-to-consumer channel. You have a predictable, scalable business that works for you — not the other way around.

This is the freedom you were looking for when you first became a loan officer.
