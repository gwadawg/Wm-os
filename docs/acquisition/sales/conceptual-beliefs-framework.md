# Conceptual Beliefs Framework

> **North star:** Before a prospect can decide, they must hold seven internal beliefs — checked through discovery, not interrogation — so the pain of waiting feels worse than the pain of acting.

Universal sales framework for **Waiz team training** and product playbooks. Product-specific lines live in client playbooks (e.g. [RM Conceptual Beliefs Playbook](../../client-fulfillment/client-sales/playbook-rm-conceptual-beliefs.md)).

---

## Purpose

Give setters, closers, and fulfillment coaches a shared map of **prospect-side beliefs** — what must be true in the buyer's mind before a close is realistic, and how to uncover them without running a checklist interrogation.

This is **not** LO limiting beliefs (internal mindset). It is **not** objection word tracks. It is the belief architecture underneath both.

---

## The seven beliefs

| # | Belief | One-line test |
|---|--------|---------------|
| 1 | **Pain / Problem** | They admit a real problem blocking their ideal state — not a casual "nice to have" |
| 2 | **Doubt** | They believe your solution *and you* can get them there vs alternatives |
| 3 | **Cost of inaction** | Pain of doing nothing > pain of acting now (**action threshold** crossed) |
| 4 | **Desire** | You know the emotional outcome behind the surface goal |
| 5 | **Support** | Decision makers are known and ideally involved |
| 6 | **Trust** | They see you as advisor, not vendor |
| 7 | **Money** | Outcome value > cost / friction of proceeding |

---

## Belief definitions

### 1. Pain / Problem

The prospect has self-awareness of a defined problem. Defense mechanisms minimize pain ("just browsing," "not urgent"). Your job: help them **feel** the problem through qualifying questions — income, timeline, consequences — until they articulate need in their own words.

**Psychology:** Loss aversion — people move to avoid pain more than to gain equivalent upside.

### 2. Doubt

Two layers: (a) **solution doubt** — wrong product, wrong path, can solve it themselves; (b) **advisor doubt** — shopping, past bad experiences, trust another provider more.

Resolved through education, comparison over time (not feature dumps), and advisor differentiation.

### 3. Cost of inaction (action threshold)

The prospect must cross the **action threshold**: current + future pain of **status quo** exceeds pain of fees, timing, or change.

**Mechanisms:**

- Forward risk — what gets worse if nothing changes
- Backward cost — what waiting already cost (time, money, stress)
- Values anchor — tie inaction to what they protect most (security, reputation, family, asset)

**Psychology:** Loss framing often outperforms gain framing at equal magnitude.

### 4. Desire

Surface goals ("save money," "get cash," "remove a payment") hide **emotional drivers** (peace, dignity, family, freedom, legacy). Capture these for pitch personalization — weave into mechanics, don't recite their story back robotically.

### 5. Support

Identify influencers (spouse, partner, business partner, family). If they must sell internally without you, close rates drop. Schedule joint conversations before burning a full pitch on a non-decider.

### 6. Trust

Earned throughout the call — curiosity, education including "no" paths, transparency, long-term advisor frame. Eliminates "I've been burned before" and category skepticism before they become objections.

### 7. Money

**Resources + willingness** — proceeding is worth friction (fees, appraisal, out-of-pocket). When value is obvious, people become resourceful; "can't afford" a small fee often means Cost/Desire unchecked, not literal inability. Compare outcome to friction using **their** validated pain — no guaranteed ROI claims.

---

## Operating principles

1. **Sprinkle, don't interrogate** — beliefs emerge from normal discovery, not seven sequential questions read aloud.
2. **Smoke screens are data** — "just looking" = belief gap, usually Pain or Cost of inaction.
3. **Earn the pitch** — unchecked Pain or Trust → more discovery, not premature presentation.
4. **Listen before you label** — decode surface vs emotional problem first.
5. **Bad answers ≠ dead leads** — build trust and re-approach.

## Call phase map

| Phase | Beliefs to prioritize |
|-------|----------------------|
| Open | Trust (start) |
| Discovery | Pain, Desire, Support |
| Education | Doubt, Cost of inaction, Trust |
| Pitch / close | Desire (weave), Money, Support confirm |

---

## Decision rules

| Condition | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Pain not checked | Stay in discovery; emotional follow-ups |
| Doubt weak | Compare solutions; advisor differentiation |
| Cost of inaction not checked | Values + timeline consequence questions |
| Desire unknown | Branch on outcomes they mention |
| Support gap | Book joint call; limit full pitch |
| Trust low | Slow down; education-first |
| Money friction | Outcome vs cost using their numbers |
| All checked | Personalized pitch + clear next step |
