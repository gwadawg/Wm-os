# Demo Appointment Confirmation Script

## Purpose

Word-for-word language for the setter to **confirm closer demo appointments** and **tie the prospect down to watching pre-call content** before the strategy/demo call. Manual phone confirmation is one of the highest-leverage show-rate levers (~30%+ lift when done consistently).

## Scope

- **Closer demo** on the calendar (post-intro, qualified for strategy/demo with Gabriel or assigned closer).
- **Not** intro qualification — use [Intro Call Script](script-intro-call-basic.md) for intros.
- **Not** closer discovery — do not pitch programs, pricing, or handle deep objections on this touch.

## Owner

See [domain owners](../../_inventory/domain-owners.md): **setter**.

## When to use

| Window | Action |
|--------|--------|
| **Morning of demo day** | Call every demo on today’s calendar (primary use of this script). |
| **24–48 hours before** | Same script; attempt to **move demo earlier** if a sooner slot opens ([show-rate levers](no-shows-maximizing-show-rates-setter-levers.md#manual-confirmation-protocol)). |
| **Unresponsive 24hrs out** | Phone first; then WhatsApp text + voice note, Instagram DM per [setter checklist P3](setter-daily-checklist.md#priority-3--confirm-demo-appointments-closer-calls). |

Daily priority: [Setter Daily Checklist — P3](setter-daily-checklist.md#priority-3--confirm-demo-appointments-closer-calls).

---

## Before you dial

1. Open **GHL** — lead notes, closer name, demo time, timezone, contact fields.
2. Note **pre-call video already sent** (which asset + link) from intro notes or CRM custom field.
3. Note **main objection / motivator** (their words) — use for content tie-down framing.
4. Have **one** [prospect page URL](../marketing/pre-call-objection-videos.md#quick-send-by-objection) ready if nothing was sent or the wrong video was sent.

**Do not** quote pricing, guarantees, or unit counts not on the approved offer. Proof = [pre-call videos](../marketing/pre-call-objection-videos.md) and approved case studies only.

## Call flow (in order)

Run top to bottom. Skip only what already happened on a prior confirmation call the same week.

### 1 — Opening

**Morning of (demo today):**

> Hey, [NAME]?
>
> Hey [NAME], it’s [SETTER NAME] from Waiz Media — quick call. You’re still good for **[TIME]** today with **[CLOSER NAME]** for the strategy call, right?

**24–48 hours out:**

> Hey, [NAME]?
>
> Hey [NAME], it’s [SETTER NAME] from Waiz Media. I’m calling to lock in your **[DAY]** at **[TIME]** with **[CLOSER NAME]** so nothing falls through the cracks and [CLOSER NAME] can come prepared. Got two minutes?

**If they push back (“why are you calling?”):**

> Totally fair — we do a quick confirmation before strategy calls so you’re not waiting on a no-show link and [CLOSER NAME] isn’t walking in cold. Saves you both time on the call. Cool if I run through it real quick?

---

### 2 — Double tie-down (time + commitment)

> So you’re confirmed for **[DAY] at [TIME] [TIMEZONE]** — you’ll be there, right?

> Perfect. And you’ll have your **calendar blocked** and be at your **computer** for the Zoom — usually about 45 minutes, but block an hour so you’ve got room for questions. Sound good?

**If vague (“should be fine” / “maybe”):**

> I want to make sure we’re not guessing — is there anything that would realistically pull you away at that time, or are we locked?

📋 If they reveal a conflict → reschedule on the **closer calendar** while on the phone (BAMFAM). Update GHL and re-run content tie-down for the new time.

**Optional — push earlier** (especially 24–48hrs out):

> Actually — a slot just opened **[TOMORROW MORNING / TODAY AFTERNOON]** at **[TIME]**. Would that work better for you?

---

### 3 — Calendar invite check

> Did you get the calendar invite for **[DATE AND TIME]**? Could you **accept it** on your end so nothing double-books over it?

**If no / wrong email:**

- Fix contact in GHL on the call.
- Resend invite; confirm they see it before you hang up.

---

### 4 — Pre-call content tie-down

**Goal:** They verbally commit to watching **before** the demo. Use the video that matches their gap — [quick send by objection](../marketing/pre-call-objection-videos.md#quick-send-by-objection). Always send **prospect_page_url**, not YouTube.

#### A — Video already sent at intro (re-tie)

> When we booked you, I sent that short video on **[TOPIC — e.g. how we’re different / burned by agencies / leads aren’t everything]** so [CLOSER NAME] isn’t repeating the basics. Did you get a chance to watch it yet?

**If yes:**

> Perfect — anything stand out, or should we save it for [CLOSER NAME]?

📋 Log one line they mention — gold for closer notes.

**If no / not yet:**

> No stress — it’s **[~12 min / short]** and it’ll make today’s call way more productive. Can you watch it **before [TIME]**? I’ll resend the link right now while we’re on the phone.

→ Send link (SMS or email they confirm they use). Then:

> If I send that now — you’ll watch it before the call, yeah?

#### B — Nothing sent yet (or wrong video)

> Based on what you mentioned — **[THEIR OBJECTION OR MOTIVATOR IN THEIR WORDS]** — I’ve got a short video that’ll help you get the most out of the strategy call. Want me to send it?

📋 Pick asset from [quick-send table](../marketing/pre-call-objection-videos.md#quick-send-by-objection).

> It’s about **[12 minutes / a few minutes]** — not a pitch, just context so [CLOSER NAME] can go deeper on **your** situation instead of the basics.

> If I send that — you’ll watch it before the call, yeah?

> I’ll send it with a text right after this call. Our socials are there if you want extra research, but **that video is the main prep**.

**Send while on the phone** when possible (text: “Here’s the prep video for [TIME] today: [link]”).

#### C — Hard accountability (use when they’ve no-showed before or seem flaky)

> Quick favor — when you watch it, notice **[one specific thing — e.g. the three pillars / what we guarantee vs don’t]**. [CLOSER NAME] will ask what stood out so you’re not starting from zero. Fair?

Do not invent claims — only point at themes from the [video index](../marketing/pre-call-objection-videos.md#index).

---

### 5 — Closer handoff line (no selling)

> You’re set. I’ll make sure **[CLOSER NAME]** has your notes — **[ONE LINE: motivator or objection from CRM]**. See you **[DAY/TIME]**.

**Do not** explain packages, DFY vs Boot Camp, or guarantee language on this call. Tag Gabriel per [Watchshift SOP](sop-watchshift.md) if they raise new objections or pricing.

---

## Close checklist (every confirmed demo)

- [ ] Verbal **yes** on date/time + computer/Zoom
- [ ] Calendar invite **accepted** (or fixed contact + resent)
- [ ] Pre-call video **sent** (correct asset) + verbal **watch before call**
- [ ] GHL note: confirmation done, video sent/re-sent, watch commitment (Y/N), any line they said about the video
- [ ] Attempted **earlier slot** if within 24hrs and calendar allows

## SMS backup (no answer / after voicemail)

Use [Setter Lead Messaging](setter-lead-messaging.md) for general rules. Demo-specific patterns:

**Morning-of — confirmation + video**

> Hey [NAME], [Rep] from Waiz — confirming your strategy call with [CLOSER] **today at [TIME] [TZ]**. Zoom link is on the invite — block ~1 hr at your computer. Did you get a chance to watch the prep video I sent on [topic]? Link again: [prospect_page_url]. See you at [TIME].

**24–48hr — confirm + tie-down**

> Hey [NAME] — [Rep] from Waiz. Locked for **[DAY] [TIME]** with [CLOSER]. Accept the calendar invite when you can. Short prep video (~12 min) so the call isn’t basics: [prospect_page_url]. You’ll watch before we hop on, yeah?

**Voicemail drop (then text above)**

> Hey [NAME], [Rep] from Waiz — confirming **[DAY/TIME]** with [CLOSER]. Call me back at [NUMBER] or reply here. Sending the prep video link by text so you’re set before the call.

## CRM note template (paste after touch)

```
Demo confirm [DATE]: Spoke / VM+SMS / No contact
Time locked: Y/N — [DAY TIME TZ]
Calendar accepted: Y/N
Pre-call video: [asset # or title] — sent [date] / re-sent today
Watch commitment: Y/N
Prospect quote re video: “…”
Earlier slot offered: Y/N — outcome
Closer flags: [motivator / objection / logistics]
```

## Escalation

| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Wants to cancel or “not sure” | Light curiosity — what changed? — then tag **Gabriel** if fit is real; do not hard-close |
| New pricing / custom deal / guarantee push | Tag **Gabriel** — [Watchshift SOP](sop-watchshift.md) |
| Wrong person / bad fit discovered | Update stage + note; do not waste closer time |
| No contact 24hrs out | Multi-channel per [no-show levers](no-shows-maximizing-show-rates-setter-levers.md#24-hour-unresponsive-protocol) |
