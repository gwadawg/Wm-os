# Intro Call Basic Script

## Purpose

Basic intro call script aligned with qualification framework.

## Scope

Stage 1 intro calls.

## Owner

See [domain owners](../../_inventory/domain-owners.md): **setter**.

## When To Use

Use when executing or improving the workflow described in the source document. For offer routing and pricing rules, see [Money Model And Offer Architecture](../../company/overview-money-model-april-26.md).

## Quality Bar

- Follows Waiz voice: qualified conversations, acquisition system, strategic partner (see [Identity Core](../../company/doctrine-identity-core-april-26.md)).
- Pricing and deal structure escalated to founder when applicable.
- Boot Camp downsell offered when prospect is not DFY-qualified.

## Metrics

- Define or track metrics in [KPIs](../../kpis/README.md) as this workflow is operationalized.

## Operating Content

### 1️⃣ Inbound Intro Script
## WAIZ MEDIA
## Intro Call Script
### Internal Use Only  |  Sales & Setting Team  |  Updated: March 2026
### 📌  The One Job of This Call
Get the prospect to show up to the Demo Call. Qualify them. Warm them up. Book the meeting.Do NOT try to close. Do NOT handle every objection. Save your best material for the Demo.

### SECTION A — INBOUND LEAD (Already Booked)
Use this opening when the prospect booked an intro call through an ad or landing page.

## STAGE 1 — CONNECTION
### Hey, [NAME]?
### Hey [NAME], this is [SALES REP] from Waiz Media — how's it going?
I know that we have an appointment set for this time and I saw you're looking into potentially seeing how you can grow your reverse mortgage business. Sounds about right?

📋  Wait for confirmation. Clarify if needed before moving to the frame.

### IF They Ask "What Do You Guys Do?"
Yeah, so on a high-level basis, we help LOs in the reverse space scale through a consistent flow of leads and appointments. The way we operate is a bit different than what you might be used to with most lead gen companies — what we do is customized to each LO we work with, that way we can actually ensure results.

## STAGE 2 — FRAME
Try to transition cleanly… Good way is just to be like  “Well, pleasure to meet you!”

## High-Level Agenda
Cool so, What I found that works best on these calls is to first, get some clarity on you and the business to see what would be the best direction for you.

And of course, I’ll make sure you are caught up to speed with everything we do and how/if we can help you so you don’t waste any time.

### Sounds good?
### Buy-In (Permission)
Sooo ya, If it’s cool with you, maybe you can just kick us off here.. what got you to click on the ad in the first place?

### SHARED SCRIPT — Both Inbound & Self-Called
Everything below applies to both lead types. Run this in full on every call.

## DISCOVERY — STAGE 1: HELL (THE PROBLEM)
📋  Surface the main problem. Your job is to get them talking — listen more than you speak. Do NOT offer solutions yet.

### Q  What do you feel is the biggest bottleneck in the business right now?
### Q  Got it — and where are most of your leads coming from?
📋  Dig deeper if the answer is surface level. Don't accept vague answers — get them to articulate it clearly.

### Q  How many transactions did you do last year?
### Q  Okay, and this year so far?
### Q  So roughly [X]/month — is that consistent?
### Q  And are you a solo LO, or are you trying to feed a team?
### Q  About how long has [PROBLEM] been going on?
📋  PROBLEM = inconsistency, bad leads, low lead flow, etc.

### Q  Man — has that affected anything else?
## DISCOVERY — STAGE 2: BRIDGES (WHAT THEY'VE TRIED)
📋  Understand their history. Listen for objections brewing — note them, but do NOT handle them on this call. Save the reframes for the Demo.

### Q  Alright, talk to me — what have you tried to fix [PROBLEM] before?
### Q  How'd that go?
### Q  Anything else?
## DISCOVERY — STAGE 3: HEAVEN (THE GOAL)
📋  Shift the conversation forward. Where do they want to be? Why does it matter to them personally? These answers are your most powerful demo material.

Q  Okay, and just so I'm clear — where are you trying to take the business? How many loans do you want to be closing monthly?

Q  What's important to you in a company you partner with to make sure you actually hit that?

## DISCOVERY — STAGE 4: FUEL (COMMITMENT & URGENCY)
📋  Lock in their timeline and surface the real driver behind it. These are the three questions — ask them in order.

### Q  When are you looking to put something in place?
### Q  Okay gotcha — why is that?
### Q  Anything else you want to add before we start talking next steps?
### 💡  Pro Tip
If they say 'I'm not really sure when' or 'whenever' — gently push on question 2. Ask: 'If you could wave a magic wand, when would you want this solved?' Most people have a real answer — they just haven't been asked.

## ⏸  PAUSE — FUN QUALIFICATION CHECK
📋  Before moving forward — ask yourself: Is there ANYTHING you noticed in this conversation that could prevent them from buying on the Demo Call? Partners, finances, timing, etc.

💰  THE LEAD MUST BE F.U.N.

### F — Financially Qualified: Can they actually invest?
### U — Urgency: Are they looking to move in the next few weeks?
N — Need: Is their problem something Waiz Media actually solves?IF YES to an obstacle → address it directly before moving on.IF CONFIDENT they'd buy → move on.

## LABEL — CONFIRM YOUR UNDERSTANDING
📋  Reflect their situation back to them in their own words. This builds trust, confirms you were listening, and gets them to re-affirm their own problem out loud.

Gotcha — so correct me if I'm missing anything, but basically...

You're doing [CURRENT DEALS] per month and want to get to [DESIRED DEALS] per month...

But haven't been able to because of [PROBLEM]...

So you just need [WHAT THEY NEED IN A COMPANY] — and if you had that, you'd for sure be able to hit [DESIRED DEALS]...

And this is something you're looking to put in place [TIMELINE] because [FUEL].

### Am I understanding you correctly, [NAME]?
📋  Wait for their response. Do not rush past this — let them confirm or correct you.

### Are you cool if I share my thoughts with you?
## OFFER — PRE-PITCH
## Certainty — Grant Confidence You Can Help
With that being said, I really do think we can help you get to [GOAL].

## Reasons — Plant the Right Mindsets
We've helped tons of LOs who came to us with literally the exact same problem of [PROBLEM] — so while I wish I could say it's unique, it's not. On the bright side, this is our bread and butter.

And just so you know… what we're really looking for right now are for LOs that are serious about growing their business, who will follow through on their word, and willing to put in the work — but just need the right infrastructure and system to be put in place so they can focus more on sales calls.

## Lean Out — Let THEM Claim It
I mean… Would you think that describes you, [NAME]? ( Do you think you’re in the right position right now to take the next step, and really put in the work that’s necessary to start scaling your production?)

## Permission — Buy-In to Move On
So at this point, I can share what I think the best next step would be — if you're up for it.

## CONSULTATION — PITCH THE DEMO CALL
📋  You are NOT pitching the service here. You are pitching the Demo Call. Make them want to show up.

So what I think would be best from here is connecting you with [CLOSER NAME]. He's one of our top growth consultants and has helped hundreds of LOs just like you get clarity on their marketing strategy. (or what it’s going to really take for you to be able to hit {Goal} consistently every month)

I'll pass him the notes from our conversation — and when you guys connect, he can find the holes in your operations, show you the exact strategies we've used to help hundreds of LOs scale their businesses, and even cross 7 figures a year in commissions.

## If has experience
He'll also be able to show you what you've been missing, why [PAST BRIDGES] didn't work, and what you should actually be doing in 2026.

### Doesn’t have experience
He'll also be able to show you the biggest mistakes most LO’s make when coming into the reverse space, so that you will have clarity on the right path you need to take in order to be successful in this industry.

And also, if you want to know what it looks like to become a private client with us to implement the system for you — he can go over those details too. But at the very least, you walk out with a crystal-clear game plan for hitting [GOAL].

### Does that sound fair?
## TEMP CHECK
### Do you think that would help you?
📋  This is your soft close on the appointment. If hesitation surfaces here, handle the objection before booking. Do not push past resistance — address it.

### Investment / Logistics
Cool — so it'll be a Google meets call, so I'd just need you to be at your computer. Usually takes 45 minutes, but let's set aside an hour so you guys have room to go through any questions.

## CLOSE — BOOK THE APPOINTMENT
## Try Live Transfer First
Are you near a computer right now? I can see if one of our consultants is actually free to chat with you right now.

## Same Day
### What time later today works for that meeting?
## Next Day
### No worries — what about tomorrow? What time works there?
## 2 Days Out
### Gotcha — and the next day?
### 3+ Days Out
Gotcha, no worries. He's a little packed — let me see if I can find a spot. It usually doesn't let me book out this far, but I can make an exception if there's a time that FOR SURE works for you.

### And is there any reason [DATE AND TIME] wouldn't work for you?
## SELL THE PRE-CALL GUIDE (BEFORE BOOKING)
Cool — so I'll put you in for [DATE AND TIME]. You'll get that calendar invite here in just a second...

Now, I know you mentioned {INSERT UNIQUE ATTRIBUTE}  — I just had a thought. I've got a video that's only a couple minutes long, but it'll really help you guys move swiftly through that next call. Would you like that?

> **Video pick:** See [Pre-Call Objection Video Assets](../marketing/pre-call-objection-videos.md#quick-send-by-objection) — send the matching **prospect page** link (not YouTube).

### So if I sent that to you — you'll watch it, yeah?
Okay cool — I'll send that over with the calendar invite. I'll also drop you links to our socials so you can do extra research, but the Pre-Call Guide should give you everything you need.

### 🔴  ACTION: Book the Call Through GHL NOW
Do not confirm the appointment verbally and then forget to log it. Book through GoHighLevel immediately while they are on the line.

### CONFIRM THAT THEY GOT THE TEXT AND EMAIL!!!
## WRAP-UP — CONFIRM & HAND OFF

Now could you do me a solid and confirm that invite? Just want to make sure no one gets booked over it.

### 📋  Once confirmed:
Alright — you're all set! I'll send these notes over to [CLOSER NAME] and he'll see you at [DATE AND TIME].
