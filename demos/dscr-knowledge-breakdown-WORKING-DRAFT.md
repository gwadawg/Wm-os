## 1. The core positioning

**One big idea:** *The property qualifies itself.*

We’re not selling a cheaper loan. We’re selling a **different basis of approval**:
- Conventional underwrites *you* (W-2s, tax returns, DTI, property-count caps).
- DSCR underwrites *the rental* (rent vs payment).

**Waiz line = refinance only.** Business-purpose investment property the borrower already owns. No purchase. No primary residence. No consumer “relief” tone.

**Frame:** Investor-Partner (competence) — not Commodity-Lender (rate shop).

**Tone:** Peer-to-operator. Name DSCR openly. Never senior / fear / household relief.

**Honest trade:** rates are often higher than conventional. We win on access, freed equity, and process — not “best rate.”

**What they’re buying:** a better position on property they already own — equity redeployed, payment fixed, or exit from short-term debt — without W-2/DTI underwriting.

## 4. Strongest creative mode: equity velocity

**Limiting belief:** idle equity = safe / prudent.

**Reframe:** sitting equity is dead capital. Scalers don’t wait for new cash — they pull capital from what they already own and redeploy it.

**Don’t pitch a loan. Reveal how sophisticated investors already operate.**

| Mode | Viewer feels | Job |
|------|--------------|-----|
| Ego stroke | “That’s already me.” | Confirm strategy; invite next move |
| Aspiration | “I want to operate like that.” | Model the habit; show the path |

**Signature lines:**
- “Real investors don’t wait for capital. They take it from what they already own.”
- “If you already have a portfolio, you’ve done the hardest part.”
- “Your equity isn’t a trophy. It’s inventory.”
- “You don’t have to sell a door to fund the next one.”

**Pattern:** identity call-out → break idle-equity belief → model the move → name DSCR lightly → CTA (“see what your doors may unlock”) — not “apply for a loan.”

## 5. The five proven creative angles (the slate)

Cold default test order: **Angle 3 → 2 → 1/5**. Angle 4 is warm/BOF only.

### Angle 1 — The Idle-Equity Tax

**Persona:** Portfolio Scaler · **Stage:** TOF · **Slug:** `cashout-grow`

**Big idea:** Unused equity is an invisible monthly cost. Leaving it parked isn’t “safe” — it’s an expense.

**Psychology:** loss aversion, mental accounting, behavioral modeling.

**Hooks:**
1. Your equity is just sitting there. Put it to work.
2. The most expensive thing you own is the equity you’re not using.
3. You don’t have to sell a door to get your capital back.
4. Real investors don’t wait for capital. They take it from what they already own.
5. If you already have a portfolio, you’ve done the hardest part.

**Primary text (canonical):**

> Every month your equity sits locked inside doors you already own, it quietly costs you something — the down payment on the next deal, the rehab you keep postponing, the capital that should be compounding.
>
> A DSCR cash-out refinance pulls it out and puts it back to work. The property qualifies on its own rent — not your W-2, not your tax returns, not your DTI…
>
> See what the doors you already own may qualify for.

**CTA:** See If Your Property Qualifies

**Visual world:** row of rental doors / locked vault / equity sitting still vs moving. Operator energy. No consumer/family imagery.

---

### Angle 2 — The Deadline

**Persona:** Hard-Money / Balloon · **Stage:** TOF · **Slug:** `balloon-exit`

**Big idea:** The note has a date — map the exit before it. Only DSCR lane where urgency is *real*, not manufactured.

**Voice:** calm operator mapping an exit — never panic / red-alarm aesthetics.

**Hooks:**
1. Balloon coming due? Refinance before it hits.
2. Hard money got you in. It was never the plan to stay.
3. The worst time to refinance a balloon is the month it matures.

**Primary text (canonical):**

> That bridge loan did exactly what it was supposed to do — got you the property, funded the rehab, got it rented. But it has a date on it…
>
> A DSCR refinance is the standard exit: a long-term loan that qualifies on the property’s rent, not your income…
>
> Don’t price your exit the week the balloon hits.

**CTA:** Map Your Exit

**Visual:** calendar with circled maturity / short-term vs long-term timeline.

### Angle 3 — “Did You Know?” / Program Call-Out

**Persona:** Self-Employed / Write-Off · **Stage:** TOF · **Slug:** `nodocs-speed`

**Big idea:** Most investors don’t know this program exists for them. Curiosity + “wait, that’s me.”

Lane that includes: **tax returns say you’re broke / write-offs kill DTI.**

**Hook family A — Did you know?**
1. Did you know there’s a loan program for RE investors that doesn’t look at your income — at all?
2. Did you know you can refinance a rental with zero tax returns? Most investors don’t.
3. Did you know your rental can qualify for a refinance all by itself — no W-2, no 1040, no DTI?

**Hook family B — ICP call-out**
4. Real estate investors: there’s a refinance program where your income docs stay in the drawer.
5. If you own rental property, there’s a program that qualifies the property — not you.
6. Attention rental owners: the program most lenders never mention — no tax returns, no income verification.

**GOLD example:**

> Your tax returns say you’re broke. Your rentals say otherwise.
> A DSCR refinance qualifies on the property’s rent — not your W-2.
> See if your rental may qualify.

**Primary text (canonical):**

> There’s a loan program built specifically for real estate investors — and most have never been offered it.
>
> It’s called a DSCR refinance… no tax returns, no W-2s, no pay stubs, no personal DTI. The property’s rent does the qualifying. If the rental covers its own payment, it may qualify — even if your write-offs make your 1040 look tiny…
>
> Most investors find out about this program two denials too late. Now you know.

**CTA:** See If Your Rental Qualifies

**Visual:** big “DID YOU KNOW?” card, or direct-to-camera LO with the question in first 3 seconds.

**Compliance:** avoid literal “exclusive.” Prefer “a program most investors have never been offered.”

## 17. Campaign architecture (working — fundamental launch structure)

> This section is the proposed spine for how we launch Meta campaigns.
> It separates three things that were previously mashed together:
> **persona (constraint)** · **awareness / temperature** · **creative job**.
> Plus a fourth creative job that client custom work depends on:
> **lender authority**.

### 17a. Stop using one word for three things

| Axis | Question it answers | What it is NOT |
|------|---------------------|----------------|
| **Persona / constraint bucket** | What is blocking them from acting? | Not “profile,” not ambition, not property type |
| **Awareness + temperature** | Where is their head, and where are they relative to us? | Not the same as persona |
| **Use case** | What would they do with the money? | Not a persona, not a cold angle |
| **Creative job** | What is this ad trying to make them believe or do? | Not a fourth persona |

### 17b. Three constraint buckets (personas that earn an ad set)

| Bucket | Constraint | Enemy | Job they hire DSCR for | Old labels that collapse here |
|--------|------------|-------|------------------------|-------------------------------|
| **BLOCKED** | Would fail conventional underwriting | The bank’s basis of approval | Qualify me on a different basis | Write-Off, property-count, STR income, Foreign National |
| **CORNERED** | Has a date on the note | The calendar | Get me out before maturity | Hard-money / balloon / bridge |
| **TRAPPED** | Nothing’s broken — capital is idle | Their own status-quo bias | Put my equity back to work | Portfolio Scaler / idle equity |

**STR and Foreign National are modifiers inside BLOCKED**, not peer personas.
Same constraint (fails conventional), same job, different reason for the failure.

### 17c. Temperature vs awareness (don’t conflate)

- **TOF / MOF / BOF** = where traffic sits relative to *us* (cold / engaged / high-intent). Media decision.
- **Awareness** = where their head sits relative to the *problem and product* (unaware → most-aware). Message decision.

These are independent. A balloon due in 60 days who’s never heard of you = cold traffic at high awareness. An equity clicker in retargeting who still thinks parked equity is safe = warm traffic at low awareness.

| Bucket | Typical entry awareness | Cold creative job | Warm creative job |
|--------|-------------------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| CORNERED | Solution / most aware | Deadline + mechanism | Offer + authority |
| BLOCKED | Problem aware, solution unaware | Program reveal (“tax returns say broke”) | Checklist + authority |
| TRAPPED | Problem *unaware* | Belief break (idle equity) — sequence, not one ad | Outcome uses → offer → authority |

**TRAPPED cold is a sequence**, not a single lead ad: belief-break first (judge on engagement), then mechanism, then offer.

### 17d. Use cases = mid-funnel currency

Pay off balloon · fund rehab · build reserves · lower payment · move into LLC — these are **not personas**. They prove relevance once someone is already solution-aware. Live at MOF. Also re-segment warm traffic (form “goal” question should route nurture).

### 17e. Creative jobs (what the ad is for)

Every DSCR ad has one primary job. Don’t average two jobs into one bland ad.

| Creative job | Funnel home | What it does | Existing / proposed slug |
|--------------|-------------|--------------|--------------------------|
| **Belief / problem** | TOF (esp. TRAPPED) | Create or name the pain | `cashout-grow` (Angle 1) |
| **Program reveal** | TOF (BLOCKED) | Show a different basis of approval exists | `nodocs-speed` |
| **Urgency / exit** | TOF (CORNERED) | Map the exit before the date | `balloon-exit` |
| **Outcome / uses** | MOF | Make the capital vivid | `cashout-grow` Angle 5 variant |
| **Offer / checklist** | BOF / warm | Publish the stack; self-filter | Checklist — **needs slug** (e.g. `qualify-stack`) |
| **Lender authority** | MOF→BOF / warm (sometimes light MOF) | “Why *this* LO / shop” — brand, access, multi-product, competence | **`lo-authority`** (proposed) |

### 17f. Lender authority — the client-custom layer (fundamental)

Most repo creative talks about **DSCR the product** in generic voice. Client fulfillment also needs ads that sell **the specific LO / company** as the person to work with.

**What this ad is:**
- Client name / face / brand on the creative
- “We run DSCR” · access to multiple programs / products · investor specialist positioning
- Competence and trust — Investor-Partner frame, not rate shop
- Goal: make the viewer choose *this* shop once they already want (or nearly want) the product

**What this ad is NOT:**
- A fourth persona
- A cold “introduce DSCR from zero” vehicle (usually)
- A substitute for the three constraint buckets

**Funnel home (default):** **warm / BOF**, with optional **MOF** for engagers who already know the product category.

Why mostly bottom: cold traffic has to care about the *problem* before they care *who* solves it. Authority before problem = wasted impressions unless the LO already has personal-brand recognition in that market.

**When it can sit higher:**
- LO has local / niche fame (content, BiggerPockets, referral brand)
- Retargeting people who already watched a problem/reveal ad
- Search / high-intent adjacent (product-aware traffic)

**Psychology this job uses:** Authority bias · Liking / similarity (“built for investors by someone who gets investors”) · Unity · Pratfall (honest on rates) · Social proof when real · Mere exposure (client face repeating across warm creatives).

**Variants inside `lo-authority` (same concept family, different hooks):**
1. **Face + specialist claim** — “I’m [Name]. DSCR is what I do.”
2. **Access / multi-product** — “We shop multiple DSCR programs so the property finds a fit — not one rigid box.”
3. **Investor fluency** — “You don’t have to explain write-offs, LLCs, or STR income to your LO.”
4. **Proof of process** — funded outcomes, speed-to-clarity (only with real substantiated proof)
5. **Brand + CTA** — logo/name dominant, soft “talk to [Shop] about your rental”

**Naming (proposed — register when promoted out of draft):**

```text
dscr_lo-authority_st_v1          # static, client-branded
dscr_lo-authority_ugc_v1         # LO talking head
dscr_lo-authority_st_v1a         # sibling: multi-product access variant
```

- Concept slug: `lo-authority` (2 tokens, noun-led, no funnel stage in the name)
- Client identity lives in **overview / summary / visual_notes** and on-image copy — **not** in the ad_name token (keeps Mr. Waiz rollups clean across clients)
- Optional later: client slug in campaign/ad-set name only: `{client-slug}_dscr_warm` → ad set `warm_all_authority`

**Do not use:** `brand`, `client-brand`, `about-us`, `trust` as competing slugs — fold under `lo-authority`.

| `warm_trapped_outcome` | TRAPPED | Outcome / uses | `cashout-grow` (Angle 5) |
| `warm_all_offer` | All | Checklist / offer | `qualify-stack` (proposed) |
| `warm_all_authority` | All | Lender authority | `lo-authority` (proposed) |

**Ad =** `dscr_{concept}_{format}_v{n}` — existing convention. Authority creatives are client-custom *inside* that concept, not a separate naming system.

### 17h. Per-bucket KPIs (don’t kill equity on CPL)

| Bucket | Entry awareness | Touches to lead | Judge cold on |
|--------|-----------------|-----------------|---------------|
| CORNERED | High | 1–2 | CPL → CPQL |
| BLOCKED | Mid | 1–2 | CPL → CPQL |
| TRAPPED | Low | 3+ | Cost per engaged view / LP engagement first — not CPL |
| Authority (warm) | Product / most aware | 1 | CPQL, booked rate, show rate — not vanity CTR |

Barbell: ~80% Cornered + Blocked while proving; ~20% Trapped as sequence. Authority opens when warm pool exists (week 1–2), not as the only cold creative.

### 17i. How authority fits the “who to work with” question

Once an investor is solution- or product-aware, the remaining decision is often **which DSCR shop**, not **whether DSCR**. Generic product ads stop working here; client-branded authority ads start working.

Funnel picture:

```text
TOF  → Problem / reveal / deadline / belief   (product idea, often generic voice)
MOF  → Mechanism + uses + light competence    (can start naming the LO)
BOF  → Checklist + LO authority + book        (client face/brand is the point)
```

Custom client ads (“we run DSCR, multiple products, leading professionals”) belong in that **BOF / warm_all_authority** lane by default — and can be tested as MOF for engagers.

## 18. Proposed creative standard v0.2 (post-council — brainstorm)

> Outcome of the 2026-09-16 council stress test. Creative layer only —
> campaign structure deferred. Candidate standard of truth once approved.

### 18a. The one governing question

Every DSCR prospect sorts by a single question: **what stops this investor
from acting today?** Not who they are, not the property, not the use of
proceeds. The constraint. Creative IS the targeting (Special Ad Category),
so the constraint is the only segmentation that changes what an ad says.

### 18b. Buckets renamed by situation (council: emotion-names collide)

BLOCKED / CORNERED / TRAPPED are near-synonyms in English — outsiders and
AI agents will confuse them. Same buckets, situation-named:

| Bucket | Old name | One-line definition | Enemy | Entry awareness |
|--------|----------|---------------------|-------|-----------------|
| **DENIED** | Blocked | Would fail conventional underwriting today | Bank's basis of approval | Problem-aware, solution-unaware |
| **DEADLINE** | Cornered | Note has a maturity date forcing action | The calendar | Solution / most-aware |
| **IDLE** | Trapped / Dormant | Nothing broken — equity idle by choice | Their own status-quo bias | Problem-unaware |

> **Promoted draft SOT:** [dscr-creative-taxonomy.md](../docs/client-fulfillment/dscr-dna/dscr-creative-taxonomy.md)
> (still `status: draft` until founder approval).

### 18c. Sorting rule (mutual exclusivity — protects the rollup)

Overlap is real (balloon borrower with write-offs = both). Fixed priority,
checked in order — one ad / lead / concept = exactly one bucket:

1. **Date on the note?** → DEADLINE (urgency dominates any message)
2. **No date, but conventional would say no?** → DENIED
3. **Could act but isn't?** → IDLE
4. **Already shopping DSCR terms?** → IN-MARKET

### 18d. Modifiers, not buckets

STR income, foreign national, LLC vesting, free-and-clear = modifiers
inside a bucket (almost always DENIED). They change the example in the
creative, never the constraint. Rule: same enemy + same job-to-be-done =
one bucket, different proof points.

### 18e. Use cases are fuel, not buckets

Pay off balloon / fund rehab / reserves / LLC answer "what would you do
with it," not "what stops you." Ads are never organized by use case —
only illustrated by one. Lives in MOF creative + form goal question.

### 18f. Each bucket owns ONE cold creative job

| Bucket | Cold job | Ad must make them think… | Emotion | Slug |
|--------|----------|--------------------------|---------|------|
| DENIED | Program reveal | "Wait — that's me. Why hasn't anyone offered me this?" | Recognition / vindication | `nodocs-speed` |
| DEADLINE | Mapped exit | "Price the exit now, not the month it matures." | Calm control — never panic | `balloon-exit` |
| IDLE | Belief break | "Idle equity isn't safety — it's cost." | Ambition / loss aversion | `cashout-grow` |

Warm jobs (outcome/uses, checklist/offer, lender authority) are **shared
across buckets**, not bucket-owned. Cold creative speaks one bucket's
language; warm creative can speak to everyone. Campaign mapping deferred.

### 18f-2. The in-market exception (rate-card / terms ads)

Proven winner that fits no bucket: straight "DSCR cash-out refinance" +
terms card (`ratecard-centered` layout, Angle 4 family). The governing
question doesn't apply — nothing stops this viewer; they're already
shopping. Awareness: product / most-aware. The terms card IS the filter:
non-shoppers scroll, shoppers stop. Effectively search intent harvested
on Meta.

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Bucket tag | `in-market` (bucket-agnostic — no constraint to speak to) |
| Awareness | Product-aware / most-aware |
| Primary job | Offer / terms (`qualify-stack` / `ratecard-centered`) |
| Secondary job | Lender authority when "our program" framing present |
| KPI profile | DEADLINE-like: 1–2 touches, CPL → CPQL |
| Known weakness | Shoppers rate-compare — watch quality per close separately |

Rules: not a fourth constraint bucket (buckets move people; this harvests
people already moving). Numbers from confirmed token sheet only. Never
literal "exclusive" — use "a program most lenders don't offer."

### 18g. Old angles → new addresses (nothing cut)

- Angle 3 (Did You Know) → DENIED cold
- Angle 2 (Deadline) → DEADLINE cold
- Angle 1 (Idle-Equity Tax) → IDLE cold
- Angle 5 (What You Could Do With It) → shared warm outcome, IDLE-flavored
- Angle 4 (Checklist / rate-card) → shared warm offer, all buckets **and** cold `in-market` harvest (§18f-2)
- Lender authority → shared warm, all buckets

### 18h. Honesty clause per bucket (KPI expectations, set now)

- **DEADLINE**: fastest convert (calendar sells), smallest audience
- **DENIED**: fast once revealed (pain exists), medium audience
- **IDLE**: largest audience, slowest convert — belief-change project.
  Council hard rule: still gets a terminal CPQL metric + pre-committed
  review date, or it becomes the lane where pet creative hides forever.
